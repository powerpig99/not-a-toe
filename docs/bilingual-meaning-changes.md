# Bilingual Conversion: Meaning and Framing Changes

Running log for the owner's end review of the bilingual conversion run. From 2026-10-16 the owner asked that review fixes be applied without per-post confirmation, and that every change to meaning or framing be listed here. Mechanical fixes (layout, link anchors, ZH/EN wording drift that keeps the meaning, "costume" glosses spelled out as "in the case of X") are not listed. Each entry names the post, what changed, and why.

## technology-is-residual-of-human-compounding

- Subtitle: was "Human multiplied by successive factors", which is the whole modified continuum (H + T); now "the residual of Human compounding through successive self-modification", which is T, matching the title and the identity.
- Section 1: technology as externalized artifact is now named as the durable part *within* the residual, not a second definition of it; T still covers skills and lost abilities.
- Section 2: (1 + dHₙ) is the factor and dHₙ its increment; T is now "the difference between the product and one", an excess above one and a shortfall below, not only an "excess".
- Section 2: the "N → ∞" mark is removed (no formula contains it); the sentence keeps that further change stays possible without a completed infinity or an exterior destination.
- Section 3: "amplification and regression" is now "amplification and contraction", so a factor below one shrinks magnitude without reversing the arrow of shaping; added that each factor stays above zero (1 + dHₙ > 0) while the center continues.
- Section 3: Openness Is Consistency and Production, Consumption are now two further axes (structure; registration), no longer "the same cut" as factor alignment.
- Section 4: technology is no longer named only once the residual is "large enough"; public discourse mostly names it at the aggregate scale. "Markets" dropped from the public-object list.
- Section 5: the one-step look is ordinary; Image lag is freezing it as a permanent second locus (matching The Model Never Becomes a Second Edge and Causality Stays at the Edge That Steers).
- Close: AI is now the newest form the residual takes and, per The Ladder of Abstraction, a new order of abstraction executing outside the skull, while initiation does not move; was "the latest rung of that residue", which dropped the novelty that post names.
- Link glosses corrected to their targets: the Phase 3 readout is years of prior work crossing a newsworthy threshold (residue sits on the platform); Not Predicting is the compounded continuum; The Average is the decisive edge in the right tail; Hierarchy's gaps widen by diverging sequences; the Local Trap adds hype or panic; Essential Control adds bounds.

## the-free-mind-is-displaced-by-what-claims-to-protect-it

- Core verb unified: initiation never leaves the locus; what moves is the account (attribution) of where freedom sits. Lead, subtitle, section 4 ("given away", "relocates the lever") and section 6 ("displaced") now say this; before, they asserted a transfer of agency that section 5 denied.
- Lead: dropped "recognized as the most valuable thing in the world" as the traditions' shared claim (it is Steinbeck's valuation); the lead's subject is now a thinker or a tradition's founders, not a tradition.
- Section 1: the nesting happens "within the same teaching or in the hands of those who keep it", so the Church-versus-mystics case fits.
- Section 2: retitled "Steinbeck's Last Claim"; the error is placed on the verbs ("limits", "destroys") that make residue the agent, not on the fight. "The first claim holds" now reads: what holds is the self-origination the first two claims point to.
- Section 3: the Upanishadic identity with Brahman and the "divine" spark are now named as where the nesting already begins (placing the locus inside a larger same).
- Section 3: Stoicism no longer "reinserts" the mind into the Logos; meeting fate is meeting residue, and the cut is that freedom's proper expression is given an external condition. ZH 主导理性 → 主导机能.
- Section 3 close: what is managed/moved is the account of who holds the freedom, not the seeing.
- Section 4: protective institutions are stabilized loops of free variables; what is surrendered is the holding that change originates at the locus (variable still exercised, update stops). Dropped "to avoid friction". "Value and direction depend on no outer authority" → "need no outer authority to authorize" them.
- Section 5: retitled "Resonance Does Not Finish Another Mind's Return" (residue arriving as signal is causal); the second reading is now "the picture the cut refuses, returning", not a corollary. "Agreement densifies as reinforcement" reversed to "reinforcement registers as agreement".
- Link glosses corrected to their targets: No Universal Salvation (no cross-inner completion, no terminus); Liberation Rhetoric (install held as what they are); Externalized Virtue (densified by compliance into its opposite); Form of Enlightenment (a neighboring mechanism, both inversions); Welfare State (both errors; loss function for competence, not "growth").

## the-rational-choice-postulate-is-the-original-sin

- Title shortened (conversion): "…Is the Original Sin of All the Wrongs in Modern Economics Theories" → "The Rational Choice Postulate Is the Original Sin"; subtitle shortened to "Uniform consistency is installed as foundation while choice continues only under private, provisional premises."
- Conversion: "Few examine… Most, including many practitioners and experts, are drawn instead by the labels of success" → the theories "are seldom examined at the level of their foundational premise; the labels of success attached to them draw attention instead" (no claim about what most people do). "Untenable premise" → "an axiom the generating premises cannot meet".
- Lead: "every later wrong is the logical consequence" narrowed to every later theoretical move within this apparatus; section 5's subject narrowed from "modern theories" to theories built on the postulate; consciousness and success literature now "across adjacent domains", not consequences of the postulate.
- One variable and many levers: each choice is its own lever, and choice is the sole independent variable (section 1 gloss and section 8; EN no longer says "variables").
- "The prior" no longer names the ground without a referent (the linked Rationality essay uses "prior" for the postulate): section 2 now says the generating fact, choice under private, provisional premises; section 5 says the privacy and provisionality of premises deny stability (was "irreducible choice").
- Section 2: the structure cannot be maintained as a "stable, shared or representative" property (was "universal or even typical").
- Section 3: "residual difference" → "unshared difference"; the postulate demotes opposition to "a negligible perturbation" (was "residual"); exogenous "taste" is named as a label (ZH 口味, not 偏好).
- Section 4: averaging collapses a distribution into one number, and what the postulate licenses is promoting that quotient to an independent variable (was: averaging "arrives only after the postulate"); "redistributes" → "compresses"; "zero predictability" now attaches to the quotient, "weak" to the theories. The drivers of change are the discrete acts, not "the differences".
- Section 5: retrospective fit is cheap where it is asked to travel as foresight or foundation; section 8: as a foundation the postulate is not a useful approximation, and released it serves local retrospective reconstruction.
- Section 6: institutions "pattern the field of payoffs and constraints" (was "shape the premises", which gave them a hand in the next choice); retitled accordingly.
- Link glosses corrected: Price as Utterance (bids and asks cross in price, size, timing); Sovereignty (sovereignty never transfers; the belief that it can hardens into procedure); Causality All the Way (now the restore, not the mistake); Validation (the mind's own scrutiny vs responses marked external); Rationality Never Travels ("serviceable here" dropped); Consciousness ("public effects").
