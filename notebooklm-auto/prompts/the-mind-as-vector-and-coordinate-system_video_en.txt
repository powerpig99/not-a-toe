### [Monologue Script]
Why do minor disagreements in everyday life so quickly escalate into defensive hostility?

Because we subconsciously cling to the illusion of a shared objective coordinate system—believing that facts sit inside a single neutral container, and that anyone failing to see what we see must be irrational or hostile.

In causal reality, that "view from nowhere" is a complete fiction.

There is no transcendent coordinate frame outside of mind. Each conscious awareness is not merely a vector inside a static room, but the living generator actively spanning the coordinate axes of that room.

What you perceive of the other is never an unmediated external object, but an inward orthogonal projection—a scalar dot product of their signal cast upon your own intentional axis.

And here is the crucial breakthrough: true resonance is never about forcing two minds into "parallel alignment." Alignment still treats the other as an alienated entity across a dualistic void.

True resonance happens when you stop seeing the projection of the other as a foreign piece, but recognize it as an integral, inseparable part of your own living totality.

When you make that integration, you effectively pivot 90 degrees relative to everything you perceive, opening a brand-new orthogonal dimension above the plane of objects.

Two truly resonant minds do not collapse into flat uniformity. They dance like the electric and magnetic fields in an electromagnetic light wave: strictly orthogonal (E ⊥ B), mutually generating, weaving together into a self-propagating beam of radiant light.
