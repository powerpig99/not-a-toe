### [Monologue Script]
Why do disagreements in everyday life so easily trigger defensiveness?

Often because we subconsciously assume a shared objective coordinate system—believing facts sit inside a single neutral container, and that differing perspectives imply error or hostility.

In reality, there is no transcendent coordinate frame outside of mind. Each conscious awareness is both a state vector and the active generator spanning its own coordinate axes.

What you perceive of the other is an inward projection—a scalar dot product of their signal cast upon your own intentional axis.

And here is a helpful distinction: resonance is not about forcing minds into "parallel alignment." Pursuing alignment often leads to suppressing differences or demanding conformity.

Resonance happens when you stop seeing the other's projection as an alienated piece, and recognize it as part of your own perceptual continuum.

When you make that shift, you open a 90-degree orthogonal dimension above the plane of objects.

Resonant minds do not collapse into uniformity. They interact like the electric and magnetic fields in a light wave: holding orthogonal balance (E ⟂ B), mutually generating, and moving forward together.
