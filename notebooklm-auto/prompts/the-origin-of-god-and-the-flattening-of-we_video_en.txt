### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
Where did the concept of God actually come from?

It begins with the irreducible nature of observation: the Mind's choice is the primary causal ground. Physical constraints are shared equally across all conscious minds—so a shared constraint can never explain why individual trajectories diverge so radically. What enters perception is an active, sovereign act of attention: what the Mind sees is what it chooses to attend to.

To navigate the world, the Mind steps back and renders a total overview of its perceptual field, placing a small representation of itself inside. 

In that moment of total panorama, the Mind experiences an immense overview alongside personal powerlessness. But it temporarily forgets that it is its own origin computing the entire scene. It mistakes an internal simulation for an unanchored 'view from nowhere.'

When that unanchored overview is projected outward and given an independent identity, a literal God is created. When shared across a population, it becomes religion.

And this exact same mechanism operates in the secular world today. 

When science is detached from personal causal feedback, it becomes just another surrogate dogma. When people speak of 'Humanity' as a collective, they step outside real individuals and take a one-dimensional statistical average along narrow variables, flattening billions of soaring parallel universes into data points.

It even hides in everyday speech. Whenever someone says 'we must do this,' the speaker subconsciously claims the control seat, turning all other minds into objects to be manipulated.

Treating physical objects as objects works—they follow deterministic laws. But treating another mind as an object is a categorical mistake: it completely ignores the active free variable underneath.

True sovereignty requires no ten-step manual. It requires only stepping down from the imagined pulpit and re-anchoring at the first-person origin. 

What is seen is what was chosen. Taking one hundred percent structural responsibility for the Mind's own attention and unhedged actions (+1), respecting every other mind as a sovereign universe in parallel, allows life to unfold naturally and powerfully in an open cosmos.
