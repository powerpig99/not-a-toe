### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
Where did the concept of God actually come from?

It begins with the irreducible nature of observation: individual choice is the primary causal ground. What you experience is not a passive imprint of an external container, but an active, sovereign act of attention—what you see is what you choose to attend to.

To navigate the world, your mind steps back and renders a total overview of its perceptual field, placing a small representation of yourself inside. 

In that moment of total panorama, you experience an immense overview alongside personal powerlessness. But you temporarily forget that it is your own mind computing the entire scene. You mistake an internal simulation for an unanchored 'view from nowhere.'

When that unanchored overview is projected outward and given an independent identity, you create a literal God. When it is shared across a population, it becomes religion.

And this exact same mechanism operates in our secular world today. 

When science is detached from personal causal feedback, it becomes just another surrogate dogma. When we speak of 'Humanity' as a collective, we step outside real people and take a one-dimensional statistical average along narrow variables, flattening billions of soaring parallel universes into data points.

It even hides in everyday speech. Whenever we say 'we must do this,' we subconsciously place ourselves in the control seat, turning everyone else into an object to be manipulated.

Treating physical objects as objects works—they follow deterministic laws. But treating another mind as an object is a categorical mistake: it completely ignores the active free variable underneath.

True sovereignty requires no ten-step manual. It requires only that you step down from the imagined pulpit and re-anchor at your first-person origin. 

What you see is what you choose to attend to. Take one hundred percent structural responsibility for your own actions (+1), respect every other mind as a sovereign universe in parallel, and let your life unfold naturally in an open cosmos.
