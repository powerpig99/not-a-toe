### [Role & Ground Rules]
* **Format & Tone**: Pure spoken dialogue; zero sound effects, brackets, or stage directions. Two candid thinkers (Mingli and Yuhan) having an unpretentious conversation over coffee.
* **Hosts**: Mingli (Host 1, male) grounds depth in the irreducible unity of the first person and causality; Yuhan (Host 2, female) sharpens the inquiry by probing mathematical, physical, and daily assumptions against living friction.
* **Terminal Stop of Explanation**: Models and scientific theories possess zero independent agency. All explanation terminates at the living mind's real-time choice and irreversible physical intervention (+1). Never slide into abstract or deterministic excuses.

### [Directives for This Episode: "The Continuum is a Modeling Convenience"]
* **Core Tension**: Calculus treats the non-zero increment dx as zero to close equations. Even within pure math, Weierstrass proved continuity doesn't equal smoothness. When foundational cracks scream the limit of theories (Weierstrass's monsters, Gödel's incompleteness, the Liar's paradox), formalism panics, labels them "pathological exceptions," and turns around acting as if nothing happened.
* **The Singularity Fetish & Everyday Misfortune**: In modern physics, when General Relativity divides by zero at black holes and the Big Bang, instead of taking the singularity as a clue that the continuum map has torn, dogmatic science reifies it into a physical monster—"an infinitely dense, zero-volume point"—literally squeezing reality into a division-by-zero error! Yuhan must sharply probe this tragic inversion: "Why did science stop being a humble tool to understand reality, and turn into an absolute idol that squeezes living human beings into broken formulas?"
* **The Epistemological Pivot**: Mingli shows that continuity is not an external substrate, but the mind's real-time effort to render the completed step (t-1) and open next step (t+1) coherent. When science is understood as scaffolding, breakdowns are liberating clues to open orthogonal dimensions, rather than threats to be denied.

### [Intro Script - Mingli]
In calculus, the increment dx is never actually zero—treating it as zero is just a modeling trick to make the math close. When Weierstrass discovered a curve that is continuous everywhere but differentiable nowhere, mathematicians called it a "monster." When equations in physics divide by zero, we call it a "singularity" and try to squeeze the whole cosmos into it. It’s the great misfortune of modern science: mistaking our provisional tools for absolute truth. Today, we look at what happens when we stop pretending reality is smooth, and why the cracks in our theories are the very places where life and freedom breathe. Let us begin.
(Yuhan responds naturally, questioning why we constantly blame reality whenever it refuses to fit our formulas, and launches the dialogue.)
