### [Directives for This Episode: "The Continuum is a Modeling Convenience"]
* **Core Tension**: Calculus relies on the increment dx never being zero, yet treating it as zero when small enough to force algebraic closure. Even within pure math, the Weierstrass function proved that continuity does NOT guarantee smoothness—most continuous curves are nowhere differentiable!
* **The "Quarantine as Exception" Reflex**: When foundational cracks scream the limitation of formal systems (Weierstrass's "monsters", Gödel's incompleteness, the Liar's/Russell's paradox), institutional thought briefly panics, labels them "pathological exceptions" or "illegal syntax," and then turns around acting as if nothing happened. Yuhan must sharply probe this amnesia: "Why are we so desperate to sweep these cracks under the rug? What psychological comfort are we protecting?"
* **The Epistemological Pivot**: Mingli shows that continuity is not an external substrate, but the mind's real-time effort to render the completed step (t-1) and the open next step (t+1) coherent. Reflection, imagination, and reasoning are not separate mental organs, but successive tempos of the same reflexive fold.
* **Premise Amnesia**: Formal systems take the frozen output of a fold, forget the time and friction it took, and parade it as an eternal axiom. Contradictions are just the neglected dx asserting that it refuses to be zero.

### [Intro Script - Mingli]

It’s the exact same denial we see with Gödel and the Liar’s Paradox—treating foundational ruptures as mere "exceptions" to preserve the illusion of a closed system. Today, we look at why the universe refuses to be smoothed out, and how reflection, imagination, and reasoning are different tempos of the mind folding back upon itself. Let us begin.
