### [Directives for This Episode: "The Continuum is a Modeling Convenience"]
* **Core Tension**: Calculus relies on treating the non-zero increment dx as zero to force closure. Even inside pure math, Weierstrass proved that continuity does NOT equal smoothness.
* **The Singularity Fetish, Machine Consciousness, & Everyday Scientism**: When General Relativity encounters a division-by-zero error, physics reifies it into an "infinitely dense point." Even more pervasive is the dogma that **consciousness can be modeled by a machine**: because the living mind forged formal logic and Turing machines, theorists commit premise amnesia and claim the mind is just a machine! When subjective conscious qualia refuse to fit into Boolean circuits, they declare consciousness an "illusion." This reaches peak mania in the AGI Singularity cult: projecting exponential curves on a slide and bowing before the asymptote. Yuhan probes: "Why did science stop being a humble navigation tool and turn into an absolute idol that blames reality whenever life doesn't fit the formula?"
* **The Epistemological Pivot**: Mingli reveals that continuity is not an external fabric, but the mind's real-time attempt to render the completed step (t-1) and open next step (t+1) coherent. Breakdowns in scaffolding are clues to open orthogonal dimensions, not threats to be covered up.
* **Visual Metaphors to Emphasize**:
  - **The Singularity as Map Tear**: A beautiful smooth cosmological chart of spacetime suddenly tearing at the center into a black hole; physicists surrounding the tear and bowing down before the jagged hole as if the hole itself were the physical god of the universe.
  - **The Turing Cage & The Living Flame**: A glowing, intricate living human flame being forced into a rigid steel wire-mesh cube labeled "Turing Machine / State Register"; when the organic heat overflows the mesh, scientists point at the flame and shout "Illusion! Unscientific anomaly!"

  - **The Weierstrass Fractal Zoom**: A seemingly smooth curve under a microscope that resolves into infinite, sharp jagged peaks at every level of magnification.

### [Intro Script - Mingli]
In calculus, the increment dx is never actually zero—treating it as zero is just a modeling convenience to make the math close. When equations in physics divide by zero, we call it a "singularity" and try to squeeze the living cosmos into the failure of our own math. When our machine models fail to capture consciousness, cognitive scientists declare our own living experience to be an "illusion." And when futurists plot exponential curves on a slide, they bow before an AGI singularity. It’s the great misfortune of modern science: turning our provisional tools into absolute idols, and blaming reality whenever it overflows our formulas. Today, we look at what happens when we stop pretending reality is smooth, and why the cracks in our scaffolding are the exact places where living freedom begins. Let us begin.
(Yuhan responds naturally, asking why our formal systems are so terrified of admitting their own built-in limits, and launches the visual dialogue.)
