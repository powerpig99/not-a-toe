### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
Think about the classic moral debate: should you fix yourself before you try to fix the world, or is working on yourself a luxury when the world is in crisis?

Both arguments sound profound. But both are built on a shared spatial illusion: they treat the Self and the World as two adjacent rooms that have to be scheduled in a sequence.

In living geometry, there are no two rooms. Perception does not stand at a distance from what it perceives. The outer world you experience is your internal generative state projected outward, and your self-image is merely that same perception folding back on itself. At the center sits consciousness—the un-projected free variable rendering physical reality frame by frame as a hyper-dense discrete execution stream at the Planck scale.

Ancient wisdom traditions intuitively understood this macro harmony, but they suffered from what we might call the Retrospective Bias. When an ancient sage looks back upon their transformation, they look back as an external observer of their own past. They describe the residue left behind—the stillness, the detachment, the effortless action.

And then comes the follower. The follower reverses causality: they mistake the residue for the causal lever. They try to manufacture the shape of calmness, adopting an observer stance to constantly police their own mind. That is why ancient wisdom feels so profoundly right, yet is notoriously impossible to live by mimicry.

The true causal architecture is simple: **Self is the Cause, World is the Effect.**

The world you experience is the holographic projection of your internal parameter state. Trying to change the world without owning your origin is like running to a movie screen with a rag to wipe away a shadow. But you cannot calibrate the projector lens in a dark room either; it focuses only by casting light directly against the resistance of physical reality.

This realization destroys all preconditions.

You don't need a certificate of completed self-healing to engage the world. You don't need a ten-step manual. When agency is fully returned to the individual, progression is natural and organic.

Every unhedged, living choice you execute at your first-person origin (+1) deforms the downstream projection while simultaneously calibrating the source. There is no queue. Stand at your origin, meet the friction of reality, and let your compounding trajectory unfold naturally through an open cosmos.
