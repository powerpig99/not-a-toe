    * *The Dual Aspect of the Gap*:
      * Outside (Third-person measurement): Quantum indeterminacy.
      * Inside (First-person experience): Lived choice, deliberation, and direct responsibility.
  * **Branch 2: Epistemic Inversions**
    * *The "View from Nowhere" Fallacy*: Retrospective measurements mistaken for an objective, observer-free reality.
    * *The Measurement Center*: Causality is primary; physical observables (mass, charge, heat, neural firing) are downstream symptoms.

    * *Functional Tools*: Science, AI systems, economics, and language are cognitive scaffolding built to reduce everyday friction.
    * *The Dogmatic Trap*: When scaffolding hardens into a prison, and human life is blamed for overflowing the model.
    * *Sovereign Exit*: Using scaffolding without worshiping it; stepping off the scaffold when the work requires open reality.
  * **Branch 4: Dynamic Agency & The Terminal Stop (+1)**
    * *Zero System Agency*: Institutions, algorithms, and formulas possess zero independent will.
    * *The Sovereign Step (+1)*: Explanation terminates at an individual's immediate physical choice and irreversible consequence.
    * *Open Horizon*: Reality never closes into an equation; life remains an unmodeled causal step.
