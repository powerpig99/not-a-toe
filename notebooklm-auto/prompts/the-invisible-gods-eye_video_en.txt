### [Role & Style]
Reflective video monologue. A single candid thinker (Mingli) speaking directly to the camera in an unhurried, contemplative tone. Zero preaching, zero moral language. Explanation terminates at living action (+1).

### [Directives: "The Invisible God's Eye"]
* **The Core Blind Spot**: Brilliant macroscopic thinkers forgetting they are situated minds inside reality, unconsciously adopting a disembodied God's eye.
* **The Follower Echo Chamber**: Brilliant models attracting vast followings, reinforcing mutual certainty and mutating into dogmatic orthodoxy.
* **The Historical Recurrence**: Prescriptions failing to solve the original pathology, re-enacting the same human tragedy across history under new labels.
* **Visual Motifs**: A colossal obsidian lens hovering in an overcast sky casting a spotlight on a grid of flattened cardboard silhouettes, while multidimensional travelers walk freely outside the beam toward the horizon; a faint human face reflected in the sky lens.

### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
The greatest trap for a brilliant mind is the illusion that it is looking from above.

History’s most formidable thinkers—from Laplace and Einstein to Bertrand Russell, Karl Popper, Ayn Rand, Thomas Sowell, and David Deutsch—shared a rare gift: the bandwidth to perceive vast macroscopic patterns across nature and society.

Yet almost all of them succumbed to the identical amnesia. They forgot that there is no view from nowhere. In the very moment their models achieved radiant clarity, they unconsciously floated into an imaginary spectator box outside the universe.

And once a mind adopts an invisible God's eye, fellow humans cease to be seen as parallel, sovereign universes. They are flattened into low-dimensional silhouettes inside the master's private sandbox: Russell's demographic quotas, Rand's moral caricatures, Popper's engineering cogs, Sowell's incentive units, Deutsch's universal computers.

Because of their brilliance, they understandably accumulate vast legions of devoted followers. This creates a closed social echo chamber: disciple adulation reinforces the master's conviction, turning an analytical lens into sacred dogma.

This dynamic never solves the original problem. Instead, it reintroduces the identical tragedy again and again across human history under a fresh intellectual label.

The universe has no VIP gallery. Real wisdom does not climb into an imaginary sky to look down on humanity. It stands shoulder-to-shoulder with sovereign peers on the open earth, honoring the unfathomable depth of each other’s uncharted horizons.
