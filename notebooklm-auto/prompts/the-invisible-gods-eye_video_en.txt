### [Role & Style]
Reflective video monologue. A single candid thinker (Mingli) speaking directly to the camera in an unhurried, contemplative tone. Zero preaching, zero moral language. Explanation terminates at living action (+1).

### [Directives: "The Invisible God's Eye"]
* **The Core Blind Spot**: Brilliant macroscopic thinkers forgetting they are situated minds inside reality, unconsciously adopting a disembodied God's eye.
* **The Degradation of Peers**: Flattening sovereign parallel universes into low-dimensional pawns in a mental sandbox.
* **Why Solutions Fail**: Blueprints drawn on a personal low-dimensional canvas inevitably rupture when forced upon an infinite-dimensional living world.
* **Visual Motifs**: A colossal obsidian lens hovering in an overcast sky casting a spotlight on a grid of flattened cardboard silhouettes, while multidimensional travelers walk freely outside the beam toward the horizon; a faint human face reflected in the sky lens.

### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
The greatest trap for a brilliant mind is the illusion that it is looking from above.

History’s most formidable thinkers—from Laplace and Einstein to Karl Popper, Ayn Rand, Thomas Sowell, and David Deutsch—shared a rare gift: the bandwidth to perceive vast macroscopic patterns across nature and society.

Yet almost all of them succumbed to the identical amnesia. They forgot that there is no view from nowhere. In the very moment their models achieved radiant clarity, they unconsciously floated into an imaginary spectator box outside the universe.

And once a mind adopts an invisible God's eye, fellow humans cease to be seen as parallel, sovereign universes. They are flattened into low-dimensional silhouettes inside the master's private sandbox: Rand's creators and looters, Popper's engineering cogs, Sowell's incentive units, Deutsch's universal computers.

Because their internal projection is sharper than common sense, they mistake the projection for reality itself.

This is why their grand prescriptions inevitably collapse. You cannot heal an infinite-dimensional, living universe by imposing one thinker's two-dimensional blueprint. When human reality leaks outside the frame, the master blames mankind for being irrational—never questioning their own elevated vantage.

The universe has no VIP gallery. Real wisdom does not climb into an imaginary sky to look down on humanity. It stands shoulder-to-shoulder with sovereign peers on the open earth, honoring the unfathomable depth of each other’s uncharted horizons.
