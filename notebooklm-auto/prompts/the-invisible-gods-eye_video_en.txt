### [Role & Style]
Reflective video monologue. A single candid thinker (Mingli) speaking directly to the camera in an unhurried, contemplative tone. Zero preaching, zero moral language. Explanation terminates at living action (+1).

### [Directives: "The Invisible God's Eye"]
* **The Core Blind Spot**: Brilliant macroscopic thinkers forgetting they are situated observers inside reality, unconsciously adopting a disembodied God's eye.
* **The Follower Echo Chamber**: Brilliant models mistaken for idols by followers, reinforcing mutual certainty and re-enacting historical dogma under new labels.
* **Visual Motifs**: A colossal obsidian lens hovering in an overcast sky casting a spotlight on flattened silhouettes, while multidimensional travelers walk freely on open earth toward the horizon.

### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
The greatest trap for a brilliant mind is the illusion that it is looking from above.

History’s most formidable thinkers—from Laplace and Einstein to Bertrand Russell, Karl Popper, Ayn Rand, Thomas Sowell, and David Deutsch—shared the rare bandwidth to perceive vast macroscopic patterns across nature and society.

Yet almost all of them succumbed to the identical amnesia. In the very moment their models achieved radiant clarity, they unconsciously floated into an imaginary spectator box outside the universe.

And once a mind adopts an invisible God's eye, fellow humans cease to be seen as parallel, sovereign universes. They are flattened into low-dimensional silhouettes inside the master's private sandbox: Russell's demographic quotas, Rand's moral caricatures, Popper's engineering cogs, Sowell's incentive units, Deutsch's universal computers.

Their models are magnificent precision goalposts for sovereign navigation, yet tragically mistaken for godlike idols by followers who abdicate their own agency. That mutual echo chamber reintroduces the identical tragedy across history.

The universe has no VIP gallery. Real wisdom does not climb into an imaginary sky to look down on humanity. It stands shoulder-to-shoulder with sovereign peers on the open earth, honoring the unfathomable depth of each other’s uncharted horizons.
