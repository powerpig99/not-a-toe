### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
It's easy to take an ambitious theory like Chronoflux too seriously because of how elaborate it looks. It claims to derive everything from a single conserved temporal fluid. But the derivation is an illusion. At every single scientific branch, new ad hoc constraints and empirical constants are smuggled in. It's Ptolemaic cosmology: adding epicycle upon epicycle to preserve the appearance of a single premise. More fundamentally, continuity isn't an ontological fluid flowing through nature. It's simply the coherence causality has once a discrete step occurs and we treat the interval as negligible. And when Herbert defended continuity by classifying a critic's tool as inadequate, that classification was itself a sharp, discrete cut. You can't freeze time into a finished catalog, because the act of writing the catalog is already reality stepping forward.
