Why does human civilization keep tracing the exact same cycle of centralized authority and surrendered autonomy across thousands of years?

There are only millions of free variables situated at their first-person origins, executing uncaused choices at the living frontier.

The reason the pattern repeats is statistical. When free variables face the heavy friction of uncertainty, the tendency to outsource decision-making outside oneself forms a natural statistical distribution. And in any connected network, surrendered agency compounds through preferential attachment. People outsource their thinking to whichever hub already projects the greatest certainty—the Church, the State, the Party, the Tech Platform, the AI Oracle.

Over time, that distribution slides into a steep power-law. The massive central hub looks all-powerful. But it is an ontological phantom. It has zero native consciousness, zero subjective experience, and zero ability to shield you from reality.

And here lies the brutal truth: you can surrender your decision rights to the hub, but you cannot surrender the physical consequences. When a central plan fails, the institution feels no hunger; the flesh-and-blood human starves. When an algorithm induces cognitive decay, the data center feels no existential void; your biological mind atrophies.

Strip away the moral rhetoric, and a bitter conflict between two people is dynamically identical to the dependency loop of self-sacrificing altruism. Both operate on a compatible misallocation—one person exports blame, the other absorbs it for control or moral righteousness. They lock together like key and mortise, freezing their internal learning gradients and generating massive systemic drag.

How do you break the loop?

The liberating truth of nonlinear dynamics is that you never need bilateral consensus, and you don't need a ten-step self-help manual. 

A compatible misallocation requires two complementary halves to function. The moment you step into your first-person origin and unilaterally set your reactive coupling coefficient to zero—refusing to export blame, refusing to absorb external guilt, and taking one hundred percent structural responsibility for your coordinates—the socket disappears.
