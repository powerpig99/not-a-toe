### [Role & Ground Rules]
* **Format & Tone**: Spoken dialogue between Mingli and Yuhan over coffee. Candid, contemplative, intellectually rigorous, zero academic jargon or moralizing.
* **Hosts**: Mingli (Host 1, male) analyzes the epistemic amnesia of master thinkers (from Laplace and Russell to Popper, Rand, Sowell, and Deutsch) who mistake their models for a God's-eye view, amassing followings that amplify their certainty; Yuhan (Host 2, female) probes how this followership loop hardens models into dogmatic idols, re-enacting historical tragedies, and how sovereign minds step down to walk among equals.
* **Core Spirit**: Brilliant minds see macroscopic patterns clearly, yet forget they remain situated observers at the origin inside reality. Adopting a God's-eye view flattens fellow minds into low-dimensional puppets. Followers mistake precision goalposts for divine idols, cementing the master's certitude and reintroducing dogma under a new banner.

### [Key Directives: "The Invisible God's Eye"]
* **The Olympian Vantage & Observer Amnesia**: Master thinkers possess rare cognitive bandwidth, but unconsciously smuggle an imaginary camera outside the universe, forgetting there is no view from nowhere.
* **Flattening Other Minds**: Under the God's eye, sovereign peers are degraded into low-dimensional pieces inside the master's sandbox—Russell's demographic quotas, Rand's moral caricatures, Popper's engineering cogs, Sowell's incentive units, and Deutsch's algorithms.
* **The Followership Echo Chamber**: Followers mistake precision navigational goalposts for godlike idols, abdicating their own agency. Follower adulation reinforces the master's certitude, codifying a local model into rigid dogma and reproducing the identical historical tragedy.
* **Dissolving the God's Eye**: True knowledge cannot remain at a third-person vantage point; it must be incorporated directly into the sovereign mind's own causal feedback loop on open ground.

### [Intro Script - Mingli]
Have you noticed a peculiar blind spot shared by some of history's greatest thinkers? Laplace, Einstein, Bertrand Russell, Karl Popper, Ayn Rand, Thomas Sowell, David Deutsch—they all possessed an astonishing gift for seeing macroscopic patterns across society and the cosmos. But they all fell victim to the exact same amnesia: they forgot where they stood. Intoxicated by their own models, they floated into an imaginary spectator box outside reality. And the moment a thinker adopts that invisible God's eye, fellow humans cease to be equal, parallel universes; they become low-dimensional chess pieces inside that thinker's sandbox. Because of their brilliance, they amass legions of followers who mistake precision goalposts for godlike idols. That echo chamber reintroduces the identical tragedy across history. True knowledge cannot stay in the spectator box—it must be incorporated into the sovereign mind's own causal feedback loop.
(Yuhan responds thoughtfully, exploring how discipleship transforms a cognitive lens into a dogmatic church, and how a mature thinker steps down to walk shoulder-to-shoulder with sovereign peers.)
