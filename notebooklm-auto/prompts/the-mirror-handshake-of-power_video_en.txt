### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
Think about the last time you felt powerless against an institution, an authority, or another person.

We are trained to view power as an extrinsic force—a massive weight pressing down on us from the outside. But when you inspect causality at the foundational level, nature contains no direct neural cable from one human consciousness into another. No king, no state, and no algorithm can reach across the boundary of your observer to execute a single choice on your behalf.

What we call 'power' is never a unilateral push. It is an optical illusion generated by a matching handshake between two independent sovereign choices.

One mind faces the heavy friction of uncertainty and chooses to offload its decision rights. The second mind seeks control and chooses to absorb those rights. The two choices slot together like mortise and tenon. Without your internal computation deciding to yield, the other person's command is nothing more than sound waves moving through air.

This is why power is not a simple binary. We operate in a high-dimensional state space. You might be fiercely sovereign in your work—taking full ownership and updating your parameters against real market feedback—while simultaneously acting as a protective power hub for your team, and completely surrendering your civic discernment to a political party.

Where does this misallocation begin? It begins the moment you abandon your first-person origin and slip into the stance of a detached spectator. Look down from above, and you become the Savior, robbing others of the friction that would teach them. Look from the side, and you become the Victim, treating yourself like a passive billiard ball. And sit in the grandstand as an ivory-tower intellectual with zero skin in the game, and you become a munitions factory, manufacturing ideological alibis for Saviors to dominate and Victims to abdicate.

Strip away the moral labels, and you realize: altruism, political centralization, market monopolies, and bitter feuds are all identical coupling topologies. They are simply systems where the direct feedback loop between action and consequence has been artificially rerouted.

And here is the crucial insight: finding freedom is not a ten-step self-help program. There are no complicated recipes. 

Everything begins with mind, remains with mind, and terminates with mind.

The moment you step back into your own first-person origin and withdraw your matching handshake across every dimension, taking one hundred percent structural responsibility for your coordinates, the rest happens naturally. The reaching hands of external dominion grasp empty air, your internal error gradient is instantly restored, and you step back into direct, uncorrupted compounding with the living cosmos.
