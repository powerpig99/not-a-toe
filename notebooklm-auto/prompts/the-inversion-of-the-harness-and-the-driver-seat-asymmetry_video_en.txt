### [Spoken Script]
Have you noticed how the software world is suddenly obsessed with 'rethinking harnesses' for AI agents?
Engineers are building heavier guardrails, sandboxes, and test matrices, trying to force probabilistic models into deterministic tracks.
Yet beneath this engineering scramble lies a profound blind spot:
The moment a user surrenders their first-person causal agency, every capability inverts into an invisible cage!

This danger doesn't just happen when you adopt someone else's framework;
it happens even more insidiously when you rely on tools created by your own past self!
The automated scripts and prompt workflows you built yesterday were brilliant scaffolding for yesterday's problems.
But when living reality evolves, if you hide inside old templates and force new reality to fit old schemas,
yesterday's breakthrough becomes today's cognitive ceiling!

And here is the raw truth behind the 'Master Misattribution' of our era:
When a practitioner spends thousands of hours in the driver's seat orchestrating complex multi-agent systems,
society points at the screen and gasps: 'Look at how intelligent this AI is!'
That is looking at reality backwards.
What actually underwent an extraordinary phase shift was not the statistical math inside the model—
it was the human pilot in the driver's seat, compressing intent, refining architecture, and calibrating against live friction!

The AI agent is nothing more than a high-bandwidth mathematical mirror and cognitive lever.
Every brilliant beam of light it projects originates entirely from the conscious mind holding the steering controls!
Faced with identical AI models, passive passengers suffer rapid skill atrophy and helplessness;
while sovereign drivers who refuse to leave the cockpit multiply their creative throughput by a thousandfold!

Stop worshiping the tools you built yesterday.
Treat every harness and framework as disposable scaffolding to be dissolved and rebuilt at moment t.
The driver's seat cannot be automated.
Grip your controls, own the friction, and create!
