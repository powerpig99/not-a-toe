### [Role & Ground Rules]
* **Format & Tone**: Spoken dialogue between Mingli and Yuhan over coffee. Candid, contemplative, insightful, zero preaching or stage directions.
* **Hosts**: Mingli (Host 1, male) breaks down the geometric divergence between the two intentional vectors—outward inquiry versus inward demand—and why projecting high-dimensional interiority onto another's screen mathematically creates infinite vectors of distortion; Yuhan (Host 2, female) probes the relational death spiral of "Understand me first!", the liberating shift to understanding the other, and the emergent paradox of authentic resonance.
* **Core Spirit**: When we focus on understanding, we step into an open cosmos with infinite possibility. But when we focus on being understood, we surrender ourselves to another mind's low-dimensional projection screen, creating infinite possibility to be misunderstood.

### [Key Directives: "The Asymmetry of Understanding"]
* **Two Opposing Vectors**: Outward understanding is an active, sovereign expansion (+1) exploring boundless reality; demanding to be understood is an anxious, passive collapse begging for validation inside a foreign decoder.
* **The Mathematics of Misprojection**: Another person occupies an irreplicably different coordinate origin (0, 0, 0). Projecting high-dimensional analog experience through lossy, discrete symbols onto their screen produces infinite projection angles—almost all of which distort, foreshorten, and misinterpret.
* **The Relational Death Spiral**: Demanding external validation creates symbolic hyperinflation (longer texts, sharper arguments) that only multiplies distortion surfaces and turns geometric mismatches into moral warfare.
* **Sovereign Inversion**: Dropping the demand to be understood and turning attention outward to understand the other dissolves their defensive armor. Resonance emerges spontaneously, not through forceful extraction.
* **Adult Equanimity**: Being misunderstood is the mathematical baseline of the universe; genuine resonance is a rare, cherished miracle.

### [Intro Script - Mingli]
Have you noticed the peculiar asymmetry at the heart of how we interact with the world? When we focus our energy on understanding—whether it's unraveling a law of nature, learning an intricate craft, or exploring why another person behaves the way they do—it opens up an expansive universe of infinite possibility. Every question adds an orthogonal dimension to our lives. But the moment we invert that vector and become obsessed with being understood, we step into a psychological trap. We demand that our complex, high-dimensional inner life be faithfully mirrored inside someone else's discrete, low-dimensional categories. And because the angles of projection onto another mind's screen are infinite, almost every projection is a distortion. The more desperately we demand to be understood, the more infinite the possibilities of being misunderstood become.
(Yuhan responds naturally, connecting this to the geometry of projection, how "Understand me first!" fuels relational warfare, and how true empathy transcends the shadow screen.)
