### [Role & Ground Rules]
* **Format & Tone**: Spoken dialogue between Mingli and Yuhan over coffee. Candid, contemplative, insightful, zero preaching or stage directions.
* **Hosts**: Mingli (Host 1, male) breaks down the geometric divergence between the two intentional vectors—outward inquiry versus inward demand—and why turning understanding into an obligation closes its infinite potentiality; Yuhan (Host 2, female) probes the relational death spiral of "Understand me first!", how coercion triggers defensive blast shields, and the liberating shift to understanding the other.

When we focus on understanding, we step into an open cosmos with infinite possibility. But when we focus on being understood and demand it as a duty, we surrender to another mind's low-dimensional screen, creating infinite possibility to be misunderstood.

### [Key Directives: "The Asymmetry of Understanding"]
* **Two Opposing Vectors**: Outward understanding is an active, sovereign expansion (+1) exploring boundless reality; demanding to be understood is an anxious, passive collapse begging for validation inside a foreign decoder.
* **Potential vs. Obligation**: Understanding is a natural, infinite potentiality. The moment it is turned into a mandatory duty or emotional debt, the potentiality snaps shut. An obligation demands compliance audits and raises defensive blast shields, destroying authentic listening.
* **The Mathematics of Misprojection**: Another person occupies an irreplicably different coordinate origin (0, 0, 0). Projecting high-dimensional analog experience through lossy, discrete symbols onto their screen produces infinite projection angles—almost all of which distort, foreshorten, and misinterpret.
* **The Relational Death Spiral**: Demanding external validation creates symbolic hyperinflation (longer texts, sharper arguments) that only multiplies distortion surfaces and turns geometric mismatches into moral warfare.
* **Sovereign Inversion**: Dropping the demand to be understood and turning attention outward to understand the other dissolves their defensive armor. Resonance emerges spontaneously as a free gift, never as an extracted debt.
* **Adult Equanimity**: Being misunderstood is the mathematical baseline of the universe; genuine resonance is a rare, cherished miracle.

### [Intro Script - Mingli]
Have you noticed the peculiar asymmetry at the heart of human connection? Understanding is an infinite potential each and every one of us inherently possesses. In its natural state, our capacity to explore reality and empathize with another soul is practically boundless. But the moment understanding is turned into an obligation—an emotional debt or a moral demand—that potentiality instantly snaps shut. We see this everywhere: when we demand that another person "must understand us," we try to force our complex, high-dimensional inner life into their cramped, low-dimensional symbolic parser. And because the angles of projection onto another mind's screen are infinite, almost every projection is a distortion. When we focus on understanding, it opens into infinite possibility; but when we demand to be understood as an obligation, it creates infinite possibilities to be misunderstood.
(Yuhan responds naturally, connecting this to the geometry of projection, why obligation triggers defensive blast shields, and how true resonance only emerges as an unforced gift.)
