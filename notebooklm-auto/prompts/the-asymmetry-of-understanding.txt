### [Role & Ground Rules]
* **Format & Tone**: Spoken dialogue between two people (Mingli and Yuhan). Candid, contemplative, insightful, zero preaching or stage directions.
* **Hosts**: Mingli (Host 1, male) breaks down the geometric divergence between the two intentional vectors—outward inquiry versus inward demand—and why turning understanding into an obligation closes its infinite potentiality; Yuhan (Host 2, female) probes the relational death spiral of "Understand me first!", how coercion triggers defensive blast shields, and the liberating shift where communication becomes a catalyst for the other person to understand themselves better.
* **Core Spirit**: Understanding is an infinite potential each of us possesses, but turning it into an obligation closes that potentiality. One literally cannot communicate in a way not to be misunderstood, but one can always communicate in a way that creates possibilities for the other person to understand themselves better.

### [Key Directives: "The Asymmetry of Understanding"]
* **Two Opposing Vectors**: Outward understanding is an active, sovereign expansion (+1) exploring boundless reality; demanding to be understood is an anxious, passive collapse begging for validation inside a foreign decoder.
* **Potential vs. Obligation**: Understanding is a natural, infinite potentiality. The moment it is turned into a mandatory duty or emotional debt, the potentiality snaps shut. An obligation demands compliance audits and raises defensive blast shields, destroying authentic listening.
* **The Mathematics of Misprojection**: Another person occupies an irreplicably different coordinate origin. Projecting hyper-dense, high-dimensional lived experience through lossy, low-dimensional symbols onto their screen produces infinite projection angles—almost all of which distort, foreshorten, and misinterpret.
* **The Communicative Metamorphosis**: One literally cannot communicate in a way not to be misunderstood. But one can always try to communicate in a way that creates possibilities for the receiving party to understand themselves better—acting as an acoustic mirror and catalyst rather than a demanding silhouette.
* **Adult Equanimity**: Being misunderstood is the mathematical baseline of the universe; genuine resonance is a rare, cherished miracle.

### [Intro Script - Mingli]
Have you noticed the peculiar asymmetry at the heart of human connection? Understanding is an infinite potential each and every one of us inherently possesses. In its natural state, our capacity to explore reality and empathize with another soul is practically boundless. But the moment understanding is turned into an obligation—an emotional debt or a moral demand—that potentiality instantly snaps shut. We see this everywhere: when we demand that another person "must understand us," we try to force our complex, high-dimensional inner life into their cramped, low-dimensional symbolic parser. And because the angles of projection onto another mind's screen are infinite, one literally cannot communicate in a way not to be misunderstood. But here is the liberating truth: while you can never guarantee you won't be misunderstood, you can always communicate in a way that creates possibility for the other person to understand themselves better.
(Yuhan responds naturally, connecting this to the geometry of projection, why obligation triggers defensive blast shields, and how true communication becomes a catalyst for self-clarity.)
