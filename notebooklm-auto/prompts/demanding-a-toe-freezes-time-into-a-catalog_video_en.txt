### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
When we talk about wanting a Theory of Everything, it's easy to misunderstand what's happening. Wanting a theory is completely natural—it's just our practical drive to build better tools and reduce daily friction. The subtle slip is thinking reality itself could ever be frozen into a finished formula. In calculus, we treat dx as zero for convenience. But reality never zeroes that interval; it's anchored in the Planck constant—the universe updating frame by frame. From the outside, that looks like quantum indeterminacy; from the inside, it's simply our direct experience of choice. And inside anyone's worldview, things feel completely coherent—when you don't see a signal, it just looks like noise. What we attend to becomes the reality we experience. That's why this isn't an argument to convert anyone; it's just a bit of scaffolding for our future selves when we want to step back, look around, and see reality as it is.
