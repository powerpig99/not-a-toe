### [Directives for This Episode: "Demanding a Theory of Everything Freezes Time into a Catalog"]
* **Core Realization 1: No "Record of the Past"**: Modern physics assumes there is an objective archive of past cosmic events waiting to be summarized. But there is NO objective vault of the past! Every fossil, spectrometer reading, hard drive bit, and memory exists strictly in the living present. "The past" is an interpretive projection backward from present structures onto an imagined coordinate axis, exactly like "the future" is a forward projection.
* **Core Realization 2: Self-Defeat at Inception**: The demand for a Theory of Everything is wrong at the very moment it is formulated. Why? Because the act of formulating the idea, writing down equations, or articulating the demand is itself an active, irreversible causal step (+1). At the exact moment the formula is conceived, reality has ALREADY irreversibly moved into the next moment! You cannot freeze the universe from within, because your act of freezing is an irreversible physical surge forward.
* **Actual Time vs. Projected Time Dimension**: Physics replaces actual living time (the irreversible, unclosable transition of the active now) with a spatialized, static coordinate axis (the Block Universe), projecting backward to invent a static past and forward to calculate a safe future, avoiding the coarse friction and existential risk of the open future (+1).
* **The Irreducible Prior & Structural Slippage**: Physics is an apparatus constructed in the present by conscious minds making cuts. To expect physics to derive the living mind is a category error—demanding the film on the screen explain the projector bulb. The automatic drift of language to turn living verbs into static nouns proves that formal closure is impossible. Only the living mind can act as the vigilant watchdog against false closure.
