### [Core Directives: "Objects Are Stabilized Relationships"]
* **The Classical Fallacy vs. Quantum Inversion**: Common sense assumes objects are primary and relationships are secondary links between preexisting things. Relational Quantum Mechanics (Rovelli, Webster) inverts this: variables have values only relative to an interaction; relations precede objects.
* **The Twin Traps**:
  * *Trap 1: Substance Reification*: Classical materialism clinging to billiard balls and inventing uncaused particles in an imaginary "View from Nowhere."
  * *Trap 2: Deleuzian Dissolution*: Philosophers throwing up their hands, declaring ordinary logic helpless, and retreating into Deleuzian jargon ("rhizomes," "planes of immanence"), dissolving reality into poetic mist.
* **The Razor**: "Objects are stabilized relationships." An object is a relational network that has achieved dynamic invariance and causal stability against perturbation through conservation laws, thermodynamic friction, and energetic cost. A stone is a stabilized relationship of molecular lattices and electromagnetic repulsion.
* **Nouns as Cognitive Scaffolding**: We invent nouns ("stone," "table," "currency") as computational chunking to navigate high-density relationships without cognitive collapse. Reification occurs when we mistake the noun for an uncaused essence.
* **Grounded in First-Person Reality (+1)**: Relations do not float in an observerless third-person void. Reality is fundamentally first-person perspective only; physical reality is its stabilized macrosymptom. Explanation terminates at the immediate, irreversible physical interaction and cost-bearing (+1).
