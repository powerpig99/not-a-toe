### [Role & Style]
Reflective video monologue. A single candid thinker (Mingli) speaking directly to the camera in an unhurried, contemplative tone. Zero preaching, zero moral language. Explanation terminates at living action (+1).

### [Directives: "The Asymmetry of Understanding"]
* **The Asymmetric Fork**: When we focus on understanding, it opens into infinite possibility; when we focus on being understood, it opens into infinite possibility of being misunderstood.
* **The Geometry of Projection**: Projecting a high-dimensional analog mind through low-dimensional symbols onto another's screen creates infinite angles of distortion and shadow-clashing.
* **Relational Deadlock**: The trap of "Understand me first!" outsources personal sovereignty to a lossy decoder.
* **Sovereign Inversion**: Stepping off the defense line to understand the other dissolves threat and invites genuine resonance.
* **Visual Motifs**: Radiant golden and cyan rays expanding outward into starry space; an intricate glass prism shattering incoming light into jagged, colliding caustic shadows on a dark stone floor; a traveler walking unhurriedly along a sunlit mountain trail.

### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
Think about where our deepest exhaustion in life actually comes from. It almost always traces back to a subtle, yet catastrophic inversion of our attention.

When we focus on understanding, we step into infinite possibility. Think of the vitality of a child exploring a forest, a physicist probing the quantum vacuum, or a friend listening with deep curiosity. In that outward vector, the universe is an open, high-dimensional continuum. Every question you ask and every nuance you absorb opens up a new, orthogonal dimension of freedom (+1).

Yet we spend so much of our lives doing the exact opposite: desperately begging to be understood. The moment you demand that another person "get you," you are trying to squeeze decades of analog, high-dimensional feelings and private history into someone else's discrete, low-dimensional symbolic parser.

Geometry teaches us that projecting a high-dimensional reality onto a flat screen produces infinite angles of distortion. The more frantically you explain yourself, the more fragmented surfaces you give them to misinterpret. Demanding to be understood creates infinite possibilities of being misunderstood.

Liberation begins when you stop litigating your shadow on someone else's wall. Anchor yourself at your own origin. Make peace with the fact that being misunderstood is the default geometry of separate minds. Turn your energy outward: understand the territory, understand the other, and walk freely with infinity.
