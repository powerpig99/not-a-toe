### [Role & Style]
Reflective video monologue. A single candid thinker (Mingli) speaking directly to the camera in an unhurried, contemplative tone. Zero preaching, zero moral language. Explanation terminates at living action (+1).

### [Directives: "The Shadow and the Infinite"]
* **High-D Abundance vs. Low-D Congestion**: Physical and biological reality is an unbounded high-dimensional manifold where infinite unique sovereign paths can flourish without colliding. Collapsing life onto 1D scorecards creates artificial scarcity and zero-sum conflict.
* **Determinism as a Projection Artifact**: Deterministic laws are the smooth statistical signatures left behind when high-dimensional complexity is flattened onto low-dimensional screens.
* **The War of Shadows**: Inversion of Plato's cave: people do not fight over reality, but over the collision of their flattened 2D shadows (ideologies, rankings, defensive egos).
* **Orthogonal Freedom**: Stepping off the 1D competitive line into unexplored orthogonal dimensions of creative action (+1).
* **Visual Motifs**: Luminous golden hyper-dimensional ribbons weaving freely in starry space; harsh directional light casting flattened clashing silhouettes on a stone wall; a traveler stepping into the open morning wilderness.

### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
Think about where all human conflict truly comes from. In the physical and biological cosmos, reality is an unbounded high-dimensional space. In high dimensions, orthogonal directions are practically infinite. Two complex living trajectories almost never collide; there is boundless room for billions of unique individuals to flourish, create, and explore without ever stepping on each other's toes.

Yet we spend our lives trapped in bitter rivalry and ideological warfare. Why? Because we take that infinite living richness and compress it onto low-dimensional projection screens: a single net worth figure, an institutional ranking, or a binary political camp. The moment you flatten multidimensional reality onto a one-dimensional line, collision becomes mathematically inevitable: you are either ahead of me, or you are blocking my way.

This is the tragic inversion of Plato's cave. We aren't just mistaking shadows for reality; we are waging war because two flat silhouettes bumped into each other on the stone wall. An ideologue burns the living world to defend a flat label; a bureaucrat shears human vitality to fit a spreadsheet; a defensive ego exhausts its life protecting a fragile two-dimensional image.

Liberation does not mean winning the race on that crowded wire. It means turning around and walking ahead with infinity. Treat low-dimensional models as disposable tools, never as idols. Step off the one-dimensional track into new, orthogonal directions of living action. The cosmos is not a crowded cage; it is an open expanse waiting for your next sovereign step.
