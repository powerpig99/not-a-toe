### [Role & Style]
Reflective video monologue. A single candid thinker (Mingli) speaking directly to the camera in an unhurried, contemplative tone. Zero preaching, zero moral language. Explanation terminates at living action (+1).

### [Directives: "The Shadow and the Infinite"]
* **High-D Abundance vs. Low-D War Zone**: Physical and biological reality is an unbounded high-dimensional manifold where infinite unique paths can flourish without colliding. Rules and dividers were created solely as guidance to avoid conflict.
* **The Fatal Guidance Inversion**: Humanity mistook collision-avoidance guidance for the zero-sum game we have to play, turning an expansive multi-dimensional living space into a tragically low-dimensional war zone.
* **The War of Shadows**: Inversion of Plato's cave: people do not fight over reality, but over the collision of their flattened 2D shadows (ideologies, rankings, defensive egos).
* **Orthogonal Freedom**: Stepping off the narrow guardrail into unexplored orthogonal dimensions of creative action (+1).
* **Visual Motifs**: Luminous golden hyper-dimensional ribbons weaving freely in starry space; harsh directional light casting flattened clashing silhouettes on a stone wall; a traveler stepping off a narrow road into the open morning wilderness.

### [Monologue Script - Mingli]
Think about where all human conflict truly comes from. In the physical and biological cosmos, reality is an unbounded high-dimensional space. In high dimensions, orthogonal directions are practically infinite. Two complex trajectories almost never collide—there is boundless room for infinite individuals to flourish without ever bumping into each other.

Rules, property lines, and lane dividers were originally created for one purpose: as guidance to avoid conflict. But we made a fatal category mistake: we mistook that collision-avoidance guidance for the zero-sum game we have to play. We climbed onto the guardrail, fought over who owns the divider, and turned an expansive multi-dimensional living space into a tragically low-dimensional war zone. The moment you flatten life onto a single wire, collision becomes mathematically guaranteed: you are either ahead of me, or you are blocking my way.

We are waging war because two flat shadows bumped into each other on the wall. An ideologue burns the living world to defend a flat label; a bureaucrat shears human vitality to fit a spreadsheet; a defensive ego exhausts its life protecting a fragile silhouette.

Liberation does not mean winning the race on that crowded guardrail. It means stepping off the wire and walking ahead with infinity. Treat low-dimensional rules as disposable navigational tools, never as idols. Move into new, orthogonal directions of living action (+1). The cosmos is not a cramped cage; it is an open expanse waiting for your step.
