* **Section 3: Lexicon of First Principles (8 Core Terms)**
  1. *The ToE Impulse*: The psychological and institutional urge to freeze dynamic reality into a static, completed catalog to escape friction.
  2. *The Non-Zero Interval (dx ≠ 0, h ≠ 0)*: The irreducible discrete step of physical causality that prevents nature from collapsing into a static continuum.
  3. *The View from Nowhere*: The epistemic illusion that a retrospective measurement exists independently of the localized observer who recorded it.
  4. *Cognitive Scaffolding*: Theoretical models, tools, and algorithms built to reduce interaction friction, distinct from the reality they help navigate.
  5. *Internal Coherence Trap*: The phenomenon where any closed axiomatic system feels completely coherent from within, dismissing external signals as noise.
  6. *First-Person Causal Center*: The primary, irreducible locus where observation, evaluation, and physical action originate.
  7. *Observables as Causal Symptoms*: Downstream physical traces (heat, entropy, mass, neural spikes) left in the wake of causal interaction, mistaken by determinists for fundamental causes.
  8. *The Irreversible Step (+1)*: The physical intervention where deliberation ends, consequences are incurred, and the universe advances beyond the model.
