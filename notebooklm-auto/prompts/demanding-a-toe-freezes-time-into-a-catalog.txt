### [Directives for This Episode: "Demanding a Theory of Everything Freezes Time into a Catalog"]
* **Core Tension**: Demanding a Theory of Everything (ToE) is fundamentally an attempt to take the cross-section of reality available at the very present (all registered distinctions, coupling constants, detector traces up to t-1) and reify it into an exhaustive, closed catalog. But a catalog can only ever list what has already settled.
* **Freezing Actual Time for a Projected Coordinate Dimension**: To make the equations close, theoretical physics executes a sleight of hand: it freezes actual, irreversible, living time and replaces it with a spatialized, geometric coordinate axis (t) on a manifold (the Block Universe). In this picture, past, present, and future are treated as an already-printed tapestry surveyed from an impossible "View from Nowhere." Time is spatialized into an axis where the future is merely a coordinate waiting to be calculated, rather than a frontier waiting to be lived.
* **The Evasion of the Future (+1)**: Why the relentless obsession with a ToE? It is an existential defense mechanism—an escape from the ontological risk, coarse friction, and sovereign responsibility of taking the actual irreversible step into the open future (+1). Calculating coordinates on a static map feels safe; stepping into the unwritten void requires real causal courage.
* **Why Physics Cannot Resolve It (The Irreducible Prior)**: Physics operates entirely downstream of the living observer, measurement, and temporal asymmetry. Treating the irreducible prior as a physical hypothesis to be explained by quantum gravity or cosmology is a catastrophic category error: it demands that the movie on the screen explain the projector bulb!
* **The Structural Slippage & Mind as the Living Watchdog**: Yuhan highlights the subtle trap: why do even sharp thinkers constantly slip back into treating the irreducible prior as an "assumption" or "hypothesis" that needs proof? Mingli reveals that this is the structural slippage of language and formalism: syntax naturally nominalizes verbs into static nouns. This inevitable slippage is itself proof that closure is impossible! Only the conscious mind in the active present can resist this linguistic gravity, acting as the living watchdog that refuses false closure.
