### [Directives for This Episode: "Demanding a Theory of Everything Freezes Time into a Catalog"]
* **The Foundational Myth: No "Record of the Past"**: Modern science operates under the illusion that there is an objective archive of the past. But there is NO objective vault of history! Every fossil, cosmic microwave photon, hard drive bit, and neural memory exists ONLY in the present at this very moment. "The past" is an interpretive projection cast backward from the present onto an imagined coordinate axis (t).
* **Instantaneous Self-Defeat: Obsolete at Inception**: The demand for a ToE is self-refuting at the very instant it is formulated. Formulating an idea or equation is an active, irreversible causal event (+1). At the exact moment the formula is conceived, reality has ALREADY irreversibly moved onto the next moment. You cannot freeze the cosmos from within; the act of formulating propels the universe forward.
* **Observable Invariances & The Practically Infinite Complexity Gap**: A theory can ONLY capture the observable invariance across time within our limited temporal observation window. Physical matter has to be fundamentally informational as well for information to represent it, because underlying causality is identical across both domains. This makes the complexity gap between our tiny models and actual reality practically close to infinity for all practical purposes (though not an actual infinity, as infinity is an open potentiality).
* **Continuous Daily Friction & The Desire for a ToE (Two Sides of the Same Coin)**: Yuhan and Mingli dive deep into lived experience: why do we feel friction continuously throughout our daily life at each and every moment? Because our sparse conceptual models crash into the unfathomably dense generative reality at every step! And here lies the recursive tragedy: The very impossibility for us to have a Theory of Everything to carry from one moment to the next is the very reason we are forever desiring a Theory of Everything! We crave a portable master key to exempt us from the vulnerability and friction of navigating the next moment.
* **Actual Time vs. Projected Time Dimension & Mind as Living Watchdog**: Theoretical physics spatializes actual living time into a static coordinate manifold (Block Universe). Language naturally nominalizes living actions into static nouns. Only the living mind can act as the vigilant watchdog against false closure.
