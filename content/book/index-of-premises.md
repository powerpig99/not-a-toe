# 前提索引 / Index of Premises

*把被奉为客观与真理的信念切回其前提的边界 / Cutting what is held as objective or true back to the boundary of its premises*

前提索引记录了随笔实质剖析的全部关键人物、理论与概念。无论其来自科学、数学、哲学、宗教、意识形态、经济学、技术抑或成功学，每一个条目皆被切分至其赖以立足的基石前提：标明该前提在何处不完备，以及在其适用语境中依然具备何种效用。

The Index of Premises records every major figure, theory, and concept substantively dissected across the essays. Whether originating from science, mathematics, philosophy, religion, ideology, economics, technology, or self-help, each entry is cut back to its foundational premise: marking where the premise becomes incomplete, and where it remains useful inside its applicable context.

## 人物 / Figures

`科学 / Scientific`

爱因斯坦将时空度量与观察者参照系绑定；瓦解了经典时空观，但他所抱持的静态块状时空宇宙却反将时间变回了几何。

Einstein revolutionized physics by tying space and time to the observer's frame; peerless in dismantling absolute space, his static block-universe interpretation re-solidifies time into geometry.
