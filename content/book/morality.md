# 善恶界限与共情陷阱 / Moral Boundaries and the Empathy Trap

*地狱是由善意铺就的镜像，失去第一人称自卫界限的共情终将滑向自毁 / Hell is the mirror of good; empathy without first-person self-defense slides into destruction*

道德教条常常承诺一条纯净通往救赎之路，却不知善恶本是同一道划分边界时同时生成的对拓两极。将某种愿景确立为不可质疑的至善，便在暗处筑起了通往地狱的台阶。失去边界排序的泛滥同情，极易沦为操纵者敲诈主权的道德武器。

Moral dogmas promise spotless roads to salvation, oblivious that good and evil are antipodal poles generated by the very act of drawing a boundary. Reifying any vision into unquestioned virtue constructs steps toward hell. Boundless empathy without self-defense becomes a weapon for moral extortion.

这里的篇目剖析了受害者脚本的生成机制、自杀式共情的定义悖论，以及将抽象口号等同于具身责任的荒谬。真正的道德实践不需要崇高修辞；它始于在第一人称受力点上守住自卫底线，对身边具体相遇的他者承担清晰的契约责任。

These essays dissect victimhood scripts, suicidal empathy inversions, and the absurdity of equating abstract slogans with embodied duty. Authentic morality demands no grand rhetoric; it begins by holding self-defense boundaries at lived friction points, honoring explicit obligations to those encountered.
