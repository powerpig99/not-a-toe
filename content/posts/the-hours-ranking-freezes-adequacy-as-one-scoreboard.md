# 工时排名把充足度冻结成一张记分牌 / The Hours Ranking Freezes Adequacy as One Scoreboard

*一条每周工时的轴，把工资下限、相对贫困线与福利退坡冻结成一份充足度的排名。 / One weekly-hours axis freezes wage floor, relative line, and phase-out as a ranking of adequacy.*

一份流传的 OECD 排名，测的是一个单身成年人从保障性福利起步、福利随收入增加而被收回，按法定最低工资每周要工作多少小时，才能达到定在本国可支配收入中位数百分之五十的相对贫困线。在最常被转发的版本里，日本要 14 小时，大多数欧洲国家在 20 到 45 小时之间，美国是 80 小时。在这些工具之下，美国的 80 是确切的：一个低的联邦下限，一条高的相对贫困线，以及经济审查式的退坡。当这份残余被冻结为福利不足或下限不够，再被用来为扩大同样的转移与下限辩护，而正是它们与相对贫困线的交互产生了这些工时时，这份排名便成了一道谜题。

A circulated OECD ranking measures the weekly hours a single adult must work at the statutory minimum wage, starting from guaranteed benefits that are withdrawn as earnings rise, to reach a relative poverty line set at 50 percent of national median disposable income. In the version most often shared, Japan requires 14 hours, most European countries fall between 20 and 45, and the United States stands at 80. The 80 is exact under those instruments: a low federal floor, a high relative line, and means-tested withdrawal. The ranking becomes a puzzle when that residue is frozen as inadequate benefits or an insufficient floor, then used to justify expanding the same transfer and floor whose interaction with the relative line produces the hours.

## 一、 排名把三种工具压到一条工时轴上 / 1. The Ranking Compresses Three Instruments onto One Hours Axis

自我区分的活动在发生，无因，不息。称它为心智：那个已经在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。观察握住的是效果。一根标着 80 的柱子，一根标着 14 的柱子，一行写着“摆脱贫困”的说明，这些都作为效果被登记下来。产生它们的工具，始终比把它们冻结成一份国家充足度排名的那一眼领先至少一步。

![OECD 国家截至 2022 年的数据：一个领取福利的单身者按法定最低工资每周须工作多少小时，才能达到本国可支配收入中位数的百分之五十。日本 14 小时，美国 80 小时。](figures/oecd-hours-statutory-minimum.jpg)

Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. Observation holds effect. A bar labeled 80, a bar labeled 14, a caption that says “escape poverty”: these register as effects. The instruments that produced them remain at least one step ahead of the look that freezes them as a ranking of national adequacy.

![Weekly hours a single person receiving benefits must work at statutory minimum wage to reach 50 percent of national median disposable income, OECD countries as of 2022. Japan 14, United States 80.](figures/oecd-hours-statutory-minimum.jpg)

这张图用单一的每周工时数给各国排序。进入这个数字的是三种彼此独立的工具。贫困线是相对的：各国可支配收入中位数的百分之五十，所以中位数越高，以金额计的门槛越高。所用的工资是法定最低工资，它相对于中位数的高度各国差别很大。保障性福利已经算在起始收入里，而它们的退坡安排决定了每多工作一小时能留下多少净收入。把这三者压到一条工时轴上，就把彼此独立的区分硬塞进同一张记分牌，直到“所需工时”读起来像是这个社会的整体品格，而不是这些工具的交互。[拥有成本把滞后的平均值冻结为车辆属性](../ownership-cost-freezes-lagged-averages-as-vehicle-property/)是同一种压缩，换成了拥有汽车的处境：几条分开的渠道被压在一起，直到一张记分牌撰写了对象。这里的对象是国家的充足度。

The chart ranks countries by a single weekly-hours figure. Three independent instruments enter that figure. The poverty line is relative: 50 percent of each country’s median disposable income, so a higher median raises the threshold in money terms. The wage used is the statutory minimum, whose height relative to that median varies widely. Guaranteed benefits are already in the starting income, and their phase-out schedules determine how much of each additional hour remains as net. Compressing those three onto one hours axis forces independent distinctions onto a single scoreboard until “hours needed” reads as the total character of the society rather than as the interaction of the instruments. [Ownership cost freezes lagged averages as vehicle property](../ownership-cost-freezes-lagged-averages-as-vehicle-property/) is the same compression in the setting of car ownership: separate channels collapsed until one scoreboard authors the object. Here the object is national adequacy.

在这份排名里，贫困、最低工资与福利在各国指的并不是同一样东西。相对贫困线随各自的中位数伸缩。法定下限离中位数的距离各不相同。福利下限与退坡速率的设计各有差异。工时数已经是对这些差异的一次冻结。一个中位数更高、法定工资下限相对中位数更低的社会，会印出更多的工时，而这份印出的数字并不衡量这些工具之外的某个短缺。

Poverty, minimum wage, and benefits do not name the same thing from one country to the next. Relative lines scale with each median. Statutory floors sit at different distances from that median. Benefit floors and withdrawal rates differ in design. The hours figure is already a freeze of those differences. A society with a higher median and a statutory wage floor set lower against it will print more hours, without that print measuring a shortfall apart from the instruments.

## 二、 美国这个异常值在这些工具之下是确切的 / 2. The United States Outlier Is Exact under Those Instruments

美国联邦最低工资自 2009 年以来一直是每小时 7.25 美元。美国的可支配收入中位数在 OECD 中位居前列，所以以金额计的相对贫困门槛也相应地高。补充营养援助计划（SNAP）这样的项目提供了一个不高的收入下限；这些以及相关的经济审查式福利的退坡安排，造成了很高的有效边际税率。从那个下限出发，再面对退坡，算下来要按法定工资工作很多小时，净收入才能越过相对贫困线。

The U.S. federal minimum wage has remained $7.25 an hour since 2009. American median disposable incomes are among the highest in the OECD, so the relative poverty threshold is correspondingly high in absolute dollars. Programs such as SNAP supply a modest income floor; the phase-out schedules of those and related means-tested benefits produce high effective marginal tax rates. Starting from that floor and then confronting withdrawal, the arithmetic demands many hours at the statutory rate before net income clears the relative line.

在所陈述的假设之下，这是确切的。它是一个低的联邦下限、一条高的相对门槛与经济审查式的退坡三者的交互。美国许多州的下限已经高于联邦下限；这份排名用的是法定的联邦数字。把这一个下限冻结为“美国”，是同一类的又一次压缩：一件工具被当作这个国家工资的整体品格。

That is exact under the stated assumptions. It is the interaction of a low federal floor, a high relative threshold, and means-tested withdrawal. Many U.S. states already sit above the federal floor; the ranking uses the statutory federal figure. Freezing that one floor as “the United States” is a further compression of the same kind: one instrument held as the country’s total wage character.

## 三、 换一个更现实的低工资基准，异常值便消散 / 3. A Realistic Low-Wage Benchmark Dissolves the Outlier

OECD 的一份平行计算把法定最低工资换成一个更现实的低工资基准，即全国平均工资的百分之六十七。按这个口径，美国约需 26 小时，接近 OECD 平均的 21 小时。日本按同一基准约为 16 小时。可见那个极端的异常值取决于法定工资这一输入：相对贫困线与福利退坡不变，美国便落在平均值附近。换掉一件工具，也就是工资基准，排名便重新排列。

A parallel OECD calculation replaces the statutory minimum with a more realistic low-wage benchmark, 67 percent of the national average wage. Under that metric the United States requires about 26 hours, near the OECD average of 21. Japan, on the same benchmark, sits near 16. The extreme outlier therefore turns on the statutory wage input: with the relative line and the phase-outs unchanged, the United States sits near the average. Change one instrument, the wage benchmark, and the ranking reorders.

[平均值是痕迹，不是引擎](../the-average-is-residue-not-the-engine/)是相邻的那次冻结：一个总量被当作模式的作者。这里扮演那个角色的是工时排名：三种工具合成的一个数，被当作充足度的引擎来打分。换一个工资输入便重新排列，这表明第一张记分牌从未在工资输入之外握住充足度。

[The average is residue, not the engine](../the-average-is-residue-not-the-engine/) is the adjacent freeze, when an aggregate is held as author of the pattern. Here the hours ranking plays that part: a composite of three instruments scored as if it were the engine of adequacy. The reordering under a different wage input is evidence that the first scoreboard never held adequacy apart from its wage input.

## 四、 退坡速率使多出的工时留不下净收入 / 4. Withdrawal Rates Disable Net Gain from Additional Hours

转移一旦到位，它的退坡设计便让领取者从多出的工时里所得无几。转移本身是在重新分配已经生产出来的资源。它被收回的速率，抵消了在法定下限上工作的大部分所得。这就是被称为贫困陷阱的机制：延续靠的是经营那份被记录的收入，而多出工时的相对信号被压制了。

Once transfers are in place, their phase-out design leaves recipients with little net from additional hours. The transfer itself reallocates resources already produced. The rate at which it is withdrawn cancels most of the gain from work at the statutory floor. That is the mechanism named a poverty trap: continuation is drawn from managing the income register while the relative signal of additional hours is suppressed.

这个陷阱，是低法定下限与高相对贫困线之下经济审查式退坡的结构。[财富转移政策与价格下限中的原因错置](../the-reversal-of-causality-in-wealth-transfer-policies-and-price-floors/)是相邻的一面：测得的工资或补贴被当作可以由法令设定的变量装上去，原本会弥合差距的相对信号被压制。这里测得的工时被同样读取：一份排名被当作下一道法令要去填补的缺口。[当效果撰写了框架](../when-the-effect-authors-the-frame/)是同一种拓扑：一份登记被赋予因果上的优先，可用的回应便围绕着经营它重新组织。这里的登记是那根工时柱。

The trap is the structure of means-tested withdrawal under a low statutory floor and a high relative line. [The misallocation of cause in wealth-transfer policies and price floors](../the-reversal-of-causality-in-wealth-transfer-policies-and-price-floors/) is the adjacent face: the measured wage or payment installed as the variable to set by decree, with the relative signal that would have closed the gap suppressed. Here the measured hours are read the same way: a ranking taken as the gap the next decree should close. [When the effect authors the frame](../when-the-effect-authors-the-frame/) is the same topology: a register is granted causal priority, and available responses reorganize around managing it. The register here is the hours bar.

## 五、 记分牌撰写了下一次转移 / 5. The Scoreboard Authors the Next Transfer

这张图的流传，并不围绕着把三条轴还原回来。它被援引为证据，说明福利仍不充足，或工资下限还须再提高。在这种框架下，测得的症状，也就是所需的工时，被当作理由，去扩大正是以其结构促成了这道测得差距的同一类政策。还原这些工具的读法，更平缓的退坡、在职支持的协调、承认许多州的下限已经高于联邦数字，流传得较少；它们并不加强那个已被偏好的方向。

The chart’s circulation does not turn on restoring the three axes. It is invoked as evidence that benefits remain inadequate or that the wage floor must be raised further. In that framing the measured symptom, hours required, is treated as justification for expanding the same class of policies whose structure contributes to the measured gap. Readings that restore the instruments (smoother phase-outs, coordination of in-work supports, recognition that many state floors already sit above the federal number) circulate less; they do not reinforce the direction already preferred.

被偏好的参照自我更新：更多同样的转移，更高的下限，被当作地基握住。保住这份参照的后果，又作为工时数重新进入，然后以陌生的面目到来，成了“美国出了什么事”，一道等待更好答案的谜题。那一眼继续寻找一个会加强同一类政策的答案。[进步叙事中的症候与原因](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/)是同一种倒置的归因：响亮的残余被装成杠杆，而生成的序列仍在更早处。这里响亮的残余，就是那根 80 小时的柱子。[剩余个人主义](../residual-individualism/)把它追溯回源头，换成了着眼集体的处境：这份排名与它被用来辩护的政策，都是许多离散行动的残余，而不是能撰写下一小时净收入的独立引擎。

The preferred reference renews itself: more of the same transfer, a higher floor, held as ground. Consequences of that preservation re-enter as the hours figure, then arrive as alien, “what happened in the United States,” a puzzle awaiting a better answer. The look keeps searching for an answer that would reinforce the same class of policy. [Symptom and cause in the narratives of progress](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/) is the same reverse attribution: the loud residue is installed as lever while the generating sequence stays earlier. Here the loud residue is the 80-hour bar. [Residual individualism](../residual-individualism/) traces it back to its source in the setting of collective focus: the ranking and the policies it is used to justify are residue of many discrete acts, not independent engines that can author the next hour of net income.

## 六、 这个数字登记的是交互 / 6. The Figure Registers the Interaction

工时数是低法定下限、高相对贫困线与经济审查式退坡的残余。退一步说成“数字是中性的，只有它们的用法不是”，便把只有一个序列运作的地方拆成了两件事实，一个中性的数字和一种不中性的用法；而运作的是同一个序列：工具产生了残余，这份残余被冻结成了充足度的排名。谜题出现在这份冻结被用来为扩大产生这些工时的同一种转移与下限辩护的时候。把三条轴还原回来，这个数字便可以用。它登记的是工具如何交互，并不衡量同一类政策还需要多少。[AI 争论中的观察划界](../the-observational-cut-in-ai-debates/)是同一次冻结，出现在两种劳动力的移动，再加上一次份额转移，被记成同一个就业数字的时候。

The hours figure is residue of a low statutory floor, a high relative line, and means-tested withdrawal. Softening into “the numbers are neutral; only their use is not” splits into two facts, a neutral number and a non-neutral use, what operated as one sequence: instruments produced a residue, and that residue was frozen as a ranking of adequacy. The puzzle appears when the freeze is used to justify expanding the same transfer and floor that produce the hours. Restore the three axes and the figure is usable. It registers how the instruments interact. It does not measure how much more of the same class of policy is required. [The observational cut in AI debates](../the-observational-cut-in-ai-debates/) is that freeze when two labor movements and a shift in share are scored as one employment number.
