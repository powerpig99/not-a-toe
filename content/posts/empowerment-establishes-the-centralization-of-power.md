# 赋权确立权力的集中 / Empowerment Establishes the Centralization of Power

*赋权或被赋权，就是追认能力从一个已经持有它的中心移出。 / To empower or to be empowered is to ratify that capacity moves from a center that already holds it.*

这个词把行动的能力框定为一份礼物，由一个被授权的源头赠予或解锁。提出与接受稳定的是同一个不对称：权力看上去从一个持有它的中心向外流动，而发起早已在每一个做选择的所在上进行，那个被假定的中心也在其中。选择的自由未被认出之处，一个凌驾于另一个之上的权力，便作为支配与依赖的稳定样式而变密，而相继的选择不断重塑着可得之物的场。赋权的语言把选择的朝向引向请求与许可，于是以它的语汇做出的每一个动作，都再一次把那道门确立为下一步所从出的地方。

The word frames the capacity to act as a gift that an authorized source can bestow or unlock. Offer and acceptance stabilize the same asymmetry: power appears to travel outward from a center that holds it, while initiation is already underway at every locus that chooses, the presumed center included. Where the freedom of choice goes unrecognized, the power of one over another densifies as a stabilized pattern of domination and dependence, while successive choosing keeps reshaping the field of what is available. Empowerment language redirects the orientation of choice toward petition and permission, so each act made in its terms establishes the gate again as the place the next step comes from.

## 一、 这个词编码了授予 / 1. The Term Encodes Bestowal

去赋权于某人，就是预设一个凌驾于他的位置：他行动的能力有所欠缺，而自己握有它，可以授予或解锁它。被赋权，是从另一个方向预设同一个层级：自己的选择要等待一个已经拥有它所缺之物的外部源头。这两个预设是不同的登记，给予者登记的是对方的欠缺，接受者登记的是自己的欠缺，然而这笔交易，不论是提出还是接受，追认的都是同一个不对称。权力被当作存量，从一个中心向外移动，而从不被当作只在被做出之处闭合的离散活动。

To empower someone is to presume a standing over them: that their capacity to act is lacking, and that one holds it and can bestow or unlock it. To be empowered is to presume the same hierarchy from the other direction: that one's own choosing waits on an external source that already possesses what one lacks. The two presumptions are different registrations—the giver registering the other's lack, the receiver registering their own—yet the transaction, whether offered or accepted, ratifies the same asymmetry. Power is treated as a stock that moves from a center outward, never as discrete activity that closes only where it is enacted.

这个框定，把一个所在上已经在进行中的离散动作，当作可以被搬过所在之间那道间隙的存量。在所在之间被登记为“权力”的，是可得性的样式：早先动作稳定下来的痕迹，塑造着后来的动作会找到哪些对齐是可得的。确有许多东西越过那道间隙，改变那份样式：资源、信息、一笔被放出的预算、一扇被打开的门、别人的回应。它们没有一样能替另一个完成他的选择；每一个动作只在它被做出的地方闭合。日常言语里的范畴错误，是把选择本身当作一份随资源一同越过去的礼物。[验证中因果权能的分配](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/)指出验证中的因果权能不是认可或其缺席的属性，而是心智从中汲取自身区分之延续的那个所在；当被框定为供给之物的是能动性本身，赋权命名的就是这次错放。[能动性的错配](../the-misallocation-of-agency/)是同一次颠倒：自由起于每个心智的自我认出，把它的样式命名为权利、交给制度守护，再把名称当作行为的引擎，便颠倒了因果。

That framing treats a discrete act already underway at a locus as a stock that can be carried across the gap between loci. What registers as “power” between loci is a pattern of availability: stabilized traces of earlier acts that shape which alignments later acts find available. Much does cross the gap and change that pattern: resources, information, a budget released, a door opened, the responses of others. None of them completes another's choosing; each act closes only where it is enacted. The category error in ordinary speech is treating the choosing itself as a gift that crosses with the resources. [The Allocation of Causal Power in Validation](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/) holds that causal power in validation is not a property of approval or its absence, but the locus from which the Mind draws continuation of its own distinctions; when what is framed as supplied is agency itself, empowerment names that misplacement. [The Misallocation of Agency](../the-misallocation-of-agency/) is the same reversal: freedom begins as self-recognition in each Mind, and naming its pattern rights for a system to guard, then treating the name as the engine of the behavior, reverses causality.

[外化善意走向其反面](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/)是道德依据情形里的集中：对自己道德可靠的确信，加上对不同意者有所欠缺的确信，被当作压过他人的依据；自由的顺从让持守这对确信的人成为容器；善一旦被视为理所当然，依据便已过期，权力与地位却还在，而从被压过者那一面看，以善之名执行的东西，正是它声称要防止的那种侵犯。相继的选择在交易的两边始终是杠杆。[主体性的折现](../the-discount-on-agency/)在慈善的情形里画出界线：住所、清水、学校与基础医疗，拓宽的是个人在世界中呼吸与生存的边界，错误起于把支票记作后来轨迹之原因的那个叙事；把一个孩子的生命轨迹算作资本投资的回报，折掉的正是孩子自己的因果，而受助者被教会把自己的未来看作由别人的支票解锁。资源越了过去；轨迹的作者身份没有。

[Externalized Virtue Becomes Its Opposite](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/) is concentration in the case of moral warrant: certainty of one's own moral reliability, joined to certainty that those who disagree are deficient, is made a warrant to override others; free compliance makes the holder of that pair a vessel; once the good is taken for granted, the warrant expires while the power and the standing remain, and from the side of those overridden, what is enforced in the name of the good is the violation it claimed to prevent. Successive choosing remains the lever on both sides of the transaction. [The Discount on Agency](../the-discount-on-agency/) draws the boundary in the case of philanthropy: shelter, clean water, schooling, and basic medical care expand the biological horizon within which a person breathes and acts, and the error begins with the narrative that credits the check as the cause of the later trajectory; treating a child's life trajectory as return on investment discounts the child's own causality, while the recipient is taught to view their future as unlocked by another's check. Resources cross; the authorship of the trajectory does not.

## 二、 重申同一不对称的诸多词汇 / 2. Vocabularies That Reaffirm the Same Asymmetry

这个语言结构并不新。几个世纪以来，同一个不对称被不同的词汇命名过：君权神授、恩庇、教化使命、提升、盟友关系、能力建设。较早的名字公开地把权威放在一个中心；较新的名字声称要解放或提升，同时不出声地重申，那份解放或提升的权力在别处。两边的欲望，给予赋权的与接受赋权的，让这个安排稳定。想给予的一方确认自己是源头；想接受的一方确认自己是依赖者。这个不对称之所以古老，只是作为一种样式，而一种样式只在后来的动作重新追溯它时才成立。以赋权之名做出的每一次提出与每一次接受，都再一次确立这次集中，而较新的名字是以分配权力之名这样做的，于是接受者自己的请求，把那个源头安放了上去。

This linguistic structure is not new. Across centuries the same asymmetry has been named in different vocabularies: divine right, patronage, civilizing mission, uplift, allyship, capacity-building. The older names locate authority openly in a center; the newer ones claim to liberate or elevate while silently reaffirming that the power to liberate or elevate resides elsewhere. The desire on both sides, to bestow empowerment and to receive it, stabilizes the arrangement. The would-be giver confirms their standing as source; the would-be receiver confirms their standing as dependent. The asymmetry is old only as a pattern, and a pattern holds only as later acts re-trace it. Each offer and each acceptance made in the name of empowerment establishes the centralization anew, and the newer names do it in the name of distributing power, so the receiver's own petition seats that source.

[主权、信念与规制结构的生成](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/)是同一前提在制度上的一面：个人的主权无法被委托，生成自律组织与政府的，是相信它可以被委托这一信念的集体表达，而所在始终留在原处。[没有普渡，只有自渡的无限可能](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/)是救度情形里的界限：普度众生是一个未收回的分别，它把内心里的「他人」当成了他人的内心，又把自己的动作登记为替对方完成了返回。没有哪个心智替另一个完成递归；外显的痕迹只有在另一边被选取时才进入。赋权共有的正是那份登记：把给予者的动作登记为接受者已经完成的选择。[解放的言辞始于定义囚禁](../liberation-rhetoric-begins-by-defining-captivity/)是同一几何在先的一面：声称解放他人，先装下的是他们不自由，当作他们之所是，而非对一个行为的读法，于是授予自由的主张得以作为源头立住。[能动性被转置入形式模型](../agency-relocated-into-the-formal-model/)是源头为一个形式模型时的同一次转置：自变量从设计与回应的心智，被移到了人们被催促去掌握的抽象里，而形式知识让约束变得可见，却不授予杠杆。

[Sovereignty, Belief, and the Generation of Regulatory Structures](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/) is the institutional face of the same premise: individual sovereignty cannot be delegated, and what generates a self-regulatory organization and a government is the collective expression of the belief that it can, while the locus stays where it was. [No Universal Salvation, Only Boundless Possibilities of Self-Salvation](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/) is the bound in the case of salvation: universal salvation is an unretracted distinction, which takes the “other” inside one's own mind for the other's mind and registers one's own act as having completed their return. No mind completes another's recursion; a mind's outward traces enter another only by being chosen there. What empowerment shares is that registration: the giver's act registered as the receiver's completed choosing. [Liberation Rhetoric Begins by Defining Captivity](../liberation-rhetoric-begins-by-defining-captivity/) is the prior face of the same geometry: claiming to free another first installs that they are unfree, as what they are rather than one reading of an act, so the claim to bestow freedom can stand as source. [Agency Relocated into the Formal Model](../agency-relocated-into-the-formal-model/) is the same relocation when the source is a formal model: the independent variable is moved from the minds that design and react into the abstractions they are urged to master, while formal knowledge makes the constraints visible, yet does not bestow the lever.

## 三、 选择的自由已经在场 / 3. Freedom of Choice Is Already Present

这个安排未认出的，是选择的自由，它不从一个所在越到另一个所在。自我区分的活动在发生，无因，不息。称它为心智：那个已经在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。那份自由不是被授予的，也不是从外面获得的。它已经在场于每一个人，作为在诸可能之间选取的能力，不论这些可能在任何一刻看上去受到怎样的约束。在所在之间传递的，资源与条件也在其中，是早先动作的残余，它塑造着可得性；选择本身从不越过所在的门槛。

What this arrangement leaves unrecognized is the freedom of choice, which does not cross from one locus to another. Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. That freedom is not bestowed, nor is it acquired from outside. It is already present in every individual as the capacity to select among possibilities, however constrained those possibilities may appear at any given moment. What passes between loci, resources and conditions included, is residue of earlier acts that patterns availability; choosing itself never crosses the threshold of the locus.

当这份自由未被认出，心智便向外寻找替代。它设法控制别人，或顺从于被控制。层级的权力，一个凌驾于另一个之上的权力，作为那份内在未认出的外化而出现：不是收集在一个所在上的存量，而是许多所在上的未认出所留下的、支配与依赖的稳定样式。每一个所在仍在选择。选择者回绝某项局部代价，把由此产生的限制体验为由外部强加的自由缺失，又隐去自身对低代价路径的持续选择，这正是[能动性、矛盾与权力的移置](../agency-contradiction-and-the-relocation-of-power/)所追溯的投射；这时被挪到外部源头上去的，是对这份选择的登记，于是解除约束与迈出下一步，看上去都需要授权。[个体抉择是唯一的因果杠杆](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/)把发起留在相继的动作里：因果只在个体所持续的离散动作中登记，每一个抉择都是一根独立的杠杆，从不是共享的地板；条件与样式是残余与场，从不是动作的共同作者。[自我价值感与所有权](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/)是同一回路的运作：心智只在后果作为自己的后果重新进入时复利，而那同一个回路就是自我价值感。把能力框定为来自别处的礼物，让这次更新变薄，杠杆却始终在它一直所在的地方。[未历摩擦的心智与借来的全能](../the-unfrictioned-mind-and-borrowed-omnipotence/)是保护情形里那份被挪走的登记：以创伤预设清除成长的摩擦，把抚育者未消解的代际投影落在孩子身上，在两个孩子身上催生出朝向不同的结果：召来强制的孩子身上，是借来的全能与脆弱的傲慢；试探被预先判为伤害的孩子身上，是先验负罪；而每一种都经由那个孩子自己的接受而结晶，从不是从外面雕刻出来的。

When this freedom goes unrecognized, the Mind looks outward for a substitute. It seeks to control others or submits to being controlled. Hierarchical power, the power of one over another, arises as the externalization of that internal non-recognition: not a stock collected at one locus, but the stabilized pattern of domination and dependence that non-recognition across many loci leaves. Every locus still chooses; when a chooser declines a local cost, experiences the resulting limitation as an externally imposed lack of freedom, and omits its own ongoing selection of the lower-cost path—the projection traced in [Agency, Contradiction, and the Relocation of Power](../agency-contradiction-and-the-relocation-of-power/)—what is relocated to an outside source is the registration of that choosing, so that lifting the constraint and taking the next step appear to require authorization. [Individual Choices as the Only Causal Levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) keeps initiation with successive acts: causality registers only in the discrete acts individuals sustain, each choice is an independent lever, never a shared floor, while conditions and patterns remain residue and field, never co-authors of the act. [Ownership and Self-Worthiness](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/) is the same loop at work: the Mind compounds only when consequences re-enter as its own, and that same loop is what self-worthiness is. Capacity framed as a gift from elsewhere thins the update while the lever stays where it always was. [The Unfrictioned Mind and Borrowed Omnipotence](../the-unfrictioned-mind-and-borrowed-omnipotence/) is that relocated registration in the case of protection: trauma scripts that clear the friction of growth lay the caregiver's unresolved generational projection on the children and produce a differently aimed result in each: borrowed omnipotence and fragile arrogance in the child who summons enforcement, preemptive guilt in the child whose probes are judged in advance as harm, each crystallizing through that child's own uptake, never sculpted from outside.

## 四、 选项空间经由相继的动作复利 / 4. Option Space Compounds Through Successive Acts

“赋权”这个词，在那份未认出之内兴盛。它把那唯一从未缺席的东西，即选择，装扮成确实在所在之间移动的条件，以此上演一场转移。把能力框定为可以被授予的礼物，它便冻结了可能之场被持守的方式：一只被授权的手被假定掌控着场的成员资格，于是选项显现为一份固定的菜单，由那只手解锁其中的条目，也由它决定是否加长，而不是一个由选择的行使本身不断实现、不断扩展的场。

The term “empowerment” thrives inside that non-recognition. It stages a transfer of the one thing that was never absent—choosing itself—by dressing it up as conditions that do move between loci. By framing capacity as a gift that can be bestowed, it freezes how the field of possibilities is held: an authorized hand is presumed to control membership of the field, so options appear as a fixed menu whose items that hand unlocks and whose length it decides whether to extend, rather than as a field continuously realized and expanded by the exercise of choice itself.

每一个离散的选择动作，都改变下一个选择出现时的条件。技能加深，关系移动，知识累积，资源变化，别人的回应重新排列。被登记为扩大中的选项空间的，是相继的选择动作累积下来的残余。这份扩展是时间性的、累积的；当下这一刻的约束仍然成立，而这些约束本身，是先前选择暂时的结晶。当自由被认出并被付诸行动，结晶便移动，场便生长。[一个提示词之遥](../whatever-is-one-prompt-away/)是自动化情形里的这份生长：看起来是底层的永久失去，其实是难度被同一个活动挪到了新生成的表面上，先前的选项仍然可得，只是在一个扩展着的场里所占的份额在缩小；把这份下降的份额读作一套封闭课程的删除，正是它所消解的冻结，而那份须由被授权之手解锁的固定菜单，是它的反面。[拥有更多从不是原因](../having-more-is-never-the-cause/)是占有情形里的同一次冻结：优势的复利，是留下所偏爱之残余的持续区分，占有命名的是当前的呈现，而不是一股独立的力量，把占有当作进一步优势的作者，就是冻结，正如在这里把被授权之手当作场的作者。[你的代码库即是记忆](../your-codebase-is-memory/)在外化的代码库与自动化脚手架中追溯同一次冻结：代码、模型权重与智能体框架都是先前区分层叠下来的残余，把判断委托给未经修剪的代码库或自动化脚手架，便是把发起的前提拱手让给先前规则累积而成的引力井，正如授予或接受赋权预设并固化由中心权威握有框架，而不是在活的边缘重新切分残余。

Each discrete act of choosing alters the conditions under which the next choice appears. Skills deepen, relationships shift, knowledge accumulates, resources change, and the responses of others rearrange. What registers as an expanding option space is the cumulative residue of successive acts of choosing. The expansion is temporal and cumulative; the constraints of the present moment still hold, yet those constraints are themselves temporary crystallizations of prior choices. When freedom is recognized and enacted, the crystallizations move, and the field grows. [Whatever Is One Prompt Away](../whatever-is-one-prompt-away/) is that growth in the case of automation: what looks like permanent loss of an underlying layer is the relocation of difficulty to a new surface generated by the same activity, with prior options remaining available as a shrinking share of an expanding field; reading that falling share as deletion of a closed curriculum is the freeze it dissolves, and the fixed menu an authorized hand must unlock is its inverse. [Having More Is Never the Cause](../having-more-is-never-the-cause/) is the same freeze in the case of possession: advantage compounding is continued distinguishing that leaves preferred residue, possession names the current rendering rather than an independent force, and treating possession as the author of further advantage is that freeze, just as treating the authorized hand as the author of the field is here. [Your Codebase Is Memory](../your-codebase-is-memory/) traces the same freeze across externalized codebases and automated scaffolds: code, model weights, and agent harnesses are stacked residues of prior distinctions, and delegating judgment to an unpruned codebase or automated scaffold surrenders the initiating premise to the accumulated gravity well of prior rules, just as granting or accepting empowerment presupposes and entrenches a central authority that owns the frame rather than re-cutting the residue at the living edge.

## 五、 请求与许可加固那道门 / 5. Petition and Permission Reinforce the Gate

赋权的语言并不让选择停下；它把选择的朝向引向请求与许可。人们不在敞开的场里直接做出选择，而是选择去等待授权。在被授权之手掌控场之成员资格的预设之下，请求解锁一个条目，与请求加长那份菜单，是同一个形状的两个请求。请求也是选择，它像任何动作一样留下残余：那份残余把后来的动作塑造得朝向那道门，于是场围绕着那道门在其中的位置而生长。声称有权赋权的人，被当作准入的守门人，寻求赋权的人则始终朝向那道门。集中不是这个词偶然的副产品；它是基于这个词所编码之前提做出的每一个动作所留下的残余，每重复一次便变密一次。

Empowerment language does not halt choosing; it redirects the orientation of choice toward petition and permission. Instead of choosing directly within the open field, individuals choose to await authorization. Under the presumption that the authorized hand controls membership of the field, asking for an item to be unlocked and asking for the menu to be enlarged are two requests of the same shape. Petition is choosing too, and it leaves residue like any act: that residue patterns later acts toward the gate, so the field grows around the gate's place in it. Those who claim the authority to empower are cast as gatekeepers of access, while those who seek empowerment remain oriented toward the gate. Centralization is not an accidental byproduct of the term; it is the residue left by each act taken on the premise the term encodes, densified with every repetition.

[来自个体差异的层级](../hierarchy-from-individual-difference/)是排序出现的方式：排序，是彼此不同的个人在一个共同的尺度上被计分时出现的有序样式，而封闭的持守有两面：把那份秩序当作独立于它所排列之差异的外部结构，或是把一个较低的位置登记为从自己的序列之外到来的原因。把授权的地位持为一个授予或扣下场的外部源头，就是那份持守；尺度塑造着后来的动作会找到哪些对齐是可得的，而发起留在动作里。[共同改变的本源](../the-source-of-shared-change/)把共同的改变放回它的起点：它只始于私下重新处理被登记为既定的东西，残余可能落为共同的地基，也可能不会，从不是一次在所在之间完成的转移。[卓异、感知与自我设限的问题场域](../exceptionality-perception-and-the-self-limiting-field-of-problems/)把卓异放为一个共享感知之场里的关系性地位，把制度放为自我选择的沉积，而不是引擎；赋权所请求的那道门，正是这样的沉积，由每一次请求维持在原处。[政客是责任扩散的显性症状](../politicians-appear-as-visible-symptoms-of-responsibility-diffusion/)在剩余控制权的迁移里追溯同一次分配：所有权是剩余控制权，它的登记经由许多个体的选择而迁移，后果却留在原处，而政客标记的是这种迁移，而不是它的源头。寻求所许诺的好处，是把政治之手当作下一步的供给者，正如在这里向那道门请求；瞄准政客，是把这个样式当作一个可以从构成它的那些决定之外去纠正的对象，而责难挪开的是目光，而不是负荷，正如在这里责难那道门。[自我价值、反向财富转移与过去成功的陷阱](../self-worthiness-reverse-wealth-transfer-and-the-trap-of-past-success/)在财富里追溯同一次颠倒：财富是追求自我价值的下游痕迹，陷阱在后来的归因：把累积起来的工具当成能动性之源，让过去的成功成了拖累。

[Hierarchy from Individual Difference](../hierarchy-from-individual-difference/) is how ranking appears: ranking is the ordered pattern that appears when individuals who differ are scored on a shared measure, and the closed hold has two faces: that order taken as exterior structure independent of the difference it orders, or a lower place registering as a cause arriving from outside one's own sequence. Holding the standing to authorize as an external source that bestows or withholds the field is that hold; the measure patterns which alignments later acts find available, while initiation stays with the acts. [The Source of Shared Change](../the-source-of-shared-change/) returns shared change to where it begins: only as private reworking of what registers as given, whose residue may land as common ground or may not, never as a transfer completed across loci. [Exceptionality, Perception, and the Self-Limiting Field of Problems](../exceptionality-perception-and-the-self-limiting-field-of-problems/) places exceptionality as relational status in a shared field of perception and systems as sediment of self-selection, not engines; the gate that empowerment petitions is such sediment, kept in place by each petition. [Politicians Are Visible Symptoms of Responsibility Diffusion](../politicians-appear-as-visible-symptoms-of-responsibility-diffusion/) traces the same allocation in the migration of residual control: ownership is residual control, whose registration migrates through many individual choices while consequences stay in place, and the politician marks that migration, not its origin. Seeking the promised benefit treats the political hand as the supplier of the next step, as petitioning the gate does here; targeting the politician treats the pattern as an object to be corrected from outside the decisions that constitute it, and blame relocates the look, not the load, as blaming the gate does here. [Self-Worthiness, Reverse Wealth Transfer, and the Trap of Past Success](../self-worthiness-reverse-wealth-transfer-and-the-trap-of-past-success/) traces the same reversal in wealth: wealth trails the pursuit of self-worthiness, and the trap is the later attribution that takes the accumulated tool for the source of agency, turning past success into drag.

## 六、 认出在边缘找到杠杆 / 6. Recognition Finds the Lever at the Edge

这个安排里的改变起于别处：不在权力的授予里，而在认出选择的自由从未缺席。这份认出并不消解物质的限制或利益的冲突。它消解的只是一个冻结，而这个冻结有两份登记。对接受者，从内部将既有安排与所感到的约束登记为自身持续选择的同时面向，会把局部代价从未经许可便无法行动的理由，重新变为附着在可见替代选项上的标价；这消解的是“自己的能动性必须由别人来授权”这个冻结，对外部赋权的要求便失去地基。对给予者，认出没有哪个所在能替另一个完成选择，消解的是“对方的能力有所欠缺、而由自己来授予”这个预设，想握有凌驾于别人之上之权力的欲望，也失去了曾为它供能的那份登记。每一份认出只在它自己的所在上发生。留下的是选择本身持续的工作：对诸可能相继的实现，经由每一个动作留下的痕迹，不断拓宽选择得以行使的那个对齐之场。

What changes in this arrangement begins elsewhere: not in the bestowal of power, but in the recognition that the freedom of choice was never absent. This recognition does not dissolve material limits or conflicts of interest. It dissolves only a freeze, and that freeze has two registrations. For the receiver, registering both the existing arrangement and the felt constraint from within as simultaneous aspects of one's own ongoing choosing turns local costs from reasons one cannot move without permission back into prices attached to visible alternatives; this dissolves the freeze that one's agency must be authorized by another, and the demand for external empowerment loses its foundation. For the giver, recognizing that no locus completes another's choosing dissolves the presumption that the other's capacity is lacking and one's own to bestow, and the desire to hold power over others loses the registration that had fueled it. Each recognition occurs only at its own locus. What remains is the ongoing work of choice itself: the successive actualization of possibilities that, through the traces each act leaves, continually widens the field of alignments in which choice is exercised.

赋权的语言做不了这份工作。凭它的结构，它预设了它声称要克服的层级，又把对更宽之场的欲望引回对那个层级的维持。被使用时，这个词不只是描述一个权力关系：以它的语汇做出的每一次提出与每一次接受，都追认那个前提，让权力的集中变密为稳定的残余，而选择的自由保持未被认出。每一个选择者的朝向停在外部的许可上，把它当作下一步的供给，而正是这份朝向让这个安排可被管控：下一步经由许可去寻求，于是谁握有许可，谁看上去就是它的作者，而作者身份始终留在做选择的那个人身上。因果停留在当下之场里继续选择的那个边缘上，也包括这样一个选择：继续去寻求一份从来不是所缺之项的礼物。[对乌托邦的信念是通往反乌托邦之路](../the-belief-in-utopia-is-the-path-to-dystopia/)是善之尺度上的那道门：一个具体的善的理想，一旦被立为每个心智须接受的尺度，便把那些心智所偏好的目的计为有待纠正的缺陷，对每一个目的不同的心智就已经是反乌托邦。这个尺度支配另一片场，只在那个位置上的又一个动作，即顺从或授予，让它支配的地方；被授权的秩序声称要打开别人被假定所缺之场，而那片场从未缺席，它所求的，正是那个位置上的这份授予；每一次接受赋权，都在预演它。

The language of empowerment cannot perform this work. By its structure it presupposes the hierarchy it claims to overcome and channels the desire for a wider field back into the maintenance of that hierarchy. In use, the term does more than describe a relation of power: each offer and each acceptance made in its terms ratifies that premise and densifies the centralization of power into stable residue, while the freedom of choice remains unrecognized. Each chooser's orientation rests on external permission as the supply of the next step, and that orientation is what makes the arrangement governable: the next step is sought through permission, so whoever holds permission appears to author it, while authorship remains with the one who chooses. Causality stays at the edge that continues to choose within the present field, including the choice to keep seeking a gift that was never the missing item. [The Belief in Utopia Is the Path to Dystopia](../the-belief-in-utopia-is-the-path-to-dystopia/) is that gate at the scale of the good: a concrete ideal of the good, made the measure every Mind must accept, scores the ends those Minds prefer as defects to be corrected, and is already dystopia for every Mind whose ends differ. The measure rules another field only where a further act at that locus, compliance or grant, lets it; an authorized order claims to unlock a field others are presumed to lack, a field that was never absent, and what it asks for is that grant from the locus it would rule; each acceptance of empowerment rehearses it.
