# 索求万物理论是在将当下冻结为清单 / Demanding a Theory of Everything Freezes Time into a Catalog

*把投影出的时间当作时间，回避了未来，也把不可化约的前提降成了假说 / Taking projected time for time avoids the future and demotes the irreducible prior to a hypothesis*

万物理论的设想，靠着一份关于过去的完备记录与一条预先画好的未来轨迹，而能指出来的每一件证据都在此刻，过去与未来是从当下向两侧投出的坐标。索求这样一套理论的举动本身就是一次新的因果步进，清单写成的那一刻，现实已经走到了下一步。理论捕获的是有限窗口内跨越时间的可观测不变量，它与所经验的现实之间隔着近乎无限的复杂度，这道间隔在日常里显现为摩擦，也正是人对万物理论的渴望的来处。渴望推动一次次渐进的改进，把闭合当作可以达到的，则是把工具倒置成了本体。

The idea of a theory of everything leans on a complete record of the past and a future trajectory drawn in advance, while every piece of evidence that can be pointed to is in this moment, and past and future are coordinates projected to either side from the present. The act of demanding such a theory is itself a new causal step, and at the moment the catalog is written, reality has already moved to the next. What a theory captures is observable invariance across time within a limited window; between it and the reality experienced lies a nearly unlimited complexity, a gap that shows up in daily life as friction and is also where the desire for a theory of everything comes from. The desire drives one incremental improvement after another, and taking closure to be attainable turns a tool into an ontology.

## 一、 没有“过去的客观记录” / 1. There Is No "Objective Record of the Past"

关于万物理论的经典设想里有一个很少被反思的前提：科学之所以能总结出终极的规律，是因为有一套客观而完备的“历史记录”。只要挖出足够多的古老地层，捕获足够微弱的宇宙微波背景辐射，记下足够详尽的粒子对撞数据，便有了一份关于过往全部物理事实的底账，最终的方程可以立在它上面。

The classical idea of a theory of everything carries a premise that is seldom examined: science can sum up ultimate laws because there is an objective and complete "historical record." Dig out enough ancient strata, capture a faint enough cosmic microwave background, log particle collisions in enough detail, and there is a ledger of all past physical facts on which a final equation can be raised.

从当下出发去找，找不到一座存放“过去”的仓库，也找不到一卷写满“历史”的底片。能指出来的每一件证据都在此刻。手里的三叶虫化石，是此刻躺在掌心的一块排列特殊的方解石结晶。射电望远镜接收到的微波背景光子，是此刻在天线传感器里激起的微弱电流。硬盘上的实验读数，是此刻保持着特定磁化取向的磁畴。脑中的记忆，也是此刻神经元网络维持着的电化学构型。

Searching from the present, no warehouse storing "the past" is found, nor any reel of film inscribed with "history." Every piece of evidence that can be pointed to is in this moment. The trilobite fossil in the hand is a calcite crystal of a particular arrangement lying in the palm now. The microwave background photons a radio telescope receives are a faint current excited in the antenna's sensor now. The experimental readings on a hard drive are magnetic domains holding a particular orientation now. A memory in the brain, too, is an electrochemical configuration that a network of neurons is maintaining now.

“过去”由此是一种投影：心智在当下把此刻的构型向后投到一条画出来的几何轴线上。是心智在当下指着一块石头说“这代表一亿年前的海洋”，是理论家在当下指着一段光谱说“这代表宇宙初期的暴胀”，从唯一的当下出发，向后画出一条标着坐标 t 的射线，把此刻的结构解释为那条轴上的点。这种投影极为有用，地质学与宇宙学都靠它工作。它的边界在于被当作一个盛放实体的容器的时候。“在任何观察者出现之前的几十亿年”便是这样一处：它是测量的割点被推到因果链最远一端的说法，[割点可移不可除](../the-cut-moves-but-never-vanishes/)说明割点移到哪里预测都不变，在哪里接受它是心智的抉择。过去与未来在这一点上没有分别，都是从当下向两侧画出的射线，以“过去的客观记录”为底去编织万物理论，第一步便把投影当成了现实。

"The past" is thus a projection: a Mind in the present casts the configuration of this moment backward onto a geometric axis it has drawn. It is a Mind in the present that points to a rock and says "this stands for an ocean a hundred million years ago," and a theorist in the present who points to a spectrum and says "this stands for inflation in the early universe"; starting from the only present, a ray marked with the coordinate t is drawn backward, and the structure of this moment is read as a point on that axis. The projection is extremely useful, and geology and cosmology work by it. Its boundary lies where it is taken for a container holding things. "Billions of years before any observer" is one such place: it states the measurement cut pushed to the farthest end of the causal chain, and [The Cut Moves but Never Vanishes](../the-cut-moves-but-never-vanishes/) shows that the predictions stay the same wherever the cut is moved, while where it is accepted is the Mind's choice. Past and future do not differ in this respect; both are rays drawn to either side from the present, and weaving a theory of everything on the basis of an "objective record of the past" takes the projection for reality at its first step.

## 二、 构想即过时 / 2. Obsolete at Inception

时间既然不是一条现成的维度，而是现实不可逆的展开，索求万物理论便遇到一处绕不开的自指：构想一个念头、写下一组方程、发出“要一个终极理论”的索求，本身就是一次因果事件。物理学家坐在黑板前，心里浮起把全部规律闭合为一组方程的念头，或在草稿上写下最后一个统一的算符时，现实并不停下来等。在念头萌生、粉笔划过黑板的那一瞬，因果已经向前推进，现实已经走到了下一步（+1）。

Since time is not a ready-made dimension but the irreversible unfolding of reality, the demand for a theory of everything meets a self-reference it cannot get around: conceiving a thought, writing down a set of equations, voicing the demand for "a final theory" are themselves causal events. When a physicist sits before a blackboard and the thought of closing all laws into one set of equations rises, or when the last unified operator is written on a draft, reality does not stop to wait. In the instant the thought forms and the chalk crosses the board, causality has already moved forward and reality has taken the next step (+1).

这里无需罗列能量耗散或信息擦除之类的注脚。因果不可逆地向前推进，本身就是缘由。神经元的放电、热量的耗散与熵的增减，是因果的流动在下游显出的伴生症状，把这些症状列为“原因”，反而把不可化约的因果降成了局域的机械决定。

There is no need here to list footnotes such as the dissipation of energy or the erasure of information. That causality moves forward irreversibly is itself the ground. The firing of neurons, the dissipation of heat and the rise or fall of entropy are accompanying symptoms the flow of causality shows downstream, and listing those symptoms as "causes" would instead reduce irreducible causality to local mechanical determination.

万物理论的初衷，是给现实制作一份穷尽全部状态与法则的静态全景，而构想与记录这个动作本身就是因果正在发生的新的一瞬。在以为终于把一切“打包封口”的那一刻，现实已经跨过门槛，所捕获的全景在落笔的第一毫秒已是留在身后的旧投影。它想用系统内部的一个局域动作去定住整个系统的流动，而这个动作的存在，正是流动不曾停下的直接见证。画不出一张盖住全部领土的地图，因为画这张地图的墨水，就是领土上正在出现的新地貌。

The original aim of a theory of everything is to make of reality a static panorama exhausting all states and laws, while the act of conceiving and recording is itself a new instant of causality taking place. At the moment everything is believed to be "packed and sealed" at last, reality has already crossed the threshold, and the panorama captured is, within the first millisecond of the pen coming down, an old projection left behind. It tries to hold the flow of the whole system still by one local act inside the system, and the existence of that act is direct witness that the flow has not stopped. No map can be drawn that covers the whole territory, because the ink drawing the map is new terrain appearing on the territory.

## 三、 理论能捕获什么 / 3. What a Theory Can Capture

那么物理学里那些极受珍视的理论，捕获的是什么？任何理论所能捕获的，是在有限的观测窗口之内，跨越时间的可观测不变量。能量守恒、动量守恒、规范对称性、麦克斯韦方程与爱因斯坦场方程，是心智在文明短暂的观测尺度里提炼出的高度抽象的概念模型：在有限的观测历程中，某些数学比例与代数结构保持着稳定的一致，这些可观测的不变性被命名为“定律”。块状宇宙正是这样冻结时间的，[双生子佯谬与隐秘观察者](../the-twin-paradox-and-the-hidden-observer/)找出了它藏起的观察者。

What, then, do the most treasured theories of physics capture? What any theory can capture is observable invariance across time within a limited window of observation. Conservation of energy and momentum, gauge symmetry, Maxwell's equations and the Einstein field equations are highly abstract conceptual models a Mind has distilled within the brief observational scale of a civilization: over a limited course of observation, certain mathematical ratios and algebraic structures hold a stable consistency, and these observable invariances are named "laws." The block universe freezes time this way, and [The Twin Paradox and the Hidden Observer](../the-twin-paradox-and-the-hidden-observer/) finds the observer it hides.

这个概念模型的复杂度，与正在直接经验的现实相比，是微小的。这里有一个常被略过的命题，关乎信息与物质的关系。人们常把“信息”与“物质”对立起来，或把信息看作浮在物质之上的次级产物。而物质须同样具有信息性，信息才可能表征它。数学符号与信息模型之所以能与物理世界咬合，是因为跨越两个领域的因果是同一个：每一次物理的相互作用，一次光子的吸收、一次电子自旋的翻转、一次分子的碰撞，在物理上是一次不可逆的因果步进，在信息上是一次区分。

The complexity of this conceptual model is tiny beside the reality being directly experienced. There is a proposition here that is often passed over, concerning the relation of information and matter. "Information" and "matter" are commonly set against each other, or information is seen as a secondary product floating above matter. Yet matter must be informational as well for information to be able to represent it. Mathematical symbols and informational models can mesh with the physical world because the causality across the two domains is one and the same: each physical interaction, a photon absorbed, an electron's spin flipped, a collision of molecules, is physically an irreversible causal step and informationally a distinction.

正因为物质的每一个微观自由度每时每刻都在作着这样的因果演进，模型与现实之间的复杂度差距，在一切实用的意义上近乎无穷。这不是“实无穷”：无穷只是一种潜在的开放，不是一个已经完成的数值。现实在离散地生成，不是一个现成的无穷集合，而对任何能写下的有限符号模型来说，现实所含的因果复杂度与模型的容量相比，呈现出近乎无穷的断层。即便是最优 N 与最差 N，也仍停在清单之内，[闭合的N与开放的现实](../the-closed-n-and-the-open-reality/)说明了这一点。

Precisely because every microscopic degree of freedom of matter is carrying out such causal evolution at every moment, the difference in complexity between model and reality is, for all practical purposes, close to infinite. This is not an "actual infinity": infinity is only a potential openness, not a completed quantity. Reality is generated discretely and is not a ready-made infinite set, yet for any finite symbolic model that can be written down, the causal complexity reality holds, set against the model's capacity, presents a break that is nearly infinite. Even Best-of-N and Worst-of-N stay inside a catalog, as [The Closed N and the Open Reality](../the-closed-n-and-the-open-reality/) shows.

索求万物理论的结构性局限在这里显出来。写下几行由几十个符号构成的拉格朗日量，并设想这几行信息已经穷尽了物质宇宙的全部，是一种可以理解的思想上的越界，好比几滴凝住的水珠宣称自己容纳了那片不曾停歇的海。

The structural limit of demanding a theory of everything shows itself here. Writing down a few lines of Lagrangian made of a few dozen symbols and supposing that these lines of information have exhausted the whole of the material universe is an understandable overextension of thought, like a few congealed drops of water claiming to contain the sea that never stops moving.

## 四、 日常的摩擦与对万物理论的渴望 / 4. Daily Friction and the Desire for a Theory of Everything

在这道间隔上，日常处境里的一个机制显出来：每一个瞬间都在经验到的粗粝摩擦，与对万物理论的长久渴望，是同一枚硬币的两面。不论计划多周密、科学模型多高深、算法工具多精密，从这一瞬迈进下一瞬时，仍会撞上意外、挫败、阻滞、没有预见的变化与消耗。这是那道近乎无限的复杂度间隔在生命里的直接显现。

On this gap a mechanism of the everyday situation shows itself: the coarse friction experienced at every instant and the long-standing desire for a theory of everything are two faces of one coin. However thorough the plan, however advanced the scientific model, however precise the algorithmic tool, stepping from this instant into the next still runs into surprise, setback, drag, unforeseen change and expenditure. This is the nearly unlimited gap in complexity appearing directly in a life.

抽象的认知模型只抓住了几条可观测的不变量，所处的现实却在每一个微秒里以近乎无限的自由度演进。带着一个复杂度极低的模型跨进下一瞬时，模型没有包含的因果细节便撞上预期。这种撞击在第一人称的经验里叫作“摩擦”。摩擦不是系统的故障，它是现实装不进行囊的直接读数。

An abstract cognitive model grasps only a few observable invariances, while the reality one is in evolves at every microsecond with nearly unlimited degrees of freedom. Carrying a model of very low complexity across into the next instant, the causal detail the model does not contain strikes against expectation. In first-person experience this impact is called "friction." Friction is not a malfunction of the system; it is the direct reading that reality does not fit in the bag.

心智面对摩擦时展开的是一个递归：正因为不可能有一套能从这一瞬完好带到下一瞬的万物理论，才一直渴望着一个万物理论。假如真有一套完备的理论，像一把万能钥匙从此时带到彼时而没有信息的泄漏，跨步时便感受不到摩擦与意外，而那样的宇宙是一台上紧了的发条，抉择也无从谈起。于是“无法一次带走”被反过来构想成一个可以解决的问题：只要有更完备的模型，就能跨越时间带着它，消除没有预测到的摩擦。罗盘之所以一直被寄予期望，正因为海浪一次又一次把指针打偏。

## 五、 渴望与闭合的假定 / 5. Desire and the Assumption of Closure

这里有两种需要分开的东西：渴望，与假定闭合可以实现。前者是减少摩擦的动力，对确定与可预测的需求推动着工具的创造、技术的演进与经验的积累，轮子、算盘、经典力学与微积分都由它而来，一次又一次渐进的改进也由它而来。后者把工具倒置为本体：一旦把作为认知界面的局部模型当成宇宙的全貌，临时搭起的脚手架便成了世界的边界，模型囊括不了的生成与未决则被归为“测量的噪音”或“主观的幻觉”。也可以说，正是这个未经审视的假定，在不断地助长那份渴望。

Two things need to be kept apart here: desire, and the assumption that closure can be achieved. The first is the drive to reduce friction; the need for certainty and predictability pushes the making of tools, the development of technique and the accumulation of experience, and the wheel, the abacus, classical mechanics and the calculus come from it, as does one incremental improvement after another. The second turns a tool into an ontology: once a local model that serves as a cognitive interface is taken for the full extent of the universe, scaffolding put up for the time being becomes the boundary of the world, and the generation and openness the model cannot include are filed as "measurement noise" or "subjective illusion." It can also be said that this unexamined assumption is what keeps feeding the desire.

把可求当作可达，与把“无穷”当作一个已经存在的封闭全体而非敞开的潜在生成，是同一种做法。相信万物理论可以完成，如同相信数数可以数到无穷大。它与微积分里的那一步也成镜像：为了让求导闭合，微积分把非零的、不可逆的因果微元 dx 宣布为零；为了让全景的解释闭合，万物理论把原因与结果之间那道不可化约的、始终非零的离散间隙宣布为已被统一方程合上，[连续性作为建模的权宜之计](../the-continuum-is-a-modeling-convenience/)展开了前一步。

Taking what can be sought for what can be reached is the same move as taking "infinity" for an existing closed totality instead of an open potential generation. Believing a theory of everything can be completed is like believing counting can reach infinity. It also mirrors a step in the calculus: to let differentiation close, the calculus declares the non-zero, irreversible causal element dx to be zero; to let a panoramic explanation close, a theory of everything declares the irreducible, always non-zero discrete gap between cause and effect to have been sealed by a unified equation. [The Continuum is a Modeling Convenience](../the-continuum-is-a-modeling-convenience/) lays out the first of these.

在可观测的物理世界里，这个归不了零的最小因果步长，被物理学记为一个基本常数，普朗克常数 h。因为 h ≠ 0，微观的作用存在不可再分的离散跃迁，模型无法被无限平滑地微分。它是现实的刷新率：现实不是一张平滑的连续画布，它在摩擦中一帧一帧地生成。

In the observable physical world, this smallest causal step that cannot be set to zero is recorded by physics as a fundamental constant, the Planck constant h. Because h ≠ 0, microscopic action involves discrete transitions that cannot be divided further, and a model cannot be differentiated with unlimited smoothness. It is the refresh rate of reality: reality is not a smooth continuous canvas, and it is generated frame by frame within friction.

在这道合不上的间隙里，量子效应与主观的自由感是同一枚硬币的两面。从外部，以第三人称去观测这道间隙时，没有哪一个确定性的方程能跨过那个非零的步长，测得的现象便呈现为概率性的塌缩、不确定性与非决定的跃迁，这被命名为“量子效应”。作为第一人称的主体，在因果发生的前沿承担后果并迈出那一步时，同一道敞开的间隙，在直接的经验里是自由感、决断与对后果的承受。无穷是潜在的，因果的间隙始终非零，所以无法有万物理论；第三人称的形式无法事先决定那一步，所以微观呈现为量子效应；第一人称的主体直接承担每一步的后果，所以生命经验到自由与摩擦。这些都是因果与第一人称视角作为一枚硬币两面的显现。

## 七、 理性的假定与后视的“非理性” / 7. The Assumption of Rationality and "Irrationality" in Hindsight

No one who is awake deliberately makes, at the moment of deciding, a choice that they clearly judge to have no reason at all.

这种在内部经验到的自洽，从外部被另一个心智审视时，便呈现为所谓的“非理性”。观察的一方无法经验行动一方的第一人称因果历史，只能站在自己的坐标系里，用自己投射出的那张现实切片与价值尺度去丈量对方。对方在自身约束下的自洽反应套不进这张预设的网格，便被判为“非理性”或“有认知偏差”。这与时空模型是同构的：物理学把感知的不变感抽象为客观的四维时空，理性的理论把某个局域观察者的偏好抽象为“客观的理性标准”。

## 八、 理论内部的自洽与抽身 / 8. Coherence Inside a Theory and Stepping Out

That reality does not present itself as a closed theory of everything is not the setting of some outside will; it comes from the openness of causal generation itself. The preference of one Mind does not automatically become the preference of another, still less of all. On the projected time dimension everything is already complete; in living time, every breath, every observation, every choice pushes the whole map, at an irreducible cost, to somewhere irreversibly new.

## 九、 回避未来 / 9. Avoiding the Future

任何理论都穷尽不了生成着的现实，许多智识传统却长久地追求一套闭合的终极理论。这出于认知对确定与稳定的偏好：想靠一个完备的静态模型，避开面对未决的未来时的不确定。

No theory exhausts a reality that is being generated, and yet many intellectual traditions have long pursued a closed final theory. This comes from cognition's preference for certainty and stability: the wish to avoid, by means of a complete static model, the uncertainty of facing an undecided future.

活的时间是不可逆的。在当下，倒带撤销不了一个动作，改变坐标的符号也消除不了一次已经发生的事。迈向未来的方式只有一种：承受摩擦，背负不可逆的代价，踩出那一步没有任何既有公式担保的险步（+1）。在这一步落地之前，没有哪组主方程能打包票，没有哪个拉格朗日量能分担犯错的痛，未来的形态取决于行动者在粗粝的现实里作出的决断。

Living time is irreversible. In the present, rewinding does not undo an act, and changing the sign of a coordinate does not remove something that has happened. There is only one way into the future: bearing friction, carrying an irreversible cost, and taking the risky step that no existing formula guarantees (+1). Before that step lands, no master equation can vouch for it, no Lagrangian can share the pain of being wrong, and the shape of the future depends on the decision an actor makes within coarse reality.

万物理论是消除这种不确定的一种构想。把现实看作一份基于当下的全知清单，把未来表述为一条预先写定的轨迹，它给出的是一个确定的承诺：未来只是一段已经算好的坐标，只需推演它的代数解。索求万物理论，于是是用对坐标维度的代数计算，代替了踏入未来的那一步因果。

A theory of everything is one way of conceiving the removal of this uncertainty. Seeing reality as an all-knowing catalog based on the present and stating the future as a trajectory written in advance, it offers a definite promise: the future is only a stretch of coordinates already computed, and its algebraic solution need only be worked out. Demanding a theory of everything thus puts algebraic calculation over a coordinate dimension in place of the causal step into the future.

## 十、 物理学在前提的下游 / 10. Physics Stands Downstream of the Prior

分析到这里，常见的回应是：再给几十年，建成能标更高的对撞机，完成量子引力与时空涌现的数学，物理学自会把时间、因果与观测者解释清楚。这个回应的边界在次序上：物理学的全部方程，都是建立在这个前提之上的下游产物。
