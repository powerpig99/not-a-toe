# 可延展的地平线 / The Extendable Horizon

*正和的能动性不是外缘的精英坐标，而是在寄存器任何位置都能发起的取向 / Positive-sum agency is no elite coordinate at the outer edge but an orientation open at any position in the register.*

从外部去看一个文明的容量，能看到社会的富余怎样供养零和的摩擦；可这幅图容易长出一个错觉：非零和的能动性是少数站在寄存器最外缘的开拓者的专利，其余位置只能困在地位之争里。这是把一个心智缩成了一个建制坐标。从第一人称看，正和的创造不是社会账本上的一个地理位置，而是面对因果的一种取向；地平线不是固定的天花板，注入自由变量的能力在系统里每一个坐标上都在场。

Looking at a civilization's capacity from outside shows how social abundance subsidizes zero-sum friction; but the picture easily grows an illusion: that non-zero-sum agency is the preserve of the few pioneers standing at the register's outermost edge, while every other position is stuck in the scramble for status. That shrinks a Mind to an institutional coordinate. From the first person, positive-sum creation is not a geographic location on society's balance sheet but an orientation toward causality; the horizon is not a fixed ceiling, and the capacity to inject a free variable is present at every coordinate in the system.

## 一、 外部视角的陷阱 / 1. The Trap of the Outside View

[寄存器的算术](../the-arithmetic-of-the-register/)勾勒出一个宏观的结构：一位探索者实例化一个新的最高位 bₙ，产生的富余向低位逐级流下，在那里被有界的感知投射成低维的地位竞赛。对公共协调，这幅图是有用的描述。

[The Arithmetic of the Register](../the-arithmetic-of-the-register/) sketched a macro structure: when an explorer instantiates a new most significant bit bₙ, the slack it produces cascades down to the lower bits, where bounded perception projects it into low-dimensional status contests. As a description of public coordination, the picture is useful.

只从第三人称的宏观视角看它，会留下一个解释上的盲区。它诱人相信，只有在地址空间最外缘工作的少数人才有清明去玩正和游戏，身处较低位 [b₃, b₈] 的人则在数学上注定相互蚕食。它分析了舞台，却略过了台上每个人自己的取向，把人当成由地址决定的棋子，忘了一个人在公共账本上的坐标，对其内部的取向什么也没有说。这正是[寄存器的算术](../the-arithmetic-of-the-register/)所说的本体膨胀：把为公共协调设计的表征，当成了活着的意识本身。寄存器模型描述的是公共记账的脚手架，不是一个心智的边界。

Seen only from the third-person macro view, it leaves an explanatory blind spot. It invites the belief that only the few working at the outermost edge of the address space have the clarity to play positive-sum games, while those in the lower bits [b₃, b₈] are mathematically bound to devour one another. It analyses the stage and passes over each actor's own orientation, treating people as pawns fixed by their address and forgetting that a person's coordinate in the public ledger says nothing about their inner orientation. This is the ontological inflation named in [The Arithmetic of the Register](../the-arithmetic-of-the-register/): taking a representation built for public coordination for living awareness itself. The register model describes the scaffolding of public accounting, not the boundary of a Mind.

## 二、 任何坐标上的正和 / 2. Positive-Sum at Any Coordinate

在共享界面的某个局部坐标上做事的人，维护软件基础设施，经营一家社区诊所，照看一片农场，教一个班级，并不注定陷入零和的争斗。正和的能动性不是一个索引位置，而是面对现实的一种内部取向。

Someone working at a local coordinate of the shared interface, maintaining software infrastructure, running a neighbourhood clinic, tending a farm, teaching a class, is not bound to zero-sum struggle. Positive-sum agency is not an index position but an inner orientation toward reality.

带着这种取向，最高位的扩张不被登记为对自身位置的否定，或对相对地位的威胁；新生的富余被当成一份可以借用的启发，一份延展自身作业地平线的邀请。不去争夺既有的行政预算或社会声望的份额，问的是生成性的问题：新解锁的能力怎样用来解决自己领域里尚未解决的摩擦，怎样在这里精进手艺、提高通量、减少浪费。通俗所说的“成长型思维”，就是不把当前的地址窗口当成一只守恒的封闭盒子：价值来自实际做出的体力与心智工作，不来自对既有剩余的重新分配。把自己的局部领域当作一片关于质量的开放前沿，任何坐标都可以成为生成的现场，在共享界面之内玩一场无限游戏。

In this orientation the expansion of the top bit does not register as a denial of one's own position or a threat to relative status; the new slack is taken as an inspiration to borrow from, an invitation to extend one's own working horizon. Instead of competing for shares of an existing administrative budget or social prestige, the questions asked are generative: how can the newly unlocked capability resolve unresolved friction in one's own domain, how can craft be refined, throughput raised and waste cut right here. What is popularly called the "growth mindset" is not treating the current address window as a conserved, closed box: value comes from physical and mental work actually done, not from redistributing claims on existing surplus. Treating one's local domain as an open frontier of quality turns any coordinate into a site of generation, playing an infinite game inside the shared interface.

## 三、 反应与注入 / 3. Reacting and Injecting

那么“零和之坑”是从哪里来的？不是来自所占的坐标，而是来自把现实当成静止的封闭取向。每一次假定现实是固定的、完成的、早被操纵好的，自身的自由度就被自己交了出去。

Where then does the "zero-sum pit" come from? Not from the coordinate occupied, but from the closed orientation that treats reality as static. Each time reality is assumed to be fixed, finished or rigged in advance, one's own degrees of freedom are handed over by oneself.

把局部坐标上的规则、分配与边界当成不可动摇的，就不再生成原因。[决定论机器](../the-deterministic-machine/)剖开的正是这一层：心智缩成一台封闭的状态机，对进来的刺激作出反应，守护脆弱的自我形象，把模型确定的相变当成外部的命运。[心智的几何学](../the-geometry-of-mind/)走过同一处：死守一个僵硬的坐标，是想从现实加速的反馈回路里退出，用活的能动去换一个冻结形象的安全。

Taking the rules, allocations and boundaries of a local coordinate as unshakable stops the generating of causes. [The Deterministic Machine](../the-deterministic-machine/) dissects exactly this layer: the Mind shrinks into a closed state machine, reacting to incoming stimuli, guarding a fragile self-image, and taking its model's determined phase shifts for an outside fate. [The Geometry of Mind](../the-geometry-of-mind/) walks the same place: clinging to a rigid coordinate is an attempt to step out of reality's accelerating feedback loop, trading living agency for the safety of a frozen image.

另一条路从未被收回。[唯一自洽的无因之果](../the-only-coherent-uncaused-effect/)：注入自由变量不需要建制的许可、巨额的资本或最外缘的地址；在作决定的第一人称刀锋上，任何一刻都可以不让这台机器的先例决定下一步。在预料会敷衍的地方把一件手艺认真做完，在惯于含糊的地方把事情说清楚，局部的闭环就被打开，新的因果从内向外发起。

The other road is never withdrawn. [The Only Coherent Uncaused Effect](../the-only-coherent-uncaused-effect/): injecting a free variable needs no institutional licence, vast capital or outermost address; at the first-person knife-edge of decision, at any moment, the machine's precedent can be kept from deciding the next step. Finishing a piece of craft with care where carelessness was expected, saying a thing clearly where vagueness was the habit, opens the local closed loop, and new causality starts from the inside out.

## 四、 有限游戏与无限游戏 / 4. Finite Games and the Infinite Game

不必等到爬上建制阶梯的“顶端”，才去更新自己的地平线。在社会的任何一层，都可以把因果链一直追到底。[遗忘的脚手架](../the-scaffolding-we-forget/)：学历证书、官僚的把关、企业里的声望阶梯，不是首要的地形，而是在某个历史基线上为协调活动搭起来的、临时的人造脚手架。它们的用处在于协调个体之间的行动；它们的边界，在被当成地形本身的那一刻。

There is no need to wait until the "top" of an institutional ladder to update one's horizon. At any level of society the causal chain can be traced all the way down. [The Scaffolding We Forget](../the-scaffolding-we-forget/): credentials, bureaucratic gatekeeping and corporate prestige ladders are not primary terrain but temporary, human-built scaffolds put up to coordinate activity against some historical baseline. Their use is coordinating actions among individuals; their boundary is the moment they are taken for the terrain itself.

詹姆斯·卡斯区分了两种游戏。有限游戏在大家同意的规则之内、为了赢而玩：拿到头衔，占下市场份额，击败对手。无限游戏为了让游戏继续下去而玩：发现新的自由度，扩展游戏本身的边界。看清这一点，共识网格上的有限地位竞赛就不必再看得那么重；即使在低位上履行一份操作性的职责，忠诚也可以落在现实的无限游戏上。地平线不再被职位说明书框住，框住它的只有一次次碰到的摩擦。

James Carse distinguished two kinds of game. A finite game is played inside agreed rules in order to win: securing a title, capturing market share, defeating a rival. The infinite game is played in order to keep the play going: discovering new degrees of freedom and extending the boundary of the game itself. Seeing this, the finite status contests of the consensus grid need not weigh so much; even while carrying out an operational role in the lower bits, loyalty can rest with the infinite game of reality. The horizon is no longer framed by a job description; what frames it is only the friction met, time after time.

## 五、 S 曲线的解剖 / 5. The Anatomy of the S-Curve

个体的取向怎样变成宏观的形态，在一个极端的假定下看得最清楚：现实是封闭的。在这个假定下从底部起步的个人或企业，一开始并不觉得停滞；从自身的位置看，起步阶段像是指数式的。掌握成文的规则，吸收现成的最佳做法，摘下框里还没人摘的低垂果实，进步来得很快，轨迹看上去像无界的增长。可系统被假定为封闭的，市场总量固定，资源有限，边界条件不动，这条指数式的攀升迟早撞上框架的承载上限。[封闭的假设把复利挤成 S 曲线](../closed-assumptions-squeeze-compounding-into-s-curves/)：曲线减速，压平成一条逻辑斯蒂 S 曲线。

How individual orientation becomes macro pattern is clearest under one extreme assumption: that reality is closed. A person or firm starting from the bottom under that assumption does not feel stagnation at first; from its own position the opening phase looks exponential. Mastering the codified rules, absorbing existing best practice, picking low-hanging fruit no one has yet picked inside the frame, progress comes fast and the trajectory looks like unbounded growth. But with the system assumed closed, a fixed total market, finite resources, unmoving boundary conditions, that exponential climb sooner or later hits the frame's carrying capacity. [Closed Assumptions Squeeze Compounding into S-Curves](../closed-assumptions-squeeze-compounding-into-s-curves/): the curve slows and flattens into a logistic S-curve.

这说明了成熟的“既定行业”为什么呈现相似的症状。汽车制造、传统航空、商业银行、传统出版，回头去看，都是一种固定现实在建制上的结晶：标准被定死，监管的护城河被筑起，增长被建模成对一块封闭的饼的争夺。行业的 S 曲线饱和之后，框里的竞争容易转成对零碎份额的内耗、行政寻租与品牌诉讼。[闭合的N与开放的现实](../the-closed-n-and-the-open-reality/)看到了同一处：在 N 个已知选项里择优，地平线保持封闭，自由变量活在余数里。

This explains why mature, "defined" industries show similar symptoms. Car manufacturing, legacy aviation, commercial banking, traditional publishing are, looked at in retrospect, the institutional crystallization of a fixed reality: standards set hard, regulatory moats built, growth modelled as a contest over a closed pie. Once the industry's S-curve saturates, competition inside the frame tends to turn into infighting over fractional shares, administrative rent-seeking and brand litigation. [The Closed N and the Open Reality](../the-closed-n-and-the-open-reality/) sees the same place: choosing the best among N known options keeps the horizon closed, and the free variable lives in the remainder.

可没有哪个行业一直静止。把自己所在行业的 S 曲线压平，当成现实本身压平了，是一个常见的错置，于是有“增长已到物理极限”“进步的时代结束了”的哀叹。回头看去，经济从来不是一条单独的逻辑斯蒂曲线，而是一束相互重叠的 S 曲线：一条成熟的曲线还没耗尽，就有人走出既定的定义，实例化一个新的自由度，在邻近的、尚未被渲染的领域点燃一条新曲线的底部。

Yet no industry stays still. Taking the flattening of one's own industry's S-curve for the flattening of reality is a common misplacement, and it yields laments that "growth has hit its physical limits" or "the age of progress is over". Looked back on, the economy was never a single logistic curve but a bundle of overlapping S-curves: before a mature curve is exhausted, someone steps outside the established definition, instantiates a new degree of freedom, and lights the bottom of a new curve in an adjacent domain not yet rendered.

这一束重叠的 S 曲线，是两种取向持续交互留下的痕迹。封闭的取向把当前的 S 曲线当成世界的全部，在已知的天花板下优化，收割平台期，最后把富余花在保卫既有坐标的零和地位战上。成长的取向把每一条 S 曲线看作临时的脚手架，不等到顶端才更新地平线，把因果追回开阔的场地，从饱和的曲线跳到正在出现的曲线上，让现实的复利保持敞开。这是两种姿态，不是两类人；同一个心智，在不同的日子里两种都走过。

This bundle of overlapping S-curves is the trace left by two orientations interacting without pause. The closed orientation takes the current S-curve for the whole world, optimizes under the known ceiling, harvests the plateau, and in the end spends its slack on zero-sum status wars defending an established coordinate. The growth orientation sees every S-curve as temporary scaffolding, does not wait for the top to update its horizon, traces causality back to the open field, jumps from the saturated curve to the emerging one, and keeps reality's compounding open. These are two postures, not two kinds of people; the same Mind walks both on different days.

## 六、 复利的数学 / 6. The Arithmetic of Compounding

注入自由变量的能力在每一个心智里都在场，所以它是普遍而免费分布的。每天醒来，都有发起选择的可能。在任何一天里，注入自由变量还是被动反应，在人群中大致呈正态分布：大多数时刻落在习惯里，一些时刻稍稍向能动倾斜，少数时刻是大胆的发起。

The capacity to inject a free variable is present in every Mind, so it is distributed universally and for free. Every morning brings the possibility of originating a choice. On any given day, injecting a free variable or reacting passively is roughly normally distributed across a population: most moments fall into habit, some lean slightly toward agency, a few are bold originations.

可随着时间推移，实现出来的能力不再呈正态，而是分化成极端的幂律。[能动性、复利与关于 AI 封建主义的争论](../agency-compounding-and-the-argument-over-ai-feudalism/)走过这里：一次次迭代的选择不是线性相加，而是相乘，Sₜ = S₀ · ∏ₖ₌₁ᵗ (1 + εₖ)，其中 εₖ 是第 k 天注入的正和自由变量。持续行使自由变量，把反常当作信息，延展地平线，不去守护静止的自我形象，斜率 S 便一天天复利增长；倚靠起点的禀赋，也就是截距 I₀，同时玩封闭的零和游戏，曲线便保持平直。时间拉得足够长，斜率压倒截距。日常微小决定的正态分布，经过与现实的直接接触一轮轮复利，就生出能力、见识与负熵生成上的幂律分布。

Over time, however, realized capability stops being normal and diverges into an extreme power law. [Agency, Compounding, and the Argument Over AI Feudalism](../agency-compounding-and-the-argument-over-ai-feudalism/) walks through this: iterated choices do not add linearly but multiply, Sₜ = S₀ · ∏ₖ₌₁ᵗ (1 + εₖ), where εₖ is the positive-sum free variable injected on day k. Exercising the free variable steadily, treating anomalies as information, extending the horizon, not guarding a static self-image, compounds the slope S day after day; relying on the starting endowment, the intercept I₀, while playing closed zero-sum games keeps the curve flat. Given enough time, slope overwhelms intercept. A normal distribution of small daily decisions, compounded round after round through direct contact with reality, yields a power-law distribution of capability, insight and negative-entropy generation.

这幅图也要看清它的边界。分布是事后从第三人称读出来的沉淀；[个体抉择是唯一的因果杠杆](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/)与[自由的测不准](../zi-you-de-ce-bu-zhun/)都看到，统计只在选择发生之后登记它们，正态曲线是选择的沉积，不是它的作者。复利的结果里也不只有选择，还有落在身上的东西，运气留下了谁，不由谁挣来。把走在前面的人回头讲成“只是一直在注入自由变量的人”，就落进了[成功学](../cheng-gong-xue/)剖开的倒放。能从这幅图里带走的，不是对别人的排名，而是此刻这一个选择的分量：它会被乘进下一步。

The picture's boundary needs seeing too. A distribution is sediment read off afterwards from the third person; [Individual Choices as the Only Causal Levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) and [The Uncertainty of Freedom](../zi-you-de-ce-bu-zhun/) both see that statistics register choices only after they are made, and the normal curve is the sediment of choice, not its author. Nor do compounded outcomes hold only choices; they also hold what fell to someone, and whom luck spared was not earned. Telling those ahead, looking back, as "simply the ones who kept injecting free variables" falls into the playback that [Theories After Success, Mistaken for Theories Leading to Success](../cheng-gong-xue/) dissects. What the picture leaves is not a ranking of others but the weight of this one choice now: it gets multiplied into the next step.

## 七、 可移动的地平线 / 7. The Movable Horizon

寄存器是一个共享的、有限的、低维的公共协调网格，是作出感知的那个心智所感知到的样子。它从来不是一座客观的牢笼，也装不下任何一个心智的内部。[折叠的连续谱](../the-continuum-of-the-fold/)与[维度即投影](../dimensions-are-projections/)都走过这一处：观察的地平线是一条操作上的边界，不是一堵不可移动的墙，它是第一人称视野在当下的开口界限。

The register is a shared, limited, low-dimensional grid of public coordination, as perceived by the Mind doing the perceiving. It was never an objective prison, and it cannot hold the interior of any Mind. [The Continuum of the Fold](../the-continuum-of-the-fold/) and [Dimensions Are Projections](../dimensions-are-projections/) both walk this place: an observational horizon is an operational perimeter, not an immovable wall; it is the aperture limit of the first-person view at present.

不必推翻公共寄存器，也不必征服它的最高峰才得自由。不再把共识的绘图网格当成存在的全部，天花板就消散了。地平线从来不是固定的，向前迈一步，它就退一步；正和的能动性从所站的地方开始，就在选择注入下一个自由变量的那一刻。

Freedom needs neither the overthrow of the public register nor the conquest of its highest peak. Once the consensus drafting grid is no longer taken for the whole of existence, the ceiling dissolves. The horizon was never fixed; it recedes by a step with each step forward. Positive-sum agency begins where one stands, at the moment the next free variable is chosen.
