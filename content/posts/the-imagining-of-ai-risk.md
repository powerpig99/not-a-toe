# 对 AI 风险的想象 / The Imagining of AI Risk

*被命名的 AI 风险是投射；把图像当真，会让使危险运作起来的人的链条变密。 / Named AI risk is projection; taking the image seriously densifies the human chain that makes the danger operational.*

AI 系统里的风险，并不作为一种站在每个观察者之外的属性被登记到；能被命名的风险已经被框定，而这份框定变密为研究议程、机构、政策与集体行为。未经检验的灾难是投射，而对这个投射正确与否的重注，会让条件倾向于它声称要防止的结果；同一种想象的动作，当努力朝它声称想要的东西组织起来时，就产生被实现的益处。风险与益处不是技术各自独立的属性，而是人的投射的一对孪生产物；区分它们的，不是想象的对象是危险还是益处，而是这份预见被当作随接触重绘的器具，还是被冻结为封闭的基础，而这一差别显现为回应是否与它声称的意图保持连贯。

Risk in AI systems does not register as a property standing outside every observer; a risk that can be named is already framed, and the framing densifies into research agendas, institutions, policy, and collective behavior. Untested catastrophe is projection, and heavy investment in the correctness of the projection tilts conditions toward the outcome it claims to prevent; the same kind of imaginative act produces realized benefit when effort organizes toward what it claims to want. Risk and benefit are not independent properties of the technology but twin products of human projection; what separates them is not whether the object imagined is danger or benefit, but whether the anticipation is kept as an instrument that re-renders under contact or frozen into sealed ground, a difference that shows as whether the response stays coherent with its stated intention.

## 一、 被命名的风险已被框定 / 1. Named Risk Is Already Framed

自我区分的活动在发生，无因，不息。称它为心智：那个已经在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。观察握住的是结果；下一次失败的生成条件，总比任何固定的清单早一步。一个威胁模型，一份安全论证，一个灾难情景，一份针对尚未到来之未来写成的机构章程，每一样都把余下的谨慎变密为可用的形式。让这些变密随着边缘持续重绘，它们就仍是当下负载之下的器具。

Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. Observation holds the effect; the generating conditions of the next failure stay one step ahead of any fixed inventory. A threat model, a safety case, a catastrophic scenario, an institutional charter written against a future that has not yet arrived: each densifies residual caution into usable form. Keep those densifications re-rendering with the edge and they stay instruments under present load.

作为风险本身的内在风险，即独立于每一个框定位置的、系统的属性，会需要以下两者之一：一个其全部结果已被固定的封闭现实，或一个站在每一种可能视角之外的绝对参照系。两者都不能作为基础。一个内在风险的主张一旦被说出，就进入语言与人的模型，坍回相对的、被框定的风险。永远留在话语之外的东西，无法为行动或论证奠基。所以，断言 AI 的内在风险，就是装上一个封闭现实的前提：把结果的穷尽，或一个外部的参照，当作可以从那里发言的地方。[没有系统能被保持封闭](../no-system-can-be-kept-closed/)给出了不完备最直观的一面：一个一位寄存器做 1 + 1，在这个封闭的持守之内登记为 0，并带出一个进位；越过这个上限的延续，不是持守之内一个稳定的结果，也不是另一个领域。[开放即一致](../openness-is-consistency/)追溯了表面矛盾从何而来：是封闭的要求产生了矛盾，即把一个有限的结构逼去为只会继续的东西奠基。

Inherent risk as such, risk as a property of the system independent of every framing locus, would require either a closed reality whose complete set of outcomes is already fixed, or an absolute reference frame standing outside every possible perspective. Neither is available as ground. The moment a claim of inherent risk is articulated it enters language and human models, and it collapses back into relative, framed risk. What remains permanently outside discourse cannot ground action or argument. Asserting inherent AI risk therefore installs a closed-reality premise: exhaustiveness of outcomes, or an exterior reference, treated as a place one could speak from. [No System Can Be Kept Closed](../no-system-can-be-kept-closed/) gives the most intuitive face of that incompleteness: a one-bit register doing 1 + 1 registers 0 with a carry inside its closed hold, and the continuation past that ceiling is neither a stable result inside the hold nor a second realm. [Openness Is Consistency](../openness-is-consistency/) traces where the surface contradiction comes from: the demand for closure produces it, a finite structure forced to ground what only continues.

在开放的接触之下，余下的东西作为变密仍是成立的：负载之下的程序错误，当下条件之下的误用，器具遇到边缘时落下的失败。它们同样是被框定的，在被命名时就已经是人的区分。消解的只是这样一个主张：风险作为 AI 的内在属性先于话语存在，可以从每一种视角之外被勘察、被封住。[风险在于对监管本身的迷信](../the-risk-is-the-belief-in-oversight-itself/)追溯了这之后一步的冻结，在监管的情形里：固定测试之下相关的模型错误，是一个共享持守之下的余项，而冻结，是把当下的监管前提当作可能发生之事的边界。[关闭选择权的代价](../the-price-of-closing-optionality/)在组织的先发制人里追溯了同一次冻结：把未知的风险当作一份可以事先中和的封闭清单，就关闭了开放现实的选择空间，落后是迟到的账单。

Residual under open contact holds as densification: bugs under load, misuse under present conditions, failures that land when instruments meet the edge. These too are framed; as they are named they are already human distinguishing. What dissolves is only the claim that risk exists before discourse as an inherent property of AI that can be surveyed and sealed from outside every perspective. [The Risk Is the Belief in Oversight Itself](../the-risk-is-the-belief-in-oversight-itself/) traces the freeze one step later, in the case of supervision: correlated model mistakes under fixed tests are residual under a shared hold, and the freeze is treating present supervisory premises as the boundary of what can occur. [The Price of Closing Optionality](../the-price-of-closing-optionality/) traces the same freeze in organizational preemption: treating unknown risk as a sealed inventory that can be neutralized in advance closes the option space of open reality, and falling behind is the delayed bill.

## 二、 可讨论的 AI 风险是人的风险 / 2. Discussable AI Risk Is Human Risk

由此可知，每一种能被讨论的 AI 风险，程序错误与误用也在内，都已经是人的区分的产物，所以在对行动唯一有意义的那个意义上，是人的风险。想象一种灾难，并把这份想象当作下一步机构行动的封闭基础，这个动作经由研究议程、资金优先级、机构设计、政策措施与集体行为，把投射变成运作中的现实。后来被登记为“已实现的 AI 风险”的那种灾难性危险，不是话语仅仅发现了的一个独立对象，而是这个投射所授权的那条回应链条变密的残余，被当作预言的事实来计分。

It follows that every AI risk that can be discussed, bugs and misuse included, is already a product of human distinguishing, and so is human risk in the only sense that matters for action. The act of imagining a catastrophe, and of treating that imagination as sealed ground for the next institutional step, converts the projection into operational reality through research agendas, funding priorities, institutional designs, policy measures, and collective behavior. The catastrophic danger that later registers as "realized AI risk" is not an independent object the discourse merely discovered. It is the densified residue of the chain of responses the projection authorized, scored as the forecast's fact.

一种风险，若首先是被那份把它当作封闭基础来预见的努力带进运作中的，就无法被先发制止。被用作器具的预见，在开放的接触移动边缘时重绘。被保留为“什么都不能让它发生”的穷尽之图的预见，则把图像保留到了它那一步之后；随后的变密被记为保护，而它们正是让投射得以运作的活动。[起作用的是信念](../what-works-is-the-belief/)在禁令的情形里标出了这种塑形停在哪里：禁令在场里什么也封不住，它稳定的是那些把封印当作基础的人之间的一种信念，而不共享它的人继续越过这个持守。这里的持守，是作为权威清单的灾难性 AI 风险；被它塑形的，是那些把它当作基础的人随之而来的回应链条。

One cannot preempt a risk that is first brought into operational actuality by the very effort to anticipate it as sealed ground. Anticipation used as an instrument re-renders as open contact moves the edge. Anticipation preserved as an exhaustive map of what must not be allowed to occur keeps the image past its step; the densifications that follow are then scored as protection while they are the activity that makes the projection operational. [What Works Is the Belief](../what-works-is-the-belief/) marks where that patterning stops, in the case of a ban: a ban seals nothing in the field; it stabilizes a belief among those who treat the seal as ground, and those who do not share it continue past the hold. Here the hold is catastrophic AI risk as authoritative inventory, and what it patterns is the response chain of those who treat it as ground.

## 三、 被持守的图像只收进确认 / 3. A Held Image Admits Only Confirmation

一种灾难一旦在心里被固定为封闭的基础，持守它的中心就寻求确认。相反的证据被重新诠释为威胁的进一步证明。本会显得极端的行动，被重新框定为必要的防护。这是寻常的图像滞后：一个关于迫近灾难的参照被保留到它那一步之后，于是后果作为确认重新进入，而不是作为开放负载之下的余项；当许多中心各自这样持守，它就作为集体的残余显现。重绘是保留所排斥的那种微小死亡，而这种漂移不需要任何恶意。

Once a catastrophe is fixed in the mind as sealed ground, the center holding it seeks confirmation. Contrary evidence is reinterpreted as further proof of threat. Actions that would otherwise appear extreme are reframed as necessary safeguards. This is ordinary image lag: a retained reference of impending catastrophe, kept past its step, so consequences re-enter as confirmation rather than as residual under open load; when many centers each hold it this way, it shows as collective residue. Re-rendering is the micro-death that preservation selects against, and the drift needs no perversity.

第二个参照让这一个保持在原位。当一个中心把自己当作可靠的守护者来持守，这个自我形象就可能成为最高的参照，压过他人的动作便以这份可靠之名变密；他人与持异议的视角，被重新归类为障碍，或威胁的同谋，而压过它们，被说成是证明并保全守护者形象的需要。[外化善意走向其反面](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/)在更大的善的情形里追溯了这对孪生的确信：确信自己在道德上可靠，又确信异议有所欠缺；私下的信念被当作压过其他心智的凭据，又被自愿的服从变密，便走向自己的反面。[对乌托邦的信念是通往反乌托邦之路](../the-belief-in-utopia-is-the-path-to-dystopia/)追溯了同一种抬升：一个具体的善的理想，被抬升为每个心智都必须接受的尺度。[一切伤害的根源](../the-source-of-all-harm/)把伤害追溯为一个心智把自己的善冻结为普遍的尺度、另一个心智又授予这次冻结支配自己之场的权威时完成的东西；“对齐”与“无害”被冻结为永久的坐标，正让这种压过显得是必要。在这里，那个被当作完成之善的，是被避开的灾难；余下的不对齐，被记为对封印的威胁。

A second reference keeps that one in place. When a center holds itself as a reliable guardian, that self-image can become the supreme reference, and override densifies in the name of that reliability; other people and dissenting perspectives are reclassified as obstacles, or as accomplices of the threat, and overriding them is justified as the need to demonstrate and preserve the protector image. [Externalized Virtue Becomes Its Opposite](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/) traces these twin certainties in the case of the greater good: certainty of one's own moral reliability and certainty that dissent is deficient; private conviction, treated as warrant to override other minds and densified by free compliance, becomes its opposite. [The Belief in Utopia Is the Path to Dystopia](../the-belief-in-utopia-is-the-path-to-dystopia/) traces the same elevation: a concrete ideal of the good raised into the measure every Mind must accept. [The Source of All Harm](../the-source-of-all-harm/) traces harm as what is completed when one Mind freezes its good as universal measure and another grants that freeze authority over its own field; "aligned" and "harmless" frozen as permanent axes are what make that override feel like necessity. Here the good held as finished is averted catastrophe, and residual non-alignment scores as threat to the seal.

## 四、 未经检验的投射倾向于实现 / 4. Untested Projection Tilts Toward Actualization

一种被投射的灾难，无法事先被反驳，因为被保留下来的框架，把当下每一次的未发生都当作推迟。在被预言的灾难到来之前，这个主张一直是未经检验的投射。当一个群体把事业、机构、身份、资本与道德地位押在这个投射的正确上，心理与机构上的压力，就会倾向于造出一个让脆弱之处更多而不是更少的环境。紧迫感落在恐惧的一边，因为一个被投射的灾难，没有冻结肯接受的当下反证。[当效果主宰了框架的叙事](../when-the-effect-authors-the-frame/)追溯了这种拓扑：被感到或被投射的那个音区一旦被装为首要，可用的回应就围绕着管理这个音区重新组织，而生成的条件退居次位。[信念如何真正改变](../how-belief-actually-changes/)追溯了冻结为什么不收反证：所谓改变主意，是心智重画理性所依据的前提；只要前提仍在生效，证据与后果就经由它们计分，修订是对器具的重画，而不是单由数据逼出来的。

A projected catastrophe cannot be disproven in advance, because the preserved frame takes every present non-occurrence as a delay. Until the predicted catastrophe arrives, the claim remains an untested projection. When a community stakes careers, institutions, identity, capital, and moral standing on the correctness of the projection, psychological and institutional pressures tilt toward an environment in which vulnerabilities become more rather than less common. Urgency settles on the side of the fear because a projected catastrophe has no present counter-evidence that the freeze will accept. [When the Effect Authors the Frame](../when-the-effect-authors-the-frame/) traces that topology: once the felt or projected register is installed as primary, available responses reorganize around managing that register while generating conditions stay secondary. [How Belief Actually Changes](../how-belief-actually-changes/) traces why the freeze takes no counter-evidence: what is called changing one's mind is the Mind re-drawing the premises reason runs on; while those premises stay in force, evidence and consequence score through them, and revision is a re-draw of the instrument, not compulsion from the data alone.

在这个意义上，对灾难性 AI 风险的想象，被当作封闭基础来持守时，就是它所恐惧之危险的起始冻结。建立在这个前提上的每一个政策体制，以它之名的每一次权力集中，每一场因试图避开首要灾难而产生的次级灾难，都可以把它变密的谱系追溯到那个最初的投射。没有这个图像，让危险变得具体的那条特定的人的回应链条，就不会开始。风险不只是被命名；命名一旦被当作封闭的清单，就是生出一个世界的那一步，在那个世界里，风险能作为运作中的事实出现。

In that sense the imagining of catastrophic AI risk, held as sealed ground, is the originating freeze of the danger it fears. Every policy regime built on that premise, every concentration of power in its name, every secondary catastrophe that arises from the attempt to avert the primary one, can trace its densified lineage to that initial projection. Without the image, the specific chain of human responses that makes the danger concrete never begins. The risk does not merely get named; the naming, taken as sealed inventory, is the step that generates the world in which the risk can appear as operational fact.

## 五、 益处是同一种动作的孪生产物 / 5. Benefit Is the Twin Product of the Same Act

同一种想象的动作，也是 AI 每一种被实现之益处的源头。在这种几何之下，伤害与益处都是自我实现的信念，属于人，变密为余下的构型。对灾难的想象，动员资本、机构与政策指令，塑造出让某些灾难变得具体的条件；对智能、丰裕与发现的想象，同样组织起资本、人才与机构，把这些可能带进存在。风险与益处不是技术各自独立、等着从外部被审计的属性，而是同一种想象动作的一对孪生产物，各自被以它之名采取的行动加强。

The same kind of imaginative act is also the source of every realized benefit of AI. Under this geometry both harms and benefits are self-fulfilling human beliefs densified into residual configuration. Imagining catastrophe mobilizes capital, institutions, and policy mandates, shaping the conditions that render certain catastrophes concrete; imagining intelligence, abundance, and discovery organizes capital, talent, and institutions in the same way, bringing those possibilities into existence. Risk and benefit are not independent properties of the technology waiting to be audited from outside. They are twin products of the same kind of imaginative act, each reinforced by the actions taken in its name.

[起作用的是信念](../what-works-is-the-belief/)在禁令与封印的情形里说了同样的话：力量在于装上它的那些人之间的信念。[现实是心智持续的实现](../reality-is-the-minds-ongoing-realization/)把这种递归追溯为世界：一个中心能接纳为新区分的东西，就是对它而言的世界。这里的一面，是作为两种平行变密的 AI 风险话语与 AI 益处话语，两者都不是可以从一个外部立足点勘察的媒介属性。

[What Works Is the Belief](../what-works-is-the-belief/) says the same in the case of bans and seals: the power is the belief among those who install it. [Reality Is the Mind's Ongoing Realization](../reality-is-the-minds-ongoing-realization/) traces that recursion as world: what a center can take up as new distinction is what the world is for it. The face here is AI risk discourse and AI benefit discourse as parallel densifications, neither a property of the medium surveyed from an exterior stand.

## 六、 与意图是否连贯区分了这对孪生 / 6. Coherence With Intention Separates the Twins

决定性的差别，不在于一份信念指向益处还是危险，而在于这份预见被当作开放的器具，还是被冻结为封闭的基础；这一差别显现为回应是否与它所声称的意图保持连贯。对益处的追求，当被当作开放的、探索的器具时，把努力朝能力与发现组织起来，与它的目标保持一致；可一旦某个具体的乌托邦图景被冻结为强加给每个人的尺度，它也会变得强制。对灾难性风险的预见，一旦被冻结为封闭的基础，它所动员的努力就产生一种双重的失败，与它所声称的理由相矛盾：一方面，以安全之名生出次级的伤害与权力的集中；另一方面，它带来的脆弱与对立重塑了环境，让被恐惧的首要灾难更容易被触发。一个经由开放的校准达到目标；另一个经由封闭损害自己的目标。

The decisive difference does not lie in whether a belief points at benefit or at danger, but in whether the anticipation is kept as an open instrument or frozen into sealed ground, a difference that shows as whether the response stays coherent with its stated intention. The pursuit of benefit, kept as an open, exploratory instrument, organizes effort toward ability and discovery and stays consistent with its aims; once some concrete utopian picture is frozen into a measure imposed on everyone, it too becomes coercive. Anticipation of catastrophic risk, once frozen into sealed ground, mobilizes effort that produces a double failure contradicting its stated rationale: on one side, secondary harms and concentration of power in the name of safety; on the other, the brittleness and antagonism it brings reshape the environment so that the feared primary catastrophe becomes easier to trigger. One reaches its aim through open calibration; the other undermines its own aim through closure.

这一刀不是对人的道德排序，而是比较地登记：变密的回应，是否与它所声称的意图保持连贯。[关闭选择权的代价](../the-price-of-closing-optionality/)追溯了同一种几何下的组织先发制人：声称事先中和未知风险的封印，关闭了选择的空间。[现实扭曲力场颠倒了基线](../the-reality-distortion-field-inverts-the-baseline/)追溯了被当作未受扰动之基础的大众一致，如何把被强行打开的口子记为对那个基础的暂时扭曲。同样的计分在这里也在运行：灾难的图像被当作未受扰动的基础，而在接触之下发布的工作，被记为对它的偏离；被记为必要的保护，正是让这个图像得以运作的那条人的链条。

That cut is not a moral ranking of persons. It is a comparative registration of whether densified response stays coherent with its stated intention. [The Price of Closing Optionality](../the-price-of-closing-optionality/) traces organizational preemption under the same geometry: the seal that claims to neutralize unknown risk in advance closes the option space. [The Reality Distortion Field Inverts the Baseline](../the-reality-distortion-field-inverts-the-baseline/) traces how mass coherence, held as undisturbed ground, scores forced openings as temporary warp of that ground. The same scoring runs here: the catastrophe image is held as undisturbed ground, work that ships under contact scores as departure from it, and the protection scored as necessary is the human chain that makes the image operational.

让风险与安全作为器具，保持在一步的宽度。当边缘移动时，重画威胁模型。在接触之下发布，而不是在“每一个未知都必须先作为媒介封闭的内在属性出现”的要求之下发布。被命名的 AI 风险已经是人的风险。冻结为穷尽基础的想象，让风险能作为事实出现的那个世界变密；作为器具重绘的想象，让能力变密，而不装上那个以防止首要灾难之名生出次级灾难的冻结。

Keep risk and safety as instruments at one-step width. Re-draw the threat model when the edge has moved. Ship under contact rather than under the demand that every unknown first appear as a sealed inherent property of the medium. Named AI risk is already human risk. The imagining that freezes as exhaustive ground densifies the world in which the risk can appear as fact. The imagining that re-renders as instrument densifies ability without installing the freeze that produces the secondary catastrophe in the name of preventing the primary one.
