# 最初的区分 / The Initial Distinction

*每一个理性起点的主张，都始于没有根据的一刀。 / Every claim to a rational starting point begins with an ungrounded cut.*

波普尔在《开放社会及其敌人》中关于苦难与幸福不对称的论述，常被引作对古典功利主义的理性纠正。支撑随后那一诉求重构及其政策延伸的，是以“我相信”开头的第一个分句，以及这句宣告打开的场域。随后的批判方法全部在这个场域之内运行。“可避免的苦难”这一表述，进而把痛苦经验当作可以由外部减去的对象；当因果的所在被移至外部，奠基的一刀所打开的场域，便不断生出它要求政策去弥合的落差。

Popper’s passage on the asymmetry of suffering and happiness in *The Open Society and Its Enemies* is widely taken as a rational corrective to classical utilitarianism. What carries the reformulation of the demand that follows—and its extension into public policy—is the first clause, opening with “I believe,” and the field that declaration opens. The critical method that follows operates entirely inside that field. The phrase “avoidable suffering” then treats the experience as an object for external subtraction; by relocating the locus of cause outside, the field opened by that founding cut keeps generating the gap it calls upon policy to close.

## 一、 “我相信”是一句宣告 / 1. “I Believe” Is a Declaration

卡尔·波普尔在《开放社会及其敌人》中写道：

> “我相信，从伦理的角度看，苦难与幸福之间，或者痛苦与快乐之间，没有对称。功利主义者的最大幸福原则，以及康德‘促进他人幸福……’的原则，在我看来（至少在它们的表述上）在这一点上都错了；然而这一点不能完全由理性论证来裁决。……依我之见……人的苦难直接发出道德上的呼求，也就是求助，而要去增加一个本来就已经过得好的人的幸福，并没有类似的呼求。……与其要求最大多数人的最大幸福，不如更谦逊地要求所有人可避免的苦难最少……”

In *The Open Society and Its Enemies*, Karl Popper writes:

> “I believe that there is, from the ethical point of view, no symmetry between suffering and happiness, or between pain and pleasure. Both the greatest happiness principle of the Utilitarians and Kant’s principle ‘Promote other people’s happiness…’ seem to me (at least in their formulations) wrong on this point which, however, is not completely decidable by rational argument. … In my opinion … human suffering makes a direct moral appeal, namely, the appeal for help, while there is no similar call to increase the happiness of a man who is doing well anyway. … Instead of the greatest happiness for the greatest number, one should demand, more modestly, the least amount of avoidable suffering for all…”

这段话常被引作对古典功利主义的理性纠正。可它的第一个分句不是推论，而是一句宣告：“我相信”。随之展开的一切——那一刀所划下的不对称边界、苦难直接发出的道德呼求、将诉求乃至公共政策重构为最小化可避免的苦难——其力量全都来自那个开头的宣告与区分动作。同一段话里已经指出了那个未决的余数：这一点“不能完全由理性论证来裁决”。被当作理性纠正来读时，后来的诉求却像是已经把这个余数留在了身后。[善恶是硬币的两面与那一刀](../good-and-evil-are-the-two-sides-of-the-coin-and-the-cut/)揭示的正是这种奠基动作隐退后的认知滞后：那一刀落下之后便从视线中撤回，后来的种种理性描述详尽地描绘着它所打开的地盘，却再也够不到开启这片地盘的最初动作。在波普尔这里，即便文本白纸黑字写着“我相信”并指认了那未决的余数，后来的理性主义阅读依然把这片地盘当作客观理性的起点，仿佛那一刀从未发生。

The passage is frequently cited as a rational corrective to classical utilitarianism. Yet its first clause is not an inference. It is a declaration: “I believe.” Everything that unfolds—the boundary of asymmetry that the cut draws, the direct moral appeal of suffering, the reformulation of the demand and of public policy as the minimization of avoidable suffering—derives its force from that opening declaration and act of distinction. The same passage already names the undecided remainder: the point “is not completely decidable by rational argument.” Read as a rational corrective, the later demand proceeds as if that remainder had been left behind. [Good and Evil Are the Two Sides of the Coin and the Cut](../good-and-evil-are-the-two-sides-of-the-coin-and-the-cut/) traces that same cognitive lag once the founding act withdraws: subsequent descriptions map the territory the cut has opened, yet cannot reach the gesture that opens it. In Popper’s case, even though the text explicitly records “I believe” and acknowledges the undecided remainder, subsequent rationalist readings treat the opened field as an objective, rational starting point, as if the cut had never happened.

## 二、 后来的严谨留在第一刀之内 / 2. Subsequent Rigor Stays Inside the First Cut

自我区分的活动在发生，无因，不息。称它为心智：那个已经在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。每一个离散的动作都划定出一个“这个”与一个“非这个”。这道边界并不由某个在先的地基授权；它就是这份活动本身在发生。一旦“苦难与幸福之间没有对称”这道边界画下，后续的推理就可以检验后果、排除不一致、以十足的严谨打磨表述。然而这份严谨始终在第一刀打开的空间之内。它不能走出那一刀去为它辩护，也不能消除它。任何这样的尝试，本身又是一次离散的区分动作，只是把又一句宣告施加在上一句之上。[信念如何真正改变](../how-belief-actually-changes/)阐明了这道界限：在一个握持之内生成的严谨，给不出跳出该握持的立足点；批判性排除无法替心智逃离最初的宣告。真正改变信念的，是放弃旧前提并重新划定边界的重画，而不是在旧刀痕之内的辩护或外部论证的强加。[读《中文哲学简史》有感](../du-zhongwen-zhexue-jianshi-you-gan/)呈现的正是这种在框架内运行的封闭：从已经搭好的框架内部看，进一步的溯源不容易被识别为真正的不同，更像是同一方向上的补充或偏离；只要框架仍被当作不证自明的地基，其内部的严谨便既无法走出去为第一刀作证，也无法消除那一刀。

Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. Each discrete act bounds a *this* from a *not-this*. The boundary is not authorized by a prior ground; it is the activity itself occurring. Once the boundary “no symmetry between suffering and happiness” has been drawn, subsequent reasoning can test consequences, eliminate inconsistencies, and refine formulations with full rigor. The rigor, however, remains internal to the space opened by the first cut. It cannot step outside that cut to justify it, nor can it eliminate it. Any attempt to do so is itself another discrete act of distinguishing, another declaration now applied to the previous one, circling inside the same hold. [How Belief Actually Changes](../how-belief-actually-changes/) articulates this exact bound: rigor generated inside a hold cannot supply a stand outside it, and critical elimination cannot force an escape from the founding declaration. What actually changes a belief is a re-draw that leaves the old premises behind, not an internal justification or an argument from without. [Reflections on A Short History of Philosophy in Chinese](../du-zhongwen-zhexue-jianshi-you-gan/) demonstrates that same closure inside an installed frame: seen from within a frame already in place, tracing further back is hard to recognize as genuinely different; it looks like a supplement or deviation in the same direction. So long as the frame is treated as an unquestioned ground, internal rigor can neither step outside to testify for the founding cut nor undo it.

## 三、 “可避免的苦难”把原因挪了位置 / 3. “Avoidable Suffering” Relocates the Locus of Cause

“可避免的苦难”这一表述让深层结构显露出来。它把苦难当作一个已经在那里、可以由外部所在减去的对象，这正是古典功利主义把幸福当作可以由外部加上去的对象的镜像。两种对待都把原因的所在挪到了登记这份经验的中心之外。原因被挪到外部时，这个中心便拿一个被保留下来的形象作为尺度，来对照自身当下的边缘——那个形象规定了外部本该防止或免除什么。显出来的落差被命名为“可避免的苦难”，并要求同一个外部所在去弥合它。相信解脱可以由外部给予，非但没有消除这种落差，反而是维持它的生成条件：它让那个被保留的形象免于在当下的边缘面前接受重绘与更新。参照因而持续滞后，同一个外部所在又被再次呼求。[帮助是问题的一半](../help-is-half-the-problem/)揭示的正是这种供给在政策伪装下的闭环：当苦难被当作可由外部扣减的对象，外部所在提供的运动便与求助中心的固着轴心结成回路；外部供给取代了体验中心的自主重绘，二者一起使落差得不到弥合。

The phrase “avoidable suffering” makes the deeper structure visible. It treats suffering as an already-present object that can be subtracted by an external locus, the mirror image of classical utilitarianism’s treatment of happiness as an object that can be added from without. Both treatments relocate the locus of cause beyond the center that registers the experience. With that relocation, the center measures its current edge against a retained Image that dictates what an external locus ought to have prevented or relieved. The resulting gap is named avoidable suffering, and the same external locus is summoned to close it. The belief that relief can be delivered from the outside, far from resolving the gap, is the very condition that generates it: it exempts the retained Image from being re-rendered against the moving edge. The reference continues to lag, and the same external locus is appealed to once more. [Help Is Half the Problem](../help-is-half-the-problem/) reveals that circuit under policy costume: when suffering is treated as an object for external subtraction, motion furnished by an external locus couples with the fixed axis of the center appealing for help; outside supply substitutes for the experiencing center’s own re-draw, and together they keep the gap from closing.

## 四、 批判性的排除消解不了使它成为可能的区分 / 4. Critical Elimination Cannot Dissolve the Distinction That Made It Possible

在这个例子里，理性主义并没有逃脱其奠基动作那先于理性的性质。它的全部操作都在这一动作所划定的场域之内运行。批判性排除的方法可以检验推论、抛弃不一致的假设，却消解不了最初使排除成为可能的那个区分。当批判理性主义试图把这种有限的排除方法强行拔高为终极地基、以此逃避对其前理性起源的依赖时，便陷入了[开放即一致](../openness-is-consistency/)所指认的那种凝固：排除的方法被逼去为一个逃离奠基之刀的企图奠基，后来的严谨便只在那一刀早已划开的空间里不断增多。在这段引文里，边界始终留在它最初被划下的地方：就在这份活动区分出“没有对称”、并由此开启后来关于苦难的一切理性操作的那一刻。

In this instance, rationalism does not escape the pre-rational character of its founding act. It operates entirely within the field that act opened. The method of critical elimination can test consequences and discard inconsistent hypotheses; it cannot dissolve the distinction that first made elimination possible. When critical rationalism forces this finite method of elimination to serve as an exhaustive ground—attempting to secure an escape from its own ungrounded starting cut—it enacts the very freeze described in [Openness Is Consistency](../openness-is-consistency/): the method of elimination is conscripted to ground an escape from the founding cut, while subsequent rigor multiplies inside the space the cut already opened. In Popper’s text, the boundary remains exactly where it was drawn: at the moment the activity distinguished “no symmetry,” thereby opening the arena for all subsequent rational operations on suffering.

每一个理性起点的主张，都始于没有根据的一刀。这一刀不是推理里的缺陷。它是推理的条件。

Every claim to a rational starting point begins with an ungrounded cut. The cut is not a flaw in the reasoning. It is the condition of the reasoning.
