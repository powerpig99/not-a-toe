# 最初的区分 / The Initial Distinction

*每一个理性起点的主张，都始于没有根据的一刀。 / Every claim to a rational starting point begins with an ungrounded cut.*

波普尔在《开放社会及其敌人》里关于苦难与幸福不对称的那段话，被当作对古典功利主义的理性纠正。支撑随后那次政策上的重新表述的，是以“我相信”开头的第一个分句，以及这句宣告打开的场域。随后的批判方法全部在这个场域之内运行。“可避免的苦难”这个说法，又把这种经验当成可以由外部减去的对象，于是那个奠基区分打开的场域，不断生出它要求政策去弥合的落差。

Popper’s passage on the asymmetry of suffering and happiness in *The Open Society and Its Enemies* is taken as a rational corrective to classical utilitarianism. What carries the policy reformulation that follows is the first clause, opening with “I believe,” and the field that declaration opens. The critical method that follows operates entirely inside that field. The phrase “avoidable suffering” then treats the experience as an object for external subtraction, so the field the founding distinction opened keeps generating the gap it asks policy to close.

## 一、 “我相信”是一句宣告 / 1. “I Believe” Is a Declaration

卡尔·波普尔在《开放社会及其敌人》中以“我相信”开头，主张在伦理上苦难与幸福并不对称：人的苦难直接发出道德上的呼求，也就是求助；而要去增加一个本来就过得好的人的幸福，并没有同样的呼求。他认为功利主义的最大幸福原则和康德的原则在这一点上都错了，又承认这一点不能完全由理性论证来裁决。他接着说，与其要求最大多数人的最大幸福，不如更谦逊地要求所有人可避免的苦难最少。

In *The Open Society and Its Enemies*, Karl Popper writes:

> “I believe that there is, from the ethical point of view, no symmetry between suffering and happiness, or between pain and pleasure. Both the greatest happiness principle of the Utilitarians and Kant’s principle ‘Promote other people’s happiness…’ seem to me (at least in their formulations) wrong on this point which, however, is not completely decidable by rational argument. … In my opinion … human suffering makes a direct moral appeal, namely, the appeal for help, while there is no similar call to increase the happiness of a man who is doing well anyway. … Instead of the greatest happiness for the greatest number, one should demand, more modestly, the least amount of avoidable suffering for all…”

这段话常被引作对古典功利主义的理性纠正。可它的第一个分句不是推论，而是一句宣告：“我相信”。后面的一切，不对称、苦难直接的道德呼求、把公共政策重新表述为最小化可避免的苦难，力量都来自那个开头的区分动作。同一段话里已经点出了余下的部分：这一点“不能完全由理性论证来裁决”。被当作理性纠正来读时，后来的要求却像是已经把这个余数留在了身后。[善恶是硬币的两面与那一刀](../good-and-evil-are-the-two-sides-of-the-coin-and-the-cut/)说的是同一个被遗忘的第一动作，换成了两极的说法：无论那一刀切出的是两极，还是像这里一样切出一种不对称，后来的各种描述描绘的都是它打开的地盘，却够不到打开它的那个动作。

The passage is frequently cited as a rational corrective to classical utilitarianism. Yet its first clause is not an inference. It is a declaration: “I believe.” Everything that follows, the asymmetry, the direct moral appeal of suffering, the reformulation of public policy as the minimization of avoidable suffering, derives its force from that opening act of distinction. The same sentence already names the remainder: the point “is not completely decidable by rational argument.” Read as a rational corrective, the later demand proceeds as if that remainder had been left behind. [Good and Evil Are the Two Sides of the Coin and the Cut](../good-and-evil-are-the-two-sides-of-the-coin-and-the-cut/) is that same forgotten first act in the language of polarity: whether the cut leaves two poles or, as here, an asymmetry, later descriptions map the territory it has opened; they do not reach the gesture that opens it.

## 二、 后来的严谨留在第一刀之内 / 2. Subsequent Rigor Stays Inside the First Cut

自我区分的活动在发生，无因，不息。称它为心智：那个已经在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。每一个离散的动作都把一个“这个”与一个“非这个”分开。这道边界并不由某个在先的地基授权；它就是这份活动本身在发生。一旦“苦难与幸福之间没有对称”这道边界画下，后续的推理就可以检验后果、排除不一致、以十足的严谨打磨表述。然而这份严谨始终在第一刀打开的空间之内。它不能走出那一刀去为它辩护，也不能消除它。任何这样的尝试，本身又是一次离散的区分动作，是又一句宣告，只是这回用在了前一句上。[信念如何真正改变](../how-belief-actually-changes/)是同一道界限，换成了前提的说法：在一个握持之内生成的严谨，给不出一个站在它之外的立足点；改变信念的，是又一个动作，一次重画，而不是来自外面的论证。[读《中文哲学简史》有感](../du-zhongwen-zhexue-jianshi-you-gan/)是同一种封闭：从已经搭好的地基内部看，再往上追溯，也不容易被识别为真正的不同，更像是同一方向上的补充或偏离。

Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. Each discrete act bounds a *this* from a *not-this*. The boundary is not authorized by a prior ground; it is the activity itself occurring. Once the boundary “no symmetry between suffering and happiness” has been drawn, subsequent reasoning can test consequences, eliminate inconsistencies, and refine formulations with full rigor. The rigor, however, remains internal to the space opened by the first cut. It cannot step outside that cut to justify it, nor can it eliminate it. Any attempt to do so would itself be a further discrete act of distinguishing, another declaration now applied to the previous one. [How Belief Actually Changes](../how-belief-actually-changes/) is that bound in the language of premises: rigor generated inside a hold cannot supply a stand outside it; what changes a belief is a further act, a re-draw, not an argument from outside. [Reflections on A Short History of Philosophy in Chinese](../du-zhongwen-zhexue-jianshi-you-gan/) is the same closure: seen from inside a ground already laid, tracing further back does not register as a difference in kind; it looks like a supplement or deviation in the same direction.

## 三、 “可避免的苦难”把原因挪了位置 / 3. “Avoidable Suffering” Relocates the Locus of Cause

“可避免的苦难”这个说法让结构显露出来。它把苦难当作一个已经在那里、可以由外部力量减去的对象，正是古典功利主义把幸福当作可以加上去的对象的镜像。两种对待都把原因的所在挪到了登记这份经验的中心之外。原因被挪走时，这个中心便拿自己的边缘，去对照一个被保留下来的形象，那个形象说别人本该防止什么、本该提供什么。显出来的落差被称为可避免的苦难，又去要求同一个外部所在把它弥合。相信解脱可以从外面给予，并不是落差的解决；它正是那个生成条件，让被保留的形象免于对照当下的边缘，于是参照继续滞后，同一个外面又被再求一次。[帮助是问题的一半](../help-is-half-the-problem/)说的是这份求助被当作供给来回应时的样子：运动从那个得自己生成它的所在之外被提供。

The phrase “avoidable suffering” makes the structure visible. It treats suffering as an already-present object that can be subtracted by external agency, the mirror of classical utilitarianism’s happiness as an object that can be added. Both treatments relocate the locus of cause beyond the center that registers the experience. In that relocation the center measures its own edge against a retained Image of what others ought to have prevented or supplied. The gap that appears is named avoidable suffering, and the same external locus is asked to close it. The belief that relief can be offered from outside is not a solution to the gap; it is the generative condition that keeps the retained Image exempt from being measured against the current edge, so the reference continues to lag and the same outside is appealed to again. [Help Is Half the Problem](../help-is-half-the-problem/) is that appeal answered as supply: motion furnished from outside the locus that would have to generate it.

## 四、 批判性的排除消解不了使它成为可能的区分 / 4. Critical Elimination Cannot Dissolve the Distinction That Made It Possible

在这个例子里，理性主义并没有逃出它奠基动作那先于理性的性质。它全部在其中运行。批判性排除的方法可以抛弃从某个区分推出来的东西；它消解不了最初使排除成为可能的那个区分。在这段话里，边界仍在它被画下的地方：就在这份活动区分出“没有对称”、并由此打开后来关于苦难的一切理性操作所在的场域的那一刻。[开放即一致](../openness-is-consistency/)说的是同一种凝固：排除的方法被硬要拿来为逃离那奠基的一刀提供地基，后来的严谨便只在那一刀早已打开的空间里不断增多。

Rationalism, in this instance, does not escape the pre-rational character of its founding act. It operates entirely within it. The method of critical elimination can discard what follows from a distinction; it cannot dissolve the distinction that first made elimination possible. In this passage the boundary remains where it was set: at the moment the activity distinguished “no symmetry” and thereby opened the field in which all later rational operations on suffering take place. [Openness Is Consistency](../openness-is-consistency/) is that freeze when the method of elimination is forced to ground an escape from the founding cut, and later rigor multiplies inside the space the cut already opened.

每一个理性起点的主张，都始于没有根据的一刀。这一刀不是推理里的缺陷。它是推理的条件。

Every claim to a rational starting point begins with an ungrounded cut. The cut is not a flaw in the reasoning. It is the condition of the reasoning.
