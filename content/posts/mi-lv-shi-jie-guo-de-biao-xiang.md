# 幂律是结果的表象 / Power Law Is the Surface of Outcomes

*幂律是已经发生的结果在一个观测窗口里的聚合形状，不是生成它的活动 / A power law is the aggregate shape of outcomes already produced in a window, not the activity generating them.*

一位清华学弟新发表的公众号文章，用价值投资九十年的一手材料指出，资本市场的回报是典型的幂律：极少数决策和公司贡献了大部分超额回报。这个形状作为对结果的统计描述是成立的；文章却据此把它提升为必须服从的底层结构，策略围绕抓住超级赢家来设计。

A newly published WeChat article by a younger Tsinghua alumnus uses ninety years of first-hand material on value investing to argue that returns in capital markets are a textbook power law: a very few decisions and companies account for most of the excess return. As a statistical description of outcomes the shape holds; yet the article raises it into an underlying structure that must be obeyed, and designs strategy around catching the super-winners.

把已经凝固的痕迹误认成生成它的活动，统计规律就被写成了生成的逻辑。要追踪的，是不同的迭代速率如何在时间中复利运作。

Mistaking a trace that has already set for the activity that generated it writes a statistical regularity as generative logic. What is to be tracked is how different rates of iteration compound over time.

## 一、 少数的暴政是已经发生的结果形状 / 1. The Tyranny of the Few Is the Shape of Outcomes Already Produced

[《幂律分布：少数的暴政与投资的真实形状》](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/4yOAQcDRglJ_s0TWTpeoFw)出自一套借助 AI 整理的价值投资文献：四十五位投资人、一千多份文档，从格雷厄姆到柏基。芒格说，去掉伯克希尔最好的十五个决策，记录几乎归零；柏基转述的美国九十年数据里，超过一半的股票超额回报来自九十家公司，持有中位数股票几乎得不到回报；巴菲特自己的可转换优先股组合，若去掉吉列，税后回报至多与它所替换的中期固定收益债券相当；ChatGPT 发布后的三年多里，英伟达、Palantir 等少数标的的涨幅远超中位数。文章据此强调，现代投资组合理论所依赖的钟形曲线假设系统性地失效，要紧的是寻找能实现极端复合成功的少数对象，幅度远比频率重要。

[Power Law Distribution: The Tyranny of the Few and the True Shape of Investing](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/4yOAQcDRglJ_s0TWTpeoFw) (in Chinese) comes out of an AI-assisted compilation of value-investing writings: forty-five investors and over a thousand documents, from Graham to Baillie Gifford. Munger said that without Berkshire's best fifteen decisions the record would be close to nothing; in ninety years of American data relayed by Baillie Gifford, more than half the excess return from equities came from ninety companies, and holding the median stock brought little reward; Buffett's own portfolio of convertible preferred stock, without Gillette, returned after tax no more than the medium-term fixed-income issues it replaced; in the three-plus years since ChatGPT's release, a few names such as Nvidia and Palantir rose far beyond the median. On this basis the article stresses that the bell-curve assumption on which modern portfolio theory rests fails systematically, and that what matters is finding the few that achieve extreme compounded success, with magnitude far outweighing frequency.

这种观察本身并不新鲜。幂律分布早已被广泛引用为事物发展的“客观规律”，从财富、创新到城市规模、语言词频，再到投资回报，它几乎成了描述复杂系统的金科玉律。在投资领域，它常被提升为必须服从的底层结构：既然结果由少数极端事件支配，策略就应围绕“抓住超级赢家、接受高失误率”来设计。柏基、塔勒布、莫布森等人都被援引来讲同一种形状，回应却各不相同：柏基主张抓住少数赢家，塔勒布主张因赢家事先未知而均匀铺开，莫布森讲的是价格变动下风险模型的失灵。

The observation is not new in itself. The power law has long been cited as an "objective law" of how things develop, from wealth and innovation to city sizes and word frequencies, and on to investment returns; it has become nearly a golden rule for describing complex systems. In investing it is often raised into an underlying structure that must be obeyed: since outcomes are dominated by a few extreme events, strategy should be designed around "catching the super-winners and accepting a high rate of misses." Baillie Gifford, Taleb, Mauboussin and others are all cited for the same shape, with different responses: Baillie Gifford would catch the few winners, Taleb would spread stakes evenly because the winner is not known in advance, and Mauboussin speaks of risk models failing under price changes.

## 二、 把统计规律当作生成的逻辑 / 2. A Statistical Regularity Taken for Generative Logic

幂律描述的是已经发生的结果在统计上的形状，不是事物如何发展的底层活动。把统计规律当作生成的逻辑，是把已经凝固的痕迹误认成生成它的活动本身。钟形曲线也没有失效：短窗口里，对数上的增量仍近似正态，钟形仍是增量的形状；失效的是把任一形状写成生成的规律，或写成必须服从的策略。

A power law describes the statistical shape of outcomes that have already happened, not the underlying activity by which things develop. Taking a statistical regularity for generative logic mistakes a trace that has already set for the activity that generated it. Nor has the bell curve failed: over a short window the increments of the logarithm are still approximately normal, and the bell remains the shape of those increments; what fails is writing either shape as generative law, or as a strategy that must be obeyed.

活动从未停止。每一个中心——无论是一位投资者、执掌一家公司的心智，还是推进一条技术路径的工程团队——都在通过离散的判断行动进行迭代，而迭代的速率并不相同。每一轮循环都取决于该中心把信号从噪声中剥离得有多精细，把模型放到当下摩擦中检验得有多直接，以及预期与实际结果之间的落差是被认领来更新下一刀的区分，还是被合理化以维护既有的自我图像。起点的差别——初始资本、既有规模或早期估值——只是初始水平的差别；一旦活动继续，学习与再追踪速率上的微小差异就会通过复利被持续放大。在足够长的时间窗口里，持续的速率差会盖过初始水平的差别。这不是一条额外的法则，而是连续性通过离散性运作的直接后果：痕迹只能单向地塑造后续，时间的箭头就是这种不对称。[前因，后果](../qian-yin-hou-guo/)追的是同一种不对称：观察抓住的是已经成形的果，因总是领先那一步。

The activity never stops. Every center—whether an investor, the minds steering a company, or an engineering team advancing a technological path—iterates through discrete acts of judgment, and the rates of iteration differ. Each cycle turns on how finely the center separates signal from noise, how directly it tests its model against present friction, and whether the gap between expected and realized outcomes is claimed to update the next cut rather than rationalized to defend a retained self-image. A difference in starting point—initial capital, incumbent scale, or early valuation—is only a difference in initial level; once the activity continues, small differences in the rate of learning and re-tracing are amplified continuously through compounding. Over a long enough window, a persisting rate differential outweighs the difference in initial level. This is not an extra law but the direct consequence of continuity operating through discreteness: traces can shape what follows in one direction only, and the arrow of time is that asymmetry. [Cause Before, Effect After](../qian-yin-hou-guo/) traces the same asymmetry: observation grasps the effect already formed, while the cause stays one step ahead.

## 三、 聚合形状不是一个独立的真实形状 / 3. The Aggregate Shape Is Not an Independent True Shape

因此，幂律不是作为一个独立之物的“世界的真实形状”，而是许多中心以不同速率迭代之后，在某个观测窗口里留下的聚合痕迹。极端值之所以支配总量，是因为速率高的中心持续把自己的痕迹叠加上去，而速率低的中心的痕迹相对被稀释。把这个聚合形状当成必须服从的“客观规律”或投资的金科玉律，等于把对结果的统计描述提升为生成的原因，把已经滞后的图像固定成了参照点。这正是同一条统计曲线会被援引来为相反策略背书的原因：莫布森的警示固然指出了短窗口的高斯近似在拉长到复利跨度时会失效，但无论是集中搜寻过往“超级赢家”的事后画像，还是在不透明的尾部上均匀铺开筹码，都把下游分布本身当成了优化的对象，而不是去追踪迭代速率由之产生的那份提取信号、从误差中更新的能力。

A power law is therefore not "the true shape of the world" as an independent thing, but the aggregate trace that many centers iterating at different rates leave within a given observation window. Extremes dominate the total because high-rate centers keep stacking their traces on top, while the traces of low-rate centers are relatively diluted. Treating this aggregate shape as an "objective law" that must be obeyed, or as a golden rule of investing, raises a statistical description of outcomes into a generating cause and fixes an image that is already lagging as the point of reference. That is why the same statistical curve is invoked to justify opposite strategies: while Mauboussin's warning rightly registers that a short-window Gaussian approximation breaks when stretched across compounding horizons, both concentrated hunting for the retrospective profile of past "super-winners" and uniform bet-spreading across an opaque tail take the downstream distribution itself as the object to optimize against, rather than the capacity to extract signal and update from error from which iteration rates arise.

[成功学](../cheng-gong-xue/)追的是同一种滞后被做成可迁移的原则：事后把已成的结果误当成引擎。[封闭的假设把复利挤成 S 曲线](../closed-assumptions-squeeze-compounding-into-s-curves/)追的是另一条被写成进步本性的统计曲线：S 形是封闭的持守把观测绑住之后的形状，不是开放活动自己的轨迹。[平均值是残余，不是引擎](../the-average-is-residue-not-the-engine/)拒绝把平均值写成引擎；这里拒绝的，是把尾部的形状写成生成的逻辑，其原因正如[统计学无法揭示的心智](../the-mind-that-statistics-cannot-reveal/)在一切聚合分布中所指出的：曲线汇集的是过往选择留下的脚印，而决定下一轮速率的源头判断在统计摘要里始终不可见。

[Theories After Success](../cheng-gong-xue/) traces the same lag made into transferable principles: finished outcomes mistaken after the fact for the engine. [Closed Assumptions Squeeze Compounding into S-Curves](../closed-assumptions-squeeze-compounding-into-s-curves/) traces another statistical curve written as the nature of progress: the S is the shape a closed hold gives the observation once it binds it, not the trajectory of the open activity itself. [The Average Is Residue, Not the Engine](../the-average-is-residue-not-the-engine/) refuses to write the average as the engine; what is refused here is writing the shape of the tail as generative logic, for the same reason [The Mind That Statistics Cannot Reveal](../the-mind-that-statistics-cannot-reveal/) locates across every aggregate distribution: the curve pools the footprints of past choices while the originating judgment that sets the next rate stays invisible to the summary.

## 四、 贝叶斯更新从来不是中立的发现 / 4. Bayesian Updating Is Never Neutral Discovery

AI 分析的运作方式，把这种滞后暴露得更清楚。那篇文章所出自的整理，做的是在给定痕迹上的贝叶斯更新。输出高度依赖被喂入的预设条件：从右尾选了哪四十五位幸存的投资人、哪一千多份文档、用什么提示词、截取哪一个九十年时间窗口。事后挑选成名赢家的动作，在生成任何一个词元之前就已经把尾部写进了先验；换掉这些条件，得到的“洞见”可以大相径庭。正如[超越投射](../beyond-the-projection/)与[回返的信号与认领](../the-returning-signal-and-its-claim/)所追踪的，自回归综合只是被喂入的有限投射内部的条件期望：经由工具回返的，是操作者自身取景的结果。当这些预设条件不被选择它们的心智所认领、不被放回生成它们的边缘去重新审视，回返的信号就会被记在载体的名下，当作由 AI 发现的外部结构，反过来加强原有的认知配置。出来的不是看见了活动如何发生，而是把某一种经幸存者筛选的特定痕迹聚合——少数的暴政、寻找超级赢家、幅度重于频率——再一次确认为必须服从的形状。

The way AI analysis operates exposes this lag more plainly. The synthesis the article came from performs Bayesian updating on given traces. Its output depends heavily on the preset conditions fed in: which forty-five surviving investors were chosen from the right tail, which thousand-odd documents, which prompts, which ninety-year window of time. Selecting celebrated winners after the fact already builds the tail into the prior before a single token is generated, and change those conditions and the resulting "insights" can differ widely. As [Beyond the Projection](../beyond-the-projection/) and [The Returning Signal and Its Claim](../the-returning-signal-and-its-claim/) trace, an autoregressive synthesis is a conditional expectation inside the finite projection fed to it: what returns through the tool is the consequence of the operator's own framing. When those preset conditions are not claimed by the Mind that chose them and returned to the generating edge for re-examination, the returning signal is entered under the carrier's name as an external structure discovered by AI, reinforcing the existing configuration of understanding. What comes out is not a view of how the activity happens, but one particular survivor-filtered aggregation of traces—the tyranny of the few, the hunt for super-winners, magnitude over frequency—confirmed once more as a shape that must be obeyed.

## 五、 活动从未完成，起点可以被超越 / 5. The Activity Is Never Finished, and the Starting Point Can Be Surpassed

要追踪的不是分布曲线本身，而是速率如何在具体的中心上持续产生。在各个中心上，起点可以被超越，是因为活动从未完成：正如[可延展的地平线](../the-extendable-horizon/)所确立的，正和的主体性——走出固定的计分板、注入新的区分以提升迭代速率——在任何坐标处都同样敞开，不论初始截距高低。在分析者那里，预设条件可以改变整理出的洞见，是因为贝叶斯更新从来不是中立的发现，而是操作者自己的区分在延伸。一旦把幂律从“客观规律”或“金科玉律”的位置上撤下来，它就只是它本来的样子：结果表象的统计规律。生成它的，始终是不同迭代速率在时间中的复利运作。

What is to be tracked is not the distribution curve itself but how rates keep arising at particular centers. At the centers, the starting point can be surpassed because the activity is never finished: as [The Extendable Horizon](../the-extendable-horizon/) establishes, positive-sum agency—stepping out of a fixed scoreboard to inject a new distinction and raise the rate of iteration—is open at any coordinate regardless of initial intercept. With the analyst, the preset conditions can change the insight the synthesis writes, because Bayesian updating is never neutral discovery but the operator's own distinctions extended. Once the power law is taken down from the place of "objective law" or "golden rule," it is just what it is: a statistical regularity of the surface of outcomes. What generates it is always the compounding of different rates of iteration over time.

[幂律是正态分布的长期后果](../power-law-is-the-long-term-consequence-of-normal-distribution/)补足了这一复利运作在短窗口下的另一面：对数上近似正态的增量经由一步步相乘，才累积成本文放回结果位置的长窗口尾部。无论这些累积的痕迹呈现为九十年的市场账本、软件代码库，还是喂给 AI 综合的一千多份文献档案，[你的代码库即是记忆](../your-codebase-is-memory/)指出了所有外化记录的共同之处：它们都是过往已作区分的存储记忆，从来不是在掌舵边缘生成下一轮回报率的自我区分活动。

[Power Law Is the Long-Term Consequence of Normal Distribution](../power-law-is-the-long-term-consequence-of-normal-distribution/) supplies the short-window face of this compounding: increments that remain approximately normal in the logarithm multiply across successive steps into the long-window tail that this essay returns to its place as outcome. Whether those accumulated traces take the form of a ninety-year market ledger, a software repository, or the thousand-document archive fed into an AI synthesis, [Your Codebase Is Memory](../your-codebase-is-memory/) locates what every externalized record shares: it is stored memory of cuts already made, never the self-distinguishing activity at the steering edge that generates the next rate of return.
