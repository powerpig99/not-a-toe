# 理性选择公设是原罪 / The Rational Choice Postulate Is the Original Sin

*齐一的一致被立为地基，而选择只在私人的、暂时的前提下继续。 / Uniform consistency is installed as foundation while choice continues only under private, provisional premises.*

经济现象只发端于个体的选择，每一次选择都是在那个心智此刻可用的前提下的一次离散行动，这些前提是私人的、暂时的，在不同心智之间、在同一心智相继的时刻之间从不全然相同。理性选择公设把一个一致地求最大化的形式结构冻结为足以为加总、均衡定理和政策处方奠基的东西；生成选择的前提满足不了这一冻结，于是这套装置里此后的每一个理论动作都继承了同一个错配：事后的拟合优雅，预见力薄弱，在结果之间寻找原因，而唯一的自变量被当作已经解决。把这个被继承的结构叫作原罪：它不是对哪个人或哪个学派的判决，而是一个生成性错误的名字，这套装置里此后的每一步都带着它的逻辑后果。

Economic phenomena originate solely in individual choices, each a discrete act under the premises available to that mind at that moment, premises private, provisional and never wholly identical across minds or across successive moments of one mind. The rational choice postulate freezes a formal structure of consistent maximization as solid enough to ground aggregation, equilibrium theorems and policy prescription; the premises that generate choice cannot meet that freeze, so every later theoretical move within this apparatus inherits the same mismatch: elegant post-hoc fit, weak foresight, and causal search among effects while the only independent variable is treated as already settled. Call that inherited structure original sin: not a verdict on persons or schools, but the name of a single generative error whose logical consequence every later move in the apparatus carries.

## 一、 私人前提下的选择是唯一的自变量 / 1. Choice Under Private Premises Is the Sole Independent Variable

自我区分的活动在发生，无因，不息。称它为心智：那个已经在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。经济学登记为选择的，就是这种登记在一个所在处的样子：这一次行动，然后下一次，在一个已被先前行动塑造的场中。前提是那一行动所保留的参照：什么算作有价值，什么算作已知，什么被预期会随之而来。在充当工具的那一刻，它们已经旧了一步。它们或重新描画，或冻结；它们从不作为一份为所有行动者共享、或在同一行动者相继的行动中保持稳定的现成存货而到来。

Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. What economics registers as choice is that registration at one locus: this act, then the next, in a field already patterned by prior acts. Premises are the retained references of that act: what ranks as valuable, what counts as known, what is expected to follow. They are one step old at the moment they serve as instrument. They re-render or freeze; they never arrive as a finished common stock shared by all agents or stable across all successive acts of one agent.

选择背后再没有别的实体。总量、价格、制度和模式，是选择相互作用后密化的残余，可作为后来行动的场，却从不是生成这些行动的自变量。[个体抉择是唯一的因果杠杆](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/)是繁荣与系统情形中的这种唯一性：每一次行动都是它自己的杠杆，从不是一个共享平面上的位置；条件改变回报，却不创作下一次行动。[价格是言说，理解是交易](../price-as-utterance-understanding-as-trade/)是市场的一面：订单经由买卖报价在价格、数量与时机上的交汇而成交，而不是经由背后的估值模型达成完成的同一而成交。中心之间只有可得之物的模式。规模不会发明一个超级中心，去承载每一个所在仍在独自承载的东西。

No further entity exists behind the choice. Aggregates, prices, institutions and patterns are densified residue of choices interacting, available as field for later acts, never independent variables that generate them. [Individual Choices as the Only Causal Levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) is that exclusivity in the case of prosperity and systems: each act is its own lever, never a position on a shared plane; conditions alter payoffs, and they do not author the next act. [Price as Utterance, Understanding as Trade](../price-as-utterance-understanding-as-trade/) is the market face: orders clear by the crossing of bids and asks in price, size and timing, not by completed identity of the valuation models behind them. Between centers there is only the patterning of what is available. Scale does not invent a super-center that can carry what each locus still carries alone.

## 二、 公设冻结了前提无法提供的结构 / 2. The Postulate Freezes Structure the Premises Cannot Supply

理性选择公设断言，行动者在给定约束下一致地选择，以求一个偏好次序的最大化，而这种一致的结构可以作为地基，推导出市场结果、均衡关系和政策处方。这里的一致不只是选择那一刻的局部连贯，它被当作足够稳定、足够共享或足够有代表性，从而可以许可加总与预测。

The rational choice postulate asserts that agents choose consistently so as to maximize a preference ordering under given constraints, and that this structure of consistency can serve as foundation for deriving market outcomes, equilibrium relations and policy prescriptions. Consistency here is not merely local coherence at the instant of choice. It is treated as stable enough, shared enough or representative enough to license aggregation and prediction.

生成选择的那个事实，即在私人的、暂时的前提下选择，恰恰不许可这一点。因为前提在同一心智之内变动，在不同心智之间相异，公设所要求的形式结构，无法作为跨越行动者与时间而稳定、共享或有代表性的属性来维持。这一冻结是工具上的形象滞后：一套保留下来的形式装置，被保持过了前提实际运作的那一步，于是私人的暂时性被登记为围绕一个坚实微观基础的噪声，而不是生成之场本身。退一步说成“近似一致”或“平均而言一致”，只是晚一步重新安装了同一个要求：装置要能迁移，仍需要一份剩余的齐一。

The generating fact, choice under private, provisional premises, forbids exactly that license. Because premises shift within a single mind and differ across minds, the formal structure the postulate requires cannot be maintained as a stable, shared or representative property across agents and time. The freeze is Image lag on the instrument: a retained formal apparatus held past the step at which premises actually operate, so that private provisionality registers as noise around a solid micro-foundation rather than as the generative field itself. Softening into “approximately consistent” or “consistent on average” reinstalls the same demand one step later: a residual uniformity still required for the apparatus to travel.

## 三、 一切交换都经由相反的前提与暂时的重叠而成交 / 3. All Exchange Clears by Opposed Premises and Temporary Overlap

看一看定价这个最基本的情形。只有当双方对同一件物品持相反的估值时，价格才会出现。买方的前提把物品排在钱之上，卖方的前提把钱排在物品之上。理性选择公设里没有任何东西生成这一对立。公设只提供一套共享的形式装置，假定每个行动者把它用在自己恰好持有的任何前提上。前提的对立先于形式装置，也独立于它。

Consider the elementary case of pricing. A price arises only when two parties hold opposite valuations of the same good. The buyer's premises rank the good above the money; the seller's premises rank the money above the good. Nothing in the rational choice postulate generates this opposition. The postulate supplies only a shared formal apparatus that each agent is assumed to apply to whatever premises they happen to hold. The opposition of premises is prior to, and independent of, the formal apparatus.

这种几何不是市场的特例。每一种经济活动都是这种交换：商品、劳动、资本、风险和时间，只在不同的私人排序重叠到足以有下一步行动的地方成交，从不在两个模型合而为一的地方成交。观念的交换也是如此。达成一致时，双方并没有开始共享同一份理解。各方从自己的视角，认出并定位落在双方差异之重叠区域里的那条边缘。一致就是那条被定位的边缘，即足以支撑下一次共同或协调行动的暂时重叠，而不是产生它的那些内部达成完成的同一。未被重叠吸纳的差异仍在；它此刻被搁置，而不是被抹去。

That geometry is not a special case of markets. Every economic activity is exchange of this kind: goods, labor, capital, risk and time clear only where differing private rankings overlap enough for a next act, never where two models become one. The same holds for the exchange of ideas. When agreement is reached, the parties do not come to share the same understanding. Each, from its own perspective, identifies and locates the edge that falls into the overlapping region of their differences. The agreement is that located edge, temporary sufficient overlap for the next joint or coordinated act, not completed identity of the interiors that produced it. Unshared difference remains; it is bracketed for the moment, not erased.

一旦装置被抬到地基的地位，实际的生成性差异就被降为“口味”“信息”或“沟通失误”这类外生的标签，此后被当作要平均掉的噪声。[价格是言说，理解是交易](../price-as-utterance-understanding-as-trade/)已经握住这种几何的两面：一笔成交价和一句说出的话都是公共的残余；资本的协调与理解的协调，都依赖永久的生产性间隙，从不依赖完成的同构。公设把共享的形式冻结为承重的，把对立贬为可以忽略的扰动。依赖的次序在第一步就被颠倒了，在市场里如此，在话语里也如此。

Once the apparatus is elevated to foundational status, the actual generative difference is relegated to exogenous labels such as “taste,” “information” or “miscommunication” and is thereafter treated as noise to be averaged away. [Price as Utterance, Understanding as Trade](../price-as-utterance-understanding-as-trade/) already holds both faces of the geometry: a clearing print and a spoken sentence are public residues; coordination of capital and of understanding rests on permanent productive gap, never on finished isomorphism. The postulate freezes the shared form as load-bearing and demotes the opposition to a negligible perturbation. The order of dependence is reversed at the first step, in markets and in discourse alike.

## 四、 平均化把结果当作原因安装起来 / 4. Averaging Installs the Effect as if It Were the Cause

平均化是一种把一个分布压成一个数的数学操作；公设所许可的，是把这个商抬为自变量，由此制造出一个自变量的印象：一个代表性行为人，一条稳定的需求曲线，一个可以被当作创作了模式的均值偏好或均值反应。平均化实际上是什么，不多也不少，就是集体症状除以个体的人数。这个程序把许多私人前提下的离散行动早已产生的残余压缩成一个统计量；它并不提供一个新的生成引擎。均值是那些行动的结果，被重新标成了它们的原因。[平均值是残余，不是引擎](../the-average-is-residue-not-the-engine/)是人口与筛选情形中的这种重新标记：大众的百分比追踪构成，决定性的边缘在右尾稀少的实际人数那里。[研究AI效应重置了因果原点](../studying-the-effect-of-ai-relocates-causality/)是这样的重新标记：人与一件工具之间相反关系的均值，被命名为 AI 的效应。这种重新标记带来完美的表面可解释性：每一个过去的总量一经除开，便“说明”了每一个过去的个人份额。那个商本身对下一次选择的分布却毫无预见力，因为唯一的自变量，即在不断变动的前提下的选择，从来不在那个商里。

Averaging is a mathematical operation that collapses a distribution into one number; what the postulate licenses is promoting that quotient to an independent variable, which creates the impression of one: a representative agent, a stable demand schedule, a mean preference or mean response that can be treated as if it authored the pattern. What averaging actually is, nothing more and nothing less, is the collective symptom divided by the number of individuals. The procedure compresses into one statistic the residue already produced by many discrete acts under private premises; it does not supply a new generative engine. The mean is the effect of those acts, re-labeled as if it were their cause. [The Average Is Residue, Not the Engine](../the-average-is-residue-not-the-engine/) is that re-label in the case of demography and selection: mass percentages track composition while the decisive edge sits with a thin number in the right tail. [Studying the Effect of AI Relocates Causality](../studying-the-effect-of-ai-relocates-causality/) is that re-label when the mean of opposed relations with a tool is named as the effect of AI. That re-label yields perfect surface explainability: every past total, once divided, “accounts for” every past individual share. The quotient itself yields zero predictability of the next distribution of choices, because the only independent variable, choice under premises that continue to shift, was never in the quotient.

被抵消进那个均值的，恰恰是推动场的离散行动：前提相异的行动者在试验、创新、退出、模仿或拒绝。随着时间推移，这些行动改变前提本身的分布、可行行动的集合，以及后续选择所面对的制度约束。只要公布的平均值仍接近旧值，这种转移在统计上就看不见。等累积的效果终于大到可以登记，先前校准过的关系已经不再描述新的选择总体。预见的失败不是因为从模型之外输入的外部冲击，而是因为把集体症状除以人数当作原因，系统性地丢弃了产生非平稳性的生成性差异。[封闭的假设把复利挤成 S 曲线](../closed-assumptions-squeeze-compounding-into-s-curves/)是增长情形中的同一种冻结：一个共享的框架被当作场的本性，而稀少的中心仍在越过被保留的天花板，重新打开复利。[现实扭曲力场颠倒了基线](../the-reality-distortion-field-inverts-the-baseline/)是大众一致情形中的这一序列：平均值被安装为未受扰动的地基，迫使人重新描画的那些开口，被计为暂时的扭曲。

What is cancelled into that mean is precisely the discrete activity that moves the field: agents with divergent premises experimenting, innovating, exiting, imitating or refusing. Over time those acts alter the distribution of premises themselves, the set of feasible actions and the institutional constraints under which further choices are made. The shift stays statistically invisible while the published average stays close to its old value. When the cumulative effect finally becomes large enough to register, the previously calibrated relations have already ceased to describe the new population of choices. Predictability fails not because of external shocks imported from outside the model. It fails because treating the collective symptom divided by headcount as if it were cause systematically discards the generative difference that produces non-stationarity. [Closed Assumptions Squeeze Compounding into S-Curves](../closed-assumptions-squeeze-compounding-into-s-curves/) is the same freeze in the case of growth: a shared frame held as the nature of the field while thin centers still reopen compounding past the retained ceiling. [The Reality Distortion Field Inverts the Baseline](../the-reality-distortion-field-inverts-the-baseline/) is that sequence in the case of mass coherence: the average is installed as undisturbed ground and the openings that force re-tracing are scored as temporary warp.

## 五、 事后的拟合廉价，预见却要求前提无法给出的迁移 / 5. Retrospective Fit Is Cheap; Foresight Needs Travel the Premises Refuse

同一逻辑说明了建立在这一公设之上的理论的典型表现：它们以相当的优雅在事后解释观察到的模式，却在样本外预测和有效干预的设计上系统性地表现不佳。

The same logic accounts for the characteristic performance of the theories built on the postulate: they explain observed patterns after the fact with considerable elegance, yet systematically underperform at out-of-sample prediction and at the design of effective interventions.

公设一旦就位，解释就很廉价，只要这份拟合被要求充当预见或地基。任何历史上的选择序列，都能在结果已知之后，通过重构合适的偏好次序和信息集而被合理化。重构出来的行动者看起来一致地求了最大化，数据就此被“解释”了。观察握住的是结果；重构出来的地图是残余的残余，这一重新描画的保真度并不成为生成行动的精确，也不把私人的前提迁入一张向前迁移的共享表格。[伪装成远见的表演性后见](../performative-hindsight-that-pretends-to-be-foresight/)是社会情形中这种迟来的形式：已经可得的结果被重新描画，仿佛迟来的认出曾是早先的能力。[因果贯穿始终](../causality-all-the-way/)指出还原所在：被握稳的模式只是横跨过去结果的一幅描画，因果仍在那条划出每一个后续标记的边缘上。

Explanation is cheap once the postulate is in place, wherever that fit is asked to travel as foresight or foundation. Any historical sequence of choices can be rationalized by reconstructing suitable preference orderings and information sets after the outcomes are known. The reconstructed agents appear to have maximized consistently; the data are thereby “explained.” Observation holds effect; the reconstructed map is residue of residue, and fidelity of that re-tracing neither becomes exactness of the generating acts nor relocates private premises into a shared schedule that travels forward. [Performative Hindsight That Pretends to Be Foresight](../performative-hindsight-that-pretends-to-be-foresight/) is that late form in the social case: already-available outcomes re-traced as if the late recognition had been the early capacity. [Causality All the Way](../causality-all-the-way/) names the restore: a secured pattern is a drawing across past effects, while causality stays with the edge still drawing every following mark.

相反，处方与预测要求前提保持足够稳定、足够共享，好让形式结构在时间上向前、或跨越不同的制度环境迁移。前提的私人性与暂时性，否认这种稳定。因此这些模型仍是回顾性叙事的有力工具，预见的乏力工具。这个模式不是一连串偶然的校准失败，而是从一个为保全装置而丢弃生成性差异的冻结出发的逻辑后果。

Prescription and prediction, by contrast, require the premises to remain stable and shared enough for the formal structure to travel forward in time or across institutional settings. The privacy and provisionality of premises deny that stability. Consequently the models remain powerful instruments of retrospective storytelling and weak instruments of foresight. The pattern is not a series of contingent calibration failures. It is the logical consequence of beginning from a freeze that discards generative difference in order to keep the apparatus intact.

## 六、 制度仍是为后续选择布下场的结果 / 6. Institutions Remain Effects That Pattern the Field of Further Choices

制度与总量因此并非无关。它们为后续的选择布下回报与约束的场。然而它们仍是结果，被它们所约束的选择不断重新创造或侵蚀。把它们当作在彼此之间相互作用的准自变量，就颠倒了私人前提下的选择所确立的依赖次序。

Institutions and aggregates are not therefore irrelevant. They pattern the field of payoffs and constraints in which subsequent choices are made. Yet they remain effects, continually re-created or eroded by the choices they constrain. To treat them as quasi-independent variables that interact among themselves is to reverse the order of dependence that choice under private premises establishes.

理性选择公设促成并正当化了这一颠倒：它提供一个看似坚实的一致最大化的微观基础，许可理论家迅速转向研究结果之间的均衡关系，而把选择的生成过程当作已经解决。[主权、信念与规制结构的生成](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/)是自律组织与政府这两道门情形中的这种密化：个体的主权从未真正转出，是许多中心共同表达“主权可以委托”的信念，直到这一信念硬化为程序，而后果仍落在做出选择的那些所在。[公共支出不创造资本](../public-spending-does-not-create-capital/)是投资情形中的同一种残余：公共账本把重新配置冻结为创造，而发起仍留在收益与损失落下的地方。[协调者的范畴错误](../the-coordinators-category-error/)是密化的共享记录被当作下一次认同的共同作者。[能动性被转置入形式模型](../agency-relocated-into-the-formal-model/)是教学的一面：对装置的掌握被当作能动性的延伸，而自变量仍留在选择那里。每一种情形里冻结都是一样的：结果被审视得仿佛它们能创作更多的结果，而无须私人前提下离散选择的持续再入。

The rational choice postulate enables and legitimates that reversal: by supplying an apparently solid micro-foundation of consistent maximization, it licenses the theorist to move rapidly to the study of equilibrium relations among effects while treating the generative process of choice as already settled. [Sovereignty, Belief, and the Generation of Regulatory Structures](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/) is that densification in the case of the two gates, the self-regulatory organization and the government: individual sovereignty is never actually transferred; many centers express the belief that it can be delegated until that belief hardens into procedure, while consequences stay with the choosing loci. [Public Spending Does Not Create Capital](../public-spending-does-not-create-capital/) is the same residue in the case of investment: the public ledger freezes reallocation as creation while initiation stays where upside and downside still land. [The Coordinator’s Category Error](../the-coordinators-category-error/) is densified shared record held as co-author of the next assent. [Agency Relocated into the Formal Model](../agency-relocated-into-the-formal-model/) is the pedagogical face: mastery of the apparatus offered as the extension of agency while the independent variable stays with the choice. In each case the freeze is the same: effects surveyed as if they could author further effects without continuous re-entry of discrete choice under private premises.

## 七、 行为与生态的补丁保全了冻结 / 7. Behavioral and Ecological Patches Preserve the Freeze

引入行为或生态层面的改进，并没有逃出这个结构。它们像添加的补丁，是为了容纳观察到的运动、同时保全公设的核心握持而引入的一轮轮周转。相对于一般的公设，每一个补丁都能认出更多已经在集体中物质化了的个体选择：更多的启发法，更多相对于环境的适应度，更多集体行动的规则，更多许多离散行动事后留下的剩余模式。这份收获作为对已发生之事更密的重构是成立的，但它不是对公设的挑战。

Refinements that introduce behavioral or ecological layers do not escape this structure. They function as added patches, successive cycles introduced to accommodate observed motions while preserving the postulate's core hold. Relative to the general postulate, each patch can identify more of the individual choices that have already materialized collectively: more heuristics, more environment-relative fitness, more rules of collective action, more of the residual pattern left by many discrete acts after the fact. That gain holds as denser reconstruction of what has already occurred. It is not a challenge to the postulate.

这些补丁仍像一般公设那样，忽略了唯一的自变量，即作为仍在进行的生成引擎的、在私人而变动的前提下的个体选择。它们扩大可接受重构的集合，把理性定义为对特定环境的适应，或把选择嵌入共享的规则，于是扩大了可以被计为“理性”的已物质化模式的范围。关于环境或制度背景的共享假设被直接插入，好让形式装置能够再次被应用。对足以许可加总与向前应用的一致、稳定与主体间齐一的那个地基要求，原封未动。这类改进所得到的认可，追踪的是它们在满足对过去集体残余作连贯叙述这一需求上的效用，而不是原初错误的任何解除。[重新审视反脆弱](../a-new-deeper-look-at-antifragility/)是无序情形中相邻的一面：统计上的假肢在被保全的坐标之内管理症状，而坐标本身被豁免。[基准刷榜中的封闭实存](../closed-reality-in-benchmark-maxing/)是测量情形中的同一种握持：剩余在一套封闭的测试上被压到零，而开放负荷下的剩余在扩大。

The patches still ignore the only independent variable, individual choice under private, shifting premises as the generative engine still underway, just as the general postulate does. By enlarging the set of admissible reconstructions, by defining rationality as fitness to a particular environment, or by embedding choice within shared rules, they expand the range of already-materialized patterns that can be scored as “rational.” Shared assumptions about the environment or the institutional setting are simply inserted so that the formal apparatus can again be applied. The foundational demand for enough consistency, stability and intersubjective uniformity to license aggregation and forward application remains untouched. Recognition of such refinements tracks their utility in satisfying the demand for coherent stories about past collective residue, not any release of the original error. [A New Deeper Look at Antifragility](../a-new-deeper-look-at-antifragility/) is adjacent in the case of disorder: statistical prosthesis that manages symptoms inside preserved coordinates while the coordinates themselves stay exempt. [Closed Reality in Benchmark Maxing](../closed-reality-in-benchmark-maxing/) is the same hold in the case of measurement: residual driven to zero on a sealed suite while residual under open load widens.

这些理论很少在其地基前提的层面上被检视；附在它们身上的成功标签，吸走了注意。援引“会长演讲”或“诺贝尔”这样的头衔，提供了一种外部的担保，替代了对公设本身的独立审视。声望是那些已把装置当作地基的中心之间密化的共振，它不是对私人前提能否提供装置所要求之物的第二次检验。[验证中因果权能的分配](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/)追踪这一安放：下一步是从心智自己对前提的检视中引出，还是从被标记为外部的回应中引出，例如声望。

The theories are seldom examined at the level of their foundational premise; the labels of success attached to them draw attention instead. The invocation of titles such as “Presidential Address” or “Nobel” supplies an external warrant that substitutes for independent scrutiny of the postulate itself. Prestige is densified resonance among centers that already treat the apparatus as ground. It is not a second test of whether private premises can supply what the apparatus demands. [The Allocation of Causal Power in Validation](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/) tracks that placement: whether the next step is drawn from the mind's own scrutiny of the premise, or from responses marked as external, such as prestige.

## 八、 一个认识，此后的每一步都继承错配 / 8. One Recognition; Every Subsequent Move Inherits the Mismatch

这个结论不需要额外的心理学、历史或经验假设。它直接得自这样的认识：私人而变动的前提下的每一次个体选择都是它自己的杠杆，选择是唯一的自变量；再加上这样的观察：理性选择公设，无论在行为上或生态上怎样被细化，都要求一种那些前提给不出的一致、稳定与主体间齐一。

No additional psychological, historical or empirical assumptions are required for this conclusion. It follows strictly from the recognition that each individual choice under private and shifting premises is its own lever, and that choice is the sole independent variable, together with the observation that the rational choice postulate, however behaviorally or ecologically elaborated, demands a degree of consistency, stability and intersubjective uniformity that those premises cannot supply.

一旦公设被安装为地基公理，此后的每一个理论动作，加总、均衡推导、政策设计或行为补丁，都继承形式结构与实际生成过程之间原初的错配。事后拟合强、预测与处方力弱这一反复出现的模式，因此不是一连串偶然的失败，而是从一条生成选择的前提满足不了的公理出发的逻辑后果。

Once the postulate is installed as the foundational axiom, every subsequent theoretical move, aggregation, equilibrium derivation, policy design or behavioral patching, inherits the original mismatch between the formal structure and the actual generative process. The repeated pattern of strong post-hoc fit and weak predictive or prescriptive power is therefore not a series of contingent failures. It is the logical consequence of beginning from an axiom the generating premises cannot meet.

作为地基，公设不是一个偶尔误导的有用近似。它是原罪，迫使此后整套装置把目光从唯一的自变量移开，到结果之间去寻找因果关系。退一步说成“部分是微观基础，部分是剩余噪声”，是在只有一条私人前提与离散行动之序列在继续的地方，重新安装了两个所在。[开放即一致](../openness-is-consistency/)指出相邻的冻结：一个有限的结构被迫为只在继续的东西奠基。这里的有限结构，就是被当作穷尽的微观基础的偏好与约束装置，而经济只作为在从不完成为共享同一的前提下的相继选择而继续。释放把装置还为工具：可用于局部的回顾性重构，却从不是向前迁移的地基，也不是下一次行动被封存的起源。[理性从不跨越心智](../rationality-never-travels-across-the-mind/)是对这一诊断本身的界限：这一认识本身也只是持有它的那个心智处的一次重新诠释，而不是一套完好地到达别处的第二种可携带演算。只要这个错误不被放下，现代经济理论就会继续把对症状的描述打磨得越来越精致，而唯一产生这些症状的生成过程，仍在它的结构所及之外。在相邻的领域里，同一种在结果之间的搜寻也一再出现。[意识从不作为数据出现在数据之中](../consciousness-never-appears-as-data-among-data/)是证据情形中的这种搜寻：第三人称的数据只握住公共的结果；要求意识出现在它们之中，便把领先的边缘冻结为又一个数据。[成功学](../cheng-gong-xue/)是成功文学情形中同一种廉价的回顾拟合：幸存者被重排成可携带的规则，当作引擎提供出来，而产生他们的序列被留在外面。

As a foundation, the postulate is not a useful approximation that occasionally misleads. It is the original sin that forces the entire subsequent apparatus to look away from the only independent variable and to search for causal relations among the effects. Softening into “part micro-foundation, part residual noise” reinstalls two loci where only one sequence of private premise and discrete act continues. [Openness Is Consistency](../openness-is-consistency/) names the adjacent freeze: a finite structure forced to ground what only continues. Here the finite structure is the preference-and-constraint apparatus held as exhaustive micro-foundation of an economy that continues only as successive choices under premises that never finish into shared identity. Release returns the apparatus to instrument, usable for local retrospective reconstruction, never a foundation that travels forward or the sealed origin of the next act. [Rationality Never Travels Across the Mind](../rationality-never-travels-across-the-mind/) is that bound on the diagnosis itself: this recognition is itself a reinterpretation at the mind that holds it, not a second portable calculus that arrives intact elsewhere. Until that error is relinquished, modern economic theory will keep refining ever more sophisticated descriptions of symptoms, while the generative process that alone produces them stays outside its structural reach. Across adjacent domains the same search among effects recurs. [Consciousness Never Appears as Data Among Data](../consciousness-never-appears-as-data-among-data/) is that search in the case of evidence: third-person data hold only public effects, and the demand that consciousness appear among them freezes the leading edge as one more datum. [Theories After Success](../cheng-gong-xue/) is that same cheap retrospective fit in the case of success literature: survivors rearranged into portable rules offered as the engine, while the sequence that produced them is left out.
