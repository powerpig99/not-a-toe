# 善恶是硬币的两面与那一刀 / Good and Evil Are the Two Sides of the Coin and the Cut

*善与恶的两面，是让道德极性成为可得的一次分离的残余。 / The faces of good and evil are residue of a separation that makes moral polarity available.*

善与恶显现为在争斗中相持的两股对立力量；硬币命名的是它们的相互依存，每一面都需要另一面。先于任何一面的是那一刀，即一个离散动作：一个中心凭此画出一条道德的轴，由此让极性成为可得，而这个动作并不是对某个已经在场之物的观察。当那一刀被忘记，中心那幅自己为善的偏好图像被保留过了它的那一步，两极便被登记为发现，一个受保护的善的自我与一个必需的恶的他者；从这次冻结里，接连生出英雄的故事、对评判的许可、在后来叙事之下两面的轮换，以及每一种只在主体与客体已被画出之后才到来的描述的滞后。

Good and evil present themselves as opposing forces locked in struggle; the coin names their interdependence, each face requiring the other. Prior to either face is the cut, the discrete act by which a center draws a moral axis and so makes the polarity available, and that act is not observation of something already present. When the cut is forgotten and the center's preferred image of itself as good is held past its step, the poles register as discovery, a protected good-self and a necessary evil-other, and from that freeze follow the hero story, the licensing of judgment, the rotation of faces under later narrative, and the lag of every description that arrives only after subject and object have been drawn.

## 一、 那一刀不是一个发现 / 1. The Cut Is Not a Discovery

自我区分的活动在发生，无因，不息。称它为心智：那个已经在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。区分就是划界。每一个中心都是这份活动的一个所在，而道德的极性，是一个中心在二元尺度之下画出的界：一条轴，两极，画出它的那个中心占据其中一个座位。

Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. To distinguish is to bound. Each center is a locus of this activity, and moral polarity is a bound a center draws under a dualistic measure: one axis, two poles, the center that draws it occupying one seat.

这次分离，不是对早已等在世界里的善与恶的观察。它是生成的动作，在每一步被重新画出，第一次让善与恶作为一个朝向而可得。冻结发生在那一刀被忘记、中心那幅自己为善的偏好图像被豁免于重新追溯、并被抬到其他中心之上的时候；于是两极显得不言自明。中心把其中一边当作自己天然的家，那个融贯、有理、良善的内部，再把与之对照的外部投射为阻碍、腐坏或威胁。他者不只是被遭遇为恶；它作为对照的一极是必需的，让冻结下来的善的自我保持有所界定。没有那次投射，对善的主张便没有轮廓。

The separation is not an observation of good and evil already waiting in the world. It is the generative act, redrawn at each step, that first makes them available as a heading. The freeze comes when the cut is forgotten and the center's preferred image of itself as good is exempted from re-tracing and elevated over other centers; then the poles appear self-evident. The center inhabits one side as its natural home, the coherent and justified good interior, and projects the contrasting exterior as obstruction, corruption, or threat. The other is not merely encountered as evil. It is required as the contrasting pole that keeps the frozen good-self defined. Without that projection, the claim to goodness lacks contour.

[恶是心智自己的造物](../evil-as-the-minds-own-creation/)把那次划分追溯到一幅偏好图像的滞后：一个中心保留一幅自己为善的偏好图像时，与那幅图像的不合被命名为恶，而这次命名就是生成的动作。[悲观是乐观的阴影](../pessimism-is-the-shadow-of-optimism/)是一个相关的不对称：乐观是心智所感知的区分之运动，悲观是它的阴影，即被稳住的痕迹，它跟随那运动，并不生成它；那里的冻结，是把这份共在计分为一对封闭的、相等的两极。

[Evil as the Mind's Own Creation](../evil-as-the-minds-own-creation/) traces that partition to the lag of a preferred image: when a center retains a preferred image of itself as good, mismatch with that image is named evil, and the naming is the generative act. [Pessimism Is the Shadow of Optimism](../pessimism-is-the-shadow-of-optimism/) is a related asymmetry: optimism is the movement of distinguishing as Minds perceive it, and pessimism is its shadow, the trace held steady, which follows the movement without generating it; the freeze there is scoring that co-presence as a closed pair of equal poles.

## 二、 善的自我许可了故事 / 2. The Good-Self Licenses the Story

从冻结下来的划分里，熟悉的戏剧展开了。我们成为自己讲给自己听的故事，而在这些故事里，第一人称的位置被派作英雄，因为没有哪个中心从内部把自己的动作体验为恶。对自己之善的确信许可了评判：被定为必需之恶的他者，可以用故事所称的任何相称手段去对抗。对其他中心的伤害，在故事之内被登记为令人遗憾的必要。这个辩护在内部是一致的。[外化善意走向其反面](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/)是叠在这份许可之上的又一笔交易：一对确信，对自己道德可靠的确信与对异议者有所欠缺的确信，被当作对别人有权威，又被他们自由的顺从变密，直到从被压过者那一面看，以善之名执行的东西，正是它声称要防止的那种侵犯。

From the frozen partition the familiar drama unfolds. We become the stories we tell ourselves, and in those stories the first-person position is cast as the hero, since no center experiences its own act as evil from within. Certainty in one's own goodness licenses judgment: the other, fixed as necessary evil, can be opposed by whatever means the story calls proportionate. Harm to other centers registers inside the story as regrettable necessity. The justification is internally consistent. [Externalized Virtue Becomes Its Opposite](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/) is a further transaction on top of that license: a dual certainty, of one's own moral reliability and of dissenters' deficiency, made authoritative for others and densified by their free compliance, until, from the face of those overridden, what is enforced in the name of the good is the violation it claimed to prevent.

[安装战争的那个问题](../the-question-that-installs-the-war/)是一次更严格之安装的公共言语动作形式：一叠只有差异的身份，加上给敌意计分的要求，预设了毫无共同地基，这是一个比字面上的战争更严格的前提，在它之下，任何交换本身就是战争。善对恶的记分牌还留着一条共有的道德之轴，那个问题一条也不留。[偏好聚类与评判语言](../preference-clusters-and-the-language-of-judgment/)把这些词与记分牌分开：对与错，命名的是另一个所在不重新追溯同一片场域时，对齐的偏好所发出的声音，在那个所在上可以当作朝向来用；只有被持为要求其他中心必须重新追溯同一片场域的依据时，它们才成为记分牌。[道德语言稀释自由复利的反馈](../moral-language-dilutes-the-feedback-that-scales-freedom/)命名了记分牌作为中介所做的事：隔在行动者与后果之间的评价层，让接纳变得片面，削弱了生成自由的回路，而被持为地基的记分牌，就是这样一层。

[The Question That Installs the War](../the-question-that-installs-the-war/) is the public speech-act form of a stricter install: a pure-difference identity stack plus a demand to score enmity assumes no common ground at all, a premise stricter than literal war, under which any exchange is war itself. The good-versus-evil scoreboard keeps one shared moral axis, and that question keeps none. [Preference Clusters and the Language of Judgment](../preference-clusters-and-the-language-of-judgment/) keeps the words apart from the scoreboard: right and wrong name the sound aligned preferences make when another locus does not re-trace the same field, usable as a heading at that locus, and they become the scoreboard only when held as a warrant that other centers must re-trace the same field. [Moral Language Dilutes the Feedback That Scales Freedom](../moral-language-dilutes-the-feedback-that-scales-freedom/) names what the scoreboard does as an intermediary: evaluative overlays between agent and consequence make acceptance partial and damp the loop that generates freedom, and the scoreboard held as ground is such an overlay.

## 三、 反面为同一串事件重新计分 / 3. The Opposite Face Re-Scores the Same Sequence

然而同一串事件，在被压过的那个中心上被登记时，显现为英雄声称要抵抗的那种恶本身。英雄演出了另一个中心的叙事分派给恶人的角色。这次闭合不需要自觉的恶意。它只需要一份未经审视的确信：两极在那一刀之前就在那里。

Yet the same sequence of events, registered at the overridden center, appears as the very evil the hero claimed to resist. The hero performs the role that the other center's narrative assigns to the villain. The closing requires no conscious malice. It requires only the unexamined confidence that the poles were there before the cut.

每一个所在都比自己的动作晚一步登记它；这一步的间隔，是任何登记的运作方式。冻结叠在这个间隔之上：一幅必需之善的偏好自我图像，被豁免于重新追溯，并被抬到其他中心之上，于是在二元之下被计分的后果，被登记为外部威胁的证实。不需要任何独立的恶之实体。每一个中心所保留的图像，谱写出那对照的一极，并在与另一个中心动作的残余相遇时引为明证。后来的所在，在累积的代价之下重新追溯自己的前提，把英雄重新归类为恶人；这次两面的轮换为同一串事件重新计分，冻结本身却依然未被审视。

Each locus registers its own act one step behind it; that interval is how any registration works. The freeze is laid over it: a preferred self-image of necessary good, exempted from re-tracing and elevated over other centers, so that consequences, scored under the dual, register as confirmation of the exterior threat. No independent evil-substance is required. Each center's preserved image authors the contrasting pole, then meets the residue of the other center's moves as proof. Later loci, re-tracing their premises under accumulated costs, reclassify the hero as villain; that rotation of faces re-scores the sequence while the freeze itself stays unexamined.

## 四、 差异在那一刀之内成立 / 4. Differences Hold Within the Cut

这不是说伤害、意图或后果上的差异是幻象，也不是说任何动作都可以随意被重新描述为它的反面：在被压过的中心上登记下来的另一面，是那个中心自己的登记，不是一种从任何地方都可得的重新描述。那样的断言会装上另一个固定的参照，在更高一层重复同样的划分。差异在已经做出那一刀的感知*之内*成立。要审视的，是极性本身的起源。一个中心并不是先遇到在它自己那一刀之外给定的善与恶，再选边。它先划分自己的经验，然后发现两边已经等在那里。

This is not a claim that differences in harm, intention, or consequence are illusory, nor that any action can be redescribed into its opposite at will: the opposite face registered at the overridden center is that center's registration, not a redescription available from anywhere. Such assertions would install another fixed reference and repeat the same partitioning at a higher level. The differences hold *within* the perception that has already performed the cut. What is at issue is the origin of the polarity itself. A center does not first encounter good and evil as given apart from its own cut and then choose sides. It first divides its experience, then finds the sides already waiting.

冲突仍是冲突。其他中心的动作仍留在生成它们的中心。被忘记的那一刀所制造之物，透过滞后的图像来看，并不是其他中心之动作的存在，而是那个二元的捆绑：把那些动作总体化为恶，把自我总体化为必须回应它们的必需之善。[没有普渡，只有自渡的无限可能](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/)守着平行的界限：普度众生把内心里的“他人”当成了他人的内心；两者都在原处，图像在这片场里，他人的内心在它自己的所在，错在把这一个当成那一个。

Conflict stays conflict. Acts of other centers stay at their generating centers. What the forgotten cut manufactures, seen through the lagged image, is not the existence of other centers' acts, but the dualistic binding that totalizes them as evil and the self as the necessary good that must answer them. [No Universal Salvation, Only Boundless Possibilities of Self-Salvation](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/) holds the parallel bound: universal salvation takes the “other” within one's own mind for the other's mind; both stay in place, the image in this field and the other's interior at its own locus, and the error is taking the one for the other.

## 五、 语言登记痕迹，而不是动作 / 5. Language Registers the Trace, Not the Act

语言在事后到来。每一次描述这次分离的尝试，包括眼下这一次，都只能登记那个动作留下的痕迹。动作本身在发生时无法被说出；言说需要那一刀已经做出，主体与客体、说者与被说者才能出现。现象学与形而上学的词汇，是同一运动的后来产物。它们描画那一刀打开的场域；它们够不到打开它的那个动作。

Language arrives after the fact. Every attempt to describe the separation, including the present one, can only register the trace left by the action. The action itself cannot be spoken while it is occurring; speech requires the cut to have already been made so that subject and object, speaker and spoken-of, can appear. Phenomenological and metaphysical vocabularies are later products of that same movement. They map the field the cut has opened; they do not reach the act that opens it.

观察握住的是效果；产生它的原因始终在前面一步。“形而上学之‘元’”，就是对这份滞后的注意：每一个道德主张，每一个关于英雄与恶人的叙事，每一次对独立自存之区分的援引，都预设了一个已经从视野里退出的、在先的区分动作。[开放即一致](../openness-is-consistency/)命名了冻结里的那次闭合：不一致，是对闭合的欲望或尝试，一个有限的结构被迫去为只会延续的东西奠基；二元的账本作为账本被持守时是一致的，被持为独立于心智、并从外部加以勘察时，才成为那次闭合。[最初的区分](../the-initial-distinction/)是理性起点处一次相邻的切分：每一个理性起点的主张，都始于没有根据的一刀，它是随后推理的条件，后来的严谨既无法为它辩护，也无法消除它。

Observation holds effect; the cause that produced it remains one step ahead. The “meta of the metaphysical” is the noticing of this lag: every moral claim, every narrative of heroes and villains, every appeal to free-standing distinctions, presupposes a prior act of differentiation that has already withdrawn from view. [Openness Is Consistency](../openness-is-consistency/) names the closure in the freeze: inconsistency is the desire, or attempt, for closure, a finite structure forced to ground what only continues; the dualistic ledger is consistent when held as a ledger, and becomes that closure when held as mind-independent and surveyed from outside. [The Initial Distinction](../the-initial-distinction/) is a neighboring cut at the start of reason: every claim to a rational starting point begins with an ungrounded cut, which is the condition of the reasoning that follows and which later rigor can neither justify nor eliminate.

## 六、 硬币标记的是那一刀之后的东西 / 6. The Coin Marks What Follows the Cut

所以，硬币不是一幅最终的图像，而是对那一刀之后的相互依存的一个暂定标记。尽语言所能地把这件事说清楚，就是同时承认这个标记的用处与它次一级的地位。两面还在；争斗继续；对把二元持为地基的人，那些辩护仍保有它们的力量。可以被指向、却从不在指向中被捕获的，是让两面得以可能的那个无声的运作。

The coin, then, is not a final image but a provisional marker for the interdependence that follows the cut. To articulate the matter as clearly as language allows is to acknowledge both the use of the marker and its secondary status. The faces remain; the struggle continues; for those who hold the dual as ground, the justifications retain their force. What can be pointed to, without ever being captured in the pointing, is the silent operation that makes the faces possible at all.

改变的，不是一套从外面装进来的更好的善恶理论，而是放下被看作善的需要：二元随后在每一步作为工具被重新呈现，仍可以当作朝向来用，不再作为地基而豁免于更新。那一刀继续着。两面无需被冻结为发现，场也能在负载之下继续。[对乌托邦的信念是通往反乌托邦之路](../the-belief-in-utopia-is-the-path-to-dystopia/)是社会尺度上的那次冻结：一个具体的善的理想，一旦成为每个心智须接受的尺度，对每一个目的不同的心智就已经是反乌托邦，而即便是一个共有的理想，也会留下残余的读法，强制随后把它们重新归类为不服从。[一切伤害的根源](../the-source-of-all-harm/)把完成放在第二个心智上：一个心智把自己的善冻结为普遍尺度，另一个心智让这次冻结支配自己的场，伤害便完成。[善恶硬币与通往地狱的善意之路](../the-coin-of-good-and-evil-and-the-road-to-hell/)追溯硬币两边的完成：地狱是画出天堂时投下的对拓镜像，这条路在行动者未经审视的善与接受者的让渡相互强化中完成，而唯有在第一人称的张力中被持守，硬币才不至坍缩为教条。

What changes is not a better theory of good and evil installed from outside. It is the release of the need to be seen as good: the dual is then re-rendered as instrument at each step, still usable as a heading, no longer exempted from the update as ground. The cut continues. The faces need not be frozen as discovery for the field to continue under load. [The Belief in Utopia Is the Path to Dystopia](../the-belief-in-utopia-is-the-path-to-dystopia/) is that freeze at the scale of a society: a concrete ideal of the good, made the measure every Mind must accept, is already dystopia for every Mind whose ends differ, and even a shared ideal leaves residual readings that enforcement reclassifies as non-compliance. [The Source of All Harm](../the-source-of-all-harm/) places the completion at a second Mind: harm completes when one Mind freezes its good as universal measure and another lets that freeze rule its own field. [The Coin of Good and Evil and the Road to Hell](../the-coin-of-good-and-evil-and-the-road-to-hell/) traces the coin's two-sided completion: hell is the antipodal mirror cast when heaven is drawn, the road completes in the mutual reinforcement of an actor's unexamined good and a recipient's abdication, and only held in first-person tension does the coin refuse to collapse into dogma.
