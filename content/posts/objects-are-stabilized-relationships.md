# 对象是被稳定化的关系 / Objects Are Stabilized Relationships

*关系先于实体，但实在并非虚无飘渺的迷雾；物体是因果摩擦在第一人称视界中沉淀出的稳定化关系。 / Relations precede relata, yet reality is not an elusive fog; objects are the stabilized invariants of causal friction precipitated within first-person perspective reality.*

---

## 一、 常识的假定与量子的颠覆 / 1. Common-Sense Intuition and the Quantum Inversion

日常生活的直觉常识向我们灌输了一套极其顽固的本体论图景：
我们理所当然地认为，**“物体”（Objects）比物体之间的“关系”（Relationships）更基本。**

在常识与经典物理学的眼中，世界首先是由一个个具有确定边界、独立存在的实体所构成的：微观上是像台球一样飞速穿梭的基本粒子，宏观上是坚硬的岩石、耸立的树木与运动的行星。关系（引力、碰撞、电磁斥力、空间距离）被视作次生性的纽带——是两个预先独立存在的物体相遇之后，才在彼此之间建立起来的临时桥梁。

然而，现代量子力学——尤其是以卡洛·罗威利（Carlo Rovelli）为代表的**关系量子力学（Relational Quantum Mechanics, RQM）**——彻底颠覆了这幅常识画卷。

在量子微观尺度上，“孤立系统拥有内禀客观性质”的假定被实验无情碾碎。一个粒子的位置、动量或自旋状态，在没有发生物理交互之前根本不存在确定的客观数值；物理变量的取值，永远且仅仅存在于**该系统相对于另一个物理系统的交互关系之中**。物理学家与哲学家（如乔治·韦伯斯特，George Webster）由此指出：在量子图景中，像对称性、干涉与纠缠这样的关系，比粒子实体本身更真实、更根本。

关系先于实体。实体并非独立存在的主角，而是关系的演化节点。

---

Everyday intuition instills in us an obstinate ontological picture:
We take it for granted that **objects are more fundamental than the relationships between those objects.**

To common sense and classical physics, the world is assembled from freestanding, self-contained entities: microscopic billiard balls bouncing through an empty spatial container, macroscopic boulders, trees, and celestial bodies. Relationships (gravity, collision, electromagnetic repulsion, spatial distance) are treated as secondary bridges—provisional links established only after two independent substances happen to meet.

Yet modern quantum theory—most pointedly articulated in **Relational Quantum Mechanics (RQM)** pioneered by Carlo Rovelli—upends this intuitive hierarchy.

At the quantum threshold, the assumption that an isolated system possesses intrinsic, observer-independent properties collapses. A particle’s position, momentum, or spin does not exist as an autonomous fact prior to interaction; physical properties manifest exclusively **relative to another system during an interaction**. Thinkers such as George Webster argue that within the quantum landscape, relations such as symmetry, interference, and entanglement are far more fundamental than the elusive "particles" themselves.

Relations precede relata. Entities are not the primary cast of reality; they are the communicative nodes of relational dynamics.

## 二、 双重陷阱：实体崇拜与德勒兹式的解构迷宫 / 2. The Twin Traps: Substance Reification vs. Deleuzian Dissolution

然而，当关系本体论被推向极致时，思想界却迅速滑入了两个互为表里的极端陷阱：

**第一个陷阱，是经典唯物主义的“实体拜物教”（Substance Reification）。**
面对关系的流动与不确定性，经典思维恐惧虚无，执拗地要把一切关系重新塞回实体模型中：如果引力表现为两极之间的吸引，他们就一定要发明出一种名为“引力子”的硬质颗粒；如果时空展现出几何张力，他们就一定要把时空想象成一张被物理填充的橡皮膜。这种本能反映了心智对不可化约的静态确定性的盲目渴望——它试图把每一段流动的因果，都实体化为一个脱离观察者的、安放在“无处之境”中的冷酷积木。

**第二个陷阱，则是后现代哲学式的“关系解构迷宫”（Deleuzian Dissolution）。**
当一些哲学家意识到经典语言与形式逻辑无法容纳“关系先于实体”时，他们感到绝望，转而求助于吉尔·德勒兹（Gilles Deleuze）那些晦涩奇诡的玄学黑话——“生成”、“块茎”、“纯粹差异”、“内在性平面”。他们宣称常识语言已经彻底失效，并暗示现实已经瓦解为一种无法被锚定、不可名状、缺乏任何稳定因果底座的虚无之雾。

这两个陷阱各执一端，却犯了同一个根本错误：
* 实体崇拜者为了捍卫“客观实在”，把关系硬性虚构成了僵死的孤立实体；
* 语言解构者为了打破“实体幻觉”，把坚固的现实生活消解成了诗意的玄学泡沫。

难道除了“孤立的实体积木”与“不可名状的流变迷雾”之外，人类心智就再无第三条清醒的认知路径了吗？

---

Yet when relational ontology is pressed to its logical conclusion, contemporary thought frequently stumbles into two complementary traps:

**The first trap is the classical materialist "idolatry of substance" (Substance Reification).**
Terrified of relational fluidity and ungrounded movement, classical habits of thought insist on stuffing every relational phenomenon back into a billiard-ball box. If gravitation exhibits mutual curvature, theorists demand a mechanical particle named the "graviton"; if spacetime displays tension, it is visualized as an objective rubber sheet. This instinct reflects the mind's craving for an observerless certainty—attempting to freeze living, dynamic interaction into immutable building blocks situated in a fictitious "View from Nowhere."

**The second trap is the postmodern "labyrinth of dissolution" (Deleuzian Dissolution).**
Recognizing that classical predicate logic struggles when relations precede objects, thinkers like George Webster despair of ordinary language and logic entirely, urging a retreat into the esoteric lexicon of Gilles Deleuze—"becoming," "rhizomes," "pure difference," and "planes of immanence." They declare that everyday comprehension is paralyzed, leaving reality floating in an elusive, poetic mist stripped of all concrete causal footing.

Both traps make the same foundational blunder:
* The substance idolater, desperate for permanence, invents mythical, independent essences;
* The linguistic deconstructionist, delighted by fluidity, dissolves the concrete, recalcitrant texture of everyday existence into semantic mist.

Must we truly choose between naive billiard balls and Deleuzian mystification?

## 三、 调和的支点：对象即稳定化的关系 / 3. The Reconciling Pivot: Objects as Stabilized Relationships

打破这一困局的解构利刃，质朴而无可辩驳：
**对象，就是被稳定化的关系。（Objects are stabilized relationships.）**

这句话同时化解了实体拜物教与后现代解构迷宫：
1. **面向实体崇拜者**：世界上根本不存在脱离关系、具有原初神圣本质的“独立客体”。任何一个被称为“物体”的东西，拆解到底，都不过是一组致密、互耦、循环运转的动态关系网络。
2. **面向虚无解构者**：关系绝不是随风飘散的幻觉。关系之所以能呈现为坚硬的宏观实体，是因为**这组关系在物理演化中实现了高度的因果自洽、动力学不变性与结构稳定**。

什么东西能够稳定一段关系？**因果摩擦、守恒定律与热力学代价。**

* 一块躺在地上的花岗岩，并不是无源自存的形而上学实体；它是微观原子之间电磁共价键、强相互作用力与晶格排列所形成的**高度稳定化的因果交互簇**。当你伸出脚踢它时，它之所以具有坚硬的“物体感”，是因为这套微观关系在受到宏观扰动时展现出了极高的一致性抗力——地面向上支撑，分子间斥力向外排斥，你的神经末梢承担着受阻的能量代价。
* 一个电子，并不是一颗悬挂在虚空中的微型小球；它是量子场在局域交互中所展现出的一组关于电荷、自旋与质量的**守恒性稳定关系**。
* 一枚通行的货币，并不是一块具有神秘购买力的金银金属；它是整个社会生产体系、信用契约与法权暴力在长久博弈中凝结而成的**高度稳定化的交易关系**。

实体从来不是原初的存在；**实体，是关系在穿过无数次碰撞、扰动与筛选之后，所沉淀出来的“不变量”（Invariants）。**
当一组关系的内部相干性足够高，以至于在面对外部交互时表现出持续的抵抗力与可预测性时，心智便将其识别为一个“对象”。

---

The razor that cleanly resolves this impasse is simple and precise:
**Objects are stabilized relationships.**

This single formulation dissolves both substance idolatry and linguistic mystification:
1. **Against the substance idolater**: There is no such thing as an uncaused, isolated essence existing outside of relational interaction. Every entity ever labeled an "object" reveals itself under inspection to be an interconnected, tightly coupled network of dynamic processes.
2. **Against the deconstructive nihilist**: Relations are not ephemeral fictions. A relational bundle presents itself as a solid macroscopic object because **it has attained dynamic equilibrium, structural invariance, and causal stability against external perturbation.**

What stabilizes a relationship? **Causal friction, conservation laws, and thermodynamic cost.**

* A granite rock resting on the mountain trail is not an uncaused metaphysical substance; it is a **densely stabilized cluster of electromagnetic lattices, nuclear bonds, and gravitational couplings**. When you kick it, it resists with stubborn firmness because its internal relational network maintains invariant structural integrity under stress. The ground pushes back, the molecular bonds hold, and your foot bears the irreversible energetic consequence.
* An electron is not a little hard sphere drifting through vacuum; it is a **stabilized set of invariant relationships** between charge, mass, and field excitations.
* A currency note is not a piece of paper possessing mystical intrinsic value; it is a **stabilized economic relationship** crystallized out of institutional credibility, legal enforcement, and market consensus.

Substances are not the primordial bedrock of nature. **Substances are invariants precipitated when dynamic relationships survive repeated trials of causal friction.**
Whenever a web of relations possesses sufficient internal coherence to preserve its boundaries and predictable responses under interaction, the mind encounters it as an "object."

## 四、 名词作为认知脚手架 / 4. Nouns as Cognitive Scaffolding

既然宇宙在根本上是一个由关系演化织就的网络，为什么人类的语言与思维总在第一反应中偏爱“名词”与“实体”？

**答案在于认知带宽的有限性，以及心智为了降低交互阻力而发明的“认知脚手架”（Cognitive Scaffolding）。**

人类的认知系统不可能在每一微秒都去实时计算花岗岩内部数万亿个原子的量子跃迁波函数，也不可能在每一次买咖啡时都去重新推演纸币背后复杂的现代信用与地缘政治博弈。如果心智必须时时刻刻去直面底层浩瀚无垠的关系迷宫，认知将在瞬间被无限的信息吞吐所瘫痪。

因此，心智演化出了一种极为精巧的计算压缩技术：**用“名词”封装“稳定化的关系”。**

* 我们把电磁力晶格的稳定排斥打包，贴上一个标签叫**“桌子”**；
* 我们把重力、摩擦与分子结合的坚固抗力打包，贴上一个标签叫**“石头”**；
* 我们把庞大的社会交换规则打包，贴上一个标签叫**“货币”**；
* 我们把数亿行代码在硅基电路上的稳定执行协议打包，贴上一个标签叫**“操作系统”**。

名词是人类发明的工程降阻脚手架。它允许我们在宏观生活中迅速调取这些高度稳定、极难破裂的关系簇，并将其作为现成的基石展开下一步行动。

但教条与幻觉的滋生，正是始于**把脚手架当成了现实的本体天花板**：
当我们日复一日地使用“桌子”、“石头”、“货币”这些名词时，我们渐渐患上了健忘症——我们忘记了这个词原本只是一个用来指代致密稳定关系的便利代码，反而误以为现实世界中真的存在某种名为“石头性”或“货币性”的独立幽灵。
所谓实体，不过是人类在认知交互中为了节约算力而定格下来的稳定快照。

---

If reality is fundamentally a dynamic tapestry of relational processes, why does human language and thought instinctually default to "nouns" and "static things"?

**The explanation lies in finite computational bandwidth, and the cognitive scaffolding forged by the mind to navigate friction.**

No living consciousness can afford to calculate the quantum wave equations of quadrillions of molecular bonds each time it reaches for a cup of tea, nor can a trader re-derive the geopolitical and monetary histories of civilization before accepting a banknote. If an organism were compelled to hold the untamed relational matrix in its active working memory, cognitive collapse would be immediate.

To function, consciousness developed an exquisite compression technology: **packaging dense, stabilized relationships into "nouns."**

* We compress the rigid electromagnetic lattice resisting our weight, and stamp it with the token **"table."**
* We compress the persistent gravitational and mineral cohesion of the mountain, and stamp it with the token **"stone."**
* We compress thousands of years of trade conventions and legal treaties, and stamp it with the token **"currency."**
* We compress billions of logic-gate transitions in silicon, and stamp it with the token **"operating system."**

Nouns are functional cognitive scaffolding. They allow an active agent to treat a highly resilient, predictable cluster of relations as a single modular component, lowering cognitive drag so higher-order action can proceed.

Pathology begins when **the scaffolding is deified into an absolute ontological idol**:
Using these shorthand nouns across generations, human thought suffers a peculiar amnesia. We forget that the noun was merely an operational tag for an invariant relational cluster, and instead begin to imagine that the cosmos is populated by eternal, observerless essences called "rock-ness" or "value-in-itself."
What we call an "object" is simply a cognitive snapshot taken of a stabilized relationship.

## 五、 第一人称视角的基石与解释的终点站（`+1`） / 5. The First-Person Ground and the Terminal Stop (`+1`)

最后，必须将关系量子力学与非大一统理论（Not a ToE）在此处达成最坚固的终极闭环。

在标准的学术讨论中，关系量子力学常常留下一个致命的理论漏洞：
如果一切性质都只是“相对于另一个系统”而存在（比如电子 A 相对于光子 B，温度计相对于粒子），而这些系统本身又处于平等的第三人称对称性中，那么整个世界就会沦为一个**毫无锚点的“关系镜像长廊”**——A 参照 B，B 参照 C，C 参照 A，所有关系悬浮在一个无人居住的虚空之中，最终再次滑回那个假想的、脱离观察者的“无处之境”。

非大一统理论为这一长廊注入了坚实的地基：
**现实绝不等同于客观物理现实；物理现实只是实在被稳定化的宏观症状。实在在最根本的本体论上，永远且仅仅是第一人称视角的实在。**

关系绝不是漂浮在第三人称虚空中的抽象张量；**任何一段真实的物理关系，都必须在具体的当事者身上、在第一人称的交互原点处被实际测量、被亲身体验、被赋予权重。**

* 当你用手按在桌面上，那一瞬间电磁斥力所构成的“对象”，并不是宇宙计算机在后台跑出的一行孤立代码；它是你的神经、你的肌肉、你的当下意向与那张桌子在第一人称视界里撞击出的真实摩擦。
* 所谓“客观物理世界”，正是千千万万个第一人称主体，在各自的视界中对这些稳定化关系进行交互测试后，所汇总出的一套高度自洽的跨主体公约数。
* 并不存在所谓“客观实在”与“主观体验”的二元割裂——第一人称视界的展开，就是实在本身；而物体，正是这个展开过程中沉淀出的坚固岛屿。

因此，解释的终点站从来不在无限嵌套的关系镜子迷宫里，更不在德勒兹那些令人眩晕的哲学黑话中。
解释彻底终结于**个体在第一人称当下所遭遇的不可逆物理介入与代价承担（`+1`）**：
当你面对一块坚硬的石头，你不需要去念叨“生成与块茎”的咒语；你知道它是一段稳定化的因果关系，你清醒地认识到绕过它需要消耗热量、撞击它需要承受痛楚。

看清了“对象是被稳定化的关系”，我们便同时从两个方向获得了自由：
我们既不再向任何僵死的宏观权威与制度图腾顶礼膜拜（因为知道它们不过是历史中暂被稳定化的关系网络，随时可以在新的因果介入中被解构）；我们也不会陷入相对主义的轻浮虚无（因为知道任何想要改变关系的企图，都必须在第一人称的现实中付出真实的物理代价）。

在流动的因果中看清它的稳定之锚，在坚固的物体面前洞悉它的关系本质——这便是不迷信万物理论、从容立足于生命现场的真正清醒。

---

Here, the reconciliation between Relational Quantum Mechanics and the core framework of *Not a ToE* locks firmly into place.

In conventional academic treatments, relational physics harbors a fatal vulnerability:
If properties exist only "relative to another system" (electron A relative to photon B, measurement apparatus relative to an atom), and all systems are treated with sterile, third-person symmetry, reality risks degenerating into an **unanchored hall of mirrors**—A defines B, B defines C, C defines A, with the entire relational web floating in an uninhabited, abstract void. This inadvertently resurrects the very "View from Nowhere" it sought to overthrow.

*Not a ToE* grounds this floating web upon bedrock:
**Reality is not identical to physical reality. Physical reality is merely the stabilized macrosymptom of reality. Reality, at its irreducible ground, is fundamentally first-person perspective reality.**

Relations do not hover in an observerless vacuum. **A relation is only ever actualized, experienced, tested, and weighted at an active, localized, first-person center of interaction.**

* When you press your hand against the desk, the "objecthood" that arises from molecular repulsion is not an abstract line of code running in a cosmic computer. It is the immediate, tactile resistance occurring between your living intent, your muscles, and the invariant lattice in the immediate present.
* The "objective physical universe" is simply the inter-subjective consensus negotiated across countless first-person perspectives verifying the same stabilized invariants.
* There is no split between "objective reality" and "subjective reality." The first-person perspective *is* the ground where reality takes place; objects are the stable islands precipitated out of its continuous unfolding.

Thus, the terminal stop of explanation does not wander off into an infinite regress of relations between relations, nor does it require refuge in Deleuzian obscurity.
Explanation reaches its absolute anchor in **the immediate, irreversible physical intervention and cost-bearing of the first-person agent (`+1`).**
When confronted with a massive boulder blocking your path, you do not need mystical incantations about "becoming." You know it is a stabilized causal relationship: bypassing it costs caloric energy, colliding with it exacts physical pain.

Understanding that **objects are stabilized relationships** grants a double sovereignty:
We are freed from the tyranny of substance idolatry—knowing that monuments, rigid hierarchies, and ossified systems are not eternal, uncaused absolutes, but provisional arrangements that can be reconfigured by deliberate intervention.
And we are shielded from frivolous nihilism—knowing that any desire to alter a relationship must pay the non-zero energetic and causal fare in the living present.

To perceive the stable invariant within the dynamic flow, and to see the relational architecture within the solid stone—this is what it means to drop the scaffolding of grand theories, and step with open eyes into the real.
