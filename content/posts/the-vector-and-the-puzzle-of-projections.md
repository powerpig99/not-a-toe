# 向量与投影的迷局 / The Vector and the Puzzle of Projections

*注意力该落在向量本身，投影只是参照 / Attention belongs on the vector itself, and projections are only references.*

一生里最耗精力的两件事，常常是用言语证明自己的能力，和靠解释去修正别人的投射。这两件事让人疲惫而徒劳，是因为它们共用一个因果倒置：把沟通当成能力的公关部，把“别人怎么看”当成行动的校准尺。这个错位常裹着务实的外衣，以为能力必须被看见才算存在，否则就会落进叙事的真空，任人定义。顺着因果推到底，这不只是南辕北辙，还是对自身心智成长的一次自我截断。

The two things that use up the most energy in a life are often proving one's ability with words and trying to correct other people's projections with explanations. Both are exhausting and futile because they share one causal inversion: treating communication as the public-relations department of ability, and "how others see it" as the gauge for calibrating action. The misplacement often wears practical clothing, the belief that ability only exists once it is seen, and that otherwise it falls into a narrative vacuum for anyone to define. Followed causally to the end, this is not only heading south to reach the north; it cuts off the growth of one's own Mind.

## 一、 群体的最大公约数 / 1. The Group's Greatest Common Divisor

向外寻求群体认可、在公共空间里“证明自己”，为什么会伤到一个人的成长？从信息论与拓扑的角度看，这是一连串的降维压缩。第一次压缩在自身：一个心智是动态演化的、多维的系统，远超公共象限，虽然有限，却可以不断扩展；而公共空间是扁平的，只结算标签、头衔、公开表态与孤立的人设。为了在那里被看见，就得亲手把高维的自身修剪成一个合乎某种标准的低维切片。[维度即投影](../dimensions-are-projections/)：把高维系统压进低维截面，必然伴随大量自由度与动态细节的不可逆丢失。

Why does seeking a group's approval, "proving oneself" in public space, harm a person's growth? From the angle of information theory and topology, it is a chain of dimensional compressions. The first compression happens to oneself: a Mind is a dynamically evolving, many-dimensional system, far larger than the public quadrant, finite yet always extendable; public space is flat and settles only labels, titles, public statements and isolated personas. To be seen there, the high-dimensional self has to be trimmed by one's own hand into a low-dimensional slice that fits some standard. [Dimensions Are Projections](../dimensions-are-projections/): pressing a high-dimensional system into a low-dimensional section necessarily loses a great many degrees of freedom and dynamic detail, irreversibly.

第二次压缩在群体：寻求的常常不是一个人的认可，而是一群人的认可。这个已经压缩过的切片，被抛进由许多心智构成的棱镜阵列；[框架的边界](../the-boundary-of-the-frame/)：每个心智都站在自己的认知边界、利益与情绪上，对它再作一次截断与局部投射。第三次是求交集的坍缩：为了让整个群体“普遍理解并认可”，只能在成百上千个彼此矛盾、高度失真的投射回流里，去找它们的最大公约数。成百上千个低维投影的共同交集，最后会缩成一个没有丰富度的、干瘪的孤立点。

The second compression happens in the group: what is sought is often not one person's approval but a group's. The already compressed slice is thrown into an array of prisms made of many Minds; [The Boundary of the Frame](../the-boundary-of-the-frame/): each Mind stands on its own cognitive boundary, interests and emotions and truncates and locally projects it once more. The third is the collapse into an intersection: to have the whole group "generally understand and approve", the only option is to look, among hundreds of mutually contradictory, heavily distorted projections flowing back, for their greatest common divisor. The common intersection of hundreds of low-dimensional projections shrinks in the end to a dry, isolated point with no richness at all.

这个交集里剩下的，是最浅的道德标签、最廉价的情绪共鸣、最陈旧的常识，和不碰任何现实摩擦的安全的话。把这个退化的交集接进自己的决策，用来指导下一步，就是让一群人的平均值替自己做了一次切除：原本能敏锐探测阻力的系统，被换成了一面只回响平均值的墙。这说的是一种做法，不是一群人；同一个心智，有时站在求认可的一端，有时站在给认可的一端。

What remains in that intersection are the shallowest moral labels, the cheapest emotional resonance, the stalest common sense, and safe words that touch no friction of reality. Wiring this degenerate intersection into one's decisions to guide the next step lets a group's average perform an amputation on oneself: a system that could sense resistance keenly is replaced by a wall that echoes only the average. This describes a practice, not a set of people; the same Mind sometimes stands at the end that seeks approval and sometimes at the end that gives it.

## 二、 零信息的沟通 / 2. Communication at Zero Information

对交集的执着，催生了人际交往里最常见的一种信息损失：把寻求认同当成沟通的目标。[信息是什么](../what-information-is/)：在香农的信息论里，有效的信息等于不确定性的消除，一个可以被预料到底的信号，信息量为零。

The fixation on the intersection produces the most common loss of information in human exchange: taking the search for agreement as the goal of communication. [What Information Is](../what-information-is/): in Shannon's information theory, effective information is the removal of uncertainty, and a signal that can be fully anticipated carries zero information.

沟通的两端，常常都在求共鸣、找同频、要确认。可两个心智重叠的那部分，只是彼此已有的先验知识；重复多少次、情绪多饱满，都没有多出一比特，像用不同的声调播放同一盘旧磁带。一遇分歧，防御就启动，不是急着驳斥，就是当成冒犯；可那些刺痛人的、反直觉的、与既有模型冲突的“不同”，恰恰是唯一带着意外值的样本。说的一方只敢说大家都能接受的话，听的一方只愿听早已合乎自己偏见的话，信道就被焊死在最大公约数上，穿过它的只能是信息量趋近于零的话。

Both ends of a conversation are often seeking resonance, looking for the same wavelength, asking for confirmation. But the part where two Minds overlap is only the prior knowledge each already has; however many times it is repeated and however full the feeling, not one more bit is added, like playing the same old tape in different tones of voice. At the first disagreement defences go up, either rushing to refute or taking offence; yet the "differences" that sting, run against intuition and clash with the existing model are precisely the only samples that carry surprise value. When the speaker dares say only what everyone can accept and the listener wants to hear only what already fits their biases, the channel is soldered shut at the greatest common divisor, and what passes through it can only be words whose information approaches zero.

沟通是一次贝叶斯更新。目的不是带着现成的答案去盖章认证，而是拿着自己未完成的模型，去收集预料之外的证据。有价值的交流对象不是一面点赞的镜子，而是一台独立的探测器，带回视线之外的异质数据。沟通的意义不在于确认“我们一样”，而在于接住那些打破既有预测的不同：反直觉的阻力，被忽略的边界条件，甚至刺痛模型的否定。[没有普渡，只有自渡](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/)：放下强求同频的念头，只有“不同”能打破闭环，逼系统调整先验分布，完成决策循环的下一次迭代。

Communication is a Bayesian update. Its aim is not to bring ready answers to be stamped and certified but to bring one's own unfinished model and gather evidence that was not expected. A worthwhile partner in exchange is not a mirror that likes everything but an independent detector bringing back foreign data from outside one's line of sight. The point of communicating is not to confirm "we are the same" but to catch the differences that break existing predictions: counter-intuitive resistance, overlooked boundary conditions, even a denial that stings the model. [No Universal Salvation, Only Self-Deliverance](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/): with the urge to force the same wavelength set down, only "difference" can break the closed loop, push the system to adjust its prior distribution, and complete the next iteration of the decision cycle.

## 三、 金句与因果的遮蔽 / 3. Maxims and the Eclipse of Causality

对零信息交集的依赖，也统治着公共的智力消费。最受欢迎的，常是打着“底层逻辑”或“人生智慧”旗号的金句。讲者多半是真诚的：在讲者的第一人称里，那个高度抽象的名词背后，实时挂着几十年撞出来的高维参数与隐性经验；听者接到的，却只是一个剥掉了边界条件与变量的低维空壳。以为记住几个抽象概念就掌握了规律，正落进[从形式迷思到逻辑闭环](../cong-xing-shi-mi-si-dao-luo-ji-bi-huan/)剖开的那个陷阱。

Dependence on the zero-information intersection also governs public intellectual consumption. What is most popular is often the maxim flying the flag of "first principles" or "life wisdom". The speaker is usually sincere: in the speaker's first person, behind the highly abstract noun hang, live, the high-dimensional parameters and tacit experience of decades of hard collisions; the listener receives only a low-dimensional shell stripped of its boundary conditions and variables. Believing that memorizing a few abstract concepts means grasping the pattern falls into exactly the trap that [From the Myth of Form to the Closed Loop of Logic](../cong-xing-shi-mi-si-dao-luo-ji-bi-huan/) dissects.

底层从来不是一组名词，底层就是因果逻辑本身。背下一个高级概念，并不免除做功的代价；顺着逻辑一环扣一环去推因果，老老实实面对阻力与反馈，才是认知提升的路。跳过具体参数的推演、想靠兜售或膜拜抽象概念直接跃迁，是用低维的幻象去廉价地抵付因果。

The ground floor was never a set of nouns; the ground floor is causal logic itself. Reciting an advanced concept does not waive the cost of the work; tracing causes link by link along the logic and facing resistance and feedback honestly is the way understanding grows. Skipping the working-through of concrete parameters and trying to leap ahead by selling or worshipping abstract concepts is paying causality cheaply with a low-dimensional illusion.

## 四、 维度本无高低 / 4. Dimensions Have No Rank

这里说的本体论，不是又一个与其他理论并列竞争的形而上学流派，而是本体论之本：所有理论、观察与现实都要经过的因果。它不是作为前提被接受下来的：否认它，就得作出一次区分，而这次区分本身就是它在运作；任何被指着、持有或建起的东西，都已经是这一活动的痕迹。所以任何假说、模型或概念在建起之前，都要依赖一次离散的区分与做功；任何观察者在宣称一种世界观之前，都跳不出因果对自身行动的结算。

The ontology meant here is not one more metaphysical school competing alongside other theories but the onto of ontology: the causality that every theory, observation and reality passes through. It is not accepted as a premise: denying it requires drawing a distinction, and that distinction is itself its operation; anything pointed at, held or built is already a trace of this activity. So every hypothesis, model or concept depends, before it is built, on a discrete act of distinguishing and work, and no observer, before declaring a worldview, steps outside causality's settlement of their own actions.

正是在这一层上，心智的维度松开了：在本体的意义上，各自选择的维度没有世俗意义上的高低之分。每个心智都是一个多维的、远超公共象限、虽然有限却可以不断扩展的独立系统。一个人可以在与公共象限毫无重叠的领域里，数理的直觉，精微的内心觉察，对某种复杂秩序的独特体悟，建起深邃而自洽的心智模型；外界量不到它，甚至感知不到它，这丝毫不减它的丰富与完整。

It is at exactly this level that the dimensions of a Mind come loose: in the ontological sense, the dimensions each one chooses have no worldly rank of higher and lower. Every Mind is an independent, many-dimensional system, far larger than the public quadrant, finite yet always extendable. A person can build a deep, self-consistent model in a domain with no overlap at all with the public quadrant, mathematical intuition, fine inner awareness, a singular feel for some complex order; outsiders cannot measure it and may not even perceive it, and that takes nothing from its richness or wholeness.

找不到一根凌驾于现实之上、给不同心智维度打分的上帝视角坐标轴。[开放即一致](../openness-is-consistency/)：开放并不需要外在的许可；任何外部的评判，能触及的只是各自心智在特定象限里的交集投射。[折叠的连续谱](../the-continuum-of-the-fold/)：另一个心智只以痕迹到来，心智之间没有不经中介的通道；沟通只在异质的信号逼出一次代价不小的贝叶斯更新时才发生。把公共象限当成衡量自我价值的唯一法庭，交出因果的裁量权，从一开始就落进了[验证中因果权能的分配](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/)所剖开的那个倒置。

No god's-eye axis is found standing above reality to score the dimensions of different Minds. [Openness Is Consistency](../openness-is-consistency/): openness needs no outside permission, and any outside judgment touches only the intersecting projection of each Mind in a particular quadrant. [The Continuum of the Fold](../the-continuum-of-the-fold/): another Mind arrives only as traces, and there is no unmediated channel between Minds; communication happens only when a foreign signal forces a costly Bayesian update. Treating the public quadrant as the only court of self-worth and handing over the discretion of causality falls from the start into the inversion that [The Allocation of Causal Power in Validation](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/) dissects.

## 五、 原生价值与残留价值 / 5. Generative Value and Residual Value

世俗的成功、认可与财富，又是从哪里来的？查理·芒格说，得到想要之物最可靠的办法，是让自己配得上它。沃伦·巴菲特说，没有人愿意慢慢变富。不愿意慢慢变富，就是不肯承认因果的做功周期，想跳过过程，直接从虚空里抓取结果。这里的混淆，在原生价值与残留价值之间。原生价值是直接作用于世界的穿透力：能不能解决问题，能不能交付确定性，能不能在混沌里建起秩序。残留价值是结果在外部世界一再迭代、碰撞、外溢之后，社会为了购买这份确定性而回馈的沉淀：货币，声誉，职位，赞赏。[价值、货币与财富的生成机制](../the-generative-mechanics-of-value-money-and-wealth/)：财富是活的生成能力的沉淀，不是驱动因果的动力源。

Where then do worldly success, recognition and wealth come from? Charlie Munger said that the surest way to get what you want is to deserve it. Warren Buffett said that nobody wants to get rich slowly. Not wanting to get rich slowly is refusing to accept causality's cycle of work, trying to skip the process and grab the result out of the void. The confusion here is between generative value and residual value. Generative value is the penetrating force acting directly on the world: whether problems get solved, certainty delivered, order built out of chaos. Residual value is the sediment society returns, to buy that certainty, after results have iterated, collided and spilled over in the outside world: money, reputation, position, praise. [The Generative Mechanics of Value, Money, and Wealth](../the-generative-mechanics-of-value-money-and-wealth/): wealth is the sediment of living generative capability, not the power source that drives causality.

“被看见”本身，对有能力的一方没有增量价值；它的价值在看见的一方，是观察者打破了自身的信息不对称、得到了外部的确定性。商业社会愿意把残留价值转过来，是因为原生价值先被建了起来。[时间是因果的不可逆方向与离散步长](../time-is-causality-not-a-dimension/)：能量守恒不容协商。把“被看见”与“得到残留价值”当成行动的前提，是因果的倒置，想抹去因与果之间的跨度，在真空里造一个无因之果。

"Being seen" adds no value in itself to the one with the capability; its value lies with the one who sees, the observer who has broken their own information asymmetry and gained outside certainty. The commercial world is willing to transfer residual value because generative value was built first. [Time Is the Irreversible Direction and Discrete Step of Causality](../time-is-causality-not-a-dimension/): conservation of energy is not negotiable. Making "being seen" and "gaining residual value" the precondition for action inverts causality, trying to erase the span between cause and effect and make an uncaused effect in a vacuum.

## 六、 调长度还是调角度 / 6. Adjusting Length or Adjusting Angle

理解了投影之后，容易退回另一种功利的执念：既然公共象限只认投影，是不是该精密计算，把自己的向量刻意偏向大众？偏向与凑手，在这里说不通。人人都说自己在“自我迭代”，分水岭在于：迭代的是向量的长度，还是向量的角度？

Having understood projection, it is easy to fall back into another utilitarian fixation: since the public quadrant recognizes only projections, should the vector be carefully calculated and deliberately tilted toward the crowd? Tilting and fitting make no sense here. Everyone says they are "iterating on themselves"; the watershed is whether what is iterated is the vector's length or its angle.

一个向量在某一维度上的投影大小，取决于两个量：向量本身的模长，以及它与这一维度的夹角。只盯着有限维度的投影，看不出这两者的区别。模长很短的向量，只要拼命转角度、贴紧某一根功利的坐标轴，迎合即时的情绪，追逐短期的流量，逢迎权威的偏好，在那根轴上的投影立刻显得很长；模长很大的向量，哪怕带着自然的倾角，在同一根轴上的投影也一样大。只看有限维度的投影，分不清是在增长实力，还是在精明地调整姿势。这说的是两种迭代，不是两类人；同一个心智，两种都会做。

The size of a vector's projection on a given dimension depends on two quantities: the vector's own magnitude, and its angle to that dimension. Watching only the projections on a few dimensions, the two cannot be told apart. A vector with a short magnitude, if it strains to rotate and hug some utilitarian axis, catering to momentary moods, chasing short-term traffic, flattering authority's preferences, at once shows a long projection on that axis; a vector with a large magnitude, even at a natural slant, shows an equally large projection on the same axis. Looking only at projections on a few dimensions, there is no telling whether strength is growing or posture is being shrewdly adjusted. These are two kinds of iteration, not two kinds of people; the same Mind does both.

单纯调角度，是一场零和游戏。模长一分没涨，在坐标轴之间来回转，只是把有限的精力从一根轴挪到另一根轴。为了迎合不同维度的外部标准，频繁的姿态管理、社交表演与人设修剪又要耗掉大量的能量；这种高耗能的空转不增加原生价值，还常因精力耗竭让核心的模长加速萎缩。高维空间里可转的角度几乎无穷，于是“不断换姿势”、“追逐新赛道”、“在圈子之间切换人设”容易被当成丰富与成长；以为在飞速演化，其实只是在空转角度，能解决现实问题的核心模长，在一次次姿态的扭曲里悄悄退化。

Adjusting angle alone is a zero-sum game. The magnitude has not grown at all; turning back and forth between axes only moves a fixed supply of energy from one axis to another. Meeting the outside standards of different dimensions also takes constant posture management, social performance and persona trimming, at great cost in energy; this costly idling adds no generative value and often, through exhaustion, makes the core magnitude wither faster. Since the angles available for turning in high-dimensional space are nearly endless, "constantly changing posture", "chasing new tracks" and "switching personas between circles" are easily taken for richness and growth; what feels like rapid evolution is only idling in angle, while the core magnitude that could solve real problems quietly degrades through one twist of posture after another.

最隐蔽的错位，是把投影的维度当成向量本身。社会在财富、头衔、权力、大众赞誉这几根低维轴上标了刻度，人就以为自己是这根轴上的一个标量；为了让刻度好看，不惜砍掉其余无数个自由生长的维度，削足适履，活成一张没有纵深的二维剪影。

The most hidden misplacement is taking the dimensions of the projection for the vector itself. Society marks a scale on a few low-dimensional axes, wealth, title, power, public praise, and a person comes to believe they are a scalar on that axis; to make the reading look good, they cut off countless other freely growing dimensions, trim the foot to fit the shoe, and live as a two-dimensional silhouette without depth.

该用功的地方，是不断增加向量本身的模长，不断拓展投影的维度，从 N 到 N + 1，而不是把精力耗在既定投影轴的角度微调上。也不必刻意迎合任何维度：按实际的需要与内在的意图，自然调整向量的指向，让它在在意的维度上有合适的投影就够了。所有的投影都只是参考坐标，注意力要一直落在向量本身，因为那个向量就是自身。[向量、坐标系与自指心智](../the-mind-as-vector-and-coordinate-system/)把心智同时看作向量与坐标系，是这里的几何。

The work belongs in steadily increasing the vector's own magnitude and steadily extending the dimensions of projection, from N to N + 1, not in spending energy on fine adjustments of angle along fixed axes. Nor is there any need to cater deliberately to any dimension: adjusting the vector's direction naturally, by actual need and inner intention, so that it has a fitting projection on the dimensions that matter, is enough. Every projection is only a reference coordinate, and attention has to stay on the vector itself, because that vector is oneself. [The Mind as Vector and Coordinate System](../the-mind-as-vector-and-coordinate-system/) sees the Mind as both vector and coordinate system, and gives the geometry here.

## 七、 第一人称与归途 / 7. The First Person and the Way Back

通过投影这面镜子，可以间接体会自己大致呈现出怎样的轮廓；可有一件事动不了：第一人称是跳不出去的。[翻袋球与魔术戏法](../the-bank-shot-and-the-magic-trick/)：旁观者看到的，是球在台面上的两次折射与最后入袋的魔术结果；只有击球的一方，在第一人称的肌肉记忆里承受着几千小时做功的反馈。外界的投射、赞美、误解或诋毁，都发生在别人的感知与认知模型里；公共幕布上的影子，从来不是本体。

Through the mirror of projection, the rough outline one presents can be sensed indirectly; but one thing does not move: the first person cannot be jumped out of. [The Bank Shot and the Magic Trick](../the-bank-shot-and-the-magic-trick/): the onlooker sees the ball's two rebounds on the table and the magic result of the pot; only the one holding the cue bears, in first-person muscle memory, the feedback of thousands of hours of work. Outside projections, praise, misunderstanding or slander all happen in other people's perception and cognitive models; the shadow on the public screen was never the source.

想跳到第三人称去成为那个投影、替那个虚构的剪影当保镖，就是交出自己的主体性。[唯一自洽的无因之果](../the-only-coherent-uncaused-effect/)：在被登记的世界里找不到无因的奇迹，能接住的唯一无因之果是自己的选择，它是向现实注入自由变量、打破内部机械模拟的唯一开端。投影之下起作用的，仍是[个体抉择是唯一的因果杠杆](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/)所说的抉择。

Trying to jump into the third person to become that projection, acting as bodyguard to a fictional silhouette, is handing over one's own agency. [The Only Coherent Uncaused Effect](../the-only-coherent-uncaused-effect/): no uncaused miracle is found in the world as registered, and the only uncaused effect that can be caught is one's own choice, the one beginning that injects a free variable into reality and breaks the inner mechanical simulation. What operates beneath the projections is still the choice that [Individual Choices as the Only Causal Levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) names.

放下“证明自己”的冲动，沟通就恢复了作为意外信息探测器的锋利；放下对“被看见”的索求，能力就找回了作为杠杆的分量。在[自我价值感与所有权](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/)的立足点上，根系才扎得稳。让局部的感知对齐整个生成的场，[心智的几何学](../the-geometry-of-mind/)给出了这一成长的几何。

Setting down the urge to "prove oneself" restores communication's edge as a detector of surprising information; setting down the demand to "be seen" gives ability back its weight as a lever. Standing on [Ownership and Self-Worthiness](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/), roots hold firm. Aligning local perception with the whole generative field, [The Geometry of Mind](../the-geometry-of-mind/) gives the geometry of this growth.

不必去外面找尺度，也不必替公共幕布上扭曲的倒影做徒劳的修补。守住向量本身，在自己决定的维度里持续做功。[前因，后果](../qian-yin-hou-guo/)：因果的链条一以贯之，它结算的是做出来的东西；什么会到来、何时到来，它并不担保，落在身上的也不全由做功挣来。把本地的地址窗口当成整个宇宙，是投影错位在算术上的样子，[寄存器的算术](../the-arithmetic-of-the-register/)对此有所展开。

There is no need to look outside for a scale, nor to patch the distorted reflections on the public screen in vain. Keeping to the vector itself, the work goes on in the dimensions one decides. [Cause Before, Effect After](../qian-yin-hou-guo/): the chain of causality holds throughout, and what it settles is what was made; it does not promise what will arrive or when, and what falls to anyone is not all earned by work. Taking the local address window for the whole universe is what this misplacement of projection looks like in arithmetic, which [The Arithmetic of the Register](../the-arithmetic-of-the-register/) develops.
