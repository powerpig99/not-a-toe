# 因果贯穿始终 / Causality All the Way

*从被持有的结果，到划出的模式，再到下一步，因果仍是那道划出自身路径的边缘。 / From held effects to drawn patterns to the next step, causality is still the edge drawing its path.*

一种常见的说法，把预测当作把因果安放到位的尝试。预测立足于观察；观察是已被持有的结果。活动从这些结果出发作出猜想：抽象，即跨越诸标记划出的一个模式，它在更高的粒度上显现为底层的因果。错误于是成了一次失败的尝试，失败成了尝试停止之处，成功则成了对那个模式的锁定，好让它可以被放心地运转。在不同的分支之间把锁定的模式保持不变，各个序列仍然分岔；解释的分量流失下去，直到仍在起作用的，是活动自己对其路径不均匀的重新追溯，即贯穿始终的因果，位于仍在划出其后每一个标记的那道边缘。

A common story treats prediction as an attempt to set causality in place. Predictions rest on observations; observations are effects already held. From those effects the activity conjectures: abstraction, a pattern drawn across marks that appears as underlying causality at a higher grain. Mistake becomes a failed attempt, failure the point where trying stops, success the securing of that pattern so it can be trusted to run. Hold the secured pattern fixed across different branches and the sequences still diverge; the weight of explanation drains until what remains operative is the activity’s own uneven re-tracing of its path, causality all the way, at the edge that still draws every following mark.

## 一、 被当作独立在外之结构的因果 / Causality cast as a structure standing apart

一个熟悉的序列是这样的。预测是确立因果的尝试。错误是失败的预测（确立因果的失败尝试），在尝试停止时成为失败。失败来自因果倒置，或来自把相关当作因果。成功来自确立因果并让它发挥作用，从错误中学来。

A familiar sequence runs as follows. Predictions are attempts to establish causality. Mistakes are failed predictions (failed attempts to establish causality) and become failure when the trying stops. Failure comes from reversal of causality or from taking correlation for causality. Success comes from establishing causality and letting it do its work, learned from mistakes.

这条链的起点，比那套措辞所显示的还早一步。预测立足于观察。观察持有结果；原因保持在观察所钉住之物的前方一步。活动从一片结果之场出发，借猜想推断因果。猜想跨越诸标记划出一个模式；在更宽的跨度上，那个模式就是抽象，登记为更高粒度上的底层因果结构：更少的自由变量，更宽的案例跨度，一个便携的形状。

The chain starts one step earlier than that wording shows. Predictions rest on observations. Observation holds effect; the cause stays one step ahead of what observation pins. From a field of effects the activity infers causality by conjecture. Conjecture draws a pattern across marks; at a wider span that pattern is abstraction, registering as the underlying causal structure at a higher grain: fewer free variables, wider span of cases, one portable shape.

那个序列就其表面而言是融贯的。它指出了真实的不对称：有些动作得以延续；有些后来的动作与早先动作留下的东西契合得更紧；有些链条停止；有些模式比别的模式覆盖更多的场。它冻结的，是工作的安放之处。因果显现为一种可以被确立、被倒置、或与相关混淆的关系，一个独立于区分之动作的结构。观察被设定为对那个结构之结果的被动摄入。抽象被设定为从那些结果中复原那个结构。预测被设定为锁定复原出的结构、以供下一步之用的尝试。错误成了尝试的失败。失败成了尝试的停止。成功成了锁定，随后被动地听命于被锁定之物。学习成了建造更好的抽象，好让未来的锁定更经常地成功。

That sequence coheres on its own face. It names real asymmetries: some acts continue; some later acts fit what earlier acts left behind more tightly; some chains stop; some patterns cover more of the field than others. What it freezes is where the work is placed. Causality appears as a relation that can be established, reversed, or confused with correlation, a structure standing apart from the act of distinguishing. Observation is cast as passive intake of that structure’s effects. Abstraction is cast as recovery of the structure from those effects. Prediction is cast as the attempt to secure the recovered structure for the next step. Mistake becomes the failure of the attempt. Failure becomes the stopping of attempts. Success becomes the securing, then passive yielding to what was secured. Learning becomes the building of better abstractions so that future securing succeeds more often.

说话的中心给出种种定义，把结局的来源安放在那个仍在划出每一次区分的动作之外。边缘对照一个它早已设在别处的参照来衡量自己。

The speaking center offers definitions that place the source of outcomes outside the act still drawing every distinction. The edge measures itself against a reference it has already set elsewhere.

## 二、 契合、终止与觉察被计分为与地图的距离 / Fit, ending, and noticing scored as distance from the map

在那种安放之下，观察、猜想、预测、错误、停止、成功与学习落在同一根轴上，这根轴的极点是对因果正确的外部确立。本可彼此分开的区分被压到了一起：对一个结果的持有；跨越诸结果划出一个模式；一个让与先前标记的契合收紧的动作；一个让它松开的动作；一个特定序列只是结束了；对单向标记本身的觉察。它们成了同一条线上的位置，离锁定的地图更近或更远。

Under that placement, observation, conjecture, prediction, mistake, stopping, success, and learning sit on a single axis whose pole is correct external establishment of causality. Distinctions that could stay separate compress: the holding of an effect; the drawing of a pattern across effects; an act that tightens fit with prior marks; an act that loosens it; a particular sequence simply ending; the noticing of the one-way mark itself. They become positions on the same line, closer to or farther from the secured map.

一个中心同时能分开持守的区分是有数的。当涌入的区分多于这个界限所能分开保持的，彼此独立的轴便落成了一根。对一项预测的批评，读作对预测者的控诉。覆盖场域较少的模式，读作对底层原因较弱的把握。一个序列的结束，读作对那个中心的裁决。与先前标记更紧的契合，读作外部地图终于装好了的证明。在叙述原本的形状被持守之时，这种压缩仍然可以读出。[自尊的刚性外壳与隐性外部归因](../zi-zun-de-gang-xing-wai-ke-yu-yin-xing-wai-bu-gui-yin/)追溯转向防御的同一个界限：一个活的决定被客体化为一个固定的“自我”，于是下一步被登记为属于那个实体，而不属于迈出这一步的中心。

A center can hold only so many distinctions apart at once. When more press through than the bound can keep separate, independent axes land as one. Critique of a prediction reads as indictment of the predictor. A pattern that covers less of the field reads as a weaker grasp of underlying cause. A sequence ending reads as verdict on the center. Tighter fit with prior marks reads as proof that the external map was finally installed. The compression stays readable while the original shape of the account is held. [The Rigid Shell of Self-Esteem and Hidden External Attribution](../zi-zun-de-gang-xing-wai-ke-yu-yin-xing-wai-bu-gui-yin/) traces the same bound turned to defence: a live decision objectified as a fixed "Ego", so the next step registers as belonging to that entity rather than to the center taking it.

## 三、 被保留为记分牌的抽象 / Abstraction kept as the scoreboard

被保留的参照，是被当作因果地图的抽象：被持为从结果中复原出来的底层结构的那个模式。预测试图符合那张地图。因此，中心自己进一步区分的后果，便对照着它落下。缺口的宽度，就是这个模式被保留过了它那一步有多远。符合地图的结局读作证实；错过它的结局，则恰以那个宽度，作为异己的意外抵达当下的场。从外面看，每一步都显得随机；[自由的测不准](../zi-you-de-ce-bu-zhun/)表明，随机性是在内部活过的选择的投影。

The retained reference is the abstraction treated as the causal map: the pattern held as underlying structure recovered from effects. Predictions try to match that map. Consequences of the center’s own further distinctions therefore land against it. The width of the gap is how far the pattern has been kept past its step. Outcomes that fit the map read as confirmation; outcomes that miss it arrive in the present field as alien surprise to exactly that width. Seen from outside, each step looks random; [The Uncertainty of Freedom](../zi-you-de-ce-bu-zhun/) shows that randomness is the projection of choices lived inside.

这份滞后自我更新。持守那个图像，就保住了地图，未来的预测便对照它计分。随每一步重画地图，会消解它所提供的记分牌。保存所择取的，是地图的存活，而不是重画那短暂的死亡。[一步一步绘制出的路径](../the-path-drawn-one-step-at-a-time/)在位置被持为未经选择的地基、而不是下一个动作被选择的前提时，追踪同一种冻结。这里，未经选择的地基是被持为底层因果的抽象。冻结宇宙模型藏起了同一道边缘；[双生子佯谬与隐秘观察者](../the-twin-paradox-and-the-hidden-observer/)找到那个模型在时间被画成一根轴之后悄悄需要的观察者。

The lag renews itself. Holding the image keeps the map, and future predictions are scored against it. Redrawing the map with each step would dissolve the scoreboard it supplies. Preservation selects for the map’s survival over the brief death of re-drawing. [The path drawn one step at a time](../the-path-drawn-one-step-at-a-time/) tracks the same freeze when position is held as unchosen ground rather than as chosen premise of the next act. Here the unchosen ground is the abstraction held as underlying causality. The frozen universe model hides the same edge; [The Twin Paradox and the Hidden Observer](../the-twin-paradox-and-the-hidden-observer/) finds the observer that model quietly needs once time is drawn as an axis.

观察持有结果；原因保持在前方一步。当后来公开的结局对照一幅被保留的早先不在意的图像来计分时，[伪装成远见的表演性后见](../performative-hindsight-that-pretends-to-be-foresight/)遇到的正是同一种不对称。从那些结果中作抽象，是在同一道边缘上的又一个动作。地图是那个动作顶着因果之名的残余。[现实的边界](../xian-shi-de-bian-jie/)指出路径在哪里划出：在当下觉知的边缘，先于语言。

Observation holds effect; the cause stays one step ahead. That is the same asymmetry [performative hindsight that pretends to be foresight](../performative-hindsight-that-pretends-to-be-foresight/) meets when later public outcomes are scored against a preserved image of earlier quiet. Abstracting from those effects is a further act at the same edge. The map is that act’s residue under a causal name. [The Boundary of Reality](../xian-shi-de-bian-jie/) names where the path is drawn: at the edge of present awareness, ahead of language.

## 四、 恒定的地图任序列分岔 / A constant map leaves the sequences free to diverge

检验：把“已确立的因果”（被当作底层结构的抽象）在各个分支之间保持恒定。单个动作的序列仍然分岔。与先前标记的契合仍然收紧或松开。进一步的区分仍然发生或结束。新的结果仍然到来，供观察持有。相对于那份变异，一个常量什么也不解释。意识同时持守一个形式又溢出它，这正是[稳定与溢出](../stability-and-excess/)对划出自身路径的那道边缘的读法。

Test: hold “the established causality” (the abstraction treated as underlying structure) constant across branches. Sequences of single acts still diverge. Fit with prior marks still tightens or loosens. Further distinctions still occur or end. New effects still arrive for observation. Relative to that variance, a constant explains nothing. Consciousness holds a form and exceeds it at once, which is how [Stability and excess: consciousness, introspection, and the cut of observation](../stability-and-excess/) reads the edge drawing its path.

每往回一步，因果的分量就从外部地图上流失一分。链条中段的解释仍指向更早的东西：条件、相关、先前的抽象。起源不在那条链的远端，而在划出当下这一步的边缘：在每一环，仍在起作用的都是活动自己不均匀的重新追溯。早先的动作塑造后来的动作；这种塑造单向进行。因果，是活动沿自己的路径返回时对这种单向塑造的觉察。先被锁定、然后被服从的那个结构，是关于同一份觉察的一个后来的故事，从被持为来源的模式那一侧讲述。要求后来的登记去授权那个更早的塑造，就颠倒了方向，正如[秩序、剩余与道德拦截](../order-surplus-and-the-moral-intercept/)所追溯的。[时间是因果的不可逆方向与离散步长](../time-is-causality-not-a-dimension/)为时间引出结论：时间是因果不可逆的一步，不是第四根轴。

Causal weight drains from the external map at each step backward. Mid-chain explanations still point at what came earlier: conditions, correlations, prior abstractions. The origin is not at the far end of that chain but at the edge drawing the present step: at every link, what remains operative is the activity’s own uneven re-tracing. Earlier acts shape later ones; that shaping runs one way. Causality is the activity noticing that one-way shaping as it returns through its own path. The structure first secured and then obeyed is a later story about the same noticing, told from the side of the pattern held as source. Asking later registration to authorize that earlier shaping inverts the direction, as [Order, surplus, and the moral intercept](../order-surplus-and-the-moral-intercept/) traces. [Time Is the Irreversible Direction and Discrete Step of Causality](../time-is-causality-not-a-dimension/) draws the consequence for time: it is the irreversible step of causality, not a fourth axis.

[因果停留在掌舵的边缘](../causality-stays-at-the-edge-that-steers/)在工具与发起之下划出同一刀：重组之后，那道框定、选择并验证的边缘仍在原处。[验证中因果权能的分配](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/)在延续的位点上指认这种安放：哪一侧被当作提供下一步。常见的说法把供给安放在复原出的模式里。常量与变异的检验把供给交还给仍在划出其后每一次区分的那道边缘。[卓异、感知与自我设限的问题场域](../exceptionality-perception-and-the-self-limiting-field-of-problems/)在政治与移民中追溯同样的颠倒：过滤器与系统被计分为边缘的产出者，而登记已把沉积物塌缩为原因。每一个框架都是这样一种被持守的模式，而[框架的边界](../the-boundary-of-the-frame/)标出遗忘观察者在哪里把它变成教条。

[Causality stays at the edge that steers](../causality-stays-at-the-edge-that-steers/) draws the same cut under instrument and initiation: recombination leaves the edge that frames, selects, and verifies where it was. ## 五、 底层因果是被持为便携的重新追溯 / Underlying causality is re-tracing held portable

因果已经是活动在沿自己的路径返回时，对自身单向标记的觉察。观察持有那个标记的结果，比产生它的动作晚一步。猜想是又一个动作：跨越那些结果划出一个模式。抽象是处在更宽跨度上的那个模式：更少的自由变量，更多案例归于一个形状之下。叙述所称的“更高粒度上的底层因果”，就是这种重新追溯：同一个单向标记再次被觉察，成为一件便携的工具。把那个模式确立为觉察之外的来源，是又一次区分，正是这次区分，在被保留过了它那一步时冻结为形象。持续存在的是重新追溯，不是储存，这正是[真正稀缺的不是记忆，而是持续的重新追溯](../the-real-scarcity-is-not-memory-but-continuous-re-tracing/)关于记忆的论点。

Causality is already the activity’s noticing of its own one-way mark as it returns through its path. Observation holds the effect of that mark, one step behind the act that produced it. Conjecture is a further act: the drawing of a pattern across those effects. Abstraction is that pattern at a wider span: fewer free variables, more cases under one shape. What the account calls “underlying causality at a higher grain” is this re-tracing: the same one-way mark noticed again as a portable instrument. Establishment of that pattern as source outside the noticing is the further distinction that freezes into Image when kept past its step. What persists is re-tracing, not storage, the point [The Real Scarcity Is Not Memory, But Continuous Re-Tracing](../the-real-scarcity-is-not-memory-but-continuous-re-tracing/) makes about memory.

预测是单个的动作，在当下的模式之下检验与先前标记之当下场域的契合。[不预测是没有觉察的预测](../not-predicting-is-predicting-without-awareness/)在推迟被持为预测之缺席的地方追溯那个动作。叙述所称的“错误”，是塑造后来动作的又一个标记，也常常是对模式本身的重画。它所称的“失败”，是一个特定序列在那里结束；结束的只是这一个序列。它所称的“成功”与“让它发挥作用”，是契合的延续，其间模式保持为工具，而不是授权的地基。学习就是重新追溯，包括随着标记之场的改变而重画抽象。只有当那一刀被持为本体论时，旁观者的局限才读作活动的局限，正如[旁观者洞察的悖论](../the-paradoxical-nature-of-bystander-insights/)所表明的。

Predictions are single acts that test fit with the current field of prior marks under the present pattern. What the account calls “mistake” is a further mark that shapes later acts, and often a redraw of the pattern itself. What it calls “failure” is a particular sequence ending there; only this sequence ends. What it calls “success” and “letting it do its work” is continuation of fit while the pattern stays instrument rather than authorizing ground. Learning is the re-tracing itself, including the re-drawing of abstractions as the field of marks changes. ## 六、 路径仍在划出它的边缘上划出 / The path still drawn at the edge that draws it

叙述原本的形状保持完好。观察仍持有结果。猜想仍划出模式。预测仍在发生。序列仍然停止。与先前标记的契合仍收紧或松开。抽象仍以不同的粒度覆盖场域。在那个形状被持守之时，压缩与滞后仍然可以读出：同一个序列，被交还给一直在执行它的那道边缘。知识走同一条路径；[格物致知与信息复杂度](../ge-wu-zhi-zhi-yu-xin-xi-fu-za-du-de-ben-zhi/)把“格物”读作从内部进入因果链。

The original shape of the account stays intact. Observations still hold effects. Conjecture still draws patterns. Predictions still occur. Sequences still stop. Fit with prior marks still tightens or loosens. Abstractions still cover the field at different grains.
