# 政客是责任扩散的显性症状 / Politicians Are Visible Symptoms of Responsibility Diffusion

*剩余控制权经由个体的选择而迁移，政客标记的是这种迁移，而不是它的源头。 / Residual control migrates through individual choices; the politician marks that migration, not its origin.*

伯尼·桑德斯宣布“我对不到一千名亿万富翁征收的 5% 财富税”时，那个“我的”已经登记下：剩余控制权已经被当作重新分配过了。这次重新分配并不是单由说话的人写下的；它由许多心智的决定共同构成，每一个心智都在独特约束下依然承担着自己先前选择的全部后果，却自由地选择把能动性安放到别处。觉得某件事不可取，又觉得这种感受为众人共有，便成了把责任推给他人、或推给被放在自己选择之外的事物的许可；推诿挪开的是目光，不是负荷。回望过来的，是这份外置的影子成了形；把矛头对准政客，等于把这个模式当成可以从构成它的那些决定之外去纠正的对象，循环也就延续下去。

When Bernie Sanders announces “My 5% wealth tax on less than 1,000 billionaires,” the possessive already registers that residual control is treated as reallocated. The reallocation is not authored by the speaker alone; it is constituted through the decisions of many minds, each freely choosing to place agency elsewhere while still bearing the full consequences of its own prior choices under unique constraints. Perceived undesirability, and the perceived sharing of that perception, arrive as license to cast blame onto others or onto things held outside one’s own choices; blame relocates the look, not the load. What stares back is the shadow of that externalization, materialized; targeting the politician continues the cycle by treating the pattern as an object to be corrected from a vantage outside the decisions that constitute it.

## 一、 所有权就是剩余控制权 / 1. Ownership Is Residual Control

所有权就是剩余控制权：把资源导向自己为自己选定的目的的能力。

Ownership is residual control: the capacity to direct resources toward ends one has chosen for oneself.

桑德斯写道：

Sanders writes:

> 我对不到一千名亿万富翁征收的 5% 财富税，可筹得超过 4.4 万亿美元，用来：
>
> 给一个四口之家的劳动家庭 12,000 美元  
> 实行全民托育  
> 把联邦医保扩展到牙科、视力与听力  
> 终结无家可归  
> 规定教师最低年薪 60,000 美元
>
> 让我们把它办成。

> My 5% wealth tax on less than 1,000 billionaires would raise over 4.4 trillion dollars to:
>
> Provide 12,000 dollars to a working family of 4  
> Enact universal childcare  
> Expand Medicare for dental, vision & hearing  
> End homelessness  
> Require a 60,000 dollar minimum salary for teachers
>
> Let's get it done.

那个“我的”引出的问题是：是谁决定了他可以把别人百分之五的钱当作自己的，用他自己的话说，“我的”税，仿佛那些人是取款机，用来支付他所列的那些项目？那个“我的”把这次重新分配登记为已经完成。私人资产只有在剩余控制权已经被当作移走之后，才会显得是一份常备的政治资源。这番话并没有完成转移。它点出的是一片场域：在那里，许多个人早已把这些资源的支配权，当作不再属于形成它们的那些心智。

The question the possessive raises is who decides he can treat five percent of other people’s money as his, in his own words “my” tax, as if those people were a cash machine for the programs he lists. The possessive registers the reallocation as already complete. Private assets appear as a standing political resource only after residual control is already treated as having moved. The speech does not perform the transfer. It names a field in which many individuals have already treated the directing of those resources as no longer belonging to the minds that formed them.

[自我价值感与所有权](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/)把所有权看作同一道界限的功能面：只有后果作为自己的东西重新进入，模型才会复利。这里，那个回路经过资源：支配资源，正是资源的后果作为自己的东西重新进入的方式。让它变薄，更新也就变薄，在形成这些资产的上游如此，在把支配权交出去的许多人那里也如此；“我的税”这种说法，是这份变薄在公共场合的登记。[私人残差与社会剩余的因果次序](../the-causal-order-of-private-residuals-and-social-surplus/)沿着创新的序列把这个动态展开：政治话术把私人财富当成一池现成的、可供再分配的静态存量，却看不到生产者从创新的社会剩余中只拿到约 2.2%，这份剩余诱因在上游被压缩，就会毁掉流向消费者的约四十五倍的剩余。同行审查与安全制度走的是另一条路，扩散的却是同一种责任：正式的责任交给了审查，后果仍落在做决定的心智上，[数字火箭、同行审查与因果闭环的稀释](../the-dilution-of-the-causal-loop-and-the-misallocation-of-agency/)说明了这一点。

[Ownership and Self-Worthiness](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/) names ownership as the functional face of the same bound: the model compounds only when consequences re-enter as one’s own. Here the loop runs through resources: directing them is how their consequences re-enter as one’s own. Thinning it thins the update, upstream where the assets were formed and among the many who hand the directing over; the language of “my tax” is the public register of the thinning. [The Causal Order of Private Residuals and Social Surplus](../the-causal-order-of-private-residuals-and-social-surplus/) unpacks this dynamic along the innovation sequence: political rhetoric treats private wealth as an existing static pool to be redistributed, missing that producers capture only about 2.2 percent of the social surplus from innovation, a residual inducement whose upstream compression destroys the roughly forty-five-fold surplus that reaches consumers. Peer review and safety regimes diffuse responsibility by another route: formal responsibility passes to the review while the consequences stay with the mind that decided, as [Digital Rockets, Peer Review, and the Dilution of the Causal Loop](../the-dilution-of-the-causal-loop-and-the-misallocation-of-agency/) shows.

## 二、 一次分配让剩余控制权迁移 / 2. One Allocation Migrates Residual Control

自我区分的活动在发生——无因、不息。称它为心智：那个已经在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。每一个个体心智都在做离散的区分，先前的动作塑造了后来的动作所能用的场域。因果，就是这份活动登记下自己痕迹的不对称，而不是一种可以搬进某个职位、某个政党，或某个被当作独立行动者的系统里的关系。

Self-distinguishing activity occurs — uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. Each individual mind draws discrete distinctions, and earlier acts shape the field available to later ones. Causality is that activity registering its own trace asymmetry, not a relation that can be relocated into an office, a party, or a system treated as an independent agent.

这次迁移是同一个分配，以许多公开的形式重复。去争取被许诺的好处，是把政治之手当作下一步的供应者。掂量抵抗的个人代价然后迁就，是把已经稠密的模式当成天气。宁愿把结果说成是别人做的，是把决定性的能动性放到做选择的心智之外。以为自己能托起另一个人，或能成为另一个人结果的作者，是把下一步放到一道无法替别人完成返回的鸿沟对面。[没有普渡，只有自渡的无限可能](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/)把这种完成看作范畴错误：痕迹可以被拾起，也可以不被拾起；没有哪个个体能替另一个人走完他的递归。正如[权力的镜像握手](../the-mirror-handshake-of-power/)所追溯的那样，在宏观尺度上显现为外部政治权威的东西，正是这两种互补选择的咬合——一方交出判断或祈求结果，另一方声称自己能写下这些结果——它借来的体量，只有在这双手还握在一起时才得以维持。别的形式也都是同一个分配。每一个都是在一个仍在自由决定、仍在独特约束下承担自己先前决定全部后果的心智处做出的离散动作。没有哪一个需要恶意。守住一个被偏好的参照，也就是“能动性在别处”，是自我形象一旦形成之后自己的指令。

The migration is one allocation, repeated in many overt forms. Seeking the promised benefit treats the political hand as the supplier of the next step. Weighing the personal cost of resistance and accommodating treats the already-densified pattern as weather. Preferring to describe the outcome as something done by others locates decisive agency outside the choosing mind. Treating oneself as able to lift another, or as author of another’s outcomes, locates the next step across a gap that cannot complete another’s return. [No Universal Salvation, Only Boundless Possibilities of Self-Salvation](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/) sees that completion as a category error: traces may be taken up or not; no individual finishes another’s recursion. As [The Mirror Handshake of Power](../the-mirror-handshake-of-power/) traces, what appears at the macroscopic scale as external political authority is the interlocking of these two complementary choices—one side handing over judgment or petitioning for outcomes, the other claiming to author them—whose borrowed mass lasts only while those handshakes are extended. Further forms remain the same allocation. Each is a discrete act at a Mind still freely deciding, still bearing the full consequences of its own prior decisions under unique constraints. None requires malice. Preservation of a preferred reference, that agency lives elsewhere, is the self-image’s own directive once formed.

这些决定在足够多的心智之间彼此相似，便造成剩余控制权有系统地迁移到政治之手中。迁移的是对这份控制的看法，也就是它被认为落在哪里；后果仍留在原处。[个体抉择是唯一的因果杠杆](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/)说的是这种唯一性：总量与条件无论以什么形式出现，都只为回报定下模式，却不撰写下一个动作。[公共支出不创造资本](../public-spending-does-not-create-capital/)是对已被取走之物的记账：先取走，后政治分配。这里是从认领者的语言而不是从账本去读同一个序列。[福利国家的幻象](../the-welfare-state-illusion-cost-diffusion-and-agency-atrophy/)从付钱的一方追溯同一种扩散的财政形式：成本被摊到千百万人身上，直到个人那条成本与后果的回路被切断。

These decisions, similar across enough minds, produce a systematic migration of residual control into political hands. What migrates is the treatment of that control, where it is held to live; the consequences stay where they were. [Individual Choices as the Only Causal Levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) states that exclusivity: whatever forms aggregates and conditions take, they pattern payoffs and do not author the next act. [Public Spending Does Not Create Capital](../public-spending-does-not-create-capital/) is the accounting of what has already been extracted: removal first, political allocation second. Here the same sequence is read from the claimant’s language rather than from the ledger. [The Welfare State Illusion](../the-welfare-state-illusion-cost-diffusion-and-agency-atrophy/) traces the same diffusion in its fiscal form, from the payer’s side: cost spread across millions until the personal loop between cost and consequence is cut.

## 三、 推诿挪开的是目光，不是负荷 / 3. Blame Relocates the Look, Not the Load

当先前决定的残余撞上一个被偏好的自我形象，作为“本不该落下的东西”到来时，把责任推给他人便登记为下一个可走的步子，对这个心智自己的结果如此，对别人的结果也如此。这一步不需要恶意。自我形象把这次错配的作者放到别处，以此保住自己。若这份残余正是回绝了局部抵抗的代价之后随之而来的限制，把那份对低代价路径的持续选择从账面略去，就会让原本由心智自己的选择生成的约束，登记为由外部强加的自由缺失，而附着在替代选项上的标价也就被读成了当时毫无选择的理由，正如[能动性、矛盾与权力的移置](../agency-contradiction-and-the-relocation-of-power/)所追溯的那样。

When residue of prior decisions arrives against a preferred self-image as something that should not have landed, shifting blame onto others registers as the next available move, for this Mind’s own outcomes and for the outcomes of others. The move requires no malice. The self-image preserves itself by locating the author of the mismatch elsewhere. Where the residue is the limitation that follows a declined local cost of resistance, leaving that ongoing selection of the lower-cost path out of the account makes a constraint generated by the mind’s own choice register as an externally imposed lack of freedom, and the price attached to an alternative reads as a reason no choice was available, as [Agency, Contradiction, and the Relocation of Power](../agency-contradiction-and-the-relocation-of-power/) traces.

后果已经是满的。归因并不能从中扣掉什么。变薄的只是那个本可以训练下一个离散动作的信号。负荷仍留在那个以先前决定塑造了场域的个人身上；被送到鸿沟对面的只是目光。[索维尔的表象洞察与根因之偏](../sowell-observed-the-surface-problem/)越过激励的说法，看到的是同一份早已满载的承担：没有系统性的解决办法；接受，登记的是一份从未被移走的负荷。[道德语言稀释自由复利的反馈](../moral-language-dilutes-the-feedback-that-scales-freedom/)说的是评价性的覆盖层隔在行动者与原始后果之间时的那种阻尼。这里的覆盖层就是推诿：给一个在做决定的心智之外的作者起个名字，好让早已满载的残余以陌生的面目到来。

The consequence is already full. Attribution does not subtract from it. What thins is only the signal that would train the next discrete act. The load stays with the individual whose prior decisions shaped the field; the look is sent across the gap. [Sowell Observed the Surface Problem](../sowell-observed-the-surface-problem/) reaches the same already-full bearing past the language of incentives: there is no systematic solution; acceptance registers a load that was never moved. [Moral Language Dilutes the Feedback That Scales Freedom](../moral-language-dilutes-the-feedback-that-scales-freedom/) is that damping when an evaluative overlay stands between agent and raw consequence. Here the overlay is blame: a name for an author outside the deciding Mind, installed so the already-full residue can arrive as alien.

## 四、 外置的影子回望过来 / 4. The Shadow of the Externalization Stares Back

觉得某件事不可取，是一次对着被偏好的自我形象的登记。觉得这种感受为众人共有，是又一次登记：共鸣被当成了凭据。两者合在一起，便成了把责任推给其他个人、或推给被放在自己选择之外的事物的许可。这份许可看上去再寻常不过。[当效果主宰了框架的叙事](../when-the-effect-authors-the-frame/)说的是同一种结构：感受到的那一层被赋予因果上的优先，可用的回应便围绕着管理它重新组织。这里被管理的是“不可取”，而感受的共有被当成了授权下一个动作的尺度。

Perceived undesirability is registration against a preferred self-image. The perceived sharing of that perception is further registration: resonance treated as warrant. Together they arrive as license to cast blame onto other individuals or onto things held outside one’s own choices. The license presents itself as ordinary. [When the Effect Authors the Frame](../when-the-effect-authors-the-frame/) is that topology when the felt register is granted causal priority, and available responses reorganize around managing it. Here the managed register is undesirability, and the sharedness of the feeling is taken as the measure that authorizes the next act.

这时回望过来的，政客、系统、某样东西，是这份外置本身的影子成了形。能动性被放到了做决定的心智之外；这次分配稠密成认领者与模式；它们如今又作为残余的表面作者回来，而那份残余正是这次分配早已产生的。正如[你的代码库即是记忆](../your-codebase-is-memory/)在层叠造物间所追溯的那样，公共纲领、竞选口号与法令档案是由稳定痕迹外置而成的代码库：它们留存着先前切分的形状，把个体心智扩散出去的分配映照回来，自身并不发起因果能动性。那个“别处”，是目光自己的残余，到来时有多陌生，鸿沟就有多宽。

What then stares back, the politician, the system, the thing, is the shadow of the externalization itself, materialized. Agency was located outside the deciding Mind; that allocation densified into claimants and patterns; those now return as the apparent authors of the residue that the allocation already produced. As [Your Codebase Is Memory](../your-codebase-is-memory/) traces across stacked artifacts, public platforms, campaign slogans, and statutory archives form an externalized codebase of stabilized traces that holds the shape of prior cuts and mirrors back the diffused allocations of individual minds rather than originating causal agency on its own. The elsewhere is the look’s own residue, arriving as alien as the gap is wide.

每一次进一步的推诿，都是进一步的分配。这个动作的残余，又作为下一份许可回来。循环就是这种自我更新，而不是第二台引擎。

Each further act of blame is a further allocation. The residue of that act returns as the next license. The cycle is that self-renewal, not a second engine.

## 五、 把模式称为系统，又是一次选择 / 5. Naming the Pattern a System Is a Further Choice

这个模式接着常被说成一个系统，或一股外部力量。这种说法本身就是同一个循环的又一转：选择把决定性的能动性放在做选择的心智之外。一旦接受这种说法，政客便被当成首要问题。归咎他们、撤换他们，或设计新的正式约束，成了意料之中的回应。每一种回应都延续了同一种扩散，因为它们都把这个模式当成可以从构成它的那些决定之外去纠正的对象。[如何在一瞬间修正你的整个人生](../how-to-fix-your-whole-life-in-one-split-second/)在个人回路上显出同一种延续：从外面的纠正，就是把归因留在外面，反馈的通道便从未重新打开。

The pattern is then commonly spoken of as a system or an external force. That description itself is a further turn of the same cycle: the choice to locate decisive agency outside the choosing minds. Once the description is accepted, politicians are treated as the primary problem. Blaming them, replacing them, or designing new formal constraints becomes the expected response. Each of these responses continues the same diffusion by treating the pattern as an object to be corrected from a vantage outside the decisions that constitute it. [How to Fix Your Whole Life in One Split Second](../how-to-fix-your-whole-life-in-one-split-second/) shows the same continuation in the personal loop: the correction from outside is the attribution left outside, so the feedback channel never reopens.

[剩余个人主义](../residual-individualism/)是同一种安放，换成了系统改革的场景：约束被留作中心参照，于是后来模式的变化被记成行动主义的功劳，而发起从未离开一个个接续的个人选择。[主权、信念与规制结构的生成](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/)是同一份残余的制度形态：持久的结构表达的是“主权可以交出去”这个先验；它们并不替个人发起。针对政客设计的新正式约束，是这个先验的又一面，把又一个操作抓手当成了纠正之处——这正是[能动性的错配](../the-misallocation-of-agency/)所追溯的那种倒置：把下游的规则或宪制保障，误当成保障互不侵犯与剩余控制权的活体引擎。

[Residual Individualism](../residual-individualism/) is that placement in the setting of system reform: the constraint kept as central reference so later pattern-shifts are scored as activism’s authorship while initiation never left successive individual choices. [Sovereignty, Belief, and the Generation of Regulatory Structures](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/) is the same residue in institutional form: durable structures express a prior that sovereignty can be handed over; they do not initiate in the individual’s place. New formal constraints designed against politicians are a further face of that prior, another operational lever treated as the site of correction—the exact inversion [The Misallocation of Agency](../the-misallocation-of-agency/) traces when downstream rules or constitutional safeguards are mistaken for the active engine that secures non-aggression and residual control.

## 六、 认领者标记的是表达，序列仍在更早处 / 6. The Claimant Marks the Expression; the Sequence Stays Earlier

只要个人继续做同一大类的选择，这个模式就会持续。当心智开始做不同的选择，收回剩余控制权、拒绝那种外置的说法时，它才会移动。政客作为被扩散的控制权的显眼认领者，会随着这种移动而调整，或被取代；这是移动的结果，而不是针对他们的纠正。他们准确地标示出这种集体的表达。凡是从把他们当成改变的主要目标开始的做法，都只是把扩散挪了个地方。

The pattern persists while individuals continue making choices of the same general kind. It shifts when minds begin making different choices, choices that reclaim residual control and decline the externalizing description. Politicians, as the conspicuous claimants of the diffused control, adjust or are displaced as that shift occurs, as its consequence rather than as a correction aimed at them. They are an accurate sign of the collective expression. Every approach that begins by making them the main target of change relocates the diffusion.

[进步叙事中的症候与原因](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/)是同一种倒置的归因：最响亮的残余来得最晚，却被装成杠杆，而发起的序列仍在更早的地方。这里响亮的残余就是政客。[赋权确立权力的集中](../empowerment-establishes-the-centralization-of-power/)是它的另一面：能力被说成来自被授权之手的馈赠，于是请求取代了行使，而杠杆原地不动。把那只手当成问题去针对，是同一种安放，只是符号反了过来。

[Symptom and Cause in the Narratives of Progress](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/) is the same reverse attribution: the loudest residue arrives late and is installed as lever while initiating sequences stay earlier. Here the loud residue is the politician. [Empowerment Establishes the Centralization of Power](../empowerment-establishes-the-centralization-of-power/) is the other face: capacity framed as a gift from the authorized hand, so petition replaces exercise while the lever stays. Targeting the hand as the problem is the same placement with the sign reversed.

有的，是个人心智之内的决定序列，被重复，或被修改。集体的表达随后而来。

What there is: the sequence of decisions inside individual minds, repeated or revised. The collective expression follows.
