# Politicians Appear as Visible Symptoms of Responsibility Diffusion

*Residual control migrates through individual choices; the politician marks that migration, not its origin.*

When a politician speaks of “my wealth tax” and treats private assets as a standing political resource, the language already registers that residual control has been reallocated. The reallocation is not authored by the speaker. It arises through the decisions of many minds — those who seek the benefits, those who weigh the personal cost of resistance and accommodate, and those who prefer to describe the outcome as something done by others. Once that description is accepted, politicians become the expected target of blame, replacement, and new formal constraints — each of which continues the same diffusion by treating the pattern as an object to be corrected from a vantage outside the decisions that constitute it.

## Ownership is residual control

Ownership is residual control: the capacity to direct resources toward ends one has chosen for oneself. When a politician claims a fraction of other people’s wealth as “mine” to allocate, the possessive registers the reallocation as already complete. Private assets appear as a standing political resource only after residual control has already moved. The speech does not perform the transfer. It names a field in which many centers have already treated the directing of those resources as no longer belonging at the loci that formed them.

[Ownership and self-worthiness](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/) is the functional face of the same bound: the model compounds only when consequences re-enter as own. Residual control is that closed loop under resource costume. Thinning it thins the update; the language of “my tax” is the public register of the thinning.

## Similar choices migrate residual control

Self-distinguishing activity occurs — uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. It occurs at loci. Each locus draws discrete distinctions; earlier acts shape the field available to later ones. Causality is that activity registering its own trace asymmetry — not a relation that can be relocated into an office, a party, or a system treated as independent agent.

The migration is three kinds of choice, repeated. Seeking the advertised benefit treats the political hand as the supplier of the next step. Weighing the personal cost of resistance and accommodating treats the already-densified pattern as weather. Preferring to describe the outcome as something done by others locates decisive agency outside the choosing mind. Each is a discrete act. None requires perversity. Preservation of a preferred reference — “they did this” — is the image’s own directive once formed.

These decisions, similar across enough minds, produce a systematic migration of residual control into political hands. [Individual choices as the only causal levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) is that exclusivity under any costume: aggregates and conditions pattern payoffs; they do not author the next act. [Public spending does not create capital](../public-spending-does-not-create-capital/) is the accounting of what has already been extracted: removal first, political allocation second. Here the same sequence is read from the claimant’s language rather than from the ledger.

## Naming the pattern a system is a further choice

The pattern is then commonly spoken of as a system or an external force. That description itself is a further individual choice: the choice to locate decisive agency outside the choosing minds. Once the description is accepted, politicians are treated as the primary problem. Blaming them, replacing them, or designing new formal constraints becomes the expected response. Each of these responses continues the same diffusion by treating the pattern as an object to be corrected from a vantage outside the decisions that constitute it.

[Residual individualism](../residual-individualism/) is that placement under system-reform costume: the constraint kept as central reference so later pattern-shifts are scored as activism’s authorship while initiation never left successive individual choices. [Sovereignty, belief, and the generation of regulatory structures](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/) is the same residue under institutional face: durable structures express a prior that sovereignty can be handed over; they do not perform a super-locus of initiation. New formal constraints designed against politicians are a further face of that prior — another hold treated as the site of correction.

## The claimant marks the expression; the sequence stays earlier

The pattern persists while individuals continue making choices of the same general kind. It shifts when minds begin making different choices — choices that reclaim residual control and decline the externalizing description. Politicians, as the conspicuous claimants of the diffused control, adjust or are displaced as that shift occurs. They correctly mark a systematic expression. Every approach that begins by making them the main target of change relocates the diffusion.

[Symptom and cause in the narratives of progress](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/) is delayed registration treated as engine: the loudest residue arrives late and is installed as lever while initiating sequences stay earlier and thinner. Here the loud residue is the politician. [Empowerment establishes the centralization of power](../empowerment-establishes-the-centralization-of-power/) is the invert: capacity framed as gift from the authorized hand, so petition replaces exercise while the lever stays. Targeting the hand as the problem is the same placement with the sign reversed.

There is the sequence of decisions inside individual minds, repeated or revised. The collective expression follows.
