# Consciousness Never Appears as Data Among Data

*Objective measurement holds only public effects; the activity that registers them remains one step ahead of every datum that would capture it.*

In a recent exchange on the biological basis of consciousness, one claim placed the strongest empirical evidence in what disappears under general anesthesia — Meyer-Overton correlation, anesthetic targets, quantum effects in neuronal microtubules — and another answered that evidence is data, scientific theories must be objectively testable, and without data there is only opinion. Both moves are real as instruments. Both freeze the same axis when pressed as final. Observations are theory-laden; physical measurements stay more stable across observers when ordinary objects neither change their behavior under the look nor observe in return — except where the grain of the hold reaches the quantum domain. Any account of human behavior that treats individual choice as less than the sole independent lever oversimplifies: it may re-trace events after the fact while foresight collapses. The deeper cut is structural. Data are effects. Consciousness is not the kind of thing that can appear among those effects as one more public datum. The demand that it do so freezes the leading edge as an object in a stratum built for non-reflexive observation — a category error built into how observation registers at all.

## Evidence freezes the edge as an object among objects

Self-distinguishing activity occurs — uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. [Causality all the way](../causality-all-the-way/) is that lag under prediction costume: conjecture draws patterns across held effects; the generating step stays ahead of every secured map. [Intelligence belongs only to the Mind](../intelligence-belongs-only-to-the-mind/) holds the prior: what can be engineered, scaled, or surveyed is residue; intelligence belongs only to the activity.

“Evidence is data” is exact inside domains where the description does not enter the described. A stratum is stable to the degree it is tested by everything against everything that can be observed and does not observe back at the level of abstraction at which it is observed. Ordinary physical objects meet that criterion under ordinary holds: they do not rewrite their trajectories because they are scored, and they do not return the look as further centers of distinction. That is why physical measurements cohere across observers more tightly than accounts of human behavior. The stability is not a moral ranking of science over mind. It is non-reflexivity of the stratum.

Domains made of minds fail the criterion by construction. The look that would inventory consciousness is already the activity under investigation. EEG signatures, anesthetic binding sites, microtubule coherence times, macroscopic entanglement correlates — every such reading is an observable effect or a necessary condition under a chosen hold. None is consciousness itself. Theories may map correlates with increasing precision. They do not convert the correlate into the phenomenon. [The hard problem of consciousness is consistent with learning](../the-hard-problem-of-consciousness-is-consistent-with-learning/) is that residual under naturalization costume: refuse dual substance, then freeze finished continuity as exhaustive ground of residual openness that learning still is. ## Theory-laden observation and the non-reflexive stratum

That observations are theory-laden does not dissolve measurement. It names that every datum arrives already under a hold — coordinates chosen, outcomes counted as outcomes, premises left unstated. Physical data remain more stable across subjective perceptions because, at ordinary scales, objects neither change their behavior under observation nor observe in return. The exception is the quantum domain, where the bound of the hold can no longer keep distinctions apart as determinate and the method by which the Mind selects coordinates is no longer optional background.

Interpretive latitude among consciousness-causes-collapse, objective reduction, and pure decoherence is not a temporary deficit of data awaiting a final experiment. It is the character of the bound: how a conscious agent draws the cut between system and observer. The data do not dictate the interpretation alone because the interpretation is already the further discrete act that converts open potentiality into registered trajectory.

Anesthesia evidence is not empty under this cut. What disappears under general anesthesia, and what returns with recovery, is real as correlation under a hold. Meyer-Overton and decades of target work can tighten maps of necessary conditions. The freeze is only the further claim that those maps close the account of consciousness — that the strongest evidence for the biological basis *is* the disappearance of a public signature, full stop. Necessary conditions remain necessary conditions. They do not become the private registration they condition.

## The demand asks the lag to capture the act

This structural asymmetry explains why the original demand for objectively testable evidence of consciousness cannot be satisfied on its own terms. The demand presupposes that consciousness is the kind of thing that could appear as data among other data. But data are effects. Consciousness is the prior openness from which effects arise. To insist on third-person evidence as the sole criterion is to ask the lagging description to capture the leading act — an impossibility built into the relation of cause and effect, potential and actual, experience and abstraction. [Data is local; intelligence is allocated](../data-is-local-intelligence-is-allocated/) is that geometry when aggregation of local productions is taken to place intelligence in the topology rather than in the allocation that names dynamics intelligent. [Shared consciousness is intersecting individualities](../shared-consciousness-is-intersecting-individualities/) is the privacy of registration under sharedness costume: experience registers only through each center’s own recursion; what is available across centers is mutual reinforcement, never another’s return completed from here.

Hypotheses about neural or quantum correlates may strengthen or weaken under further measurement. Correlates may be mapped more finely. The conversion of correlates into the phenomenon itself is not an unfinished engineering task. It is a category error: the freeze of observational stand as permanent exterior survey of mind as a system property. Release returns the instruments to ordinary use — anesthesia maps, microtubule models, EEG signatures, decoherence calculations — without requiring them to close the account they presuppose. The openness of reality ensures that the gap remains. The gap is not a container waiting for a better bridge. It is how observation registers at all: effect held, cause one step ahead, consciousness never appearing as data among data. [Causality and the irreducible prior](../causality-and-the-irreducible-prior/) is that reverse under cascade costume: every claim about the unbounded substrate is already understanding; the Mind cannot recede into mere content.
