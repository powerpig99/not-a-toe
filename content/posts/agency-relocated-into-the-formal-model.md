# Agency Relocated into the Formal Model

*The independent variable is relocated from the minds that design and react into the abstractions they are urged to master.*

A public urging asks mathematicians and the curious to study the abstraction of currency, the mechanisms of inflation, and zero-knowledge proofs — not only to keep up with AI-assisted proofs — and presents this study, together with a willingness to question dogma, as a way to extend individual agency. The stated intention is to equip people with knowledge that will let them navigate and shape the systems that govern value and exchange. The same framing treats agency as presently lacking until the formal models are mastered, and treats the mechanisms themselves as actors that operate on those who do not understand them. Formal knowledge remains necessary: without it the constraints within which choices occur cannot even be seen. The capacity to act is not conferred by the models. It is already exercised by the individuals who design systems and by those who live inside them and react.

## The urging locates agency in the constructions

The urging frames study as a path to greater individual agency. That framing silently presumes that agency is presently lacking and must be acquired by mastering the formal models. Agency is treated as something that resides in, or is granted by, the mathematical constructions and the system designs built upon them. The independent variable — individual choice — is relocated from the minds that design and react into the abstractions themselves. The stated intention locates agency in the models. That location is the displacement.

[Liberation rhetoric begins by defining captivity](../liberation-rhetoric-begins-by-defining-captivity/) is that prior under identity costume: the other is first held as unfree so that release speech can stand as source. Here the diagnosis is thinner and more technical — not that the person is a captive, but that agency waits on entry into the model. [Empowerment establishes the centralization of power](../empowerment-establishes-the-centralization-of-power/) is the conferral face: capacity framed as a gift from an authorized source. The authorized source in this urging is the construction itself.

## The formal property does not contain the independent variable

The displacement is especially clear in the case of zero-knowledge proofs. The mathematics is elegant and the implementations can be faithful; a proof can establish the truth of a statement without revealing its witness. The formal property does not itself contain or distribute agency. Agency appears only in the decisions of particular people: those who choose how the constructions will be designed, deployed, and incentivized, and those who choose how to respond once the claimed guarantees become known. By locating the source of power inside the cryptographic boundary rather than in the agents who continually cross, ignore, or renegotiate that boundary, the framing treats the model as the primary actor and the individuals as secondary.

[The coordinator’s category error](../the-coordinators-category-error/) is that allocation when a cryptographically continuous record is held as the install of coordination centers already perform. [The model never becomes a second edge](../the-model-never-becomes-a-second-edge/) is the same bound under instrument costume: densified residue extends reach; initiation stays at the edge that still distinguishes.

## Currency and inflation remain products of choice

The same pattern governs the wider abstractions of currency and inflation. These are not self-acting mathematical entities. They are the ongoing products of choices made by people who invent rules and by people who adapt to them — often by revising expectations precisely because they understand the rules exist. When agency is presumed to be missing until one enters the model, the capacity that is always already present in observation, reaction, and decision is overlooked. What remains is a transfer of the independent variable from living minds into formal descriptions.

[Individual choices as the only causal levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) keeps initiation with successive acts; conditions and patterns remain residue and field. [The rational choice postulate is the original sin](../the-rational-choice-postulate-is-the-original-sin/) is that transfer under economic-theory costume: formal apparatus installed as foundation while choice continues only under private, provisional premises. Mastery of the apparatus, offered as the extension of agency, is the pedagogical face of the same freeze.

## Reflexive activity is already the engine

Individuals already observe systems, anticipate responses, and alter their conduct in ways that feed back into the systems themselves. The dynamics that appear as mere variance or noise in any static average are the forces that reshape the order from moment to moment. By treating the mathematical model as the site where agency is gained, the original premise understates this continuous, reflexive activity. The study offered in the name of extending agency then serves the freeze it was offered to dissolve.

[The average is residue, not the engine](../the-average-is-residue-not-the-engine/) is that restore under percentage costume: mass rates track composition; the decisive edge stays with successive acts. What a closed model scores as noise is the living update.

## Knowledge makes the field visible; it does not confer the lever

Self-distinguishing activity occurs — uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. Formal knowledge of abstractions remains necessary; without it the constraints within which choices occur cannot even be seen. [The scaffolding we forget](../the-scaffolding-we-forget/) is that knowledge as instrument: usable drop-out so further acts can land; lag when the hold is preserved as the seat of agency. The capacity to act is not conferred by the models. It is exercised by the individuals who design systems and by those who live inside them and react. [The allocation of causal power in validation](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/) tracks which side is treated as supplying the next step. Return keeps supply at the edge still choosing — including the choice to study the constructions, and the choice of what to do once they are seen.
