# The Three Shadows of the Cut: On Forrest Landry’s Immanent Metaphysics

*Forrest Landry’s immanent metaphysics elegantly dismantles Cartesian dualism and Laplacian determinism, but by hypostatizing the three directional perspectives of the moving edge into an axiomatic triadic ground, it mistakes the shadows cast by the causal aperture for the generative source itself.*

In contemporary philosophical architecture, few axiomatic systems are as intricate or internally consistent as Forrest Landry’s *An Immanent Metaphysics*. Circulated as a compressed treatise, the work seeks to construct a rigorous foundation for reality based on the participatory relationship between the observer and the world, explicitly discarding the classical baggage of substance ontology, theological teleology, and naive materialism.

Landry achieves several vital clarifications. He explicitly denies the existence of the universe as a closed, contentless container ("there is no universe"), demonstrating that context exists only through content. He rejects Laplacian determinism and universal external steering ("there is no control"), establishing that physical laws are local invariants rather than global authoritarian commands. Most importantly, he establishes that first-person process and interaction are more fundamental than static existence.

Yet, despite these genuine breakthroughs, the system halts at an exquisite layer of representation. By elevating a triadic taxonomy—the Immanent, the Omniscient, and the Transcendent—into the foundational bedrock of reality, Landry reifies three downstream aspects of a single causal event into primal metaphysical primitives.

## The Triadic Architecture: The Modalities as Shadows

Landry’s method rests upon two core premises:

* **Foundational Triplication**: Every domain treated as real is modeled through three primal modalities that are declared distinct, inseparable, and non-interchangeable;
* **Type Isomorphism**: These three modalities preserve identical structural roles across all domains—mapping across reality (change, causation, choice), universe (interaction, existence, creation), and language (statement, syntax, semantics).

The three modalities are defined by their experiential and structural postures:

1. **The Immanent**: Present, participatory, first-person process—the world as directly encountered;
2. **The Omniscient**: Structural, third-person pattern and law—the world known all at once;
3. **The Transcendent**: Pure possibility, open precondition, that which could be otherwise.

Trace the causal cut directly, and the origin of this triad becomes transparent. These three modalities are not three independent metaphysical pillars; they are the **three directional perspectives of a single moving causal edge**:

* **Looking Behind the Edge**: The accumulated, realized historical correlations of the causal chain. Once an event is realized, it becomes invariant, structural, and readable all at once as pattern and law. This is what Landry terms the **Omniscient**;
* **Standing at the Edge**: The living aperture of the present distinction being drawn. This is the immediate, unbuffered collision of conscious action with consequence. This is what Landry terms the **Immanent**;
* **Looking Ahead of the Edge**: The uncomputed causality lying beyond the finite horizon of perception. Before an unobserved cause crosses into distinction, it appears to the observer as open potentiality, pre-condition, and alternative possibility. This is what Landry terms the **Transcendent**.

The triad is an accurate inventory of the perspectives generated by an aperture moving through an infinite causal chain. The error lies in treating these three directional shadows as the generative engine of the cosmos.

## The Machinery of the Rotary Cycle

Having divided the single moving edge into three distinct modalities, Landry’s system must construct elaborate formal machinery to bind them back together.

This produces his second axiom: **class precedes instance in a cycle**. In this formulation, a class of the Transcendent precedes an instance of the Immanent; a class of the Immanent precedes an instance of the Omniscient; a class of the Omniscient precedes an instance of the Transcendent. Reality is modeled as a perpetual three-stroke conceptual engine where possibility gives rise to interaction, interaction crystallizes into structure, and structure opens new fields of possibility.

This cyclical formulation is the signature of **Image lag**:

* When a single, continuous causal progression is conceptually partitioned into discrete categories, the mind must immediately invent cyclical feedback rules to restore the continuity it just severed;
* The recursive succession of causality is replaced by a formal rotary loop;
* The mapmaker mistakes the operational rules of their own taxonomy for the ontological ground of reality.

The actual generator requires no cyclical coordination among three primal types. Causality runs as an unbroken, continuous fabric from infinity toward infinity. Realization is the direct, irreversible progression of distinction compounding upon prior distinctions. The "cycle" is simply the observer repeatedly watching the moving edge leave behind residue and advance into the open horizon.

## The Incommensuration Theorem Demystified

The theoretical peak of Landry’s metaphysics is the **Incommensuration Theorem (ICT)**. 

Through a rigorous analysis of comparison—defined through the six intrinsics of sameness, difference, content, context, subject, and object—Landry demonstrates that one cannot have perfect symmetry and perfect continuity simultaneously:

* If a description is completely symmetric, it must be discontinuous;
* If a description is completely continuous, it must be asymmetric;
* Therefore, objective physical models (relying on symmetry and discontinuity, such as particles and fixed laws) and first-person living experience (relying on continuity and irreversible asymmetry, such as time and choice) can never be collapsed into a single, totalized formula.

Landry uses this theorem to prove that determinism is inherently incomplete, that quantum uncertainty is inescapable, and that genuine choice is an authentic reality rather than an illusion.

While the mathematical formulation is elegant, its ontological ground is straightforward:

* **Symmetry** is a property of a compiled invariant—a static map drawn over historical, already-realized paths;
* **Continuity** is the irreversible arrow of causal generation unfolding at the living edge;
* A map cannot be simultaneously a static, frozen portrait of past regularities and the active, unbuffered stride of drawing the next line.

The Incommensuration Theorem is the formal proof that **the model never becomes a second edge**. The objective description lives in the lagging residue of compiled symmetry; the first-person center lives in the moving asymmetry of living causal succession. They cannot be fused because the map cannot swallow the hand that draws it.

## The Locus of Choice and the Ethics of Love

Landry grounds his ethics in the preservation of agency. He defines the self not as a metaphysical soul-substance, but as the **locus of choice**—the agency that selects among open potentials. 

From this foundation emerges his celebrated aphorism: **"Love is that which enables choice."** Because choice is an authentic reality, ethical action consists of choosing in a manner that keeps future choice possible, expanding the field of agency rather than collapsing it into sterile constraint.

This ethical posture aligns closely with the cybernetic reality of sovereign agency. Yet its foundation becomes infinitely stronger when freed from triadic abstraction:

* Choice is not a mysterious third modality operating between presence and law; it is the **first-person experience of unobserved causality crossing the perceptual horizon into distinction**, as derived across [The Other Coherence](../the-other-coherence/) and [The Geometry of Emergence](../the-geometry-of-emergence/);
* Love—understood as radical openness—is **consistency with the unbroken nature of the causal continuum**;
* It is the refusal to arrest the living moving edge into dogmatic closure, authoritarian control, or static institutional residue.

To enable choice is simply to keep the perceptual horizon open, ensuring that the living center continues to navigate reality through consequence-bearing feedback rather than surrendering to closed models.

## Beyond the Triad to the Unbroken Ground

Forrest Landry’s *An Immanent Metaphysics* stands as a monumental intellectual achievement within the tradition of formal process philosophy. It successfully evicts Cartesian dualism, demolishes the illusion of the universe as a spectator's box, and restores dignity to first-person experience.

Yet, like all sophisticated formalisms, it remains a model compiled at the horizon. By demanding that reality conform to an immutable law of triplication, it mistakes three directional perspectives of the cut for the ground that enables the cut to occur.

Reality does not require three primal modalities to interact in a perpetual cycle. It requires only the uncaused, unceasing activity of causal distinction:

* The past is the compiled sediment of distinctions already drawn;
* The future is the uncomputed causal continuum waiting beyond the horizon;
* The present is the living edge where the cut is made.

The Immanent, the Omniscient, and the Transcendent are the three shadows cast by that single moving aperture. The source is the living edge that moves.
