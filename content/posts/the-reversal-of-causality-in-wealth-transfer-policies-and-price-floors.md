# The Misallocation of Cause: Residual Choices and the Appearance of Policy Reversal

*The measured wage is residue of prior conditions; intervening on it as lever does not reverse causality — what appears as reversal is densified residual choice treating that residue as independent power.*

A relative wage is effect of prior productivity, scarcity, and opportunity cost. Mandated floors and external transfers intervene on that residue. They do not reverse the direction of cause. What hardens into the visible policy is the densified symptom of many discrete choices to relocate initiation onto a shared register that cannot initiate. Once the relative signal is suppressed, entry-level opportunity contracts, early skill accumulation slows, and the original disparity compounds — still as consequence of those residual choices, not as proof that the policy became a second engine.

## Causality registers only at the edge that chooses

Self-distinguishing activity occurs — uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. Causality registers only at the edge that chooses. Each successive act under present constraints is the sole independent lever. Aggregates, patterns, institutions, and policies are residue of those acts, never co-authors of them.

[Individual choices as the only causal levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) keeps initiation with successive acts; the aggregate that looks like “the market” or “the policy” is residue of those acts, never a super-locus that authors them. [When the effect authors the frame](../when-the-effect-authors-the-frame/) is that topology under another load: the felt or projected register is granted causal priority, and available responses reorganize around managing it. Here the register is the wage print and the transfer receipt.

## Relative wage is residue of prior conditions

A relative wage transmits information about productivity differentials, scarcity, and opportunity costs. That signal shapes the field for later acts: skill investment, mobility, process change, or reallocation. The low wage is therefore effect, not cause. The corrective sequence remains possible only while the signal is left intact.

[Price as utterance, understanding as trade](../price-as-utterance-understanding-as-trade/) is the print as public residue: its only force is how each center re-traces it into the next discrete act. Treating the print as self-executing author of the next state is the same freeze under market costume. [Ownership and self-worthiness](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/) is the functional face at one locus: the model compounds when outcomes re-enter as own. A relative wage that still lands as consequence of this edge’s productivity keeps that loop closed. A floor or transfer that substitutes an external payment for that contact relocates continuation.

## Belief relocates the lever onto a register that cannot initiate

When individuals choose to believe that a mandated floor or an external transfer can author the next state of productivity or relative position, the lever is relocated. The belief is itself a discrete act. It externalizes initiation onto a shared register that cannot initiate. Once enough centers perform that relocation, the collective symptom hardens into the visible policy. The relative signal is then suppressed, entry-level opportunities contract at the least-productive margin, early skill accumulation slows, and the original disparity compounds. These consequences are real. They remain consequences of the residual choices that densified the policy and then mistook it for an independent causal power.

For those whose marginal product lies below the floor, the clearing wage is not a higher number. It is zero employment — no first job, no initial skill accumulation, no compounding of experience under real stakes. That exclusion is marginal for centers that already clear above the floor and for firms that absorb the mandated cost as a small share of total outlay. It is everything for those priced out of the opportunity to acquire the first residual of competence. Support for the policy is easiest where those consequences do not re-enter as own.

[Emotional support as causal interference](../emotional-support-as-causal-interference/) is the same geometry when the felt state is modulated so pressure that would reorganize skill, habit, and decision is dampened. [Therapy opens the feedback loop](../therapy-opens-the-feedback-loop/) is third-locus attenuation of the same geometry: management of the register lowers pressure to re-trace generating conditions. [Public spending does not create capital](../public-spending-does-not-create-capital/) is the transfer face under budget costume: extraction and reallocation first paid for by private output, then scored as if a collective locus authored creation. [The myth of population collapse explained](../the-myth-of-population-collapse-explained/) is that sequence under demographic load: transfers substitute for demonstrated agency and reinforce the externalized placement of cause until fertility registers the training.

## Soft dual reinstalls two loci where only one sequence operates

The policy does not reverse causality. The individual choice to treat the policy as capable of reversal does. Softening into “part individual, part systemic” reinstalls two loci where only one sequence operates: successive residual acts under present constraints. The pattern may impose real costs on any single agent; as initiation it remains symptom.

[Residual individualism](../residual-individualism/) is the same restore under collective-focus costume: patterns and policies are residue of many discrete acts, not independent engines that can author the next skill or the next hire. Softening into “part signal, part decree” reinstalls two loci where only prior conditions → relative wage → corrective adjustment was operating. The decree densifies residue; it does not become co-author of the productivity differential it pretends to set.

## Misallocation keeps attention on the densified residue

Visible short-term gains for those who keep the job stay politically salient because the misallocation itself keeps attention fixed on the densified residue. The expanded class of those priced out of the first residual of competence remains less visible for the same reason: the belief that relocated the lever continues to treat the symptom as the relevant register.

[Symptom and cause in the narratives of progress](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/) is delayed registration treated as engine: the loudest residue arrives late and is installed as lever while initiating sequences stay earlier and thinner. Here the loud residue is the wage gain of those who keep the job; the thinner sequence is skill formation among those who never enter. [Restriction is a selective tax](../restriction-is-a-selective-tax/) is the hold under seal costume: friction falls hardest on the least productive margin, for whom the seal is not a higher wage but exclusion from the field where relative productivity would have risen. [Production, consumption, and the Mind’s distinction](../production-consumption-and-the-minds-distinction/) is the closed hold that converts creative energy into the appearance of subtraction: under a preserved bound, expansion of capacity at the margin registers as cost to absorb rather than as signal to re-trace.

## The misallocation is the choice to believe otherwise

No additional assumptions about market power or demand inelasticity are required. Suppress the feedback by relocating the lever, and the process that would have corrected the relative gap is interrupted. The direction of causality never left the edge that chooses. The misallocation is the choice to believe otherwise.
