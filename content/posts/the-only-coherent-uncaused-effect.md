# 唯一自洽的无因之果 / The Only Coherent Uncaused Effect

*从第一人称的原点去找，不由前因推动而到来的，只有自己的选择 / Searched for from the first-person origin, the only effect that arrives without an antecedent push is one's own choice.*

在被登记的世界里，每一个结果都带着前因到来，向外去求一个无因之果是不自洽的。可从第一人称的原点去找，有一种现象到来时并不是前一刻推过来的：自己的选择。没有这一次注入的自由变量，经验就留在一台确定的状态机里，一个封闭的认知模拟被当成了外面的世界。

In the world as registered, every effect arrives with antecedents, and seeking an uncaused effect outside is incoherent. Yet searched for from the first-person origin, there is one phenomenon that does not arrive pushed by the moment before: one's own choice. Without that injection of a free variable, experience stays inside a deterministic state machine, a closed cognitive simulation taken for the world outside.

## 一、 向外求奇迹的不自洽 / 1. The Incoherence of the Outward Miracle

迷信、理所当然的索取与长久的受害感，这几种冲动共用一个错置：要求外部世界给出一个无因之果。想要脱离时间与离散劳动的横财，脱离能力的声望，不面对造成困境的条件就到来的情感救赎；要宇宙暂停它的守恒，不要种子、土壤与季节，直接交出收成。这是姿态，不是某一类人，任何一个心智都有向外伸手的时候。

Superstition, entitlement and lasting victimhood share one misplacement: demanding an uncaused effect from the outside world. Wanting windfall wealth detached from time and discrete work, acclaim detached from capability, emotional rescue that arrives without facing the conditions that produced the distress; asking the universe to suspend its conservation and hand over the harvest without seed, soil or season. These are postures, not kinds of people; every Mind reaches outward at times.

这个要求不自洽。现实结算账单，不派发奇迹。[价值、货币与财富的生成机制](../the-generative-mechanics-of-value-money-and-wealth/)：外部世界像一本冷静的结算账本，每一块钱、每一级地位、每一个后果，都是先前的劳动与已解决的熵的滞后残余。要求一个没有原因的结果，就是把能动交给了处境：等着条件变得有利，把宇宙不肯违背因果读成个人遭受的不公或恶意的命运。在被登记的世界里，每一个结果都带着前因到来；向外追一个无因的奇迹，像在空镜子里追一个影子。[寄存器的算术](../the-arithmetic-of-the-register/)：自由变量是溢出寄存器的那一次进位，它不从账本里来。

The demand is incoherent. Reality settles invoices; it does not hand out miracles. [The Generative Mechanics of Value, Money, and Wealth](../the-generative-mechanics-of-value-money-and-wealth/): the outside world works like a cool settlement ledger, every dollar, every grade of status, every consequence being the lagging residue of earlier work and resolved entropy. Demanding a result without its cause hands agency over to circumstance: waiting for conditions to turn favourable, and reading the universe's refusal to break causality as personal injustice or malicious fate. In the world as registered, every effect arrives with antecedents; chasing an uncaused miracle outside is chasing a shadow through an empty mirror. [The Arithmetic of the Register](../the-arithmetic-of-the-register/): the free variable is the carry that overflows the register, and it does not come from the ledger.

## 二、 运行在内部的发条 / 2. The Clockwork Running Inside

不去发起行动，并不能逃离因果，只是成了它被动的工具。内部模型的复杂，常被当作心智活着的证明：满是精巧的术语、细腻的社会评论、层层的心理防御与错综的委屈。可只要没有一次有意的选择，它运转起来就是一台机械的机器。[决定论机器](../the-deterministic-machine/)：拒绝改变内部模型，最常见的理由是守护一个石化的自我形象，经验到的世界就塌成一台封闭的状态机，它确定的相变被误读成外部的命运。[心智的几何学](../the-geometry-of-mind/)：这发生在意识把感知原点当成身份、把罗盘的指针焊死的时候。

Not originating action does not escape causality; it only becomes causality's passive instrument. The complexity of an inner model is often taken as proof that a Mind is alive: full of refined jargon, nuanced social commentary, layered psychological defences and intricate grievances. Yet as long as no deliberate choice is made, it runs as a mechanical machine. [The Deterministic Machine](../the-deterministic-machine/): refusing to change the inner model, most often to guard a petrified self-image, collapses the experienced world into a closed state machine whose determined phase shifts are misread as outside fate. [The Geometry of Mind](../the-geometry-of-mind/): this happens when awareness takes the sensing origin for identity and solders the compass needle in place.

确定的回路是这样转的：刺激进来，条件反射沿着磨旧的沟槽触发，可预料的情绪涌起，熟悉的叙事重播，机器以无误的规律执行它的程序。[向量与投影的迷局](../the-vector-and-the-puzzle-of-projections/)里，高维几何可以靠单纯的角度旋转造出成长的错觉，而核心的模长在萎缩；没有被驾驭的心智，也把内部计算的湍流当成了活的能动，它有无穷多种恐慌、防御与絮语的组合，自由度却是零。

The deterministic loop turns like this: a stimulus comes in, conditioned reflexes fire along worn grooves, predictable emotions surge, familiar stories replay, and the machine executes its program with faultless regularity. In [The Vector and the Puzzle of Projections](../the-vector-and-the-puzzle-of-projections/), high-dimensional geometry can produce an illusion of growth through mere rotations of angle while the core magnitude withers; an unsteered Mind likewise takes the turbulence of inner computation for living agency, with endless combinations of panic, defence and chatter and zero degrees of freedom.

这里最常见的错位，是把这个内部的、自动的模拟当成“外面的客观世界”。于是有这样的断言：“人都靠不住”，“这个系统专跟诚实的努力作对”，“现实就是乏味又累人”。与这些影子搏斗，哀叹它们的分量，围绕着躲开它们去安排生活，却看不到所对抗的那座压迫性的建筑并不是世界，而是一台没有被驾驭的发条状态机，在自身之内一遍遍重放。

The most common misplacement here is taking this inner, automatic simulation for "the objective world outside". Out come the declarations: "people can't be trusted", "the system is rigged against honest effort", "reality is dull and exhausting". These shadows are fought, their weight lamented, life arranged around avoiding them, without seeing that the oppressive structure being fought is not the world but an unsteered clockwork state machine replaying itself, over and over, within.

## 三、 向现实注入自由变量 / 3. Injecting the Free Variable into Reality

怎样打破这台机械模拟的闭环？不靠与机器争辩，不靠分析它的齿轮，也不靠重新排列它内部的标签；唯一能改变系统的，是向正在经验的现实注入一个自由变量。在数学建模与物理里，参数全被绑定的系统是封闭的，未来的轨迹由边界条件决定，它不能让自己意外，不能学习，也越不过最初的假设。[自由变量的生成拓扑](../the-generative-topology-of-free-variables/)：新颖、适应与负熵，只在系统含有不受约束、在动态地平线上相互作用的自由度时才出现。[可延展的地平线](../the-extendable-horizon/)：注入自由变量，地平线就延展了。

How is the closed loop of this mechanical simulation broken? Not by arguing with the machine, not by analysing its gears, not by rearranging its inner labels; the only thing that changes the system is injecting a free variable into the reality being experienced. In mathematical modelling and physics, a system whose parameters are all bound is closed; its future trajectory is fixed by its boundary conditions, and it cannot surprise itself, learn, or go beyond its initial assumptions. [The Generative Topology of Free Variables](../the-generative-topology-of-free-variables/): novelty, adaptation and negative entropy appear only when a system holds unconstrained degrees of freedom interacting across a dynamic horizon. [The Extendable Horizon](../the-extendable-horizon/): injecting the free variable extends the horizon.

作出选择，就是引入这个自由变量。那是意识作出区分的一刻：这台机器的先前状态，不决定下一步。先前的惯性不再自动写出下一步；就在这一处，心智跨过一道本体的门槛，不再是系统算出来的一个变量，而成为重新设定系统边界条件的那个算子。选择不打破因果，而是发起因果：它是无因的潜能落成为一个不可逆的物理原因的那个铰链，启动一串旧机器算不出来的后果。

Making a choice is introducing that free variable. It is the moment awareness draws the distinction: the prior state of this machine does not decide the next step. Prior momentum no longer writes the next step automatically; at exactly this place the Mind crosses an ontological threshold, ceasing to be a variable the system computes and becoming the operator that re-specifies the system's boundary conditions. Choice does not break causality; it initiates it. It is the hinge where uncaused potential lands as an irreversible physical cause, starting a cascade of consequences the old machine could not have computed.

这一刻从外面看，是另一副样子。心智注入的自由变量，在外部的观察里显现为量子的不确定；量子的缝隙与心智的选择，是同一件事的两个面。缝隙落在观察的边界上，而那条边界登记在哪里，是心智作出的选择，[割点可移不可除](../the-cut-moves-but-never-vanishes/)走过这一处。人的心智，正是能切身感到、并实现从信息到物理那一跳的位置，这一跳就叫作观察。

Seen from outside, this moment looks different. The free variable the Mind injects shows up in outside observation as quantum indeterminacy; the quantum gap and the Mind's choice are two faces of one thing. The gap sits at the boundary of observation, and where that boundary registers is a choice the Mind makes, which [The Cut Moves but Never Vanishes](../the-cut-moves-but-never-vanishes/) walks through. The human Mind is the very place where the jump from the informational to the physical can be felt and actualized, and that jump is called observation.

## 四、 所选择看见的世界 / 4. The World Chosen to Be Seen

“看见什么样的世界，就得到什么样的世界”，这句古老的话常被降格为一厢情愿。它说的其实是一条因果。框架的选择先于证据的登记：现实并不以未经中介的原始数据呈现，正如[框架的边界](../the-boundary-of-the-frame/)所示，观察需要一条周界；要问哪些问题、要划哪些区分、愿意承受哪些摩擦，决定了哪些信号能进入觉察、哪些被当作背景噪声丢掉。

The old saying "the world one chooses to see is the world one gets" is often degraded into wishful thinking. What it states is a causal fact. The choice of frame comes before the registering of evidence: reality does not present itself as raw, unmediated data, and [The Boundary of the Frame](../the-boundary-of-the-frame/) shows that observation needs a perimeter; which questions are asked, which distinctions drawn, which friction accepted, decide which signals can enter awareness and which are discarded as background noise.

没有选择，感知的框架就化石化：继承来的、自动的机器继续把陈旧的预期投到画布上，只收下确认既有恐惧与偏见的证据，世界显得冻结，是因为看它的那个开口钙化了。作出选择、换一个基底去看，把失败当作信息而不是控告，把障碍当作承重的检验而不是人身攻击，意料之外的证据就进入内部模型，先验分布随之改变，一直就在那里、却看不见的可供性显露出来。内部的取向变了，离散的物理选择也变了：走法不同，建法不同，承担的后果也不同。[个体抉择是唯一的因果杠杆](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/)：外部世界围绕这一处重新配置，不是靠魔法的投射，而是因为杠杆终于从内向外被扳动了。

Without choice, the perceptual frame fossilizes: the inherited, automatic machine keeps projecting stale expectations onto the canvas and admits only evidence that confirms existing fears and biases, and the world looks frozen because the aperture it is seen through has calcified. Making a choice and looking from a new basis, treating failure as information rather than indictment and obstacles as load-bearing tests rather than personal attacks, lets unexpected evidence into the inner model; the prior distribution shifts, and affordances that were there all along but unseen come into view. With the inner orientation changed, the discrete physical choices change too: one moves differently, builds differently, bears different consequences. [Individual Choices as the Only Causal Levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/): the outside world reconfigures around this place, not through magical projection, but because the lever is finally pulled from the inside out.

这里要分清两件事。什么到来，不由选择：失去、伤病、别人的决定，是不让开的摩擦。到来之后它成了什么，下一步往哪里走，始终有一个选择在场，包括放弃选择的那个选择。放弃驾驭，本身也是一次选择，把方向盘交给机械的机器；随之而来的困顿、苦涩与绝望，是过去的条件反射无人驾驭地运转下去的结算。注入一个自由变量之后随之而来的挣扎，则是现实围绕一次自主的行动重新组织时的活的摩擦。痛苦里能被改变的那一部分，就落在这里。

Two things need telling apart here. What arrives is not chosen: loss, injury, other people's decisions are friction that does not give way. What it becomes once it has arrived, and where the next step goes, always has a choice present in it, including the choice to give up choosing. Giving up the steering is itself a choice, handing the wheel to the mechanical machine; the entrapment, bitterness and despair that follow are the settlement of past conditioning running on unsteered. The struggle that follows the injection of a free variable is the living friction of reality reorganizing around a self-originated act. The part of suffering that can be changed lies here.

## 五、 在边缘接住第一推动 / 5. Catching the Prime Mover at the Edge

论证到这里落了地：一个人能接住的唯一自洽的无因之果，是自己的选择。“接住”这个动词是必要的。从世界那里接不住一个无因的结果，被登记的世界是一条已经结算的因果链；也从旁观者手里接不住认可、安全或自尊，他们的掌声只是[投在狭窄公共截面上的一道闪动的影子](../the-vector-and-the-puzzle-of-projections/)。

The argument lands here: the only coherent uncaused effect anyone can catch is their own choice. The verb "catch" is necessary. No uncaused result can be caught from the world, since the world as registered is a chain of settled causes; nor can validation, security or self-respect be caught from the hands of spectators, whose applause is only [a flickering shadow cast on a narrow public cross-section](../the-vector-and-the-puzzle-of-projections/).

唯一能用上的无因之物，是在当下移动的边缘上自己辨别的那一点火花。选择的上游，是过去的全部条件作用、祖先的生物性、社会的训练与旧习惯，那是机器；选择的下游，是整个物理世界，准备执行能量守恒、算出劳动的确切后果，那是因果。正如[你的代码库即是记忆](../your-codebase-is-memory/)所追溯的，科学账本与外化记忆代码库里记下的每一条因果链，都是先前区分留下的下游检查点；写入并修剪这座代码库的自我区分活动，本身就是栈外无因的基底。在决定的刀锋上，心智拥有一份不由那些残余推导而来的自主，可以重复机械的回路，也可以注入一个自由变量。

The only uncaused thing available is the spark of one's own discernment at the moving edge of the present. Upstream of the choice lie all past conditioning, ancestral biology, social training and old habits: that is the machine. Downstream lies the whole physical world, ready to enforce conservation of energy and calculate the exact consequence of the work: that is causality. As traced in [Your Codebase Is Memory](../your-codebase-is-memory/), every causal chain recorded in a scientific ledger or an externalized codebase of memory consists of downstream checkpoints of prior distinctions, while the self-distinguishing activity that writes and prunes the codebase is itself the uncaused ground outside the stack. At the knife-edge of decision, the Mind holds a self-origination that is not derived from anything in that residue, free to repeat the mechanical loop or to inject a free variable.

接住选择，就是接下[自我价值感与所有权](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/)的全部分量：生活若感觉像一台重复的死机器，是因为唯一能重启引擎的那根杠杆被放下了。不必再向世界讨奇迹，也不必再追打在自身之内投下的影子；走到边缘，接住只属于这一处的选择，注入自由变量，现实就从这里重新开始。

Catching the choice is taking up the full weight of [Ownership and Self-Worthiness](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/): if life feels like a dead, repeating machine, it is because the one lever able to restart the engine has been set down. There is no more need to ask the world for miracles, nor to chase the shadows cast within; at the edge, the choice that belongs to this place alone is caught, the free variable injected, and reality begins again from here.
