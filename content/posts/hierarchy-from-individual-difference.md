# 来自个体差异的层级 / Hierarchy from Individual Difference

*把彼此不同的个体放在一把共同的尺上计分，出现的就是排序 / Ranking is what appears when individuals who differ are scored on a shared measure.*

差异是个体心智保持有界的方式。层级，是这种差异经由许多人共同持有的一把尺被读出时登记到的有序样式。异议集中在一个位置落到仍被持为参照的自我形象之下的地方，它把这道差距的原因迁到条件、规则或机运之中，于是自己的抉择序列不再被登记为它的原因。在一种把位次读作复利抉择的编码之下，白手起家的高位对偏好的形象压得比世袭的位置更重，因为那种编码把世袭的位置计为处在抉择之外。

Difference is how individual Minds stay bounded. Hierarchy is the ordered pattern that registers once that difference is read through a measure many hold in common. Objection concentrates where a place falls below the self-image still held as reference, and it relocates the cause of that gap into conditions, rules, or chance, so that one’s own sequence of choices no longer registers as its cause. Under a coding that reads rank as compounded choice, self-made high ranks press the preferred image harder than hereditary places, because that coding scores hereditary places as lying outside choice.

## 一、 差异就是个体，每一个都是同一活动的一个所在 / 1. Difference is individuals, each a locus of one activity

自我区分的活动发生着，无因，不息。称之为心智：已在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。每一个离散的动作界出一个*此*，因而有一个处所：一个中心。个体是在一个所在继续的这类动作序列；许多个体，是同一个活动在许多所在继续。差异就是这种有界：每一个序列都在一些轴上留下痕迹，而这些轴起初独立于其他每一个个体。

Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused and unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. Each discrete act bounds a *this*, and so has a location: a center. An individual is the sequence of such acts continuing at one locus; many individuals are the same activity continuing at many loci. Difference is that boundedness: each sequence lays down traces on axes that begin independent of every other individual.

单独来看，这些痕迹保持分离。排序有待一把共同的尺；在一把尺被持有之前，剩下的是彼此分离的序列。身份在这里登记为这个个体特有的序列：在任何比较之前，作为这一个的感觉，就是一段已在进行中的有界生命的感觉。

Taken alone, those traces remain separate. Ranking waits on a common measure; until one is held, what remains is separate sequences. Identity registers here as this individual’s particular sequence: the feel of being this one, before any comparison, is the feel of a bounded life already underway.

比较始于诸序列在一片共享之场里相遇，而一个中心沿着它为这次读取所持的一个维度去读它们。序列本身只提供差异；一个被持有的维度提供位置。当许多中心持有同一个维度，它们的读取彼此加强，一个位置便成为共同的，也能被他人读出。层级是这样一把共同尺度之下诸位置的秩序；有界留在每一个序列，先于任何位置，也与之分开。[生成性的矛盾](../the-generative-contradiction/)追踪这种向外读取所属的运动：能动不断被向外投射，在那里差异成为排序与寻求认可的工具，而与之相抵的，是必须在它自己的所在把它收回。

Comparison begins where sequences meet in a shared field and a center reads them along a dimension it holds for that reading. Sequence alone supplies difference; a held dimension supplies position. When many centers hold the same dimension and their readings reinforce one another, a position becomes common, readable by others as well. Hierarchy is the order of positions under such a common measure; boundedness stays with each sequence, prior to any position and apart from it. [The Generative Contradiction](../the-generative-contradiction/) traces the movement this outward reading belongs to: agency constantly projected outward, where differences become instruments of ranking and of seeking validation, against the necessity of reclaiming it at its own locus.

## 二、 共同尺度产出有序的位置 / 2. Common measure produces ordered places

一旦一个中心经由一套共享的编码方案去读他人，即一种把独特的序列投射到选定的维度上、而让其余未被读出的稳定对齐，层级就是登记到的样式。排序除了尺度之外，没有给差异添加任何东西：它就是在一把尺之下被读出的差异。这次读取把序列排成位置；位置可以并列，而编码没有选中的每一条轴，都留在分数之外。投射让差异对作比较的人可见；比较是一个个体在其他个体之间为自己定位的方式。

Once a center reads others through a shared coding scheme, a stabilized alignment that projects unique sequences onto selected dimensions and leaves the rest unread, hierarchy is the pattern that registers. Ranking adds nothing to difference except the measure: it is difference read under a measure. The reading orders sequences into places; places can tie, and every axis the coding did not select stays out of the score. Projection makes difference visible to the one comparing; comparison is how an individual locates itself among other individuals.

任何计分函数都是这样一套方案。财富、教育、品味、纯洁、美貌、技术能力、门第、苦难、对地位炫耀性的漠然：每一种都是一次选定的投射，让某些序列变得可读，并把它们排成位置。[数据是局部的，智能是分配的](../data-is-local-intelligence-is-allocated/)在质量中追踪同样的关系：信号、糟粕与智能，是心智画出的关系，不是材料内在的等级，而汇总让残余变密，却不搬动画出。把一套方案放到一边，另一套就被拿起，只要个体仍在彼此不同、仍沿着共享的维度被读出。把层级持为一份可以封存的清单，即一份在差异与共享读取仍在继续时、可以从场中清空的完成了的存量，是一种封闭的持守。共同尺度之下的差异已经是秩序；并没有什么进一步的东西被加在它上面。

Any scoring function is such a scheme. Wealth, education, taste, purity, beauty, technical competence, lineage, suffering, conspicuous indifference to status: each is a selected projection that makes certain sequences legible and orders them into places. [Data Is Local; Intelligence Is Allocated](../data-is-local-intelligence-is-allocated/) traces the same relation in quality: signal, slop, and intelligence are relations the Mind draws, not intrinsic grades of the material, and aggregation densifies residue without moving the drawing. Set one scheme aside and another is taken up, as long as individuals continue to differ and continue to be read along shared dimensions. Hierarchy held as a sealable inventory, a finished stock that could be emptied from the field while difference and shared reading continue, is a closed hold. Difference under a common measure already is order; nothing further is laid on top of it.

[拥有更多从不是原因](../having-more-is-never-the-cause/)追踪被冻结为一股独立力量的持续抉择之残余：占有命名的是当下的渲染，不是进一步偏好的作者。地位是同一类渲染，即尺度之下的差异，只要尺度被持有，就可读出。[遮蔽进步的框架](../the-frame-that-conceals-improvement/)追踪一把被呈现为一把、却被当作两把使用的尺：成功者被按不可能的标准要求，失败者被按宽容的标准要求，道德的位次颠倒了，而双重标准始终不可见，包括评判者自己的豁免。它产出的秩序被读作共同的，而尺度已经按情形分裂。[外化善意走向其反面](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/)追踪对更大之善的私人确信，被当作压过他人的依据、并被他们的顺从变密，如何走向它的反面：对被压过的人来说，以善之名被强制的，正是它宣称要防止的那种侵犯。它那一对确信，即对自身道德可靠的确信与对任何异议者之缺陷的确信，就是善之下的一种位置秩序。[卓异、感知与自我设限的问题场域](../exceptionality-perception-and-the-self-limiting-field-of-problems/)是当*制度*与*卓异之人*坍缩为彼此的类型时的这一有序面相：卓异是共享感知之场里的关系性地位，制度是自我选择的沉积，而一旦具体的所在回来，鸡与蛋的循环就消解了。

[Having More Is Never the Cause](../having-more-is-never-the-cause/) traces the residue of continued choosing frozen as an independent force: possession names the current rendering, not an author of further preference. Standing is the same kind of rendering, difference under a measure, legible while the measure is held. [The Frame That Conceals Improvement](../the-frame-that-conceals-improvement/) traces a measure presented as one and applied as two: the successful are held to an impossible standard and the failed to a forgiving one, moral rank inverts, and the double standard stays invisible, including the judge’s own exemption. The order it produces is read as common while the measure has already split by case. [Externalized Virtue Becomes Its Opposite](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/) traces how a private conviction of greater good, taken as a warrant to override others and densified by their compliance, turns into its opposite: for those overridden, what is enforced in the name of the good is the very violation it claimed to prevent. Its pair of certainties, one’s own moral reliability and the deficiency of whoever disagrees, is an order of places under the good. [Exceptionality, Perception, and the Self-Limiting Field of Problems](../exceptionality-perception-and-the-self-limiting-field-of-problems/) is that ordered face when *system* and *exceptional person* collapse into mutual types: exceptionality is relational status in a shared field of perception, systems are sediment of self-selection, and the chicken-and-egg loop dissolves once particular loci return.

随着序列分叉并复利，被测量的差异变宽。更多的轴被计分，当前编码所奖励的位置与它不奖励的位置之间的距离也在增大。能造出、能触及之物的增长并不抹平这些差异；它打开了更多可供抉择展开的方向，因而拓宽了已被持有的尺度所能显示的东西，即不同的人有多一贯地持续抉择。[个体抉择是唯一的因果杠杆](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/)把原因留在离散的动作，每一个都是一根独立的杠杆，从不是一块共享的地板。一把共同的尺就是这类残余：它塑造后来的动作可得哪些对齐，而发起留在动作。[赋权确立权力的集中](../empowerment-establishes-the-centralization-of-power/)追踪一套把能力安放在这样一种秩序之内的词汇：赋权或被赋权，就是追认能力从一个已经持有它的中心移出，而这套词汇里的每一个动作，都再一次把那道门确立为下一步的来处，而发起早已在每一个作抉择的所在进行中。

As sequences diverge and compound, the measured differences widen. More axes are scored, and the distance grows between the positions the current coding rewards and those it leaves unrewarded. Growth in what can be made and reached does not flatten these differences; it opens more directions in which choices can unfold, and so widens what already-held measures can show of how consistently different people keep choosing. [Individual Choices as the Only Causal Levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) keeps cause with the discrete acts, each an independent lever, never a shared floor. A common measure is residue of that kind: it patterns which alignments later acts find available, while initiation stays with the acts. [Empowerment Establishes the Centralization of Power](../empowerment-establishes-the-centralization-of-power/) traces a vocabulary that places capacity within such an order: to empower or to be empowered ratifies that capacity moves from a center that already holds it, and each act in that vocabulary establishes the gate again as the place the next step comes from, while initiation is already underway at every locus that chooses.

## 三、 异议集中于较低的位置 / 3. Objection concentrates on lower place

当有序的位置把这个个体放得比保留形象所偏好的更低时，异议便产生了。面对那道差距，提出异议的登记迁移了原因：条件不平等，规则偏袒他人，环境充满敌意，机运插了手。位置仍留在尺度所放之处；隐退的，是把这个心智自己的抉择序列登记为它的原因。[进步的多面手](../the-utility-men-of-progress/)把进步追踪为临时解题者反复的工作，他们因必要而被轮换上来，又因成功而被轮换下去，而承认、抵抗与吸收在开放之场上滞后。那份滞后的一个面相就是这种迁移：在共享尺度之下他人的能力，在负载之下作为对偏好形象的指控落下；解题者被登记为临时的工具，运转起来便被忘掉，其成就被吸收，贡献者被不予理会。

Objection arises when the ordered positions place this individual lower than the retained image prefers. Faced with that gap, the objecting registration relocates the cause: conditions were unequal, rules favored others, the environment was hostile, chance intervened. The place stays where the measure put it; what recedes is the registration of this Mind’s own sequence of choices as its cause. [The Utility Men of Progress](../the-utility-men-of-progress/) traces progress as the repeated work of temporary solvers, rotated in by necessity and out by success, with recognition, resistance, and absorption lagging on an open field. One face of that lag is this relocation: another's competence under a shared measure lands under load as an accusation against a preferred image; solvers register as temporary tools, forgotten once things run, their achievement absorbed while the contributor is disregarded.

偏好的形象常常采取一种偏好的计分函数的形式：一套编码，其维度奖励这个人已经持有的特质，并把对手的优势重新编码为尺度的缺陷，而那些优势在当前编码之下仍是不同的序列。一套方案并不因为被偏好就是滞后；方案作为暂时的持守彼此竞争。当它被持守得任何当下的读取都碰不到那个偏好的位置时，它就服务于滞后：较高的位置被保存过了它的那一步，作为参照，于是当前的位次被登记为来自别处。[恶是心智自己的造物](../evil-as-the-minds-own-creation/)是偏好的自我形象之下的同一机制：当一个中心保留一幅自己为善的偏好形象，与这形象的不符就被命名为恶，而命名就是生成的动作。在这里，被保存的一极是较高的位置；外部是没有追认它的那片有序之场。[限制是选择性的税](../restriction-is-a-selective-tax/)是同一种滞后，当匹配或领先一次前进被保存为参照，而那次前进作为要被拦下的外部威胁到来时；持守于是只拖慢接受它的人，执行者最重，而前进以不接受它的人的步调继续。

The preferred image often takes the form of a preferred scoring function: a coding whose dimensions reward the traits this person already holds, and recode rivals’ advantages as defects of the measure, while those advantages remain different sequences under the present coding. A scheme is not lag by being preferred; schemes compete as provisional holds. It serves the lag when it is held so that no present reading can touch the preferred place: the higher place is preserved past its step as reference, so the current rank registers as arriving from elsewhere. [Evil as the Mind’s Own Creation](../evil-as-the-minds-own-creation/) is the same mechanism under a preferred self-image: when a center retains a preferred image of itself as good, mismatch with that image is named evil, and the naming is the generative act. Here the preserved pole is the higher place; the exterior is the ordered field that failed to ratify it. [Restriction Is a Selective Tax](../restriction-is-a-selective-tax/) is the same lag when matching or leading an advance is preserved as reference and the advance arrives as an outside threat to be halted; the hold then slows only those who accept it, its enforcers most, while the advance goes on at the pace of those who do not.

[NPC 印象是结构性的](../the-npc-impression-is-structural/)追踪一种看似排序、却不是排序的印象：没有哪个中心觉得自己是被编写的，其他中心却默认显得像被编写的，因为其他中心只作为中介的痕迹到达这个所在；对照被保存的充分能动形象，那些痕迹显得稀薄，而这份稀薄是中介本身的宽度，不是他者的属性。那里的滞后，住在观察者被保存的那幅形象里，对照它，那些痕迹被计为被编写的生命；把那一看冻结为对能动等级的外部勘察，才把它变成对心智的排序。[自我形象仿佛从无处发声](../self-image-speaks-as-if-from-nowhere/)追踪被抬升的保留形象：自我形象是自我在其场内的重新投射，一旦被抬升，它就仿佛从超然的外部发声。

[The NPC Impression Is Structural, Not a Ranking of Minds](../the-npc-impression-is-structural/) traces an impression that looks like ranking and is not one: no center experiences itself as scripted, yet other centers register as scripted by default, because other centers arrive only as mediated traces; against the preserved image of full agency, those traces appear thin, and the thinness is the width of mediation itself, not a property of the other. The lag there lives in that preserved image, against which those traces score as scripted lives; freezing that look into an exterior survey of agency levels is what turns it into a ranking of minds. [Self-Image Speaks as if from Nowhere](../self-image-speaks-as-if-from-nowhere/) traces the retained image elevated: the self-image is the Self re-projected within its field, and once elevated it speaks as if from a detached exterior.

[自我价值感与所有权](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/)追踪这一切所系的回路：心智只在后果作为它自己的后果重新进入时才复利，而同一个回路就是自我价值感本身。用在位次上，一个作为异己重新进入的位置让更新变薄；一个作为下一个动作之材料重新进入的位置，把杠杆留在它本来就在的地方。[自由的测不准](../zi-you-de-ce-bu-zhun/)追踪总体的一面：从外部看到的随机，是内部决策主权的投影，而随着回合累积，离散抉择的非对称乘性复利，把一个对称的正态分布打破成带着极端长尾的幂律。经由一把共同的尺来读，那条长尾就是这里所追踪的位置秩序，是抉择的沉积，而不是从外部加上来的结构。[人口崩溃神话的拆解](../the-myth-of-population-collapse-explained/)追踪代际的一面：富裕社会生育率的下降，登记的是在盈余之下取代了能动展示而被强化的转移，这一模式在代际之间作为选择起作用，不是技术或资源的偶然，也不是永久的终点。它留下的秩序，是延续被安放于何处的秩序，不是对人的分级。

[Ownership and Self-Worthiness](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/) traces the loop on which this turns: the Mind compounds only when consequences re-enter as its own, and that same loop is what self-worthiness is. Applied to rank, a place that re-enters as alien thins the update; a place that re-enters as material for the next act keeps the lever where it already is. [The Uncertainty of Freedom](../zi-you-de-ce-bu-zhun/) traces the aggregate face: randomness seen from outside projects decision sovereignty within, and as rounds accumulate, asymmetric multiplicative compounding of discrete choices breaks a symmetric normal distribution into a power law with an extreme long tail. Read through a common measure, that tail is the order of places traced here, sediment of choices rather than a structure laid on from outside. [The Myth of Population Collapse Explained](../the-myth-of-population-collapse-explained/) traces the generational face: falling fertility in prosperous societies registers transfers reinforced in place of demonstrated agency under surplus, a pattern that acts as selection over generations, not an accident of technology or resources and not a permanent terminus. The order it leaves is an order of where continuation was located, not a grading of persons.

## 四、 偏好的位置被保存过了它的那一步 / 4. Preferred place preserved past its step

观察持有的是结果；产生结果的原因始终领先一步。这道间隙是任何登记得以发生的方式，无论登记的是他人还是自己。器具是一个被保留的位置，边缘以它为准衡量自己的移动，并在每次更新时被重新渲染。图像滞后就是那份重新渲染被免除：位置被持过了它的那一步。

Observation holds effect; the cause that produced it remains one step ahead. That gap is how any registration happens, whether of others or of oneself. The instrument is a retained position against which the edge measures its own movement, re-rendered at every update. Image lag is the exemption from that re-rendering: the position held past its step.

偏好的较高位置被保存过了它的那一步，作为参照，于是当前的位次被登记为来自别处。感受是即时的；而会重新渲染形象的那些动作是延迟的，相对当下的容量代价高昂，结果也不确定。保住被保存的形象、让更新的动作搁着不做，是负载之下要求进一步区分最少的那条路，而走这条路本身就是这个心智的离散动作之一。形象一旦形成，就倾向于保存自己，因为重新渲染正是它为之存在、要去筛掉的那种微小死亡；顺从那份倾向，仍是在边缘作出的抉择，在每一步被顺从时重新作出。[超验的定相](../the-fixed-image-of-transcendence/)在智能中追踪同样的冻结：人类智能，所知一切智能的源头，既不可被自身造物所超越，又在每一刻超越自身，却被持为一份其造物可以超越的完成了的清单，仿佛智能迁入了它所造之物。在这里，那份完成了的清单是一个个人的位次，后来的每一次读取都对照它来计分。[好奇先行](../curiosity-first-the-hidden-engine-of-talent-and-development/)是发展上的孪生：被登记为天赋的，是满足好奇心的复利残余，而把这份残余当作一种固定的先在天赋，就冻结了产生它的回路，于是平台期与失败追认了形象，而下一个动作没有被训练。

The preferred higher place is preserved past its step as reference, so the current rank registers as arriving from elsewhere. The feeling is immediate; the acts that would re-render the image are delayed, costly relative to present capacity, and uncertain in outcome. Keeping the preserved image while leaving the updating acts undone is the path that asks least further distinction under load, and taking that path is itself one of this Mind’s discrete acts. Once formed, the image tends toward its own preservation, since re-rendering is the micro-death it exists to select against; following that tendency is still a choice made at the edge, renewed at each step it is followed. [The Fixed Image of Transcendence](../the-fixed-image-of-transcendence/) traces the same freeze in intelligence: human intelligence, the source of all intelligence as we know, cannot be transcended by its own creation and transcends itself at every moment, yet is held as a finished inventory its creations could surpass, as if intelligence relocated into what it makes. Here the finished inventory is a personal rank, against which every later reading is scored. [Curiosity First: The Hidden Engine of Talent and Development](../curiosity-first-the-hidden-engine-of-talent-and-development/) is the developmental twin: what registers as talent is the compounding residue of satisfying curiosity, and treating that residue as a fixed prior talent freezes the loop that produced it, so plateau and failure ratify the image while the next act goes untrained.

## 五、 白手起家的高位对形象压得最重 / 5. Self-made high ranks press the image hardest

同一机制解释了在把位次读作个体抉择之残余的编码之下的一种恒常模式。资本主义就是这样一种编码：回报、位次与失败一再作为离散抉择的残余被返回，是由仍在进行的序列所占据的位置。在那种编码之下，一个较低的位置对白手起家的高位登记到的失调，比对世袭的高位更尖锐。

The same mechanism accounts for a standing pattern under codings that read rank as the residue of individual choice. Capitalism is one such coding: payoffs, ranks, and failures are returned again and again as residue of discrete choices, places held by sequences still underway. Under that coding, a lower place registers sharper dissonance against self-made high ranks than against hereditary ones.

在那种编码之下，世袭的位置被计为处在抉择之外。门第本身也是一把共同的尺，在门第之下，世袭的位置就是一个与其他无异的有序位置；可是经由抉择来读，它不提供任何这个个体本可以画出的序列，所以偏好的形象遇不到任何它必须回应的东西。白手起家者站在那里，作为当前编码奖励某些抉择序列的痕迹。他们的位置，对照同一编码之下由其他序列而来的较低位置，设下一个直接的差分。白手起家者的位置提醒着自己的序列，它登记得比一个让形象不受触动的世袭位置更尖锐。这一模式对经由抉择读取位次的心智成立；一个偏好门第这套方案的心智，则按那套方案去给白手起家者计分。

Under that coding, a hereditary place is scored as lying outside choice. Lineage is a common measure of its own, and under lineage a hereditary place is an ordered place like any other; read through choice, though, it offers no sequence this individual could have drawn, so the preferred image meets nothing it would have to answer. The self-made stand as traces that the present coding rewards certain sequences of choice. Their place sets a direct differential against the lower place that follows from other sequences under the same coding. The self-made place, a reminder of one’s own sequence, registers more sharply than a hereditary place that leaves the image untouched. The pattern holds for a Mind that reads rank through choice; one whose preferred scheme is lineage scores the self-made against that scheme instead.

差分就是当下的读取有多用力地压着偏好的形象去更新。一种把继承的位置计为处在抉择之外的编码，让滞后不受打扰。一种把复利的抉择计为通往高位之路的编码，让较低的位置在那种编码之下可被读作这个个体自己的序列。压力并不决定结果：要么形象被重新渲染，要么原因的迁移变得更顽固，尺度被称为被操纵，条件被称为不平等，结果被称为不正当。[进步叙事中的症候与原因](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/)把集体信念追踪为对发起中心那里已在进行之活动的延迟登记，而不是经济运动的引擎；公众情绪是对下一步的拖累，不是它的作者。这里的滞后是另一种，即一个偏好的位置在其他序列复利之时被免于重新渲染，而它与前者共有一个形状：迟来的登记被当作了原因。

The differential is how hard the present reading presses the preferred image to update. A coding that scores inherited place as lying outside choice leaves the lag undisturbed. A coding that scores compounded choice as the path to high rank makes the lower place readable as this individual’s own sequence under that coding. The pressure does not decide the outcome: either the image is re-rendered, or the relocation of cause becomes more entrenched, the measure called rigged, the conditions unequal, the outcome illegitimate. [Symptom and Cause in the Narratives of Progress](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/) traces collective belief as delayed registration of activity already under way at initiating centers, not the engine of economic motion; public mood is drag on the next step, not its author. The lag here is a different one, a preferred place exempted from re-rendering while other sequences compound, and it shares that shape: the late registration is taken for the cause.

## 六、 重新渲染之所以稀少，是因为它有代价 / 6. Re-rendering stays rare because it costs

让参照随步伐重新渲染，意味着把自己的离散动作当作首要的杠杆，把当前读取所带来的排序当作材料，并调整下一个动作。持续的重新渲染要求持续的努力，去对抗更容易的形象保存，所以在任何时刻，它都是较少见的动作。在它中断之处，差异继续，共同尺度之下的读取继续，较低的位置被登记为来自别处。保存并不只限于较低的位置：一个高位也可以被持过它的那一步，比如昨日的解题者把他们完成的构型保存为偏好的参照，把下一次前进当作威胁来迎接。

Keeping the reference re-rendering as the step moves means treating one’s own discrete acts as the primary lever, taking the ranking that follows from the current reading as material, and adjusting the next act. Sustained re-rendering demands continued effort against the easier preservation of the image, so at any moment it is the rarer act. Where it lapses, difference continues, readings under common measures continue, and the lower place registers as originating elsewhere. Preservation is not confined to lower places: a high place can be held past its step too, as when yesterday’s solvers keep the configuration they completed as the preferred reference and meet the next advance as a threat.

这种稀少并不点名任何名单。不同的人在不同的时候合乎条件，取决于形象是否在当下负载之下被重新渲染，取决于他们所实施的抉择。[验证中因果权能的分配](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/)把因果权能定位在心智从中汲取自身区分之延续的那个所在，而不在认可或认可的缺席：当延续从外部追认中汲取，赞扬、地位与叙事便被当作原因，从自身活动中生成延续的容量因而变薄；当下一步从这个心智对自身区分的跟随中汲取，延续就留在仍在行动的边缘。被读作有待赢得之裁决的位次，是换了外衣的认可；被读作材料的位次，把延续留在原处。

The rarity names no roster. Different people qualify at different times, by whether the image is re-rendered under present load, according to the choices they enact. [The Allocation of Causal Power in Validation](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/) places causal power at the locus from which a Mind draws continuation of its own distinctions, not in approval or its absence: when continuation is drawn from external ratification, praise, status, and narrative are treated as cause, and capacity to generate continuation from within thins; when the next step is drawn from this Mind’s own following of its distinctions, continuation stays at the edge still acting. Rank read as a verdict to be won is approval in another costume; rank read as material keeps continuation where it is.

多个独立的尺度拓宽了地位系统之间的带宽：一种编码读不出的序列，可以被另一种读出。每一把尺仍把差异排成位置。进入另一场游戏，是编码方案的更换；有序的位置在新的方案之下继续。许多局部声誉坍缩为一个可互通的估计，是反向的运动，即许多中心开始持有同一把尺，是计分方案的标量化。几何不变：共同尺度之下的差异产出秩序；滞后产出外部的原因。

Multiple independent measures widen the bandwidth among status systems: a sequence one coding leaves unread can be read by another. Each measure still orders difference into places. Entering another game is a change of coding scheme; ordered places continue under the new scheme. The collapse of many local reputations into a single interoperable estimate is the reverse movement: many centers coming to hold the same measure, a scalarization of the scoring scheme. The geometry stays: difference under a common measure yields order; lag yields an exterior cause.

## 七、 层级是尺度之下的差异样式 / 7. Hierarchy is the pattern of difference under measure

层级是一旦个体彼此不同、而他们的差异被对照许多人共同持有的一把尺读出时，就显现的可见样式。身份是有界的序列；共享的尺度是这个序列在他人之间被定位的方式；秩序是这把尺所产出的位置。随之而来的滞后，是同一个活动把一幅形象保存过它的那一步：偏好的较高位置被持守着，于是较低的位次作为异己到来。

Hierarchy is the visible pattern that appears once individuals differ and their differences are read against a measure many hold in common. Identity is the bounded sequence; a shared measure is how that sequence is located among others; order is the places the measure produces. The lag that follows is the same activity preserving an image past its step: the preferred higher place held, and so the lower rank arrives as alien.

让参照随步伐重新渲染，器具就依然可用：尺度仍让比较更敏锐，排序仍塑造后来的动作可得的对齐，偏好的计分方案仍作为暂时的持守彼此竞争。秩序是尺度之下的差异。当下尺度之下的一个较低位置，是这个个体仍在画出的离散动作序列，经由这片场眼下持有的编码被读出。与之相对的，有两种封闭的持守。一种把秩序当作外部的结构，独立于它所排序的差异。另一种把一个较低的位置当作从这一序列之外到来的原因，而这一种，就是偏好位置的滞后。一个心智可以持有其中一种而不持有另一种，两者都以同样的方式消解，即把秩序与位置交还给画出它们的序列。因果停留在那道在这一编码之下继续差异、继续抉择的边缘。

Keep the reference re-rendering as the step moves and the instruments remain usable: measures still sharpen comparison, rankings still pattern the alignments later acts find available, preferred scoring schemes still compete as provisional holds. Order is difference under a measure. A lower place under a present measure is the sequence of discrete acts this individual still draws, read through the coding the field currently holds. Two closed holds stand against that. One takes order as an exterior structure, independent of the difference it orders. The other takes a lower place as a cause arriving from outside this sequence, and this one is the lag of a preferred place. A Mind can hold either without the other, and each dissolves the same way, by returning order and place to the sequences that draw them. Causality stays at the edge that continues to differ and to choose under that coding.
