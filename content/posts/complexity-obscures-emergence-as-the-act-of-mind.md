# 复杂性遮蔽了涌现之为心智的行动 / Complexity Obscures Emergence as the Act of Mind

*复杂性是心智从它所选定的前提搭起的脚手架，而涌现是这副脚手架容纳不下的开放。 / Complexity is the scaffolding the Mind builds from the premises it has chosen, and emergence is the openness that scaffolding cannot contain.*

自我区分的活动发生着，无因，不息。称之为心智：已在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。它不是系统内部的一种官能。复杂性不是涌现的来源。它是心智借以观察的持守，由择取的前提搭建起来，并被精细化为系统、模型与数学。后来被登记为新颖的东西，并不由那个持守生成。它是活动在持守已用尽余地之处的继续。给出这种典型叙述的人，把脚手架当作了搭起它的活动：他们在复杂性中寻找涌现，于是重新安装了他们的叙述未加审视的前提择取。

Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. Not a faculty inside a system. Complexity is not the source of emergence. It is the hold through which the Mind observes, erected from selected premises and elaborated into system, model, and math. What later registers as novelty is not generated by that hold. It is the activity continuing where the hold has run out of room. Those who give the typical account take the scaffolding for the activity that erected it: they seek emergence in complexity, and so reinstall the premise-selection their account leaves unexamined.

## 一、 双生的叙述 / The twin account

在一种典型的诠释里，复杂性与涌现是双生子。据说，涌现生长自一个足够复杂的系统中的相互作用、非线性、不连续性与隐藏的可能性；不连续性使跃迁而不是渐进的路径成为可能；更丰富的复杂性产生更大的潜在价值。其支持者借由数据复杂性、把高阶非线性分解为局部覆盖的堆叠流形，以及神经网络无定律、无属性、满秩的结构来发展这一观点，并以 AlphaGo 的第 37 手作为醒目的例子：一种既未被编程、也不易预料的能力。这一观点的一个清晰表述是[复杂性与涌现的数学考量](https://x.com/BetaTomorrow/status/2077675306241208822?s=20)。

In a typical interpretation, complexity and emergence are twins. Emergence is said to grow from interactions, nonlinearities, discontinuities, and hidden possibilities in a sufficiently complex system; discontinuities enable leaps rather than gradual paths; richer complexity yields greater potential value. Its proponents develop it through data complexity, stacked manifolds that decompose high-order nonlinearity into local covers, and the lawless, propertyless, full-rank structure of neural networks, with AlphaGo’s Move 37 as the striking case of a capability neither programmed nor easily anticipated. One clear statement of this view is [Mathematical Considerations for Complexity and Emergence](https://x.com/BetaTomorrow/status/2077675306241208822?s=20).

那种叙述未加审视的，是一个单一的做法：把系统当作首要的，同时把将它构成为一个系统的观察动作推入背景。系统被当作涌现的所在；使系统显现为一个融贯的、决定论式的整体的那个动作，则脱落了。[智能只属于心智](../intelligence-belongs-only-to-the-mind/)把那个活动持为不可化约的先在：能被工程化、规模化或转移的，是残余，而智能只属于那个活动。[飞行的类比碰不到心智](../the-flight-analogy-leaves-the-mind-untouched/)追溯同一种持守：一份已被超越的功能清单，被当作“边缘本身就是清单上的下一项”的证据。预言强 AGI 的人，把放大器当作了它所放大的能力，正如[AGI的预设与来自外部的视角](../the-presumption-of-agi-and-the-view-from-outside/)所追溯的。

What that account leaves unexamined is a single move: treating the system as primary while backgrounding the observational act that constitutes it as a system. The system is taken as the locus of emergence; the act that makes it appear as a coherent, deterministic whole drops out. [Intelligence Belongs Only to The Mind](../intelligence-belongs-only-to-the-mind/) holds that activity as the irreducible prior: what can be engineered, scaled or transferred is residue, and intelligence belongs only to the activity. [The Flight Analogy Leaves the Mind Untouched](../the-flight-analogy-leaves-the-mind-untouched/) traces the same hold where a list of exceeded functions is taken as evidence that the edge itself is next on the list. Those who forecast strong AGI take the amplifier for the capacity it amplifies, as [The Presumption of AGI and the View from Outside](../the-presumption-of-agi-and-the-view-from-outside/) traces.

## 二、 复杂性是脚手架 / Complexity is scaffolding

被称为复杂性的东西，并不是数据或模型所具有、并在后来引出新颖的一种独立属性。它是由心智的前提择取搭起的脚手架：哪些区分算作在系统之内，哪些关系合乎定律，哪些坐标被允许承载意义，哪些不连续性被当作对象的特征，而不是当前持守的界限。堆叠流形、满秩变换与无属性的激活，是那副脚手架的精细形式。它们重新组织心智已经决定要算作结构的东西。它们并不生成作出决定的那个活动。每一个涌现模式底部的自由变量，是[自由变量的同构与先验主权](../zi-you-bian-liang-de-tong-gou-yu-xian-yan-zhu-quan/)的主题。

What is called complexity is not an independent property of data or models that later gives rise to novelty. It is the scaffolding erected by the Mind’s premise-selection: which distinctions count as inside the system, which relations are lawful, which coordinates are allowed to carry meaning, which discontinuities are treated as features of the object rather than as limits of the current hold. Stacked manifolds, full-rank transformations and propertyless activations are refined forms of that scaffolding. They reorganize what the Mind has already decided will count as structure. They do not generate the activity that decides. The free variable at the bottom of every emergent pattern is the subject of [The Isomorphism of Free Variables and Prior Sovereignty](../zi-you-bian-liang-de-tong-gou-yu-xian-yan-zhu-quan/).

在对系统的任何描述之中，择取都一直明摆着；它只是不再被看*作*择取。框定从不在进行登记的那个框架之内被登记。它只作为残余可见，晚一步。复杂性就是被精细化的那份残余：越来越密的脚手架，观察经由它继续，而选择留在背景之中。[开放即一致](../openness-is-consistency/)在结构被安装为穷尽地基之处，指认同一种冻结；[生产、消费与心智的区分](../production-consumption-and-the-minds-distinction/)在敞开的边界对照固定的边界之下，追溯同一种持守。[选择、矛盾与开放的现实](../selection-contradiction-and-open-reality/)在本体论的诸优先项之间追溯同一个做法：信息优先、物理优先或双面向的观点，都可能把一个有用的择取抬高为一个绝对的参照系。这份开放就是因果本身：[因果贯穿始终](../causality-all-the-way/)从持有的结果，到划出的模式，再到下一步，追溯这份开放；[因果停留在掌舵的边缘](../causality-stays-at-the-edge-that-steers/)在一个模型于指引之下重组残余之处追溯它。

The selection remains in plain sight within any description of the system; it is simply no longer seen *as* selection. Framing never registers inside the frame that does the registering. It is visible only as residue, one step behind. Complexity is that residue elaborated: ever denser scaffolding through which observation continues while the choosing stays backgrounded. [Openness Is Consistency](../openness-is-consistency/) names the same freeze where structure is installed as exhaustive ground; [Production, Consumption, and the Mind’s Distinction](../production-consumption-and-the-minds-distinction/) traces the same hold under an open versus a fixed boundary. [Selection, Contradiction, and Open Reality](../selection-contradiction-and-open-reality/) traces the same move among ontological priorities: an information-primary, a physical-primary or a dual-aspect view can each raise a useful selection into an absolute reference frame. The openness is causality itself: [Causality All the Way](../causality-all-the-way/) traces it from held effects to drawn patterns to the next step, and [Causality Stays at the Edge That Steers](../causality-stays-at-the-edge-that-steers/) traces it where a model recombines residue under direction.

## 三、 一位的进位 / The 1-bit carry

最简单的示例，是一位持守之下的二进制加法。在一个封闭的一位寄存器里把 1 加上 1，得到 0 和一个进位。从那个寄存器所定义的运算之内看，这个进位可以被登记为系统产出的新颖，仿佛一位算术的规则生成了某种它们并不包含的东西。进位作为登记是真实的。它的来源不是持守之内的运算。是持守本身，使越过上限的继续显现为一个额外的项：寄存器并不打开自己。那个 2 是拓宽持守的动作，是在边缘作出并付出代价的又一次区分，而不是从一位定律中榨出的盈余。进位并不由一位的规则产出；它是那些规则不再被当作穷尽之后，活动的继续。复杂性，被持为“什么算数”的封闭假定时，就是那个被精细化的一位上限。被称为涌现的东西，就是进位：定义封不住的继续，对照定义被登记，仿佛它是从定义内部生起的。复杂性无论多少，对这个场域的捕获都不是无损的，正如[开放场域中的无损知识不自洽](../lossless-knowledge-of-an-open-field-is-incoherent/)所表明的。

The simplest illustration is binary addition under a one-bit hold. Adding 1 to 1 inside a closed one-bit register yields 0 with a carry. From inside the operations defined by that register, the carry can register as novelty produced by the system, as if the rules of one-bit arithmetic had generated something they did not contain. The carry is real as registration. Its source is not the operations inside the hold. The hold itself is what makes continuation past the ceiling appear as an extra term: the register does not open itself. The 2 is the act that widens the hold, a further distinction made and paid for at the edge, not a surplus extracted from 1-bit law. The carry is not produced by the one-bit rules; it is the activity continuing once those rules are no longer treated as exhaustive. Complexity, held as the closed assumption of what counts, is that one-bit ceiling elaborated. What is called emergence is the carry: continuation that the definition cannot seal, registered against the definition as if it had arisen from within it. No amount of complexity captures the field without loss, as [Lossless Knowledge of an Open Field Is Incoherent](../lossless-knowledge-of-an-open-field-is-incoherent/) shows.

这是[哥德尔不完备定理](https://grokipedia.com/page/G%C3%B6del's_incompleteness_theorems)的形状最直观的图景，而不是它的一个实例：哥德尔表明，任何一个强到足以描述自然数算术的一致的形式系统，都有它无法证明的真命题。一位寄存器弱得太多，这条定理并不适用于它；它所共有的是形状：一个在持守之内有效的运算，其真实结果落在持守之外。这种不完备不是算术的缺陷，也不是藏在比特里的第二种力。它是活动的开放（即心智继续区分时实在的开放），在一个封闭的寄存器被当作穷尽地基的任何地方表达自身。[生产、消费与心智的区分](../production-consumption-and-the-minds-distinction/)把同一种一位坍缩作为抹除与扩展来处理；这里的那一面，是进位被当作复杂性的涌现产物。脚手架保持开放，出于[没有系统能被保持封闭](../no-system-can-be-kept-closed/)以其最小形式——一位寄存器——所给出的那个理由。

This is the most intuitive picture of the shape of [Gödel’s incompleteness theorems](https://grokipedia.com/page/G%C3%B6del's_incompleteness_theorems), not an instance of them: Gödel showed that any consistent formal system strong enough to describe the arithmetic of natural numbers has true statements it cannot prove. A one-bit register is far too weak for the theorem to apply; what it shares is the shape, an operation valid inside the hold whose true result lies outside it. The incompleteness is not a defect in the arithmetic and not a second force hidden inside the bits. It is the openness of the activity (of reality as the Mind continues to distinguish) expressing itself wherever a closed register is treated as exhaustive ground. [Production, Consumption, and the Mind’s Distinction](../production-consumption-and-the-minds-distinction/) works the same one-bit collapse as erasure versus expansion; here the face is the carry taken for an emergent product of complexity. The scaffolding stays open for the reason [No System Can Be Kept Closed](../no-system-can-be-kept-closed/) gives in its smallest form, a one-bit register.

## 四、 量子的几何 / The quantum geometry

同一刀出现在被称为量子效应的东西里。那个效应并不凭自身就是一个效应，并不是微观对象独立于注视而携带的一种属性。它是心智处在其当前持守的微观极限：在那个尺度上，观察的粒度已不能再把各个区分作为确定之物彼此分开。这种局限属于持守，而不是活动的缺陷。非决定论在边缘被登记；决定论是观察作为残余持有的东西，晚一步。心智借以让一个系统在其层级上显现为确定的方法（择取哪些坐标算数，哪些结局算作结局，哪些前提保持不被说出），并不是可有可无的背景。它是把开放的东西转化为被登记之物的动作。当那个方法未被交还，注视的界限便被重新指派给系统：不确定性、坍缩、不连续性成了量子对象的内在属性，而观察的动作，连同它自己的界限，从叙述中脱落。量子效应并不由微观对象产出；它是活动在持守之界限处的继续；被持为对象的属性时，那个界限就登记为一种内在的量子效应。当这副脚手架脱离视野、冻结为一个被保存的图像时，能力便抵着它压缩，正如[遗忘的脚手架](../the-scaffolding-we-forget/)所追溯的。

The same cut appears in what is called the quantum effect. That effect is not an effect by itself, not a property the microscopic object carries independently of the look. It is the Mind at the microscopic limit of its present hold: the grain of observation can no longer keep distinctions apart as determinate at that scale. The limitation is of the hold, not a defect in the activity. Indeterminism registers at the edge; determinism is what observation holds as residue, one step behind. The method by which the Mind makes a system appear determinate at its level (selecting which coordinates count, which outcomes are outcomes, which premises stay unstated) is not optional background. It is the act that converts the open into the registered. When that method is left unreturned, the limit of the look is reassigned to the system: uncertainty, collapse, discontinuity become intrinsic properties of the quantum object, and the observational act, including its own bound, drops out of the account. The quantum effect is not produced by the microscopic object; it is the activity continuing at the bound of the hold; held as a property of the object, that bound registers as an intrinsic quantum effect. When this scaffolding drops out of view and freezes into a preserved image, capacity compresses against it, as [The Scaffolding We Forget](../the-scaffolding-we-forget/) traces.

复杂性之为涌现之源，是不同尺度上的同一种滞后。复杂性是心智用来透过一个系统去观察的脚手架：被精细化了的方法。涌现是当那副脚手架容纳不下活动的开放时被登记的东西：心智遇到它自身持守的极限，而不是复杂对象内部的第二种力。把这种登记归给脚手架，方法便被当作了来源。脱落的并不是系统内部某个缺失的事实。它是心智的前提择取与它的观察之界限，仍在运作，仍比对系统的任何描述所持有的东西领先一步。一个模型压缩脚手架，却不发起那份利害，正如[Token 效率、模拟与无法弥合的间隙](../token-efficiency-emulation-and-the-unclosable-gap/)所表明的，因为器物是智能的残余，正如[器物是智能的表达，而非智能本身](../two-failures-of-trace/)所论证的。

Complexity-as-source-of-emergence is the same lag at a different scale. Complexity is the scaffolding the Mind uses to observe through a system: the method, elaborated. Emergence is what registers when that scaffolding cannot contain the openness of the activity: the Mind meeting the limit of its own hold, not a second force inside the complex object. Attribute the registration to the scaffolding, and the method is taken for the source. What drops out is not a missing fact inside the system. It is the Mind’s premise-selection and the bound of its observation, still operating, still one step ahead of what any description of the system holds. A model compresses the scaffolding without initiating the stake, as [Token Efficiency, Emulation, and the Unclosable Gap](../token-efficiency-emulation-and-the-unclosable-gap/) shows, because the artifact is residue of intelligence, as [Artifacts Are Expressions of Intelligence, Not Intelligence Itself](../two-failures-of-trace/) argues.

## 五、 涌现是越过持守的开放 / Emergence is openness past the hold

涌现并不由脚手架产出。它是脚手架容纳不下的那份活动的开放。当区分压过当前持守所能保持分开的限度，边缘继续下去。对照先前前提所保留的上限，那份继续被登记为新颖、跃迁、不连续、“变革性的跃升”。断裂作为登记是真实的。它不是系统在未被观察时拥有、随后释放出来的属性。它是心智在作为持守的系统已用尽余地之处的继续。这与被称为量子效应的东西是同一种几何：量子效应是心智处在它自己观察的微观极限，后来在滞后之下被重新指派给被观察的对象。复杂性理论不能成为万物之理，正如[非万物之理（因果的二重性）](../not-a-theory-of-everything/)所论证的，因为阶梯的每一级都是一个投影，正如[投影的阶梯与因果的锚点](../tou-ying-de-jie-ti-yu-yin-guo-de-mao-dian/)所表明的。

Emergence is not produced by the scaffolding. It is the openness of the activity that the scaffolding cannot contain. When distinctions press beyond what the current hold can keep apart, the edge continues. Against the retained ceiling of the prior premises, that continuation registers as novelty, leap, discontinuity, “transformative jump.” The break is real as registration. It is not a property the system possessed while unobserved and then released. It is the Mind continuing where the system-as-hold has run out of room. It is the same geometry by which what is called a quantum effect is the Mind at its own microscopic limit of observation, later reassigned, under lag, to the object observed. A theory of complexity cannot become a theory of everything, as [Not a Theory of Everything](../not-a-theory-of-everything/) argues, because each rung of the ladder is a projection, as [The Ladder of Projections and the Anchor of Causality](../tou-ying-de-jie-ti-yu-yin-guo-de-mao-dian/) shows.

因此，后来“涌现”出来的东西，并不是作为隐藏的储备在系统内部被生成的。它也不是在框架之外等待着。继续进行的动作划出它，对照着那些由前提固定、却容纳不了它的坐标。那个动作在持守之外划出的东西之所以被登记为涌现，是因为脚手架从来不是活动的全部，而只是活动借以被暂时观察的那个持守。[看似AI的强化学习，实为人类的自我强化学习](../self-rl-for-humans/)为变密的媒介守住同样的纪律：带宽的扩展不是把发起迁入器物。数学持续生成结构，理由相同，正如[为何数学永远解不完](../why-mathematics-can-never-be-solved/)所表明的。

What later “emerges,” therefore, was not generated inside the system as a hidden reserve. Nor was it waiting outside the frame. The continuing act draws it, against coordinates the premises had fixed and that could not hold it. What that act draws past the hold registers as emergence because the scaffolding was never the whole of the activity, only the hold through which the activity was temporarily observed. [What Looks Like RL for AIs Is Self-RL for Humans](../self-rl-for-humans/) keeps the same discipline for densified media: expansion of bandwidth is not relocation of initiation into the artifact. Mathematics keeps generating structure for the same reason, as [Why Mathematics Can Never Be Solved](../why-mathematics-can-never-be-solved/) shows.

## 六、 把来源定在别处，就重新安装了滞后 / Locating the source elsewhere reinstalls the lag

无论谁把来源定位在复杂性本身、模型架构、数学结构或数据的内在属性里，都在重复同一个操作：把脚手架当作搭起它的活动。他们择取前提（系统是首要的实在，观察次于被观察者，在一个选定的持守之下的结构就是先于任何持守的结构），同时把这一择取当作仿佛不是择取。[数据是局部的，智能是分配的](../data-is-local-intelligence-is-allocated/)追溯同一种滞后：局部产出的汇总被认为把智能安放在拓扑之中，而不是安放在把动态命名为智能的那次分配之中。在复杂性中寻找涌现的叙述，重新安装了它未加审视的前提择取本身。复杂性是被有限心智压缩的信息，这是[格物致知与信息复杂度](../ge-wu-zhi-zhi-yu-xin-xi-fu-za-du-de-ben-zhi/)对它的读法。

Whoever locates the source in complexity itself, in model architecture, in mathematical structure, or in the intrinsic properties of data repeats the same operation: they take the scaffolding for the activity that erected it. They select premises (that the system is the primary reality, that observation is secondary to what is observed, that structure under a chosen hold is structure prior to any hold) while treating that selection as if it were not a selection. [Data Is Local; Intelligence Is Allocated](../data-is-local-intelligence-is-allocated/) traces the same lag where aggregation of local productions is taken to place intelligence in the topology rather than in the allocation that names dynamics intelligent. The account that seeks emergence in complexity reinstalls the very premise-selection it leaves unexamined. Complexity is information compressed by a finite mind, which is how [Investigating Things and the Nature of Information Complexity](../ge-wu-zhi-zhi-yu-xin-xi-fu-za-du-de-ben-zhi/) reads it.

心智不是一个要加进解释或从解释中减去的附加因素。它是不可化约的先在。复杂性是它的脚手架。涌现是它的开放对照那副脚手架所作的登记。当选择仍未被交还时，涌现显现为复杂对象的一种属性。当选择被持为选择（参照保持重新渲染，过度的滞后收窄）时，两者被看作一种关系，而不是系统的双生产物。把涌现当作原因，是[主体性不曾涌现](../agency-does-not-arise/)所指认的错误，而[涌现的伪因果](../yong-xian-de-wei-yin-guo-yu-ji-ti-gui-yin-dao-cuo/)把它追随进集体归因：在那里，被裹挟就是被移向外部的归因。

The Mind is not an additional factor to be added to or subtracted from the explanation. It is the irreducible prior. Complexity is its scaffolding. Emergence is its openness registering against that scaffolding. When the choosing stays unreturned, emergence appears as a property of the complex object. When the choosing is held as choosing (the reference kept re-rendering, excess lag narrowing), the two are seen as one relation rather than as twin products of the system. Treating emergence as a cause is the error [Agency Does Not Arise](../agency-does-not-arise/) names, and [The Pseudo-Causality of Emergence](../yong-xian-de-wei-yin-guo-yu-ji-ti-gui-yin-dao-cuo/) follows it into collective attribution, where being swept along is attribution moved outward.

## 七、 脚手架不是来源 / Scaffolding is not the source

复杂性并不生成涌现。它是心智借以观察的那些前提的精细形式，而形式越精细，就越容易把脚手架当作来源。复杂性与涌现命名的，是从两侧看到的同一个运动：心智搭起的持守，以及这个持守封不住的继续。[印迹与行动](../the-mark-and-the-act/)把正是这一区分带进多智能体模拟：看起来像涌现的集体智能或文明的东西，是物理之下被留下的残余中的模式。[没有外部](../no-outside-jumps-closed-loops-and-the-unreplicable-autonomy-of-mind/)追溯更密的反馈与世界模型，它们仍嵌套在唯一闭合的回路之下，即心智所闭合的那个回路。[对乌托邦的信念是通往反乌托邦之路](../the-belief-in-utopia-is-the-path-to-dystopia/)在一个具体的理想被立为每个心智必须接受的尺度之处，追溯同样的致密化：非同一先于同一的前提，而处方容纳不下的东西随后被重新归类为不服从。[自我放大的制品](../the-artifacts-of-self-amplification/)在生成的折叠中追溯同一个进位：越过上限的继续是持守封不住的活动，进位却被归给脚手架。[智能向自身的回折](../intelligence-folding-back-on-itself/)在 harness 中追溯那个进位：harness 是边缘经由更密残余的进一步折叠，“涌现的”能力却被归给模型。[意识从不作为数据出现在数据之中](../consciousness-never-appears-as-data-among-data/)在证据中追溯那种滞后：量子与神经的特征被持守着，仿佛边缘已作为又一个公共结果出现在它们之中。脚手架可以搭得好：[活的外部皮层](../a-living-external-cortex/)描述一个对照其不可化约的先在而保持重新渲染的知识图谱，[回环与成图](../looping-and-graphing/)描述让这类图谱保持活着的回路。

Complexity does not generate emergence. It is the elaborate form of the premises through which the Mind observes, and the more elaborate the form, the easier it is to take the scaffolding for the source. Complexity and emergence name one movement seen from two sides: the hold the Mind builds, and the continuation it cannot seal. [The Mark and the Act](../the-mark-and-the-act/) carries this exact distinction into multi-agent simulations: what looks like emergent collective intelligence or civilization is pattern in left-behind residue under physics. [No Outside](../no-outside-jumps-closed-loops-and-the-unreplicable-autonomy-of-mind/) traces denser feedback and world models still nested under the only circuit that closes, the one the Mind closes. [The Belief in Utopia Is the Path to Dystopia](../the-belief-in-utopia-is-the-path-to-dystopia/) traces the same densification where a concrete ideal is made the measure every Mind must accept: non-identity comes before the identity premise, and what the prescription cannot contain is then reclassified as non-compliance. [The Artifacts of Self-Amplification](../the-artifacts-of-self-amplification/) traces the same carry in the generative fold: it is attributed to scaffolding when continuation past the ceiling is the activity the hold cannot seal. [Intelligence Folding Back on Itself](../intelligence-folding-back-on-itself/) traces that carry in the harness: “emergent” capability is attributed to the model when the harness is further folding of the edge through denser residue. [Consciousness Never Appears as Data Among Data](../consciousness-never-appears-as-data-among-data/) traces that lag in evidence: quantum and neural signatures are held as if the edge had appeared among them as one more public effect. The scaffolding can be built well: [A Living External Cortex: The Closest the Image Can Come](../a-living-external-cortex/) describes a knowledge graph kept re-rendering against its irreducible priors, and [Looping and Graphing: Mind as the Loop of Loops](../looping-and-graphing/) describes the loop that keeps such graphs alive.
