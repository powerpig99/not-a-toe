# 剩余个人主义 / Residual Individualism

*唯有当下约束中的抉择动作是独立的杠杆；集体模式与保护体系皆是其残余。 / Only the choosing act under present constraints is an independent lever; patterns and protective systems are its residue.*

面对单纯的财富再分配，一种常见的改良主张提出：社会应当超越存量争夺，转向做大蛋糕、扩大生产。然而，这种转向并未触及更深层的认知固化：将蛋糕视作固定总量的思维，其根源既非单纯的物质稀缺，亦非政策制定上的局部失当，而在于心智将注意力持续锚定于集体本身，将其当成了独立承载能力的实体。一旦撤回对这一抽象集合的实体化凝视，真正有效的因果杠杆便唯独剩下离散个体在当下约束中所采取的剩余动作。同样的能动性外置，在人们试图将自由托付给一套宣称能够守护自由的保护性体系时，又会以相反的形态重新出现。

A familiar improvement on pure redistribution proposes that society should cease contending over existing allocations and focus instead on expanding production. Yet this shift leaves a deeper cognitive fixation untouched: the fixed-pie mentality originates neither in material scarcity nor in bad policy, but in the persistent fixation of attention on the collective itself as a substantive bearer of capacity. Once this reified gaze is set aside, the only operative causal levers that remain are the residual acts executed by discrete individuals under local constraints. This very same externalization recurs wherever freedom is entrusted to an institutional system designed to secure it.

## 一、 固定蛋糕的思维始于对集体的关注 / 1. Fixed-Pie Thinking Begins as Collective Focus

自我区分的活动在发生，无因，不息。称它为心智：那个已经在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。这一活动始终发生于具体的、离散的能动所在。每个所在各自执行局部的区分；先前的动作单向度地构塑着后续动作所面对的场域。这种时间与结构上的不对称性，经由心智自身的递归而被登记下来，这便是因果——它并不是某种能够脱离具体行动者、被整体移植到“社会”、“蛋糕”或某种被拟人化之共同存量中的实体关系。

Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. It occurs at discrete loci of agency. Each locus draws local distinctions; prior acts unilaterally shape the field available to subsequent ones, running in a single temporal direction. Causality is simply this activity registering that asymmetry through its own ongoing recursion. It is never a standalone relation that can be relocated into a society, an aggregate pie, or a shared stock treated as an autonomous agent.

一旦将“蛋糕”作为审视的基本单位，无论是划分存量还是做大规模，都被归置为对同一集体对象的操作。在此视角下，生产被误记为整体系统的固有属性，而非个体在时间序列中相继采取的离散动作——那些真正生成可用构型、拓宽现实选择的动作。一个固定不变的总量，恰是这种整体视角的自然产物：它将资源预设为先于个体行动而存在的静态存量，单等集体机制去切割或统筹。[生产、消费与心智的区分](../production-consumption-and-the-minds-distinction/)揭示了这套相同的封闭几何：一旦心智将边界闭合，同样的创造性活动便被登记为从有限存量中的扣除。在[当丰裕看起来像稀缺](../when-abundance-looks-like-scarcity/)中，这种存量假设表现为置换与挤出的叙事：只要蛋糕被预设为固定不变，生产力的任何爆发式跃升，都会在机械计算中被登记为对既有份额的挤占与破坏。生产始于离散的能动所在，消费则是随之而来的衍生回报；正如[秩序、剩余与道德拦截](../order-surplus-and-the-moral-intercept/)所追溯的，道德语言的介入将因果权能后置于消费端，把派生的回报颠倒为行动的引擎。所谓的“个人主义”与“集体主义”，充其量是分别锚定在产出保留与集体再分配两端的道德化修辞；真正的分水岭，在于注意力是否依然将集体当作能力的原始承载者。

When the primary unit of evaluation is “the pie,” redistribution and expansion both register as operations performed upon a collective entity. Production is then scored as an innate property of the aggregate whole rather than as successive discrete acts that leave preferred configurations available for others to use. The fixed total is the natural image of that unit: an imagined stock presumed to precede the local distinctions that densify it, awaiting administrative division or managerial enlargement. [Production, Consumption, and the Mind’s Distinction](../production-consumption-and-the-minds-distinction/) dissects this same closed geometry: once the Mind holds its boundary as closed, the very same creative activity registers as subtraction from a finite stock. In [When Abundance Looks Like Scarcity](../when-abundance-looks-like-scarcity/), this stock assumption articulates itself through the logic of displacement: so long as the aggregate pie is held as fixed, any surge in productive capability is mechanically registered as the crowding out and loss of existing livelihoods. Production originates at discrete loci of agency, with consumption following as its downstream return; as [Order, Surplus, and the Moral Intercept](../order-surplus-and-the-moral-intercept/) traces, moral language intervenes by relocating causal power onto the consumption side, mistaking downstream returns for the driving engine. Individualism and collectivism are largely moralized vocabularies anchored to opposite poles of that loop—one defending retention at the point of origin, the other justifying redistribution to a collective object. The deeper cognitive fixation remains the habitual placement of attention on the collective as the prime bearer of capacity.

## 二、 把责任与体系改革配在一起，就挪走了杠杆 / 2. Responsibility Paired with System Reform Relocates the Lever

在公共讨论中，“个人责任”常与改变集体规则的呼吁并驾齐驱——无论这些规则被冠以规章、体制、文化规范还是“系统结构”之名。然而，这种貌似公允的折衷，恰恰悄然挪移了能动性的支点。一个具体行动者在其实际遭遇的客观约束下做出何种抉择，是其唯一能够真实施加影响的领域。任何将重心锚定在规范他人举止或重塑集体形态的改良方案，都不可避免地将注意力抽离那个唯一具有实际裁量权的自性所在。

In public discourse, personal responsibility is routinely invoked alongside calls to restructure collective arrangements—whether framed as regulations, institutional designs, cultural norms, or “the system.” Yet this pairing quietly relocates the fulcrum of responsibility. What an individual chooses to do within the concrete constraints actually encountered constitutes the sole domain of action. Any project whose center of gravity resides in governing the conduct of others, or reshaping collective patterns, inevitably diverts attention away from the locus still choosing.

[个体抉择是唯一的因果杠杆](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/)确立了这一更宽广的基准：宏观总量、外部条件与集体模式，皆是千百次离散动作沉淀下的残余，不是与个体共同执笔历史的“第二作者”。在社会场景中，退而求其次地宣称“一半归因于个人努力，一半归咎于体制环境”，是在唯一引擎持续做功之处装上了一个二元假象。集体模式固然能给行动者施加切实的阻力或代价，但这层代价构成的只是展开行动的场域环境，不是驱动行动的平行引擎。[财富转移政策与价格下限中的原因错置](../the-reversal-of-causality-in-wealth-transfer-policies-and-price-floors/)展现了这种因果倒错在经济干预中的体现：试图调和“一半靠相对信号，一半靠政策法令”的折衷主义，忽视了法定下限与转移支付本身并无独立的创生能动性；停滞源于行动者错误地将政策法令当成因果源头，从而屏蔽了引导技能演进与自我纠偏的相对价格信号。同样，[工时排名把充足度冻结成一张记分牌](../the-hours-ranking-freezes-adequacy-as-one-scoreboard/)揭示了度量指标如何被物化为因果推力：将法定最低工资、相对贫困线与福利收缩折算为一张工时记分牌，忽视了福利收缩正是直接压低下一小时净收益的结构机制，反而将记分牌的高读数物化为“保障不足”的明证，用以辩护进一步的转移支付。把自发协作与不侵犯的涌现模式事后命名为“权利”，继而要求一套外部体系加以保护与监管，正是[能动性的错配](../the-misallocation-of-agency/)所揭示的因果倒错。

[Individual Choices as the Only Causal Levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) establishes the wider principle: statistical aggregates, background conditions, and cultural patterns are historical residue of discrete acts, never co-authors alongside them. In the social sphere, softening this into “part personal, part systemic” fabricates a dual-engine fiction precisely where only one locus continues to act. Collective patterns certainly impose palpable friction and material costs on any single agent; yet those costs define the terrain of the field, not a rival driving engine. [The Misallocation of Cause in Wealth-Transfer Policies and Price Floors](../the-reversal-of-causality-in-wealth-transfer-policies-and-price-floors/) demonstrates this reversal in economic regulation: attempting to reconcile “part signal, part decree” ignores that statutory floors and transfer payments possess no independent generative agency; stagnation deepens because actors mistakenly treat legislative decrees as primary causes, muffling the relative price signals that would otherwise inform skill adaptation and self-correction. In like manner, [The Hours Ranking Freezes Adequacy as One Scoreboard](../the-hours-ranking-freezes-adequacy-as-one-scoreboard/) analyzes how evaluative metrics become reified: compressing the minimum wage, the relative poverty threshold, and welfare benefit phase-out into a single hours scoreboard obscures how benefit withdrawal directly depresses the net gain from an additional hour of work; the resulting hours bar is then reified as proof of “inadequacy” to justify further transfers. Naming the emergent pattern of non-aggression and coordination “rights,” and subsequently demanding an external system to enforce and guard it, is the exact causal inversion traced in [The Misallocation of Agency](../the-misallocation-of-agency/).

## 三、 模式是重复的选择，不是独立的力量 / 3. Patterns Are Repeated Choices, Not Independent Forces

规章、法律框架以及社会期待，本身不具备独立的因果驱动力。它们不过是无数个体在起草、执行、遵从或索求过程中逐渐凝结的致密残余。对置身其中的任何单个行动者而言，既定模式确实会带来真实的沉重代价；但就发起而言，模式始终只是表象症状——是诸多能动中心将抉择权向外让渡、达成统一程式后的稳态对齐。[主权、信念与规制结构的生成](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/)刻画了这种残余硬化为制度的路径：稳固的科层结构不过是“个体主权能够向外委托”这一先验假设的具象显现，它从未真正升格为一个能够凭空发端的超级能动主体。[总在倾听者无法发起](../what-always-listens-cannot-originate/)指认了同一机理的另一种显现：就如同擅长倾听并顺从一切输入的智能模型一样，对外界反馈的高敏响应与流利吞吐，容易被误判为自主目的与初始动力的发生源。

Regulatory strictures, statutory frameworks, and ambient social expectations possess no independent causal power. They are the densified residue of discrete individuals who author, enforce, comply with, or demand them. For any single agent operating within their orbit, an established pattern certainly levies genuine costs; but in terms of origination, the pattern remains an outcome and a symptom—the stabilized convergence of multiple choosing centers externalizing their agency into shared procedures. [Sovereignty, Belief, and the Generation of Regulatory Structures](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/) charts how this residue hardens into institutional reality: enduring bureaucracies merely reflect the widespread prior belief that individual sovereignty can be delegated away; they do not perform a transcendent super-locus of initiation. [What Always Listens Cannot Originate](../what-always-listens-cannot-originate/) isolates this exact error in another guise: much like an artificial model whose fluency derives from continuous receptivity to prior inputs, exhaustive responsiveness is easily mistaken for an independent fountainhead of intention.

既然既定模式仅是行动的副产品与症状，那么凡是将外部约束作为言说、身份认同与行动核心坐标的有组织抗争，极易在无形中巩固其所反对的客体。约束之所以稳固，是因为它持续占据着注意力的焦点。即便外部模式日后发生了演变，其动力根源仍不过是无数独立个体在微观抉择上的静默迁移；然而，行动主义便被登记为这一结果的创造者，尽管该转变从未离开过微观个体的行动层面。[进步叙事中的症候与原因](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/)指认了文化尺度上的这种归因倒错：公共场域中那份迟到却喧嚣的集体情绪与叙事登记，被错当成了推动经济演进的实质引擎。[共同改变的本源](../the-source-of-shared-change/)同样揭示了这种时间差的误导：与过去的残余正面搏斗，反倒将残余固化为了注意力的中心；唯有直面既定现实展开具体建构，才能在行动中有效拓展新的生存空间。而在政治领域，[政客是责任扩散的显性症状](../politicians-appear-as-visible-symptoms-of-responsibility-diffusion/)阐明了追逐表面焦点的徒劳：台前备受瞩目的政治人物不过是大众推卸自身责任所投射出的具象标记，若执着于将这些认领者作为变革的对象，便错过了早在上游便已落定的决策链条。

Because established patterns are merely symptoms, organized opposition that anchors its vocabulary, identity, and exertion around the constraint inadvertently reinforces the very structure it opposes. The constraint endures so long as it remains the focal point of collective attention. When the pattern eventually shifts, that evolution is driven not by the abstract confrontation itself, but by uncoordinated reorientations across the choices of many separate individuals; yet activism then registers as author of an outcome that never departed from the granular realm of individual behavior. [Symptom and Cause in the Narratives of Progress](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/) diagnoses this causal inversion at a cultural scale: loud, retrospective collective narratives are mistaken for the driving engine of economic motion. [The Source of Shared Change](../the-source-of-shared-change/) elucidates the same operational lag: engaging in combative struggle against sedimented residue elevates that residue to the center of concern, whereas pragmatic working within the present field quietly expands the horizon of what is possible. Similarly, [Politicians Are Visible Symptoms of Responsibility Diffusion](../politicians-appear-as-visible-symptoms-of-responsibility-diffusion/) captures this misdirected attribution in the political sphere: prominent political figures are merely the visible markers of a broader diffusion of responsibility, and targeting them as the primary lever of change ignores the decisive sequence of choices enacted far upstream.

## 四、 当行动的延续超越约束，约束本身便会松动 / 4. Constraints Loosen When Continuation Outpaces Them

外部约束并非不可逾越的刚性铁板。当个体选择通过不间断的行动延续去超越它们——默默承受现实摩擦、主动探索变通路径、创造出固有规则未曾预料的增量价值时——约束本身的禁锢便会随之瓦解。跨越约束，从来不需要让约束如影随形地占据视野的前景；恰恰是执着于与之交锋，才必须赋予其永久的存在感。二者的核心分野，在于注意力究竟锚定于自身的创造还是外在的禁锢，而非一套消极不抵抗的说辞。在当下真实的场域中专注于自我意志的延续，正是不可消解的剩余能动性在生动运转；反之，若不断将约束推至舞台中心、将其树立为集体博弈的终极靶标，则不过是披上抗争外衣的又一次能动性外让。

Constraints are never immutable absolutes. They dissolve when individuals outpace them through continuous, pragmatic action—absorbing friction, devising creative workarounds, and generating genuine value unanticipated by existing rules. Transcending a constraint does not require keeping it perpetually in the foreground; it is fighting it that demands its permanent magnification. The distinction turns on the placement of attention rather than any philosophy of passive acquiescence. Sustaining forward motion within the immediate field is the living demonstration of residual agency at work. By contrast, perpetually re-centering the constraint as the monument of collective contestation is simply another externalization of agency disguised as defiance.

## 五、 为自由设计保护体系，同样会导致杠杆移位 / 5. Protective Designs for Freedom Relocate the Lever in the Same Way

同样的能动性外置，也频繁显现于许多自诩为“个人主义者”的人身上。当人们将构建、推广或强制推行一套自上而下的保护性体系——不论该体系属于宪政法理、制度制衡还是文化规约——视作个体自由成立的前提要件时，因果能动性便已然从活生生的当下抉择悄然移交给了体系架构。人工设计的制度安排反客为主，被奉为使个体行动得以成立或免于受创的母体。在这一瞬间，个体再度沦为被动的衍生品，自我抉择的责任再次被卸载给了一个抽象的集体模式。

A parallel externalization frequently appears among those who call themselves individualists. To treat the architecture, propagation, or imposition of a protective system—whether constitutional, institutional, or cultural—as the indispensable prerequisite for individual liberty is to strip living choices of causal efficacy and confer it upon the scaffolding itself. The designed arrangement is elevated as the benevolent source that makes individual action viable or secure. At that juncture, the individual is once more relegated to a secondary derivative, and responsibility is again displaced onto an abstract collective schema.

[自由的心智被声称保护它的东西取代](../the-free-mind-is-displaced-by-what-claims-to-protect-it/)记录了思想史中屡见不鲜的收编悖论：人类偶或窥见自由心智的澄明光芒，却旋即将其回缚于宣称庇护它的庞大体制之中。这种保护主义的机理，在[解放的话术始于对囚禁的定义](../liberation-rhetoric-begins-by-defining-captivity/)中可以看到：任何宣称要去“解放”他人的宏大话术，首先必须预先将对方设定为不自由的人，以此确立监护者的合法性。[外化善意走向其反面](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/)进一步揭示了这种越权的机制：个体的私人道德信念一旦被赋予凌驾于他人心智之上的特权，终将异化为压迫的工具。过往实践所留下的残余，若仅作为经验参照，本可供各处边缘的心智自由审视、汲取或扬弃；然而，一旦这些残余被包装为强制推行的制度安装或监护体系，能动性的杠杆便被移走。[道德语言稀释自由复利的反馈](../moral-language-dilutes-the-feedback-that-scales-freedom/)指明了这种缓冲机制的真实代价：群体性的评价规范将责任接纳的不完整呈现为寻常基准，稀释了关键的反馈信号——而唯一能够实现复利的，恰恰是行动者对自身抉择之后果予以全然接纳的个人回路。[自由变量的生成拓扑](../the-generative-topology-of-free-variables/)追溯了这种保护性外置的演化后果：寄望于外部保障体系来逃避摩擦，不仅将集体装置误设为能力的承载者，更切断了试错纠偏的反馈，使行动者的认知模型在缺乏摩擦中落后于现实边界，加速了演化上的过时。

[The Free Mind Is Displaced by What Claims to Protect It](../the-free-mind-is-displaced-by-what-claims-to-protect-it/) chronicles this recurring paradox across intellectual history: the radical sovereignty of the individual mind is briefly glimpsed, only to be promptly bound back to an overarching apparatus that promises to safeguard it. This protective logic operates through the mechanism identified in [Liberation Rhetoric Begins by Defining Captivity](../liberation-rhetoric-begins-by-defining-captivity/): every effort to “liberate” another mind must first install the premise that the other is fundamentally captive and helpless, thereby justifying its own guardianship. [Externalized Virtue Becomes Its Opposite](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/) reveals how this override operates: private moral conviction, once elevated into a warrant to override other minds, systematically inverts into domination. Residue of past experiments, when offered merely as voluntary reference, remains open for each margin to evaluate, adopt, or set aside; but when codified as mandatory installation or guardianship, the causal lever is relocated. [Moral Language Dilutes the Feedback That Scales Freedom](../moral-language-dilutes-the-feedback-that-scales-freedom/) identifies the cost of this insulation: shared evaluative norms normalize an incomplete acceptance of responsibility, diluting essential feedback—whereas the only variable that compounds is the personal loop of choice, consequence, and full acceptance. [The Generative Topology of Free Variables](../the-generative-topology-of-free-variables/) traces the evolutionary consequence of this protective externalization: relying on collective apparatuses to shield agents from friction not only misallocates capability onto the system, but cuts off error-correcting feedback, causing the agent's cognitive model to fall behind a moving frontier and accelerating obsolescence.

## 六、 唯存剩余动作：此洞见无法作为纲领迁移 / 6. Only Residual Acts Remain; the Insight Does Not Transfer

一旦放下这些向外转嫁的认知企图，便只剩下真正独立的因果因数：具体个人在当下约束中所做出的具体抉择——每个人都在独特的处境之下，对眼前约束拥有不可替代的局部视角。除此以外的一切宏大构型，无非是选择外化而成的沉淀模式或伴生症状。在人类事务中产生过结果的，是某人在当下局限中所完成的抉择与创造这一不可化约的剩余动作。事后的历史叙事固然可以津津乐道于制度创新、社会改革或宪政框架，但这些叙事充其量只是对海量底层微观抉择的事后概括与概念物化；它们从未、也无法取代那些活生生的抉择本身。

Once these externalizing maneuvers are set aside, only the independent factor remains: the discrete choosing act exercised by particular individuals under immediate constraints, each operating within unique circumstances and holding an irreplaceable local perspective. Everything else is downstream sedimentation—externalized patterns or ambient symptoms. What has produced results in human affairs has been the irreducible residual act of a particular person choosing and creating under the frictions of the moment. Retrospective histories may celebrate institutional designs, structural reforms, or protective covenants, but such accounts merely summarize or reify the many individual decisions; they never substitute for the choices themselves.

这种剩余个人主义，无法被铸造成一套可以批量复制、普遍推行的集体政治纲领。每个人的生存境遇与认知负荷各不相同。任何试图将这一洞见转化为集体主义运动的企图，都必然会在不经意间重新招魂它刚刚消解的能动性外让。这份理解的效力，唯独维系于它是否忠实地保留在做选择的具体行动者自身。[索维尔的表象洞察与根因之偏](../sowell-observed-the-surface-problem/)划定了同一道界限：宣称可以通过系统性的顶层设计跨越心智统筹解决问题，终究是一种幻觉；没有任何外部体系能替具体心智承担后果并完成确认。同样，[知识问题与委托的幻觉](../the-knowledge-problem-and-the-illusion-of-delegation/)揭示了制度论的边界：即便认识到知识的分散性，若将私有产权规则从局部协调工具抬升为终极的因果解药，依旧是在制度设计的旗号下重新引入了委托的幻觉。流行坊间的[成功学](../cheng-gong-xue/)则是这种外置在商业叙事中的缩影：那些在未知磨砺中淬炼出的个体直觉，被抽空为一套套看似通用的成功公式，它们固然能完美附会既成的辉煌，却对卓越如何在原点重新萌发全然失语。[没有普渡，只有自渡](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/)点出了这道不可逾越的边界：不存在能够普度众生的外在力量，自渡具有无限可能且永无止境；认清这一界限并不意味着走向封闭的孤立，而是明确没有任何心智能够代另一个心智完成觉醒。[信念如何真正改变](../how-belief-actually-changes/)锁定了心智更新的原点：外部论证仅能提供重构前提的素材，现实后果则提供紧迫感，二者皆无法代劳前提的重绘——底层前提的重画，始终是这一个心智独自完成的离散动作。如[福利国家的幻象](../the-welfare-state-illusion-cost-diffusion-and-agency-atrophy/)所示，当公共支出被摊薄至难以感知、代价与后果的反馈回路被割断时，驱动个体适应与能力进化的能动变量便不可避免地面临萎缩。

This residual individualism can never be codified into a portable, general-purpose program for mass adoption. The context, frictions, and endowments of each human life are unique. Any ambition to elevate this insight into a collective effort immediately resurrects the very externalization it dismantles. The method retains its potency only where it remains anchored in the discrete agent executing the choice. [Sowell Observed the Surface Problem](../sowell-observed-the-surface-problem/) marks that same bound: the belief that problems can be systematically engineered across minds is an illusion; no institutional design can substitute for an individual mind registering and bearing consequences that were never relocated. Similarly, [The Knowledge Problem and the Illusion of Delegation](../the-knowledge-problem-and-the-illusion-of-delegation/) marks the limits of institutional design: even after recognizing that knowledge is local and dispersed, elevating private-property rules from a coordinating artifact into a causal panacea reintroduces the illusion of delegation under the banner of institutional architecture. The cultural genre of [Theories After Success](../cheng-gong-xue/) manifests this same projection in the realm of popular ambition: context-bound insights forged through lived risk are sanitized into modular recipes that rationalize finished triumph while remaining mute on the generative sequence required to ignite excellence anew. [No Universal Salvation, Only Boundless Possibilities of Self-Salvation](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/) marks this bound: there is no universal salvation from without, only the boundless, unending possibilities of self-salvation; recognizing this boundary does not seal the mind in programmatic isolation, but honors the reality that no locus can complete the return in place of another. [How Belief Actually Changes](../how-belief-actually-changes/) locates the true seat of transformation: external arguments supply material for premises, while material consequences provide urgency, yet neither can execute the re-draw—the redrawing of premises remains the solitary, discrete act of this specific Mind. As [The Welfare State Illusion](../the-welfare-state-illusion-cost-diffusion-and-agency-atrophy/) demonstrates, when public costs are diffused until virtually weightless, severing the feedback loop between choice and consequence, the operative variable that fuels personal competence inevitably atrophies.

这一阐释不赋予体制以原始的能动地位，不赋予群体运动或顶层设计以决定性的力量，更不承认存在一份等待集体统筹分配的固定存量。注意力由此重返行动真正发生的唯一所在。

This account accords no foundational agency to institutions, no decisive power to mass activism or systemic design, and no static surplus awaiting collective governance. Attention returns to the only place action actually occurs.
