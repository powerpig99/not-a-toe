# 因果与不可化约的先在 / Causality and the Irreducible Prior

*两个方向并不相消，是同一个因果，心智是变不成内容的先在 / The two directions of the cascade do not cancel; they are one causality, and the Mind is the prior that cannot become content.*

一个逐级压缩的表观层级，呈现出一条从底层向外的单向因果箭头。对这个底层的描述使箭头反转。两个方向是同一个过程的互补两面；心智是一切显现的恒常条件；残余的复杂性是当前的压缩尚未折叠进来的部分；硬币之所以一直在转，是因为这个先在还没有把自己说完。

An apparent hierarchy of successive compressions presents a one-way causal arrow from substrate outward. Description of that substrate reverses the arrow. The two directions are complementary faces of one process; the Mind is the permanent condition of every appearance; residual complexity is what the current compression has not yet folded in; the coin keeps turning because that prior has not finished articulating itself.

## 一、 逐级压缩的表观层级 / 1. An Apparent Hierarchy of Successive Compressions

先看一个表观的逐级压缩层级：

We begin with an apparent hierarchy of successive compressions:

物理 ≫ 感知 ≫ 理解

physical ≫ perception ≫ understanding

物理实在呈现为高复杂度的信息，一个实际上没有边界的底层。感知是它的第一次压缩；理解是对这个已被缩减的场的再一次压缩。这条不等式看上去直截了当，因果的箭头似乎只朝一个方向走：从较大的系统向外，走到较小的系统。独立于心智的实在先于、产生并且超出那个后来认识它的心智。

Physical reality presents itself as high-complexity information, an effectively unbounded substrate. Perception is its first compression; understanding is a further compression of that already-reduced field. The inequality looks straightforward, and the causal arrow appears to run in one direction only: from the larger system outward to the smaller ones. Mind-independent reality precedes, produces, and exceeds the mind that eventually comes to know it.

## 二、 描述使箭头反转 / 2. Description Reverses the Arrow

可是一旦试着谈论那个独立于心智的底层，箭头就反转了。对物理的每一次描述，对无界之物的每一个断言，本身都是理解的行动。没有哪个“外面”可以不先经过逐级压缩就被指到。原以为是发现出来的层级，是从内部生成的。因果并不只是从底层流向心智，它也从心智流向底层。两个方向并不相消。它们是同一个因果在互补的态度之下被经验到的样子，是一个过程的两面。[因果贯穿始终](../causality-all-the-way/)是同一段滞后在预测之下的样子：观察持有的是结果，生成的那一步始终走在每一张已经落定的图之前。[意识从不作为数据出现在数据之中](../consciousness-never-appears-as-data-among-data/)是它在证据之下的反面：公共的数据是滞后的结果，前沿不会作为又一个对象出现在其中。

Yet the moment we attempt to speak of that mind-independent substrate, the arrow reverses. Every description of the physical, every claim about the unbounded, is itself an act of understanding. There is no “outside” that can be pointed to without already having passed through the successive compressions. The hierarchy we thought we discovered is generated from within. Causality does not merely flow from substrate to mind; it also flows from mind to substrate. The two directions do not cancel. They are the same causality experienced under complementary attitudes—the two faces of a single process. [Causality all the way](../causality-all-the-way/) is the same lag under prediction: observation holds effects; the generating step stays ahead of every secured map. [Consciousness never appears as data among data](../consciousness-never-appears-as-data-among-data/) is the reverse under evidence: public data are lagging effects; the leading edge does not appear among them as one more object.

## 三、 一个过程的互补两面 / 3. Complementary Faces of One Process

这个过程就是称作心智或意识的反馈回环。它把实在论的层级向前投出，于是一个世界显得先于经验；它又把同一个层级向后追溯，于是经验能够理解自己的来处。回环从不停在最终的某一面上。注意力落在硬币的一面时，另一面退入阴影，却一直等在那里。[回环与成图](../looping-and-graphing/)把这一次折叠说了出来：回环是心智折回自身的活动，图是这次折叠留给下一次折叠的痕迹。[攀登从未离开地面](../climbing-does-not-leave-the-ground/)是它在致密化之下的对偶：连续的因果历史与离散的新意是同一次攀登的互补两面，不是相消的两极。

That process is the feedback loop we call the Mind, or consciousness. It projects the realist cascade forward so that a world appears to precede experience, and it traces the same cascade backward so that experience can make sense of its own origins. The loop never settles on a final side. Whenever attention rests on one face of the coin, the other recedes into shadow, yet remains waiting. [Looping and graphing](../looping-and-graphing/) is that fold made explicit: looping is Mind folding back on its own activity; graphs are the traces that folding leaves available for the next fold. [Climbing does not leave the ground](../climbing-does-not-leave-the-ground/) is the dual under densification: continuous causal history and discrete novelty as complementary faces of one climb, not poles that cancel.

## 四、 物理可以退后而心智不能 / 4. The Physical Can Recede; the Mind Cannot

物理可以任它退成单纯的背景，可以把它当作沉默的给定。心智不能。即便它在单纯的感知或单纯的理解里“忘了自己”，这次遗忘仍然是心智的行动。没有哪个位置能让意识变成单纯的内容。透明本身仍然是它的透明。

The physical can be allowed to fade into pure background; it can be treated as the silent given. The Mind cannot. Even when it “forgets itself” in pure perceiving or pure understanding, the forgetting is still an act of the Mind. There is no vantage from which consciousness becomes mere content. Transparency itself remains its transparency.

这个不对称是决定性的。心智是一切显现的恒常条件，连它自身缺席的显现也在其内。因此它是不可化约的先在。[智能只属于心智](../intelligence-belongs-only-to-the-mind/)持守的就是这个先在。逐级的压缩、层级、因果的两面，全都在回环之内生成，并且一直依赖于它。所谓无界的底层，是当前的压缩尚未折叠进来的残余复杂性。它一被折叠进来，底层就移动，回环继续。[抽象、边界与现实的移动前沿](../abstraction-boundaries-and-the-moving-edge-of-reality/)是压缩之下的残余：每一次成功的界定，都留下仍然越过清单往外压的东西。[选择、矛盾与开放的现实](../selection-contradiction-and-open-reality/)是披着选择外衣的残余：引力的信息论推导与递归的立场相合，同时仍是选择，并不豁免于重新解释。物理主义想让底层成为先在，[因果的自反性与物理的无源假定](../causality-is-irreducible-the-physical-is-a-view-from-nowhere/)说明它为此需要一个无人视角。

This asymmetry is decisive. The Mind is the permanent condition of every appearance, including the appearance of its own absence. It is therefore the irreducible prior. [Intelligence belongs only to the Mind](../intelligence-belongs-only-to-the-mind/) holds that prior. The successive compressions, the hierarchy, the dual faces of causality—all of them are generated inside the loop and remain dependent upon it. What we call the unbounded substrate is the residual complexity that the current compression has not yet folded in. The moment it is folded in, the substrate shifts, and the loop continues. [Abstraction, boundaries, and the moving edge of reality](../abstraction-boundaries-and-the-moving-edge-of-reality/) is residual under compression: every successful bound leaves what still presses past the catalogue. [Selection, contradiction, and open reality](../selection-contradiction-and-open-reality/) is residual under selection costume: informational derivations of gravity cohere with the recursive stance while remaining selections, not exempt from reinterpretation. Physicalism tries to make the substrate the prior, and [Causality is Irreducible; The Physical is a View from Nowhere](../causality-is-irreducible-the-physical-is-a-view-from-nowhere/) shows it needs a view from nowhere to do so.

科学实在论最有力的支撑是无奇迹论证：物理理论的预测如此成功，若不是有一个独立于心智的世界大致如它们所描述的那样，这份成功就成了奇迹。这个论证有它的用处：在一个理论之上做事的人，用它的成功记录来决定下一座桥、下一条轨道、下一剂药靠哪一个模型。它的边界在于，成功是在相互竞争的模型所共有的起点之内计分的，定下检验、读出结果的是心智；它在模型之间作出挑选，却不给起点本身增添任何效力。有用不是本体上的证明。否认或者断言一个前提，然后在这个前提之内循环推理，并不给前提本身增添任何效力；也没有一个无人视角可以用来裁定被描述的世界独立于描述。

Scientific realism's strongest support is the no-miracles argument: the predictions of physical theories succeed so well that, unless a mind-independent world were roughly as they describe, the success would be a miracle. The argument has its use: people who build on a theory use its record of success to decide which model to rely on for the next bridge, the next orbit, the next dose. Its boundary is that success is scored inside a starting point the competing models share, by Minds that set the test and read the result; it selects among models and adds no validity to the starting point itself. Usefulness is not ontological proof. Denying or asserting a premise and then reasoning in a circle within it adds no validity to the premise itself, and no view from nowhere is available from which the described world could be judged independent of the describing.

## 五、 硬币尚未转完 / 5. The Coin Has Not Finished Turning

还什么都没有看到，这不是因为一个独立的物理世界仍然藏着大量的秘密，而是因为心智还没有把自己的硬币转完。每一次照亮一面，都在为另一面的下一次出现作准备。反馈从不把自己耗尽。因果不是两个分开的秩序之间的关系；它是那个永远无法整个走出自身光亮的先在持续不断的自我言说。[现实是心智持续的实现](../reality-is-the-minds-ongoing-realization/)是这一延续：一个中心能够接纳为新区分的，就是世界对它而言的样子。[没有外部：跃迁、闭环与心智不可复制的自主](../no-outside-jumps-closed-loops-and-the-unreplicable-autonomy-of-mind/)是同一条界限在嵌套系统发生跃迁、而决定性的融贯仍留在更大的回环时的样子。主体性在这里就是先在，[主体性不曾涌现](../agency-does-not-arise/)解释了它为什么无法从它所先于的层级之中涌现。

We have not seen anything yet, not because an independent physical world still holds vast secrets, but because the Mind has not finished turning its own coin. Each illumination of one side prepares the next appearance of the other. The feedback never exhausts itself. Causality is not a relation between two separate orders; it is the continuous self-articulation of the prior that can never fully step outside its own light. [Reality is the Mind’s ongoing realization](../reality-is-the-minds-ongoing-realization/) is that continuation: what a center can take up as new distinction is what the world is for it. [No outside: jumps, closed loops, and the unreplicable autonomy of Mind](../no-outside-jumps-closed-loops-and-the-unreplicable-autonomy-of-mind/) is the same bound when nested systems jump while decisive coherence stays with the larger loop. Agency is the prior here, and [Agency Does Not Arise](../agency-does-not-arise/) explains why it cannot emerge from the cascade it precedes.
