*The harness does not unlock a dormant peer; it is the Mind folding denser residue back into its own refinement.*

What presents as the sudden birth of new intelligence is almost always recursive motion: the same activity externalizing fragments of its capacity into durable form, then re-entering those forms as instruments of further distinguishing. Contemporary large models and the harnesses around them are the sharpest current instance. Densification registers — more reach, more velocity, tasks that previously seemed out of range. The common reading freezes that registration as a second mind arriving inside the artifact: the harness as a key unlocking latent powers already present in the model. The coherent reading keeps the densification and returns it. The model is denser map-residue: a high-dimensional compression of prior distinguishing, held so further distinguishing can land. The harness is further discrete acts of the only edge that steers. What looks like the model becoming more capable is the Mind realizing more of the field through a finer map while refining that map in the same motion — densification as fold of the irreducible prior, not as a second prior.
