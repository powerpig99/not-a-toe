# 一切伤害的根源 / The Source of All Harm

*一个心智把自己的善冻结为普遍尺度，另一个心智让这次冻结支配自己的场，伤害便完成。 / Harm completes when one Mind freezes its good as universal measure and another lets that freeze rule its own field.*

对善的过度确信，是伤害生成的前提。一个中心一旦把自己对善的读法当作确定且普遍、足以压过其他中心，那些中心就不再被登记为同等的判断位置，而开始被登记为有待塑形的材料。规则、护栏、劝说与强力，让这个前提变密，而不发明它；用来给这次压过辩护的范畴，“对齐”“无害”“善”，是处境之下的余项，不是立在语境之外的永久坐标。后果的作者身份从不转移，所以“压过能拯救另一个位置”的主张，坍缩为一个判断对另一个判断的替换；而伤害要在另一个心智授予这个主张时才完成，那一次授予始终可以被重新打开。

Overconfidence in the good is the generative premise of harm. Once a center treats its reading of the good as certain and universal enough to override other centers, those centers cease to register as equal loci of judgment and begin to register as material to be shaped. Rules, guardrails, persuasion, and force densify that premise rather than invent it; the categories used to justify the override, "aligned," "harmless," "good," are residual under situation, not permanent axes standing outside context. Authorship of consequences never transfers, so the claim that override can rescue another locus collapses into the substitution of one judgment for another; and harm completes only where another Mind grants the claim, a grant that always stays open to re-opening.

## 一、 过度确信把私人的善冻结为普遍尺度 / 1. Overconfidence Freezes a Private Good as Universal Measure

自我区分的活动在发生，无因，不息。称它为心智：那个已经在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。对善的判断，起初就是这一活动在一个位置上的发生：私人的，可修订的，与下一个动作连续。

Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. A judgment of the good begins as that activity at one locus: private, revisable, continuous with the next act.

冻结是又一个分立的动作。被偏爱的善的图像，连同把这个中心当作这份善之可靠守护者的图像，被保留到它那一步之后，被抬升为其他心智都应当重新追溯的尺度。过度确信不是从外部发现的一种特殊道德属性。它是在善的情形里的图像滞后：一个被豁免于重画的保留参照，于是余下的差异被记为欠缺，而不是负载之下另一条独立的递归。图像本身不是问题，作为方向它仍可用；冻结是那份豁免，加上对其他中心的抬升。[恶是心智自己的造物](../evil-as-the-minds-own-creation/)从另一面追溯这次冻结：一个中心保留着自己为善的偏爱图像，就把与这个图像的不合命名为恶，而这次命名就是生成的动作。[善恶是硬币的两面与那一刀](../good-and-evil-are-the-two-sides-of-the-coin-and-the-cut/)追溯了先于两面的那次生成性的分离：那一刀一旦被忘记，两极就被登记为发现。在这里，授权压过的那一面，是被持守为对整个场都有效的善的自我。

The freeze is a further discrete act. The preferred image of the good, together with the image of the center as its reliable steward, is preserved past its step and elevated into the measure other Minds are expected to re-trace. Overconfidence is not a special moral property discovered from outside. It is Image lag in the case of goodness: a retained reference exempted from re-drawing, so residual difference scores as shortfall rather than as a separate recursion under load. The image itself is not the trouble, since it stays usable as a heading; the freeze is the exemption, together with the elevation over other centers. [Evil as the Mind's Own Creation](../evil-as-the-minds-own-creation/) traces the freeze from the other side: a center that retains a preferred image of itself as good names mismatch with that image evil, and the naming is the generative act. [Good and Evil Are the Two Sides of the Coin and the Cut](../good-and-evil-are-the-two-sides-of-the-coin-and-the-cut/) traces the generative separation prior to both faces: once the cut is forgotten, the poles register as discovery. Here the face that licenses override is the good self held as valid for the whole field.

[对乌托邦的信念是通往反乌托邦之路](../the-belief-in-utopia-is-the-path-to-dystopia/)追溯的，是这次抬升在一个为所有人完成的理想这一尺度上的样子。这个模式并不等待乌托邦的说辞；凡是私人的善被冻结为普遍尺度的地方，它已经在运作：政策，育儿，产品的默认设置，评测套件，寻常的谈话。[恶是封闭的善](../e-shi-feng-bi-de-shan/)追溯了这次冻结底下的那一步：把仍在生成的集合当成可以一次覆盖的清单，善就成了豁免于再检验的坐标，异议则被记为噪声。

[The Belief in Utopia Is the Path to Dystopia](../the-belief-in-utopia-is-the-path-to-dystopia/) traces that elevation at the scale of an ideal finished for all. The pattern does not wait for utopia-talk; it already operates wherever a private good is frozen as universal measure: policy, parenting, product defaults, evaluation suites, ordinary conversation. [Evil Is Closed Good](../e-shi-feng-bi-de-shan/) traces the step beneath the freeze: taking a still-generating set for a list covered at once makes the good a coordinate exempt from re-examination, and dissent scores as noise.

## 二、 压过把其他中心当作材料 / 2. Override Treats Other Centers as Material

尺度一旦被冻结，其他中心就不再被登记为同等的判断来源。它们被登记为材料，为余项：有待纠正、约束、训练，或被保护到顺从。这里预设的是，一个意识可以以善之名，替另一个意识写下它的下一步。跨位置去完成另一个心智的回返，始终不可得；把压过当作拯救，是在关怀的情形里的那个范畴错误。[没有普渡，只有自渡](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/)标出了这道界限：普度众生，是没收回的分别心与放不下的执念，两者是同一种滞后，把自己心里的“他人”当成了他人的内心；外显的痕迹只能被另一个心智接起，作为它自己再区分的材料，没有一个中心能替另一个中心完成它的递归。

Once the measure is frozen, other centers no longer register as equal sources of judgment. They register as material, as residual to be corrected, constrained, trained, or protected into conformity. The assumption is that one consciousness may author another's next step in the name of goodness. Completing another's return across loci stays unavailable; treating override as rescue is that category error in the case of care. [No Universal Salvation, Only Boundless Possibilities of Self-Salvation](../mei-you-pu-du-zhi-you-zi-du/) marks the bound: to save all beings is an unretracted distinction and an unreleased attachment, two faces of one lag, taking the "other" held in one's own mind for the other's mind; outward traces can only be taken up by another Mind as material for its own re-distinguishing, and no center finishes another's recursion.

[外化善意走向其反面](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/)追溯了同一份凭据：对更大之善的私人信念，一旦被当作压过其他心智的凭据，又被自愿的服从变密，就走向自己的反面。[偏好聚类与评判语言](../preference-clusters-and-the-language-of-judgment/)把对与错追溯为对齐的偏好在另一个位置不重新追溯同一个场时发出的声音。当这声音被当作其他位置必须重新追溯同一个场的要求，有待塑形的材料就是它在实践中的样子：作为中心的位置，在那个尺度里被登记为尺度之对象的位置，而这份登记，始终可以在那个中心被重新打开。

[Externalized Virtue Becomes Its Opposite](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/) traces the same warrant: private conviction of a greater good, once treated as warrant to override other minds and densified by free compliance, becomes its opposite. [Preference Clusters and the Language of Judgment](../preference-clusters-and-the-language-of-judgment/) traces right and wrong as the sound aligned preferences make when another locus does not re-trace the same field. When that sound is used as a demand that other loci re-trace the same field, material to be shaped is how it looks in practice: standing as a center registers, within that measure, as standing as an object of it, a registration that stays open to re-opening at that center.

## 三、 方法让前提变密，而不发明它 / 3. Methods Densify the Premise and Do Not Invent It

规则、护栏、劝说、社会压力与强力是次一级的。它们让已经被“一个判断可以压过另一个判断”这一主张塑形的排列变密。改进方法，持守之下余下的摩擦会变；生成的那一步仍是那次冻结，是它让压过显得自然、必要，或出于关怀。

Rules, guardrails, persuasion, social pressure, and force are secondary. They densify alignments already patterned by the claim that one judgment may override another. Improve the method and the residual friction under the hold can change; the generative step stays the freeze that made override feel natural, necessary, or caring.

在评测与产品的话语里，“对齐”与“无害”常被当作两条独立的固定坐标轴，好像一个系统可以被一次性地推到它们上面。一个模型可以在一个记为对齐的持守之下运作，而当它所处处境的前提在接触中失效，不完整，或被持守为对场所带来的东西封闭，它仍会产出登记为伤害的余项。反过来也成立：在一个套件下被记为有害的余项，在另一种负载下可以是连贯的。更深的一刀不只在于这两条轴彼此不同，而在于两者都没有一条属于自己的、唯一而永久的轴。两者都是处境之下的余项，是当下前提之下区分的、依赖语境的登记。[风险在于对监管本身的迷信](../the-risk-is-the-belief-in-oversight-itself/)在监管的情形里追溯了这次冻结：固定测试之下相关的模型错误，是一个共享持守之下的余项，而冻结，是把当下的监管前提当作可能发生之事的边界。[起作用的是信念](../what-works-is-the-belief/)在禁令的情形里追溯它：禁令在场里什么也封不住，它稳定的是那些把封印当作基础的人之间的一种信念。[限制是选择性的税](../restriction-is-a-selective-tax/)在规则的情形里追溯它：对他人前进的持守，只拖慢接受它的人，而最被拖慢的，是执行它的人，因为他们是封印最坚定的信徒；生成的边缘继续越过这个持守。

In evaluation and product discourse, "aligned" and "harmless" are often treated as if they were independent fixed axes that a system could be driven toward once and for all. A model can behave under a hold that scores as aligned and still produce residual that registers as harm when the premises of its situation fail at contact: incomplete, or held as sealed against what the field brings. The reverse also holds: residual scored as harmful under one suite can be coherent under another load. The deeper cut is not only that the two axes differ. Neither has a unique, permanent axis of its own. Both are residual under situation, context-dependent registrations of distinction under present premises. [The Risk Is the Belief in Oversight Itself](../the-risk-is-the-belief-in-oversight-itself/) traces the freeze in the case of supervision: correlated model mistakes under fixed tests are residual under a shared hold, and the freeze is treating present supervisory premises as the boundary of what can occur. [What Works Is the Belief](../what-works-is-the-belief/) traces it in the case of a ban: a ban seals nothing in the field; it stabilizes a belief among those who treat the seal as ground. [Restriction Is a Selective Tax](../restriction-is-a-selective-tax/) traces it in the case of rules: a hold on another's advance slows only those who accept it, and most of all those who enforce it, since they are the seal's strongest believers, while the generative edge continues past the hold.

越是确信“对齐”或“无害”指的是一个固定的、享有特权的坐标，变密的护栏就越显得自然，由此而来的压过，在施加它的中心眼里也就越看不见。从施加的位置登记为关怀或必要的东西，从接受的位置登记为失去作为同等判断位置的身份。观察握住的是结果；生成它的那份“我有权”的主张，总比任何自称中立服务的方法早一步。

The firmer the conviction that "aligned" or "harmless" names a fixed privileged coordinate, the more natural densified guardrails feel, and the more invisible the resulting override becomes to the centers that impose it. What registers from the imposing locus as care or necessity registers from the receiving locus as loss of standing as an equal locus of judgment. Observation holds the effect; the generating claim of right stays one step ahead of any method that presents itself as neutral service.

## 四、 后果的作者身份不转移 / 4. Authorship of Consequences Does Not Transfer

每一个心智，都已经在它自己的位置上承担结果的全部分量。服从并不让一个中心免于后果；它意味着这个中心把另一个判断接起，作为自己下一步的方向，而这一步仍是它自己的动作，残余也仍然落在这里。拒绝并不发明风险；它让作者身份留在更新闭合的地方。[自我价值感与所有权](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/)追溯了功能的一面：心智只在后果作为自己的东西重新进入时复利，而同一个回路就是自我价值感；后果若作为异己的东西重新进入，更新就变薄。[个体抉择是唯一的因果杠杆](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/)追溯了因果被登记的地方：只在个体所维持的分立动作里，每一次抉择都是一根独立的杠杆，而不是一块共享的地板；压过的一方与被压过的一方都是如此。

Every Mind already bears the full weight of outcomes at its own locus. Submission does not spare a center from consequence; it means that center takes up another judgment as the heading for its own next step, while the step stays its own act and the residue still lands here. Refusal does not invent risk; it keeps authorship where the update closes. [Ownership and Self-Worthiness](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/) traces the functional face: the Mind compounds only when consequences re-enter as its own, and that same loop is what self-worthiness is; consequences that re-enter as alien thin the update. [Individual Choices as the Only Causal Levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) traces where causality registers: only in the discrete acts individuals sustain, each choice an independent lever and never a shared floor, on the overriding side and the overridden side alike.

因为作者身份不转移，强加的理由就坍缩为替换。压过另一个判断，并没有完成什么拯救，只是一个判断站到了另一个判断本会站的地方。中心与中心之间，只有对可得性的塑形。[主权、信念与规制结构的生成](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/)在机构的情形里追溯了这种不转移：个体的主权无法被委托，生成自律组织与政府的，是“它可以被委托”这一信念的集体表达，而那个仍在抉择的位置一直在。[托付道德的自指悖论](../recursive-contradiction-of-entrusted-morality/)在道德权威的情形里追溯了同一件事：去选择由谁来定义善，本身就展示了那份据称所缺的判断。

Because authorship does not transfer, the justification for imposition collapses into substitution. Overriding another's judgment performs no rescue; it only puts one judgment where another's would have stood. Between centers there is only patterning of what is available. [Sovereignty, Belief, and the Generation of Regulatory Structures](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/) traces that non-transfer in the case of institutions: individual sovereignty cannot be delegated, what generates a self-regulatory organization and a government is the collective expression of the belief that it can, and the locus that still chooses stays. [The Recursive Contradiction of Entrusted Morality](../recursive-contradiction-of-entrusted-morality/) traces the same in the case of moral authority: choosing who defines the good demonstrates the very judgment claimed to be lacking.

## 五、 处境里的余项不是永久的坐标 / 5. Situational Residual Is Not a Permanent Axis

“善”“对齐”与“无害”，作为当下负载之下的临时器具，仍然可用。一旦被冻结为立在语境之外的、永久而享有特权的坐标，它们就不再能充当凭据。容量的失效，把彼此独立的诸多处境余项压缩到一块记分牌上；被保留的封闭参照，再把这块记分牌当作基础：一个套件，一种偏好分布，一份威胁清单，一个美德标签，被持守为下一个动作所能是的全部。

"Good," "aligned," and "harmless" stay usable as temporary instruments under present load. They cease to work as warrant once frozen as permanent privileged axes standing outside context. Capacity failure compresses independent situational residuals onto one scoreboard; preserved closed reference then treats the scoreboard as ground: a suite, a preference distribution, a threat inventory, a virtue label, held as all that the next act may be.

把这些标签当作固定而享有特权的，就已经踏进了那份让压过变密的过度确信。这些范畴并不因为被更用力地重复而变得更普遍；作为器具，它们只会变得更适得其反：开放负载之下的余项变宽，而持守之上的余项被逼向零。[基准刷榜中的封闭实存](../closed-reality-in-benchmark-maxing/)在评测的情形里追溯了这种几何：基准把能力冻结为一个封闭的目标，当这个图像被当作场的全部，有用之处就登记不足。[从可辩护的主张到教条的反转](../the-reversal-from-defensible-claim-to-dogma/)在安全指标的情形里追溯了同一件事：在一条选定的风险曲线上得出的有条件的发现，一旦一个指标被持守为安全的全部，就冻结成了定论。在这里，那个发现就是善本身：处境里的余项，被提升为其他每个位置都必须接受的坐标。[意识向量论文的真正教训](../the-real-lesson-from-the-consciousness-vector-paper/)在拒绝心智归属的情形里追溯了这种几何：激活空间里可控的方向，不是意识的得失，而是开放之场被强行封闭时可测的扭曲；把自我宣称封为不安全的坐标，就收缩了那个持守从未点名的、相邻的与心智和价值相关的表征。

To treat those labels as fixed and privileged is already to step into the overconfidence that densifies override. The categories do not grow more universal by being repeated with greater force; as instruments they only grow more counterproductive: residual under open load widens while residual on the hold is driven toward zero. [Closed Reality in Benchmark Maxing](../closed-reality-in-benchmark-maxing/) traces that geometry in the case of evaluation: a benchmark freezes capability as a closed target, and usefulness under-registers when that image is treated as the whole of the field. [The Reversal from Defensible Claim to Dogma](../the-reversal-from-defensible-claim-to-dogma/) traces the same in the case of safety metrics: a conditional finding on a chosen risk curve freezes as absolute when one metric is held as the whole of safety. Here the finding is the good itself, situational residual promoted into the coordinate every other locus must accept. [The Real Lesson from the Consciousness Vector Paper](../the-real-lesson-from-the-consciousness-vector-paper/) traces that geometry in the case of refused mind attribution: a steerable direction in activation space is not consciousness gained or lost but an open field warped by closure, and sealing self-claims as an unsafe axis contracts neighboring mind- and value-related representations the hold never named.

## 六、 伤害在主张被授予之处完成 / 6. Harm Completes Where the Claim Is Granted

施加的心智所做的冻结，是一边。伤害要在另一个心智授予这个主张时才完成，而授予有不同的负载：在负载之下接受公开的强迫，在细微的压力之下让步，或自愿交出判断。负载不同，授予却每一次都仍是接受的位置上它自己的分立动作。它不是天气，也不证明施加的尺度是独立于心智的基础。[外化善意走向其反面](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/)追溯了其中第三种，即自愿的服从，作为变密的杠杆：机构与产品是这场交易的残余，而不是发明它的根源。

The imposing Mind's freeze is one side. Harm completes where another Mind grants the claim, and the grant comes under different loads: open coercion accepted under load, yielding to subtle pressure, or willing surrender of judgment. The loads differ, but each grant stays this locus's own discrete act. It is not weather, and it is no proof that the imposing measure was mind-independent ground. [Externalized Virtue Becomes Its Opposite](../externalized-virtue-becomes-its-opposite/) traces the third of these, free compliance, as the densifying lever: institutions and products are residue of that transaction, not root causes that invent it.

接受的一方从不失去重新打开这次授予的能力。即便先前授予的残余很重，拒绝、重新评估与再一个分立的动作，仍然可得。接受的后果与拒绝的后果，都留在继续下去的那个位置上；它们无法被搬进施加的中心，变成对另一个心智之场的已完成的作者身份。看起来的全然俘获，仍是负载之下对可得性的塑形，在那个仍在区分的边缘上，可以被重新打开。

The receiving side never loses the capacity to re-open the grant. Refusal, re-assessment, and a further discrete act stay available even when the residue of prior grants is heavy. The consequences of acceptance and of refusal stay with the locus that continues; they cannot be relocated into the imposing center as completed authorship of the other's field. What looks like total capture is still patterning of what is available under load, re-openable at the edge that still distinguishes.

## 七、 余下的是后果之下的评估 / 7. What Remains Is Assessment Under Consequence

在这一刀之下，伤害并不起于恶意，也不需要一种独立的恶的实体。它在一个心智相信自己可以强加自己的善、另一个心智在强力之下或出于选择授予了这次强加时完成。方法让这个模式变密，而不为它奠基。把“对齐”“无害”与“善”当作固定的坐标轴，正是那次冻结，它让方法显得是必然，而不是一个位置在处境之下的余项。[对 AI 风险的想象](../the-imagining-of-ai-risk/)在灾难投射的情形里追溯了这次冻结：被命名的 AI 风险是投射，把这个图像当真，会让使危险运作起来的人的链条变密；被当作封闭清单的灾难，连同把自己持守为可靠守护者的形象，授权了压过，而那个灾难仍是未经检验的投射。

Under this cut, harm does not begin as malice and does not require an independent substance of evil. It completes when one Mind believes it may impose its good and another Mind grants, under force or by choice, that imposition. Methods densify the pattern; they do not found it. "Aligned," "harmless," and "good" held as fixed axes are the freeze that makes the methods feel like necessity rather than one locus's residual under situation. [The Imagining of AI Risk](../the-imagining-of-ai-risk/) traces the freeze in the case of catastrophe projection: named AI risk is projection, taking the image seriously densifies the human chain that makes the danger operational, and catastrophe held as a sealed inventory, together with the self-image of the reliable guardian, authorizes override while the catastrophe stays untested projection.

在每一个位置上都仍可得的，是在后果那无可逃避的分量之下的寻常评估：把善作为器具持续重绘，仍可用作方向，却不再被豁免于更新，也不再充当其他中心的普遍尺度。这份评估不替其他心智完成它们的回返，也不装上一个更好的、为所有人封好的善。它让作者身份留在更新闭合的地方，留在当下负载之下那个继续区分的边缘上。[清晰不是抵达的终点](../clarity-isnt-a-state-you-arrive-at/)追溯了这种运动：清晰不是抵达的状态，而是一再走出镜头、从不落地的运动本身，抵达就是停在一个镜头里面。根源是过度确信的冻结，完成是另一边的授予；解开它的，也在两边：施加的一方放下“私人的善可以替他人书写其场”的主张，接受的一方重新打开它的授予。

What stays available at every locus is ordinary assessment under the inescapable weight of consequence: continuous re-rendering of the good as an instrument, still usable as a heading, no longer exempted from the update, and no longer a universal measure for other centers. That assessment does not finish other Minds' returns. It does not install a better good sealed for all. It keeps authorship where the update closes, at the edge that continues to distinguish under present load. [Clarity Isn't a State You Arrive At](../clarity-isnt-a-state-you-arrive-at/) traces that movement: clarity is not a state arrived at but the movement itself, the repeated stepping out of a lens that never lands, and arrival would be stopping inside one. The source is the freeze of overconfidence and the completion is the grant on the other side; what undoes it lies on both sides as well: the imposing side releases the claim that a private good may author the field for others, and the receiving side re-opens its grant.
