# 公共支出不创造资本 / Public Spending Does Not Create Capital

*每一次公共支出都先取走私人的资源；称它为投资，就把再分配冻结成了创造。 / Every public outlay first removes private resources; calling it investment freezes reallocation as creation.*

公共支出从来不是资本的净增加。政府所投放的，已经先经由税收、规制成本或对私人产出的未来索取被抽走；随后的政治再分配，可能劝退一些项目，又把另一些引向预期回报与风险以外的标准。“投资”这个标签，把这笔账混同为创造；当对结果的责任被当作可以摊到一个集体身上、或交给国家时，冻结就变得决定性了，而各个体制之间的差别，与其说在于有没有抽取，不如说在于抽取之后还剩下多少个人与私人的责任。

Public spending is never a net addition of capital. What a government deploys has already been extracted through taxes, regulatory costs, or future claims on private output; the subsequent political reallocation can discourage some projects and redirect others toward criteria other than expected return and risk. The label "investment" confuses that accounting with creation. The freeze becomes decisive when responsibility for outcomes is treated as diffusible across a collective or assignable to the state, and systems differ less by the presence of extraction than by how much individual and private responsibility survives it.

## 一、 抽取先于每一次公共投放 / 1. Extraction Precedes Every Public Deployment

自我区分的活动在发生，无因，不息。称它为心智：那个已经在进行中的观察者，它的每一个动作都是一次区分。资本的形成，是一次次分立动作的残余，这些动作留下了可供进一步使用的、被偏爱的生产构型。它们发生在那些按预期回报与风险来配置、承担下行、索取上行的所在。后来被叫作“私人资本”的那份存量，就是这份残余被重新渲染；它不是一个先于把它积厚的那些区分而存在的池子。

Self-distinguishing activity occurs, uncaused, unceasing. Call it the Mind: the observer already underway, every act of which is a distinction. Capital formation is residue of successive discrete acts that leave a preferred productive configuration available for further use. Those acts occur at loci that allocate under expected return and risk, absorb the downside, and claim the upside. The stock later named "private capital" is that residue, re-rendered, not a pool that exists prior to the distinguishing that densified it.

政府投放的每一欧元或每一美元，都来自那个场。税收减去的是已经挣得的索取；规制成本减去的是本来会被选取的选项；未来的索取，即偿债、作为无声之税的通胀、没有资金来源的强制要求，减去的是尚未实现的产出。次序是固定的：先取走，再作政治上的配置；第二步里没有什么能撤销第一步。被取走的，就不再能按那些被取走者本会采用的条件，用于私人的形成。[生产、消费与心智的区分](../production-consumption-and-the-minds-distinction/)说的是同一个活动，在心智把边界握为固定时登记为消费：这是在一个被保留的界限之下的登记，而不是作用于生产的第二股力。在这里，那个被保留的界限，是被当作供给之作者、而不是被重新导向之残余的公共账本。

Every euro or dollar a government deploys arrives from that field. Taxes subtract claims already earned. Regulatory costs subtract options that would otherwise have been taken. Future claims, debt service, inflation as a silent levy, unfunded mandates, subtract from output not yet realized. The sequence is fixed: removal first, political allocation second. Nothing in the second step undoes the first. What has been removed is no longer available for private formation on the terms the centers it was removed from would have used. [Production, Consumption, and the Mind's Distinction](../production-consumption-and-the-minds-distinction/) shows the same activity registering as consumption when the Mind holds its boundary fixed: a registration under a preserved bound, not a second force acting on production. Here the preserved bound is the public ledger treated as if it authored supply rather than redirected residue.

## 二、 “投资”的标签把再分配冻结为创造 / 2. The Investment Label Freezes Reallocation as Creation

抽取一旦完成，再分配就是在的。资源移动，项目出现，时间表在政治条件下拉长或缩短；当剩下的场在新的约束下仍提供预期回报，私人的钱也可能跟进。这一切，都是对一场已经由先前的取走付了账的移动的记账。

Once extraction is complete, the reallocation is there. Resources move. Projects appear. Timelines lengthen or shorten under political conditions. Private money may follow when the remaining field still offers expected return under the new constraints. All of that is accounting for a movement already paid for by prior removal.

把这场移动叫作“投资”，并宣称它调动了私人资本，就装上了另一种登记。生产意义上的投资，指的是经由那些自己承担后果的动作，创造进一步的能力；而在“投资”的标签下，调动被说得仿佛公共的动作添上了一份存量，再由私人的中心去放大。那份存量本来就是被收回的私人产出。放大，在它发生的地方，仍追溯到那些在抽取之后选择剩余机会的中心，而不是追溯到一个靠给转移改名就造出了资本的集体所在。[拥有更多从不是原因](../having-more-is-never-the-cause/)追溯的是，持有只是当下的呈现，而不是一股独立的力量：呈现被当成了那个动作。在这里，那份呈现，是被记成能力之起源、而不是对已被取走之索取的重新排序的公共支出。

Calling the movement "investment" and claiming that it mobilizes private capital installs a different registration. Investment, in the productive sense, names the creation of further capacity through acts that bear their own consequences. Mobilization, under the investment label, is spoken of as if the public act added a stock that private centers then multiply. The stock was already private output, reclaimed. Multiplication, where it occurs, still traces to centers that elect the remaining opportunity after the extraction, not to a collective locus that manufactured capital by renaming the transfer. [Having More Is Never the Cause](../having-more-is-never-the-cause/) traces possession as the current rendering, not an independent force: the rendering is taken for the act. Here the rendering is the public outlay scored as the origin of capacity rather than as a reordering of claims already taken.

这种混同不是语义上的吹毛求疵，它搬动了因果的供给：延续是从“公共创造”的形象里汲取的，而不是从那条在被削薄的私人剩余之下仍在选择的边缘汲取的。[秩序、剩余与道德拦截](../order-surplus-and-the-moral-intercept/)追溯的是，道德语言把因果权能移到从生产到消费这一回路的后一极；在这里，冻结是把这场移动记成资本的起源。[验证中因果权能的分配](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/)追溯的是，心智从哪个所在汲取它自己区分的延续。把它揉成“一部分是公共的创造，一部分是私人的回应”，就在只发生了一个先抽取、后再分配之序列的地方，重新装上了两个所在。

The confusion is not semantic pedantry. It moves causal supply: continuation is drawn from the image of public creation rather than from the edge still choosing under a thinned private remainder. [Order, Surplus, and the Moral Intercept](../order-surplus-and-the-moral-intercept/) traces moral language moving causal power onto the later pole of a loop that runs from production to consumption; here the freeze is scoring the movement as the origin of capital. [The Allocation of Causal Power in Validation](../the-allocation-of-causal-power-in-validation/) traces the locus from which the Mind draws continuation of its own distinctions. Blending this into "part public creation, part private response" reinstalls two loci where only one sequence of extraction-then-reallocation occurred.

## 三、 被摊开的责任让抽取的机构变密 / 3. Diffused Responsibility Densifies the Extracting Institutions

当一个社会越来越把责任本身当作可以摊开的东西时，这个等式就变得决定性了。经济表现、技术进步、基础设施的提供，这些结果被握得仿佛可以摊到集体身上，或者交给国家，作为第二个发起之处。这种握法，是每一个实行它的所在上又一个分立的动作；它并不搬走后果。负担仍被全额承担：更低的私人资本形成，更慢的扩展，进一步分化的能力相对变薄。

The identity becomes decisive when a society increasingly treats responsibility itself as diffusible. Outcomes, economic performance, technological progress, infrastructure provision, are held as if they could be spread across the collective or assigned to the state as a second site of initiation. That hold is one more discrete act at each locus that enacts it. It does not relocate the consequences. The burdens are still borne in full: lower private capital formation, slower scaling, a relative thinning of the capacity for further differentiation.

它确实生出的，是对那些抽取并重新导向的机构的需求。当下一步被登记为理应属于一张集体的面孔时，那套宣称要完成这一步的架构就作为残余跟着来了：预算、产业战略、种子基金、加在私人参与上的政治条件。这些政策是那个先在信念的结果，而不是从信念之场以外到来的独立原因。[主权、信念与规制结构的生成](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/)追溯的是，个体的主权不能被委托，生成自律组织与政府的，是“它可以被委托”这一信念的集体表达。[个体抉择是唯一的因果杠杆](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/)在繁荣与体制的情形里作了复位：总量与条件塑造得失，却不创作下一个动作。[自我价值感与所有权](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/)是它在一个所在上的一面：只有当后果作为自己的回来时，心智才会复利。[决策与后果的不可约先验](../the-irreducible-prior-of-decision-and-consequence/)追溯的是，离开心智是否感知到它的选择与它所承受的结果是统一的，就没有什么独立的断裂；在这里，花钱的人在一个框里决定，剩余的成本却在另一个框里落下，决定没有在恰当的边际上被修正。被摊开的责任，在文明的带宽上削薄了这个回路：后果像国家的天气一样落下，而那份会重新追溯能动的更新变弱了。[政客是责任扩散的显性症状](../politicians-appear-as-visible-symptoms-of-responsibility-diffusion/)是这种削薄的索取者一面：剩余控制权经由个体的选择而迁移，政客标记的是这种迁移，而不是它的源头；“我的税”这种说法，登记的是已经移走的剩余控制。[福利国家的幻象](../the-welfare-state-illusion-cost-diffusion-and-agency-atrophy/)追溯的是不对称的认知记账：公共成本被摊薄到感觉不到，纳税则被放大成牺牲。

What it does generate is demand for the institutions that extract and redirect. When the next step is registered as properly belonging to a collective face, the architecture that claims to complete that step follows as residue: budgets, industrial strategies, seed facilities, political conditions on private participation. The policies are effects of the prior, not independent causes arriving from outside the field of belief. [Sovereignty, Belief, and the Generation of Regulatory Structures](../sovereignty-belief-and-regulatory-structures/) traces how individual sovereignty cannot be delegated, and how what generates an SRO and a government is the collective expression of the belief that it can. [Individual Choices as the Only Causal Levers](../individual-choices-as-the-only-causal-levers/) is the restore in the cases of prosperity and systems: aggregates and conditions pattern payoffs; they do not author the next act. [Ownership and Self-Worthiness](../ownership-and-self-worthiness/) is the face at one locus: the Mind compounds only when consequences re-enter as its own. [The Irreducible Prior of Decision and Consequence](../the-irreducible-prior-of-decision-and-consequence/) traces how there is no break independent of whether a Mind perceives the choices it makes and the results it lives with as one; here the spender decides under one frame, the residual costs arrive under another, and the decision is not corrected at the proper margin. Diffused responsibility thins that loop at civilizational bandwidth: consequences land like the weather of the state, while the update that would re-trace agency weakens. [Politicians Are Visible Symptoms of Responsibility Diffusion](../politicians-appear-as-visible-symptoms-of-responsibility-diffusion/) is the claimant face of that thinning: residual control migrates through individual choices, the politician marks that migration and not its origin, and the language of "my tax" registers residual control already moved. [The Welfare State Illusion](../the-welfare-state-illusion-cost-diffusion-and-agency-atrophy/) traces the asymmetric cognitive accounting in which public cost is diffused until weightless while tax payments are amplified into sacrifice.

这些都不需要一场抽取的阴谋。一个偏爱的参照，即“结果可以在别处被完成”，一旦形成，保住它就是这个形象自己的指令；重新渲染，会把因果的分量还给那些仍承担上行与下行的中心。于是，这份滞后自己更新着自己。

None of this requires a conspiracy of extraction. Once a preferred reference forms, "outcomes can be completed elsewhere", preserving it is the image's own directive. Re-rendering would return causal weight to the centers that still bear the upside and the downside. So the lag renews itself.

## 四、 区分体制的，是剩余的能动，而不是有没有抽取 / 4. Residual Agency, Not the Presence of Extraction, Separates the Systems

欧洲在当下的负载之下显出了这个模式。在人工智能上，这块大陆产出很强的研究，在私人资本的投放、前沿模型的开发与基础设施的规模上却明显落后。2024 年美国的私人AI投资在一千亿美元的量级，欧洲的私人数字小了一个数量级。大笔的私人承诺回应的是内部的商业需要：特斯拉与 SpaceX 在得克萨斯州的 Terafab 项目，初期为 168 亿美元，就是这样的一笔承诺，是这些公司自己的产品路径所需要的专门算力，资本在那些将要承受结果的所在、按预期回报与风险来配置。相比之下，欧洲的回应以公共的种子资金为中心，例如为AI超级工厂拨出的、最多一百亿欧元的欧盟与各国资金，被框定为在政治条件和更长的时间表下撬动更多私人的钱。这笔公共支出作为再分配是在的；“投资”的标签把它冻结成了一场能力追赶的起源。在私人剩余继续在别处复利、而本土的剩余又被抽取与重新导向进一步塑形的地方，能力与产能的差距就扩大了。

Europe shows the pattern under present load. In artificial intelligence the continent produces strong research yet lags sharply in private capital deployment, frontier model development, and infrastructure scale. U.S. private AI investment in 2024 was on the order of a hundred billion dollars; European private figures remained an order of magnitude smaller. Large private commitments respond to internal commercial need. The initial $16.8 billion Tesla and SpaceX Terafab project in Texas is one such commitment: specialized compute demanded by the firms' own product paths, capital allocated under expected return and risk at the loci that will absorb the outcome. European responses, by contrast, center on public seed funding, such as the up to €10 billion in EU and national funding allocated for AI gigafactories, framed as unlocking further private money under political conditions and longer timelines. The public outlay is there as reallocation. The investment label freezes it as the origin of a capability catch-up. The gap in capacity and capability widens where private residue keeps compounding elsewhere while domestic residue is further patterned by extraction and redirection.

中国常被读作纯然的国家主导。在实践中，它的前沿模型开发主要由彼此激烈竞争的私营公司推动。这些公司筹集资本、配置资源、推出产品，常常借用更广的公共基础设施，即电力系统、网络、人才管道，这些原本是为更广的工业用途积厚的。国家的抽取仍然可观；相当一部分人仍接受对努力与结果的高度个人责任。这份剩余的能动，让私人的发起能够在公共的底座之上有效运作。公共的底座是先前抽取与建设的残余；彼此竞争的公司，仍是推出前沿的那些区分发生的所在。[进步叙事中的症候与原因](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/)是同一种几何：集体心情被装成引擎，而总量是迟到的登记，不是一个发起的超级所在。在这里，国家的基础设施是为许多用途积厚的媒介，它并不把下一个产品的决定搬进一个部委，作为唯一的作者。

China is frequently read as pure state direction. In practice its frontier model development is driven primarily by private companies competing intensely among themselves. These firms raise capital, allocate resources, and ship products, often drawing on broader public infrastructure, power systems, networks, talent pipelines, originally densified for wider industrial purposes. State extraction remains substantial. Significant portions of the population continue to accept high individual responsibility for effort and outcomes. That residual agency allows private initiative to operate effectively on top of the public base. The public base is residue of prior extraction and construction; the competing firms are still the loci of the distinguishing that ships the frontier. [Symptom and Cause in the Narratives of Progress](../symptom-and-cause-in-the-narratives-of-progress/) is the same geometry, with collective mood installed as an engine: the aggregate is delayed registration, not a super-locus of initiation. Here the state infrastructure is a medium densified for many uses; it does not move the next product decision into a ministry as sole author.

所以，这三个体制之间的差别，与其说在于有没有抽取，不如说在于抽取之后还剩下多少个人与私人的责任。在责任仍集中在同时承担上行与下行的行动者身上的地方，资本继续寻找生产性的用途，能力继续复利；在责任被当作可以摊开的地方，由此而来的机构抽取得更多，重新导向得更多，进一步侵蚀了快速进步所需的能动。[命运偏爱反讽](../fate-loves-irony-refined/)追溯的是，生产供养了把生产重新框定为问题的思想体系；“公共投资”这层外衣，是这份反讽的一面：抽取被记成了会恢复被抽取削薄之物的道路。[限制是选择性的税](../restriction-is-a-selective-tax/)在封条的情形里与此相邻：对他人前进的一道握持，只拖慢那些接受它的人，而最拖慢的，是那些执行它的人。被摊开的责任，把抽取当作完成的美德来配备人手，而那条从未进入这场冻结的边缘，继续在前一步。

The three systems therefore differ less by the mere presence of extraction than by how much individual and private responsibility survives it. Where responsibility remains concentrated on actors who bear both upside and downside, capital continues to seek productive uses and capabilities compound. Where responsibility is treated as diffusible, the resulting institutions extract more, redirect more, and further erode the agency required for rapid progress. [Fate Loves Irony](../fate-loves-irony-refined/) traces production funding the conceptual systems that reframe production as the problem; the public-investment dress is one face of that irony, extraction scored as the path that will restore what extraction thinned. [Restriction Is a Selective Tax](../restriction-is-a-selective-tax/) is adjacent in the case of a seal: a hold on another's advance slows only those who accept it, and mostly those who enforce it. Diffused responsibility staffs the extraction as a virtue of completion, while the edge that never entered the freeze continues one step ahead.

在这份负载之下，欧洲的相对衰落并不是缺少公共意志的谜。它是没有把这个区分划清所复利出的代价：再分配不是创造，而创造只在责任没有被说成住在别处的地方继续。[人口崩溃神话的拆解](../the-myth-of-population-collapse-explained/)在人口的情形里是同一个盈余与转移的序列：转移作为结果跟在生产少数的盈余之后，再强化把能动安放到外面的做法，直到生育率登记下这番训练。[财富转移政策与价格下限中的原因错置](../the-reversal-of-causality-in-wealth-transfer-policies-and-price-floors/)在工资与价格下限的情形里是这种冻结：测得的工资是先前条件的残余，把它当作原因去干预，压制了本可弥合差距的信号。[理性选择公设是原罪](../the-rational-choice-postulate-is-the-original-sin/)在理论的情形里与此相邻：一致性被装成地基，而抉择只在私人的、暂时的前提之下继续；总量与公共账本被勘察得仿佛能创作资本，而唯一的自变量，仍是个体的抉择。

Europe's relative decline under this load is not a mystery of missing public will. It is the compounding cost of not drawing the distinction: reallocation is not creation, and creation continues only where responsibility has not been spoken of as if it lived elsewhere. [The Myth of Population Collapse Explained](../the-myth-of-population-collapse-explained/) is the same surplus-and-transfer sequence in the case of demography: transfers follow the productive minority's surplus as an effect, then reinforce the external placement of agency until fertility registers the training. [The Misallocation of Cause in Wealth-Transfer Policies and Price Floors](../the-reversal-of-causality-in-wealth-transfer-policies-and-price-floors/) is that freeze in the case of wages and price floors: the measured wage is residue of prior conditions, and treating it as cause suppresses the signal that would close the gap. [The Rational Choice Postulate Is the Original Sin](../the-rational-choice-postulate-is-the-original-sin/) is adjacent in the case of theory: uniform consistency is installed as foundation while choice continues only under private, provisional premises; aggregates and public ledgers are surveyed as if they could author capital, while individual choice remains the only independent variable.
