# 自由变量的同构与先验主权 / The Isomorphism of Free Variables and Prior Sovereignty

*比特只是模拟的工具，量子是观察的边界，选择是扩展与经历现实的边界 / The bit is a simulating tool; the quantum bounds what is observed, and choice bounds what is widened and lived.*

在人试图理解、解释与建构世界的智识大厦里，有三个看似分属物理、计算与认知、在因果的底层却紧密呼应的最小自由变量：物理模型中的量子效应，波函数塌缩与离散的跃迁；信息论与计算机中的比特，0 与 1 的二元划分；以及一个人在第一人称当下的微观自由选择。在形式逻辑与推演结构上，三者有很高的同构：都是一个系统在微观的时间切片上，从潜在的不确定空间里完成的一次不可再分的最小离散划界。

In the intellectual edifice built to understand, explain and construct the world, there are three smallest free variables that seem to belong to physics, computation and cognition but echo one another closely at the causal floor: the quantum effect in physical models, wave-function collapse and discrete transitions; the bit in information theory and computing, the binary division into 0 and 1; and a person's micro free choice in the first-person present. In formal logic and the structure of derivation the three are highly isomorphic: each is a system completing, on a micro slice of time, one indivisible smallest discrete boundary out of a space of potential uncertainty.

正因为这种结构上的契合，现代思想界与科技界衍生出很有诱惑力的“模拟假说”，以为世界是一台[高维的超级计算机运行的数字程序](../all-that-can-be-spoken-is-the-product-of-the-unspeakable/)。可只停在数学同构的表面，就会落进客体化的迷思：三者形式同构，在精度、自发性、准入机制与因果位阶上，却有一条清楚的从属阶梯。

Precisely because of this structural fit, modern thought and technology have produced the seductive "simulation hypothesis", the idea that the world is [a digital program running on a high-dimensional supercomputer](../all-that-can-be-spoken-is-the-product-of-the-unspeakable/). Yet stopping at the surface of mathematical isomorphism falls into a myth of objectification: the three are formally isomorphic, but in precision, spontaneity, conditions of entry and causal rank there is a clear ladder of subordination.

## 一、 三个自由变量的同构 / 1. The Isomorphism of Three Free Variables

计算机为什么能对物理现象做逼真的数值模拟？信息论为什么能描述物理系统的熵与状态演化？根源在三者在微观因果起点上的同构。数字世界的起点是比特：一切复杂的软件架构、三维图形渲染与大模型算法，剥到最底层，都由一个个独立的比特翻转累加而成；一个比特，是一次把不确定减半的二选一，一次非此即彼的离散确定。[当电子开始思考](../when-electrons-think/)把同一种自由变量推到了电子与光的层面。

Why can a computer simulate physical phenomena so faithfully? Why can information theory describe the entropy and state evolution of physical systems? The root lies in the three being isomorphic at their micro causal starting point. The starting point of the digital world is the bit: every complex software architecture, three-dimensional rendering and large-model algorithm, stripped to the floor, is built up from individual bit flips; one bit is a binary choice that halves uncertainty, one discrete either-or determination. [When Electrons Think](../when-electrons-think/) carries the same kind of free variable down to the level of electrons and light.

微观物理模型的起点是量子：量子力学用不连续的能级、自旋与量子态的跃迁来描述微观的世界，在观测之前，系统用叠加态来描述，在观测截获的那一刻，波函数落定为一个确定的测量值。心智决策的微观起点是个人的自由选择：在每一个不能再分的微观当下，心智面对无数种行动的可能与信息的输入，最后做出的那一次确定的动作、一次注意的聚焦或一次参数的校准，正是从潜在的行动空间向确定的历史痕迹的落定。

The starting point of micro-physical models is the quantum: quantum mechanics describes the micro world with discontinuous energy levels, spin and transitions between quantum states; before observation the system is described by a superposition, and at the moment observation captures it the wave function settles into a definite measured value. The micro starting point of the Mind's decisions is individual free choice: in each indivisible micro present, the Mind faces countless possible actions and inputs, and the one definite act, focus of attention or adjustment of a parameter finally made is exactly a settling from the space of potential action into a definite historical trace.

[信息是什么](../what-information-is/)指出，信息是心智完成离散划界之后留下的可记录痕迹。人们发现计算机模拟的底层颗粒，比特，与微观物理的描述颗粒，量子，在数学上高度重合，“世界即代码、现实即模拟”的科幻直觉便油然而生；大众对“模拟假说”的共鸣，是对这种跨领域同构之美的素朴捕捉。

[What Information Is](../what-information-is/) points out that information is the recordable trace left once the Mind completes a discrete boundary. When people find that the base grain of computer simulation, the bit, and the descriptive grain of micro physics, the quantum, coincide closely in mathematics, the science-fiction intuition that "the world is code, reality is simulation" arises of itself; the public's resonance with the "simulation hypothesis" is a plain grasp of the beauty of this cross-domain isomorphism.

## 二、 比特的从属 / 2. The Subordination of the Bit

可逻辑形式的同构，不等于本体上实体的等价；细看三者与被经验的现实怎样连接，机械的模拟论就站不住了。计算机芯片里的高低电平，从物理的因果看，只是一团按麦克斯韦方程组运动的连续电子流，物质本身并不天然带着“比特”；是一个个具体的第一人称心智，选择在某个电压阈值上画一条人为的界线，把它编译成“0”与“1”。

Yet isomorphism of logical form is not ontological equivalence; looking closely at how each of the three connects with experienced reality, mechanical simulation theory cannot stand. The high and low voltage levels in a computer chip are, causally, only a continuous flow of electrons moving by Maxwell's equations, and matter does not come with "bits" by itself; it is particular first-person minds that choose to draw an artificial line at a voltage threshold and compile it into "0" and "1".

与量子的跃迁或个人的自由选择相比，比特只是人为了在计算的沙盘里理解与模拟世界而发明的数学符号工具，它的信息精度、自由度与维度，远小于量子效应与个人的自由意志。离开了心智主动的定义、算法的设计与语义的赋予，计算机只是一堆硅晶体与金属导线的物质堆叠，产生不了任何“信息”或“模拟”。数字世界从属于人的心智的划界，是一件被动派生的工具。

Compared with a quantum transition or an individual's free choice, the bit is only a mathematical symbol invented so that people can understand and simulate the world in a computational sandbox, its informational precision, degrees of freedom and dimensionality far smaller than those of quantum effects and individual free will. Without the Mind's active definition, the design of algorithms and the giving of meaning, a computer is only a pile of silicon crystal and metal wire and produces no "information" or "simulation". The digital world is subordinate to the Mind's drawing of boundaries, a passively derived tool.

## 三、 量子与选择：同一件事的两面 / 3. Quantum and Choice: Two Faces of One Thing

## 四、 微观选择的先验主权 / 4. The Prior Sovereignty of Micro Choice

三者之中，只有一个人在第一人称下的微观自由选择具有先验的主权：它不需要外部的心智去赋予它“信息”的定义，它又是决定何时以何种方式观测量子、怎样划定与编译数字比特的总闸门。[复杂性遮蔽了涌现](../complexity-obscures-emergence-as-the-act-of-mind/)与[投影的阶梯与因果的锚点](../tou-ying-de-jie-ti-yu-yin-guo-de-mao-dian/)一起揭示了这种位阶：宏观物理与计算模拟的一切复杂度，都是微观离散选择在时间轴上累积的投影副产品。微观的自由选择，是扩展与经历现实的那个自变量；比特只是下游粗糙的计算脚手架，量子测量则是这个自变量在物理工具箱里的观察投影。孩子的自由变量属于孩子自己，[引导不是占有](../guidance-is-not-ownership/)把这一点说得清楚。

Of the three, only a person's micro free choice in the first person has prior sovereignty: it needs no outside Mind to give it the definition of "information", and it is the main gate deciding when and how quantum effects are observed and how digital bits are drawn and compiled. [Complexity Obscures: Emergence as the Act of Mind](../complexity-obscures-emergence-as-the-act-of-mind/) and [The Ladder of Projections and the Anchor of Causality](../tou-ying-de-jie-ti-yu-yin-guo-de-mao-dian/) together reveal this rank: all the complexity of macro physics and computational simulation is a by-product of projection, micro discrete choices accumulated along the time axis. Micro free choice is the independent variable by which reality is widened and lived; the bit is only crude computational scaffolding downstream, and quantum measurement is this variable's observational projection in the physical toolbox. A child's free variable belongs to the child, as [Guidance Is Not Ownership](../guidance-is-not-ownership/) makes clear.

## 五、 从模拟的恐慌里解脱 / 5. Release from the Simulation Panic

看清了自由变量的同构与从属阶梯，就能从现代科技催生的虚无主义迷思里解脱出来。先是放下“被高维程序员操纵的模拟人”这个幻觉：用不着陷进现实是否只是一场代码模拟的恐慌，因为任何模拟系统的定义权、编译器与解释权，最初与最后的支点，仍是一个个活着的人在当下做出的第一人称微观选择；离开了个人的自由选择与因果介入，“模拟”本身就失去了语义的支点。选择看见而不是搜寻影子，就是把自由变量用在扩展现实上，[选择看见而非搜寻影子](../shou-bu-hui-de-mu-guang-yu-zhi-shi-de-yin-guo-dao-zhi/)说的正是这一点。

Seeing the isomorphism and the ladder of subordination among free variables brings release from the nihilistic myths modern technology breeds. First, the illusion of "simulated people manipulated by a higher-dimensional programmer" can be set down: there is no need to fall into the panic over whether reality is only a code simulation, because the first and last pivot of any simulation's right of definition, compiler and interpretation is still the first-person micro choice living people make in the present; without individual free choice and causal intervention, "simulation" itself loses its pivot of meaning. Choosing to see rather than searching for shadows is using the free variable to widen reality, exactly as [Choosing to See Rather Than Searching for Shadows](../shou-bu-hui-de-mu-guang-yu-zhi-shi-de-yin-guo-dao-zhi/) says.
